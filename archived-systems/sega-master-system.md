# Sega Master System: The Z80 Powerhouse

The Sega Master System, launched in 1985, represents Sega's ambitious entry into the home console wars and their commitment to bringing arcade-quality experiences home. Built around the versatile Z80 processor and featuring advanced graphics capabilities, the Master System showcased what 8-bit hardware could achieve with thoughtful design and creative programming.

## Sega's 8-Bit Philosophy

While Nintendo focused on family-friendly experiences, Sega pursued a different vision:

- **Arcade authenticity**: Bringing Sega's arcade hits home with minimal compromise
- **Technical superiority**: More colors, better sound, and smoother gameplay
- **Mature gaming**: Appealing to older players with sophisticated experiences
- **International appeal**: Creating games that worked worldwide, not just in Japan

## Why Master System Programming Matters

The Master System teaches essential skills that transfer across all 8-bit development:

### Z80 Assembly Excellence
- **Clean instruction set**: Learn assembly on one of the most elegant 8-bit processors
- **Memory management**: Master bank switching and memory optimization
- **Interrupt handling**: Real-time programming with VBlank and HBlank
- **Performance optimization**: Squeeze maximum speed from 3.58MHz

### Advanced Graphics Programming
- **VDP mastery**: Work with one of the most capable 8-bit video processors
- **Sprite systems**: 32 sprites with smooth animation and collision detection
- **Background graphics**: Scrolling tile-based backgrounds with priority control
- **Color management**: 32 simultaneous colors from a palette of 64

### Sound Programming
- **PSG synthesis**: Master the SN76489's 4-channel capabilities
- **Music composition**: Create memorable game soundtracks
- **Sound effects**: Design impactful audio feedback
- **Audio timing**: Synchronize sound with gameplay events

## Technical Specifications

### Core System
- **Z80A CPU at 3.58MHz**: Proven processor used in countless arcade games
- **8KB main RAM**: Efficient memory usage essential
- **16KB VRAM**: Dedicated video memory for smooth graphics
- **32KB ROM limit**: Standard cartridge size with banking for larger games

### Graphics Capabilities
- **Video Display Processor**: Custom VDP with advanced features
- **256x192 resolution**: Clean, crisp display perfect for detailed pixel art
- **32 colors on screen**: From a palette of 64 total colors
- **32 hardware sprites**: Large, colorful sprites with transparency
- **Scrolling backgrounds**: Smooth horizontal and vertical scrolling

### Audio System
- **SN76489 PSG**: Texas Instruments sound generator
- **4 audio channels**: 3 tone generators plus 1 noise channel
- **Volume envelopes**: Dynamic volume control for expressive music
- **Frequency range**: Wide range supporting both music and effects
