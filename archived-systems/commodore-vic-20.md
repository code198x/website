# VIC-20: The Friendly Computer That Started the Home Computing Revolution

The Commodore VIC-20, launched in 1981, holds a special place in computing history as the first computer to sell over one million units. Marketed as "The Friendly Computer," it brought computing to families worldwide and proved that computers could be both accessible and affordable. Despite its severe limitations, the VIC-20 fostered incredible creativity and launched countless programming careers.

## Jack Tramiel's Vision Realized

Commodore's founder Jack Tramiel believed computers should be appliances, not luxury items:

- **Mass market approach**: Price computers like consumer electronics
- **Family accessibility**: Make computers approachable for everyone
- **Retail distribution**: Sell through toy stores and department stores
- **Complete package**: Everything needed to start computing immediately

The VIC-20 embodied this philosophy perfectly, becoming the computer that introduced millions to the digital age.

## The Million-Seller Achievement

The VIC-20's commercial success was unprecedented:

- **First million-seller**: No computer had achieved such sales before
- **Global phenomenon**: Popular in North America, Europe, and Japan
- **Market creation**: Established the home computer market
- **Industry catalyst**: Proved mass-market computing was viable

This success changed how the industry viewed computers, transforming them from business tools to household items.

## Technical Specifications

### Core System
- **MOS Technology 6502**: Classic 8-bit processor at 1.023MHz
- **5KB total RAM**: Only 3.5KB available to user programs
- **20KB ROM**: Contains BASIC interpreter and operating system
- **Cartridge slot**: Expand functionality with ROM cartridges

### Graphics System
- **VIC chip**: Video Interface Chip handles display generation
- **176×184 resolution**: Low resolution but colorful display
- **22×23 character mode**: Text and simple graphics in character cells
- **16 colors**: Full palette available for characters and borders
- **Simple sprites**: Large characters can simulate sprite movement

### Audio System
- **VIC sound**: Three square-wave voices plus noise generator
- **Musical capability**: Simple melodies and sound effects possible
- **Volume control**: Basic amplitude control for each voice
- **Creative potential**: Skilled programmers achieved remarkable audio
