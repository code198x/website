# Tier 7: Strategy Games: Command & Conquer Like Dune II!

Time to think strategically! With **32 tactical lessons**, you'll create real-time strategy games that showcase why the Amiga birthed this entire genre. Command armies, build bases, and crush your enemies!

## What You'll Build

Create **DESERT COMMAND** - an RTS masterpiece featuring:
- Real-time unit control with mouse selection
- Base building with tech trees
- Resource management and economy
- Fog of war that reveals as you explore
- AI opponents with different personalities

## Tier Overview

**Lessons 1-8: "Unit Control" - Command Your Forces**
- Mouse box selection for multiple units
- Group formation and movement
- Unit states and command queuing
- Smooth scrolling across large maps

**Lessons 9-16: "Pathfinding Pro" - Smart Movement**
- A* implementation for optimal paths
- Dynamic obstacle avoidance
- Formation preservation during movement
- Traffic management for many units

**Lessons 17-24: "Base Builder" - Economic Systems**
- Building placement and construction
- Resource gathering and management
- Tech trees and unit production
- Power grids and base defense

**Lessons 25-32: "AI Commander" - Worthy Opponents**
- Strategic AI decision making
- Build order optimization
- Attack pattern variety
- Difficulty scaling that's fair

## Key Concepts

This tier masters real-time strategy:

- **Unit Management** - Controlling armies efficiently
- **Map Awareness** - Fog of war and exploration
- **Economic Balance** - Resources that drive decisions
- **AI Strategy** - Opponents that think ahead
- **Interface Design** - RTS controls that feel natural

By the end, you'll understand why Westwood chose the Amiga for Dune II!
