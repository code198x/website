Showcase your complete hardware mastery! With **32 comprehensive lessons**, you'll create the ultimate ZX Spectrum hardware demonstration that combines everything you've learned about direct programming, optimization, and advanced controls.

- Building a portfolio piece that showcases ultimate expertise
