# Tier 3: Puzzle Games: Brain-Bending Logic & Falling Blocks!

Time to twist those minds! With **32 brain-teasing lessons**, you'll create the kind of puzzle games that make players say "just one more game" at 3 AM - the digital equivalent of being unable to put down a good book.

## What You'll Build

Create **SPECTRUM BLOCKS** - an addictive falling-block puzzle game featuring:

- Tetrominoes that fall with perfect, predictable physics
- Line-clearing explosions with satisfying visual feedback
- Increasing speed that keeps players on their toes
- Score systems that reward strategic thinking
- Special effects that celebrate every cleared line!

## Tier Overview

**Lessons 1-8: "Grid Magic" - Foundation Systems**

- Grid-based coordinate systems that make sense
- Memory-efficient playfield representation
- Block placement and boundary collision detection
- Visual grid rendering with ZX Spectrum flair

**Lessons 9-16: "Rotation Nation" - Block Mechanics**

- Tetromino rotation mathematics that actually work
- Wall-kick systems for fair rotation behavior
- Block preview systems and next-piece display
- Color cycling effects for active pieces

**Lessons 17-24: "Line Clearing Lightning" - Pattern Detection**

- Line detection algorithms that run in real-time
- Smooth line-clearing animations with screen flash
- Multi-line clearing bonuses and scoring systems
- Cascade effects for satisfying chain reactions

**Lessons 25-32: "Addiction Algorithm" - Game Flow & Polish**

- Speed progression that feels natural and fair
- Level systems that provide clear goals
- High score systems with name entry
- Sound effects that make every action feel important

## Key Concepts

This tier masters ZX Spectrum puzzle game development:

- **Grid Systems** - Efficient coordinate mapping and memory usage
- **Pattern Matching** - Fast algorithms for detecting completed lines
- **Rotation Math** - Making geometric transformations feel natural
- **Flow Psychology** - Understanding what makes puzzles addictive
- **Feedback Loops** - Visual and audio cues that keep players engaged

Perfect for creating games in the tradition of Tetris, Columns, and other legendary puzzle classics!
