# Tier 11: Advanced Game Programming: Multi-Player & Social Features

Bring players together! With **32 comprehensive lessons**, you'll create engaging multiplayer experiences that provide social interaction and competitive fun.

## What You'll Build

Create **Party Games Collection** - a suite of local multiplayer games featuring competitive and cooperative modes, social interaction, and the kind of engaging multiplayer fun that brings people together.

## Tier Overview

**Lessons 1-8: Local Multiplayer Fundamentals**

- Multiple controller input handling
- Split-screen and shared-screen design
- Local multiplayer game architecture
- Player identification and management

**Lessons 9-16: Competitive Game Design**

- Head-to-head competitive mechanics
- Scoring and ranking systems
- Tournament and bracket systems
- Skill-based matchmaking simulation

**Lessons 17-24: Cooperative Game Design**

- Team-based gameplay mechanics
- Shared objectives and coordination
- Communication and teamwork systems
- Cooperative puzzle and challenge design

**Lessons 25-32: Social Features & Engagement**

- Player profiles and statistics
- Achievement and badge systems
- Social interaction and communication
- Community building features

## Key Concepts

- **Multiplayer Design** - Creating engaging shared experiences
- **Social Interaction** - Bringing players together through games
- **Balance** - Fair and competitive gameplay
- **Cooperation** - Teamwork and shared objectives
- **Community** - Building lasting player relationships
