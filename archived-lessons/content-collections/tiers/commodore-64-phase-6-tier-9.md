# Tier 9: Commercial Game Development: Publishing & Distribution

Go professional! With **32 comprehensive lessons**, you'll learn the business side of game development, creating polished, marketable games ready for commercial distribution.

## What You'll Build

Transform your games into commercial products with professional polish, marketing materials, user testing feedback, distribution packages, and the presentation quality needed for commercial success.

## Tier Overview

**Lessons 1-8: Professional Polish & Quality**

- Game testing and quality assurance
- Bug fixing and performance optimization
- Professional user interface design
- Accessibility and user experience

**Lessons 9-16: Marketing & Presentation**

- Screenshots and promotional materials
- Game trailers and demonstration videos
- Press kits and media presentation
- Social media and community building

**Lessons 17-24: User Testing & Feedback**

- User testing methodologies
- Feedback collection and analysis
- Iterative improvement processes
- Player experience optimization

**Lessons 25-32: Distribution & Publishing**

- Game packaging and distribution
- Documentation and user manuals
- Publishing platform preparation
- Commercial release planning

## Key Concepts

This tier develops commercial game development skills:

- **Professional Polish** - Commercial-quality game presentation
- **Marketing Skills** - Promoting and presenting games effectively
- **User Focus** - Creating games players actually want
- **Distribution** - Getting games to market successfully
- **Business Skills** - Understanding the commercial side of development

These commercial skills bridge the gap between hobby and professional development!
