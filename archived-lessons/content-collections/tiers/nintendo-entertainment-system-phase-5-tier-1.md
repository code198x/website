# Tier 1: Shoot 'Em Up Games: Blast Into 8-Bit Glory!

Welcome to bullet heaven! With **32 action-packed lessons**, you'll create the kind of shoot 'em ups that defined the NES era - games that made players lean left and right on their couches as if that would help dodge bullets!

## What You'll Build

Create **STARFORCE OMEGA** - a vertical scrolling shooter featuring:
- Enemy waves that'll test your sprite management skills
- Bullet patterns inspired by the arcade classics
- Power-ups that make you feel invincible (briefly)
- Boss battles that push the NES to its limits
- That satisfying "pew pew" that only 8-bit can deliver!

## Tier Overview

**Lessons 1-8: "Sprite Juggling Act" - Managing the Madness**
- Why NES sprite flicker is actually a feature (seriously!)
- Cycling sprites like a pro to show more than 8 per scanline
- Priority systems that keep important sprites visible
- The art of making limitations work for you

**Lessons 9-16: "Scrolling Like a Boss" - Background Magic**
- Smooth vertical scrolling that rivals the arcade
- Parallax effects using clever PPU tricks
- Creating the illusion of depth on a 2D system
- Star fields that make space feel infinite

**Lessons 17-24: "Bullet Ballet" - Pattern Programming**
- Circular spreads, aimed shots, and wave patterns
- Enemy AI that's predictable but challenging
- Collision detection that's pixel-perfect yet fast
- Making every bullet count (literally, due to sprite limits!)

**Lessons 25-32: "Power-Up Paradise" - Game Feel Perfection**
- Weapon upgrades that change your whole strategy
- Screen-clearing bombs for those "oh no!" moments
- Score systems that encourage risky play
- Boss patterns inspired by Gradius and R-Type

## Key Concepts

This tier teaches you the fundamentals of NES action games:

- **Sprite Management** - Making 64 sprites feel like 640 (with clever tricks!)
- **PPU Mastery** - Scrolling, split-screens, and visual effects
- **Game Feel** - That perfect balance of challenge and fairness
- **Performance** - Keeping it smooth at 60 FPS (NTSC) no matter what
- **Audio Impact** - Making the NES sound chip scream (in a good way)

By the end, you'll understand why shoot 'em ups were perfect for the NES hardware! 🚀
