# Audio Lab

**Audio Lab** unlocks the revolutionary audio capabilities of the Commodore Amiga, creating a sophisticated digital audio workstation that demonstrates the system's pioneering multimedia programming potential.

## The Game Concept

Build a professional-quality digital audio workstation:

- **4-Channel Composition**: Create rich stereo music with full channel control
- **Digital Sampling**: Record and manipulate real-world audio
- **Effects Processing**: Apply reverb, echo, and advanced audio effects
- **Sequence Programming**: Build complex musical arrangements
- **Professional Output**: Export studio-quality audio files

## Why This Game for Tiers 5-8?

**Audio Lab** introduces advanced 68000 assembly multimedia programming:

- **Digital Audio**: Learn the Amiga's pioneering audio capabilities through assembly
- **Real-Time Processing**: Create responsive, low-latency audio systems in assembly
- **Advanced Algorithms**: Implement sophisticated audio processing via 68000 assembly
- **Professional Interfaces**: Build complex, multi-window applications in assembly
- **System Integration**: Work with AmigaOS multimedia capabilities through assembly

## Amiga Audio Revolution

Leverages the Amiga's groundbreaking audio system:

- **Paula Chip**: Direct programming of the custom audio processor
- **4-Channel Stereo**: True stereo with independent channel control
- **8-Bit Sampling**: Digital audio recording and playback
- **Hardware Mixing**: Real-time audio channel mixing
- **Professional Quality**: CD-quality audio output capabilities
