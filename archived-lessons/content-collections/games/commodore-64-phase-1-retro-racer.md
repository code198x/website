# Retro Racer

**Retro Racer** is your masterpiece - a complete, polished racing game that demonstrates professional-level 6502 assembly programming and showcases everything you've learned throughout Phase 1.

## The Game Concept

Create a full-featured racing game that rivals commercial C64 titles:

- **Pseudo-3D Racing**: Perspective road effects that create the illusion of depth
- **Multiple Tracks**: City streets, country roads, desert highways - each with unique challenges
- **Intelligent Opponents**: AI cars with different driving styles and difficulty levels
- **Championship Mode**: Season-long competition with points, rankings, and progression
- **Professional Polish**: Title screens, music, sound effects, and smooth gameplay

## Why This Game for Tiers 13-16?

**Retro Racer** represents the culmination of Phase 1 learning:

- **Advanced Graphics**: Smooth scrolling and perspective effects using 6502 assembly
- **Real-time Programming**: Maintaining 50/60 FPS gameplay with optimised assembly
- **Game Architecture**: Managing multiple systems through efficient assembly routines
- **Optimization Mastery**: Achieving professional performance with assembly language
- **Complete Development**: From concept to finished, distributable assembly software

## C64 Racing Game Techniques

Implement classic C64 racing game methods:

- **Character-based Road**: Using PETSCII characters for road segments and perspective
- **Color Cycling**: Creating motion effects through palette manipulation
- **Sprite Multiplexing**: Managing multiple cars on screen simultaneously
- **Sound Integration**: Engine sounds, collision effects, and background music
- **Memory Management**: Efficiently handling multiple tracks and game data
