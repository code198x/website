**Your neon world needs incredible power-ups!** In the last lesson, you added victory progression and score tracking to your combat arena. Today, you'll create power-up and enhancement systems - collectible amplifiers, temporary boosts, and permanent upgrades that transform your progression experience into an engaging power amplification adventure!
