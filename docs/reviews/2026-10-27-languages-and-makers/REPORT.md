## Sources and limits

The articles cite original manuals, papers, signed technical writing and interviews. Period reviews independently corroborate released games and credit assignments where available. Participant memories remain attributed. Cadaver and Pasi Ojala are profiles of documented work supported chiefly by their own technical publications; independent biographical evidence is limited. No unsupported birth dates, current employment, sales totals or sole-inventor claims were added.

Important checks:

- The original *40 Best Machine Code Routines* names **John Hardman and Andrew Hewson**, with a 1982 first-edition statement. The cover and title page were visually checked.
- *Inside the Dragon* credits Duncan Smeed and Ian Sommerville; its cover design is credited to David John Rowe. The original title/copyright pages and hardware discussion were consulted.
- The BBC *Advanced User Guide* has three authors: Andrew C. Bray, Adrian C. Dickens and Mark A. Holmes. The consulted copy identifies its 2017 remaster and January 2021 revision; it is not described as an untouched 1983 scan.
- *De Re Atari* chapter credits come from its preface. L. Cross’s 1981 cover signature is not treated as proof of the release date of every copy or chapter.
- Mical’s Intuition manual credit includes **Susan Deyl**. Lynx and Amiga work retains the surrounding collaborators.
- The 1973 Pascal report and 1974 Pascal-P implementation notes were inspected visually because their PDF text extraction is empty. The latter documents a compiler for a subset targeting a hypothetical stack computer; it does not define all Pascal implementations.
- The Squeak implementation paper credits Ingalls, Kaehler, Maloney, Wallace and Kay together. Scratch 1.4’s original source README explicitly states the Squeak dependency; later Scratch versions are not generalised from it.
- The original Zynaps account supports 25-frame display work, 50 Hz interrupt synchronisation, priority scheduling and Steve Turner’s music credit. It is not used to assert an unverified music update rate.
- Joseph’s contemporary Speedball 2/Gods coverage separates Nation 12/Simon Rogers composition from Amiga adaptation/programming. Brimble’s channel-allocation example is scoped to Colonization.
- Koshiro’s Music Love recollection is on page 2 of the 14 October 2005 Kikizo interview; the article links directly to that page.

`entries.json` records the source groups and final source-file hashes. `source-fingerprints.json` identifies 20 consulted digital copies without distributing their copyrighted contents or private library locations. Original period sources also have issue/page locators in each article.
