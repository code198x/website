# Spacewar article imagery

The two header WebP sizes are original contemporary editorial illustration, generated through the built-in image_gen tool on 18 October 2026. They are not archival evidence, game frames or a reconstruction of the original display. The exact prompt, tool and transformations are in header-prompt.txt/provenance.json and each WebP's metadata sidecar. The source is 2172 × 724; derivatives use WebP conversion and proportional resizing.

The three captures and four movement diagrams are copied byte for byte from the canonical documentation study at the revision in src/previews/spacewar/source.json. Capture sidecars record origin without altering the PNG evidence. Spacewar! (MIT contributors, 1962, PDP-1) and Norbert Landsteiner's emulator retain their own rights. The capture-credits preview includes full original/emulator contributor credits, conditions and purpose; the documentation source supplies the complete provenance. Do not offer game imagery under the licence for original article prose and diagrams.

No book cover, magazine scan, game binary, audio or video is bundled. Do not edit generated MDX or evidence images independently; use the documented sync script.
