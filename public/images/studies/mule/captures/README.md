# Own captures

`manifest.json` records the original Atari setup frame. `c64-manifest.json` records the later C64 development, production and auction frames from a zero-human Beginner computer demonstration. These are separate software/configuration observations, not evidence of a completed match or human group play.

Full C64 frames are copied without alteration; the original Atari frame retains its recorded crop. Underlying game rights remain with their owners. Selected frames illustrate the study’s criticism and review; they do not grant an open image licence. No program, firmware, save-state or full recording is supplied.
