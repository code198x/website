# Code198x product context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Code198x is for everyone, with independent routes through playing, building and understanding. Beginners, experienced practitioners, families and teachers can use its content; programming is not a condition of belonging.

## Product Purpose

Explore games, computing, creativity and their human history. The current charter and authoring guidance are owned by [code198x/docs](https://github.com/code198x/docs/blob/main/PROJECT.md), not replaced by this design-context record.

## Operating Context

The existing Astro site supplies system lessons, shared Craft material, the Vault and editorial writing. Vintage systems remain the current delivery focus. Whole-game studies are being developed in documentation; their eventual site home remains undecided.

## Capabilities and Constraints

This task extends the existing editorial article composition. The owner approved a prototype and draft website change, not merging or deployment. Preserve the shared 1264px outer frame and existing page defaults. Use original labelled editorial artwork and verified, credited captures; do not present illustration as archival evidence. No new dependencies are authorised. Interactive Spacewar examples remain deferred.

## Brand Commitments

Play · Build · Understand. Enthusiastic, concrete British English; explain unfamiliar ideas without assuming prior knowledge or nostalgia. Preserve the familiar website identity while supporting ambitious, colourful editorial composition.

## Evidence on Hand

The Spacewar study, its primary-source research notes, three verified emulator captures and four original movement diagrams are retained in documentation draft PR #106. The user confirmed full-bleed openings, quiet integrated breadcrumbs, wider figures and selective pull quotes as the prototype direction. Historical/editorial human review and the publication home remain open.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable text, descriptive figure alternatives and credits, keyboard access and visible focus, strong contrast, responsive reading order and reduced-motion behaviour. Do not require animation or an interactive simulation to follow the study.
