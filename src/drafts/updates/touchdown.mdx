[Touchdown](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/touchdown/) has eleven replacement lessons for the 48K Spectrum. Begin with a falling character, learn to brake with unlimited thrust, then add retry, fuel and sideways steering. A generous practice pad prepares the way for an uneven landscape and the final approach.

Build the keyboard reader, terrain data and custom craft locally, with complete runnable checkpoints throughout. The lessons follow Volley’s movement and drawing work while explaining the BASIC they use. Finish with a game you can save, reopen and share.
