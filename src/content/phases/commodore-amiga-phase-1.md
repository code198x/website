Welcome to Phase 1 of the Commodore Amiga programming course! This phase introduces you to 68000 assembly language programming on one of the most advanced home computers of its era.

## What You'll Learn

In this phase, you'll explore the powerful Motorola 68000 processor and the Amiga's revolutionary custom chips. You'll learn how to create stunning graphics effects, smooth animations, and rich audio that made the Amiga famous in the demoscene and gaming world.

## Course Structure

The phase is divided into 16 tiers, each containing 32 focused lessons. You'll progress from basic 68000 operations to advanced techniques using the copper, blitter, and audio hardware.

Start your journey into Amiga programming and discover why it was years ahead of its competition!
