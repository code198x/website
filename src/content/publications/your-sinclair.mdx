# Your Sinclair

**Your Sinclair** (YS) was the definitive ZX Spectrum magazine, known for its irreverent humor, comprehensive reviews, and the legendary "Megagames" rating system.

## The YS Style

Your Sinclair developed a unique voice that set it apart:

- Witty, self-aware writing style
- Running jokes and recurring characters
- The infamous "crap game" reviews
- Reader interaction through letters and tips

## Essential Features

### The Tipshop

Reader-submitted POKEs, cheats, and hacks that became legendary in the Spectrum community.

### Type-in Listings

Complete game listings that readers could type in themselves, teaching programming by stealth.

### Covertapes

Free cassette tapes mounted on the cover, packed with demos and complete games.

## Cultural Impact

YS wasn't just a magazine - it was a community hub that shaped UK gaming culture. Its writers went on to found Future Publishing and influence gaming journalism for decades.
