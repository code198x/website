[Yearfall](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/yearfall/) puts the whole budget
on screen before you commit a year. Feed the settlement, plant fields, buy or
sell land, and account for what the harvest leaves. Traveller offers add a
choice about growth; decade reviews let you continue with the same settlement.

Thirteen lessons build through eleven runnable Sinclair BASIC programs, from
an inspectable ledger to editable plans and repeated years. A worked shortage
explains why an affordable year need not make recovery secure. The game uses a
stock 48K Spectrum, with no real-time deadline.
