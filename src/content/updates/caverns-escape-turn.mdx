[The Caverns](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/the-caverns/) asks you to
recover three treasures and bring them back through a twelve-room cave. Read
the clues, avoid the pit and watch the creature's patrol. If it arrives in your
room, your next valid action is a chance to escape.

Ten lessons grow through eight runnable Sinclair BASIC programs. Name a room,
connect a map and follow its links, then add changing treasure, warnings and an
independent patrol. Trace an encounter to see why update order matters, finish
the return journey and save a self-starting tape. The game uses a stock 48K
Spectrum and has no real-time deadline.
