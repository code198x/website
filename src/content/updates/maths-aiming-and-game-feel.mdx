[Maths for Games](/craft/maths-for-games/) now offers two independent starting
points: the five Dice Roller probability lessons and six lessons on aiming and
movement. Place a point, measure a triangle, explore trigonometry and radians,
then aim at a target and see how fixed-point precision changes the shot.

The [Game Feel movement playground](/experiments/game-feel/) gives
three ships the same input. Compare immediate response, sliding and slower
acceleration, then inspect the rules behind the difference. Four
[Game Feel](/craft/game-feel/) lessons now take those rules apart, starting
with how a ship responds to input.

[The Craft](/craft/) also sets out seven module homes, distinguishing available
material from planned coverage in design, AI, data and algorithms, graphics and
audio.

Two Spectrum BASIC lessons also gain editable browser playgrounds:
[Make the Spectrum answer](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/unit-01-make-the-spectrum-answer/)
and [Read the sonar](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/sonar/unit-04-read-the-sonar/).
