[Three in a Row](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/three-in-a-row/) builds a
readable opponent in ten Sinclair BASIC lessons. Eight runnable programs grow
from a numbered board through two-player rules to a computer that wins, blocks
and explains its choices.

A worked fork exposes the limits of looking one move ahead. Finish with a
session tally, alternating starters and a self-starting tape. The game uses a
stock 48K ZX Spectrum, with no timer or sound to hurry your decisions.
