[Cipher](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/cipher/) reveals every matching letter
in a hidden word. Seven distinct misses end a round; repeating a guess is free.

Six lessons build from one fixed-letter experiment through five runnable
Sinclair BASIC programs. Keep the answer separate from what the player knows,
remember attempts, update a readable board and choose words from DATA. The
finished game runs on a stock 48K Spectrum without a timer.
