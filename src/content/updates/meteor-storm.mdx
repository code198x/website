[Meteor Storm](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/assembly/meteor-storm/) follows
Meet Assembly with a complete game for a stock 48K PAL Spectrum. Dodge drifting
meteors, collect stars and hold Space to boost through five storms, each harder
than the last. One hit ends the voyage; faster storms earn larger bonuses, and
later storms multiply them.

Thirty-six lessons start with a pixel crossing a byte boundary. Build up drawing,
a frame clock, independent objects, collision, scoring and a finite course. Later
lessons investigate the cost of drawing twice and preserve collision checks while
reducing that work. The last lessons give every event a sound, play it without
slowing the game, break the ship apart when a meteor hits it and colour
the storm without colour clash. The voyage lessons chain five storms, give the
score a second byte, multiply each storm's bonus, make each storm harder from a
table of rules, remember the furthest voyage, let the title play itself and
load a picture while the tape plays.

Each lesson runs editable assembly in the browser. Inspect real bitmap bytes,
object records and the paused CPU; change the companion artwork as well as the
code. Download the finished source and tape, or build locally with Asm198x or Pasmo.
