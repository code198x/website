[Meteor Storm](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/assembly/meteor-storm/) follows
Meet Assembly with a complete game for a stock 48K PAL Spectrum. Dodge drifting
meteors, collect stars and hold Space to boost. One hit ends the run; finishing
faster earns a larger bonus, with separate records for time and score.

Twenty-four lessons start with a pixel crossing a byte boundary. Build up drawing,
a frame clock, independent objects, collision, scoring and a finite course. Later
lessons investigate the cost of drawing twice and preserve collision checks while
reducing that work.

Each lesson runs editable assembly in the browser. Inspect real bitmap bytes,
object records and the paused CPU; change the companion artwork as well as the
code. Download the finished source and tape, or build locally with Asm198x or Pasmo.
