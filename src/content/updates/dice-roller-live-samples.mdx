[Dice Roller](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/dice-roller/) is an optional
probability experiment in stock 48K Sinclair BASIC. Watch the distribution form,
repeat a fresh batch or add more rolls, and keep the previous sample beside it.

Five lessons build from twelve numbered rolls through array tallies,
percentages and a live chart to the complete experiment. Counts remain exact;
fixed-scale bars make shares comparable. The program makes no fairness verdict.
