## The probe budget

Thirty probes on a 64-cell grid is generous but not infinite. A random strategy would cover roughly half the grid — and might still miss a target by one cell. A systematic strategy (probe, read the distance, triangulate) finds all three in 10–15 probes. The budget rewards thinking over guessing.

The loss screen's coordinate reveal serves the same purpose as Locksmith's code reveal: it gives the player a post-mortem. "The target was at (2,7) — I probed (2,5) and got distance 2, but I searched the wrong direction." Failure becomes a lesson.
