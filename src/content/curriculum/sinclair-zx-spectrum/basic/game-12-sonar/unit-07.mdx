Line 180 initialises two counters: `hits` (targets found) and `guesses` (probes made). Line 210 shows both on a status line below the title. Line 430 increments `guesses` on each valid probe. Lines 570–600 handle hits: set the cell found, increment `hits`, play a celebratory beep, and check whether all three are found. Line 600 `IF hits = 3 THEN GO TO 620` triggers the win screen.

Lines 620–660 are the win screen: title, "All targets found!" in green, and the guess count. The number of guesses is the player's score — fewer is better.

## Efficiency as score

Unlike Locksmith (where the score is how many guesses you needed), Sonar's guess count measures efficiency. A skilled player triangulates from distance clues and finds all three targets in 8–10 probes. A random guesser might need 30. The guess counter creates a secondary challenge: not just "can you find them?" but "how efficiently can you find them?"
