Winning a game of battleships takes dozens of turns and careful deduction. That effort deserves a proper celebration. The win screen combines a flashing message with an ascending BEEP sequence that builds to a final high note. It is the most elaborate audio-visual moment in the game.

## How It Works

**Lines 300-310** clear the screen and display the winner message centred with BRIGHT and FLASH: "PLAYER 1 WINS!" (or Player 2). The FLASH attribute makes the text blink, which is attention-grabbing enough for a victory announcement.

**Lines 320-340** play the victory melody. Eight ascending notes with increasing pitch create a triumphant fanfare: `FOR n=0 TO 7: BEEP 0.15, 10+n*3: NEXT n`. Each note is 3 semitones higher than the last, climbing two full octaves. The regularity of the scale gives it a sense of inevitability — victory confirmed.

**Lines 350-360** display the shot statistics after the melody finishes. Total shots for each player, showing how efficiently the winner played.

**Lines 370-380** remove the FLASH after a pause. The text stays on screen in solid colour so the player can read the statistics without the distraction of blinking.

## Scaling Sound with FOR/NEXT

The victory melody uses a loop instead of individual BEEP statements. `BEEP 0.15, 10+n*3` generates notes at semitones 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 28, 31. The formula `10+n*3` creates a chromatic climb in minor thirds. This is more compact than eight separate BEEP lines and easier to modify — change the `3` to `4` for major thirds, or `2` for whole tones.

## The Emotional Arc

The game has a clear emotional progression:

- **Placement** — quiet, thoughtful, no sound
- **Misses** — dull thud, mild disappointment
- **Hits** — rising tone, excitement
- **Sinking** — fanfare, triumph
- **Victory** — full celebration, maximum impact

Each event builds on the last. By the time the victory melody plays, the player has heard dozens of hits and misses. The longer, higher melody signals that the game has reached its peak moment.

## Try This

**Compare melody shapes.** Try a descending victory melody: `BEEP 0.15, 31-n*3`. Does it feel like winning or losing? Direction matters in music.

**Add a BORDER flash.** Change the BORDER colour in a loop during the melody: `BORDER n`. The screen edge will cycle through colours as the notes play, adding visual spectacle.
