## Finish before asking for another food

The result threshold changes from one food to eight. After eating, the HUD updates. If eaten is eight, the program enters the completion state immediately. Otherwise it calls the food routine, resets the pending turn and continues.

The body starts at four and adds eight cells, so its largest length is **twelve**. That matches the arrays' capacity. There is no ninth food to demand a thirteenth live cell. Increasing the target without reviewing capacity and the movement rules would break this agreement.

The FOOD readout now says `/8`. The length is the body size, so it always equals four plus the number eaten. It is not an independent score.

## Give the player a beginning and an ending

Line 10 builds graphics before drawing the title. The title waits for S or Q. `RANDOMIZE` without a supplied seed uses the machine's clock when the player starts. The initial body and food are prepared after returning from the title routine.

R during play starts again at line 100: arrays, indices, length, counts and directions return to their initial values. The next food uses the continuing random sequence. It need not appear in the same place as before.

A wall, occupied body square or eighth food sends the program to the result routine. The movement loop is no longer running. First it waits for every key to be released, then it accepts R or Q. A held turn cannot silently start another attempt. Q works from the title, during movement and after a result.

<Question prompt="Why does the winning move not place another food before displaying the result?">

The round is already complete. Another food would suggest that movement should continue, and would do unnecessary work before showing success. Completion is checked before replacement food is requested.

</Question>

Play a whole round. Try a wall collision, retry, then deliberately catch the body after growing. Reach all eight foods and check FOOD 8/8, LENGTH 12 and a body that stays still. Try holding a direction through the result, release it, and use R for another attempt. The program is deliberately silent: the face, fruit, readout and result messages carry its feedback.
