## What Changed

**Title screen (lines 20-140).** Blue background with instructions. Tells the player what to expect: three categories, three questions each, a score at the end.

**All categories in sequence (lines 190-520).** Instead of choosing one category, the player goes through all three. The outer loop (`FOR k = 1 TO 3`) selects the category, RESTORE points to the right DATA, and the inner loop (`FOR i = 1 TO 3`) runs three questions.

**Category score display (line 500).** After each category, a brief score summary before moving to the next. The player sees how they did in each section.

![Gameplay — Science category with score](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-06-quiz-master/gameplay.png)

**Running total (line 280).** "Total: 3" at the top-right updates after each category completes. The player can track their progress across all nine questions.

**Score ratings (lines 600-630).** Four tiers based on total score out of 9:

| Score | Message |
|-------|---------|
| 9 | "Perfect! Quiz Master!" |
| 7-8 | "Well done!" |
| 4-6 | "Not bad, keep trying!" |
| 0-3 | "Better luck next time!" |

![Game over with perfect score](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-06-quiz-master/game-over.png)

**Correct/wrong paths (lines 390-460).** The same branching pattern from Word Scramble — wrong answers jump past the score increment.

## The Complete Structure

1. **Title screen** — instructions, wait for key
2. **Category loop** (×3) — RESTORE to DATA section, three questions each
3. **Question loop** — display, input, validate, score
4. **Category summary** — score for this section
5. **Game over** — total score, rating message

## Try This

**More questions.** Add two more questions to each category (lines 830, 840, 930, 940, 1030, 1040) and change the inner loop to `FOR i = 1 TO 5`. The game now has 15 questions.

**Replay option.** After the game-over screen, ask "Play again? (Y/N)". On yes, reset `t` to 0 and `GO TO 190`. The DATA is already there — RESTORE handles it.

**Custom quiz.** Replace all the questions with your own. You could make a quiz about your favourite subject, a revision tool for school, or a trivia challenge for friends.

## What You've Learnt
