## How BEEP Works

BEEP takes two numbers: duration and pitch.

```basic
BEEP duration, pitch
```

- **Duration** — time in seconds. `0.05` is a short blip. `0.5` is half a second. `1` is a full second.
- **Pitch** — semitones from middle C. `0` is middle C. `12` is one octave up. `-12` is one octave down. Higher numbers = higher pitch.

## Sound as Feedback

Different moments deserve different sounds. In Countdown:

- **"NOW!" appears** — short, high, sharp: `BEEP 0.05,20`. Says "react!"
- **Player reacts** — medium, pleasant: `BEEP 0.1,15`. Says "got it"
- **Too early** — long, low, ominous: `BEEP 0.3,-10`. Says "wrong"
- **Too slow** — medium, low: `BEEP 0.3,-5`. Says "too late"

You'll add the reaction and error sounds in later units. For now, the "NOW!" alert sound is enough.

## The Border as a Signal

The border colour change on line 85 does something the BEEP can't — it's visible even in peripheral vision. A yellow flash around the entire screen edge is impossible to miss. Later you'll add green for a successful reaction and return to black after each round.

## Try This

- Change `BEEP 0.05,20` to `BEEP 0.05,30` — higher pitch. Then `BEEP 0.05,10` — lower. Which feels most urgent?
- Change `BORDER 6` to `BORDER 2` (red). Does a red flash feel more or less urgent than yellow?
- Add `96 BEEP 0.05,22` and `97 BEEP 0.05,24` after the first BEEP — a three-note rising alert.
- Try `BEEP 2,0`. Two seconds of middle C. That's how long two seconds feels in sound.

## What You've Learnt

- **BEEP duration,pitch** — plays a tone; duration in seconds, pitch in semitones from middle C
- **Short vs long** — `0.05` for blips, `0.5` for tones, `1+` for drones
- **High vs low** — positive pitch for high notes (urgent), negative for low notes (warning)
- **BORDER as feedback** — changing the border colour signals game events visible from peripheral vision
