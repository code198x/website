The game has a problem: the delay is always the same. A player who counts "one-Mississippi, two-Mississippi" can press at exactly the right moment without reacting at all. The fix: randomise the wait so it's different every time.

## Random Wait with Cheat Detection

Replace line 70 (`PAUSE 100`) with this block:

- **Line 70** — picks a random wait between 50 and 149 frames (1-3 seconds). You learnt `INT(RND*n)+1` in Lucky Number — same formula, different numbers.
- **Line 75** — starts a FOR/NEXT loop that runs once per frame of the wait.
- **Line 76** — checks INKEY$ during the wait. If the player presses early, "Too early!" appears in bright red with a low warning BEEP, and the round restarts.
- **Line 77** — `PAUSE 1` waits exactly one frame, keeping the loop ticking at a steady rate.
- **Line 78** — end of the wait loop.

Type `RUN`. The delay is different every time. Try pressing before "NOW!" appears — "Too early!" flashes red and the round resets.

## Why PAUSE 1 Inside the Loop

You might wonder: why not just `PAUSE w` in one go? Two reasons:

1. **Cheat detection** — a single PAUSE would end early on any keypress, but we can't tell whether that's a cheat or a reaction. By checking INKEY$ on every frame, we know exactly when the press happened.
2. **Control** — the FOR/NEXT loop gives us a place to add logic per frame. Later games will use this pattern for animation and input checking simultaneously.

## The Cheat Check

`IF INKEY$<>""` during the wait means someone pressed a key before "NOW!" appeared. The response is clear: bright red "Too early!", a low ominous BEEP (`BEEP 0.3,-10`), a short pause to read the message, then `RUN` starts the whole program over.

In later units, when the game has multiple rounds, this will restart just the current round instead of the whole game.

## Try This

- Change `RND*100` to `RND*200` for longer waits (up to 5 seconds). Does the tension increase?
- Change `RND*100` to `RND*25` for very short waits. Is it harder or easier to cheat?
- Remove line 76 (the cheat check). Now you can hold a key during the wait and get a near-instant "reaction." This is why the check matters.
- Change the "Too early!" message to something more dramatic.

## What You've Learnt

- **Random PAUSE** — `LET w=50+INT(RND*100)` creates an unpredictable delay
- **Frame-by-frame waiting** — a FOR/NEXT loop with `PAUSE 1` ticks once per frame
- **Cheat detection** — checking INKEY$ during the wait catches early presses
- **RUN** — restarts the program from the beginning (used here as a simple round restart)
