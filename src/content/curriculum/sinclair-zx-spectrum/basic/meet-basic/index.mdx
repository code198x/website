## Start with something small

The Spectrum’s keyboard can enter a whole BASIC keyword with one press.
Begin with a greeting, store it as a numbered program, then change what it says.
The current lessons go on to explore input, decisions, repetition, colour,
sound and movement.

You can choose BASIC without completing another course. [Foundations](/foundations)
explains programming ideas in pseudocode when you want a fuller explanation;
these lessons work with Sinclair BASIC’s particular words and symbols.

## The Spectrum on your screen

The examples start with the 48K Spectrum. You can use an emulator on your computer;
check the model, ROM and keyboard mapping in the [setup guidance](/setup/sinclair-zx-spectrum/native).
Keep the BASIC entry instructions distinct from the assembly build workflow.

## A new introduction is taking shape

We are revising Meet BASIC around guided short projects: a greeting that becomes
[Story Builder](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/story-builder), a number-guessing
game, [Oracle Stone](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/oracle-stone) and a movement
experiment. Saving and investigating mistakes will be part of the work from the
opening lessons.

Those revised lessons are not yet published. The lesson list here links to the
current material; its existing URLs remain available while the new introduction
is developed. The [BASIC track overview](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic) explains
the direction towards [Bright Spark](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/bright-spark)
and [Touchdown](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/touchdown).
