Lucky Number now has a secret worth finding. We’ll give its clues a clear visual
setting, add brief sounds and let the player choose another round.

Continue with [Choose a new secret](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/unit-07-choose-a-new-secret/). A fresh entry can use the complete
first listing below; the preceding lessons explain the guessing and validation
code. Use Host Keyboard mode in the same 48K setup.

## Make the words easy to read

Add **lines 6, 7, 8 and 9**:

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/lucky-number/unit-08/steps/step-01.bas" lang="sinclair-basic" />

`BORDER 0` makes the surrounding border black. `PAPER 0` selects black for the
background of characters; `INK 7` selects white for their foreground. `CLS` then
clears the main display using the new paper colour. Changing `PAPER` alone does
not repaint every existing character cell.

Enter `BORDER` with B after a line number. For `PAPER`, press and release
**Shift+left Option/Alt**, then press **left Option/Alt+C**. For `INK`, use the
same extended-mode chord followed by **left Option/Alt+X**. Release the keys
between chords. `CLS` is V after the line number.

Black and white provide a clear starting contrast. The ordinary colour numbers
are 0 black, 1 blue, 2 red, 3 magenta, 4 green, 5 cyan, 6 yellow and 7 white.
Try `INK 6` in line 8, compare the readability, then restore 7. The player still
receives words for every clue; remembering a colour is not part of the rules.

## Let the clue sound as well

Add **lines 41, 51 and 61**:

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/lucky-number/unit-08/steps/step-02.bas" lang="sinclair-basic" />

`BEEP` takes two values separated by a comma: duration in seconds, then pitch
in semitones above middle C. Negative pitches are below middle C. Our notes last
one tenth of a second. The too-low clue plays middle C, the too-high clue an
octave below it, and success an octave above it. Think of the wrong-guess sounds
as suggestions to move up or down, alongside the written clues.

After `THEN`, enter extended mode with **Shift+left Option/Alt**, release it,
then use **left Option/Alt+Z** for `BEEP`. Type `0.1,0`, `0.1,-12` or `0.1,12`
as shown. A pitch number is not a volume setting.

For a listening test, temporarily use secret 7 in line 10 and guess **4, 9, 7**.
Expect three short notes with the success note highest. Restore the random line.
Repeat with host audio muted: the words must still give all the information.
If there is no sound, check the host volume and emulator audio before changing
the guessing logic.

BASIC waits while each `BEEP` plays. A long note would delay the next input;
our short notes acknowledge the result without adding a long pause. This is a
design cost of the sound, not just a choice of tune.

## Offer another round

Add **lines 81–84**. Keep line 90 and the validation handler at 100–120:

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/lucky-number/unit-08/steps/step-03.bas" lang="sinclair-basic" />

`INPUT` stores the reply in `a$`, a string variable. Line 82 accepts lower-case
`y`; line 83 accepts lower-case `n`. Any other answer reaches line 84 and repeats
the prompt. It does not print another upper-screen message, so repeated invalid
replies do not fill the screen or hide the final count.

`GO TO 9` clears the previous result, chooses the next secret at line 10 and
resets `tries` at line 15. It leaves the colour setup and the original seeding
alone. The next random value comes from the existing sequence.

<details>
<summary>Why would returning to line 30 make a poor new round?</summary>

It would keep the same secret and the old count. Line 30 is the next guess
within a round; line 9 is the start of a new round. Both are loops, but they
repeat different amounts of work.

</details>

Play two rounds. Try an invalid replay answer, then `y`, then finish the second
round and answer `n`. Expect a fresh count in round two and a `9 STOP` report
on exit. That report is our deliberate stop, not a crash. Try a correct first
guess in a labelled fixed-secret test: its count should be 1 in both rounds.

<Figure src="/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/completion/unit-08.png" width={352} alt="A fixed-secret test ends with three guesses and the choice of another round." caption="A fixed-secret test ends with three guesses and the choice of another round." />

Save with `SAVE "lucky"`, then use Emu198x's tape export as described in
[Remember a name; keep the program](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/unit-02-remember-and-save/).
Reopen the saved tape in a fresh emulator and check a round. Give someone the
game without extra spoken instructions: can they find the range, interpret a
clue and leave when they have finished? Use that observation to improve the
wording. Lucky Number is now a complete small game.
