## Rules before the first signal

A reaction game cannot teach itself by trial. The first flash *is* the test — if the player does not already know to wait for red and press fast, they lose that round to confusion, not to slow reflexes. There is no second chance to read the rules once the signal has fired.

So the instructions go on the title screen, before `PAUSE 0`, while the player is still relaxed and reading. By the time the screen says "Get ready...", the rules are already in their head and their finger is over the key. Timing-based games are the least forgiving place to leave the player guessing — which is exactly why this one spells it out first.
