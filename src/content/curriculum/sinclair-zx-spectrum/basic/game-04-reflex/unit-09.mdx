The bar fills silently. The ratings appear silently. Sound can reinforce both.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-04-reflex/unit-09/reflex.bas" />

Line 130 draws a thin rectangular outline around the bar — a frame for the bar to rise into. The frame is full width, but the bar stops at its random endpoint somewhere inside it, so the gap on the right is part of the suspense. Line 180 adds `BEEP 0.01, 10` inside the telegraph loop — a tiny tick for each pixel, so the bar fills with a rising drone that builds tension. Lines 320–350 each gain a BEEP with a different pitch: high for "Lightning!", low for "Slow..."

No new structure — just a frame, a tick, and a beep on every rating. The game is complete: title, rules, telegraph with ticking sound, red flash with alert tone, timing loop, rated result with confirming sound. Every phase has visual and audio feedback.
