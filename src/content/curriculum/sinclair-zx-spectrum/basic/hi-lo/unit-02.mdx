The player needs to guess before seeing the next number.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/hi-lo/unit-02/guess.bas" />

Line 190 asks the player for H or L — the prediction. The `INPUT` goes into `g$` (a string, because H and L are letters, not numbers). Lines 200–210 then generate and show the next number. The order matters: the player commits to a guess *before* seeing the result.

Lines 230–240 check the guess. Each line combines two conditions with `AND`: line 230 checks `g$ = "H" AND b > a` — the player said higher AND the number is higher. Line 240 checks the lower case. Both conditions on each line must be true for the `PRINT "Correct!"` to run.

<Figure src="/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/hi-lo/unit-02/screenshot.png" alt="Black ZX Spectrum screen: 'Number: 1', 'Next: 13', and 'Correct!'" caption="The next number is higher — a guess of H earns 'Correct!'." />

Line 260 catches invalid input: if the player types anything other than "H" or "L", it prints "Type H or L". But notice what is missing — there is no response for a wrong guess. If the player says "H" and the next number is lower, nothing prints at all. The program just ends silently. That gap is what the next unit fixes.
