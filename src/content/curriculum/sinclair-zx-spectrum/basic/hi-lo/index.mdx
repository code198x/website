Hi-Lo is the classic higher-or-lower card game. A card is dealt face up; the next sits face down. Will it be higher or lower? Guess right and your streak grows. Guess wrong and it is over.

<Figure src="/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/hi-lo/unit-06/screenshot.png" alt="ZX Spectrum HI-LO screen: a face-up Ace of diamonds beside a blue face-down card, under a HI-LO status bar" caption="Where you are headed: real playing cards — one face up, the next face down — and a streak to protect." />

The tension comes from the streak — the longer it gets, the more you have to lose. Each correct guess raises the stakes. Each wrong guess costs everything.

**What you will learn:**

- `AND` — compound conditions
- Loop-until-condition (vs counted FOR/NEXT loops)
- State transitions (`LET a = b`)
- User-defined graphics — drawing your own characters
- Best-score tracking across rounds
- The design concept: **Narrowing** — feedback brings you closer

**11 units. About 6–8 hours.**
