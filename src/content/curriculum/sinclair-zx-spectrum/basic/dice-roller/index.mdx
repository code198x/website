Dice Roller is not a game — it is a simulation. Roll dice, tally the results, watch the totals converge toward equal distribution. The fun is in seeing order emerge from chaos.

<Figure src="/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/dice-roller/unit-08/screenshot.png" alt="Black ZX Spectrum screen: a column of six counts above six coloured bars — red, yellow, green, cyan, magenta, white" caption="Where you are headed: roll the dice, tally the faces, and watch the distribution as a colour bar chart." />

This is where `FOR/NEXT` loops arrive — the tool for doing something a specific number of times. Unlike `GO TO` (which loops until a condition), `FOR/NEXT` counts: "do this 5 times", "do this 100 times", "do this 1000 times."

**What you will learn:**

- `FOR`/`NEXT` — counted loops
- `PRINT AT` — fixed-position screen output
- Text bar charts — turning numbers into pictures
- The design concept: **Accumulation** — patterns emerge from randomness

**8 units. About 5–7 hours.**
