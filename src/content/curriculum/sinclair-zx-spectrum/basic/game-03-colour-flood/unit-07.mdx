The game works — growing sequences, correct/wrong detection, the whole loop. But it ends silently when the player gets one wrong, and there's no reward between rounds. That's not how games feel. The player deserves a score that climbs with each round survived, a green flash of celebration when they get it right, and a proper game over screen with a rating when they finally fail.

## Game Over

Replace the simple error handling with a proper game over screen:

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-colour-flood/unit-07/game-over.bas" />

![Game over — "You scored 8" with "Incredible!" rating](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-colour-flood/unit-07-scoring-and-game-over/game-over.png)

The border turns red. A low, ominous BEEP sounds — the opposite of the cheerful round-complete tone. "Wrong! Game over." appears in bright red. Below it, the player's score and a rating. The shift from green celebration to red failure is stark and immediate — the game doesn't soften the blow, and it doesn't need to. That contrast is what makes the next attempt feel urgent.

The rating tiers use the same IF/THEN pattern from Lucky Number and Countdown — separate statements checking non-overlapping ranges.

## The Rating Tiers

| Score | Rating | Colour |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 0-3 | Keep practising! | Magenta |
| 4-7 | Good memory! | Yellow |
| 8+ | Incredible! | Green (bright) |

Design your own thresholds. On a real Spectrum, getting past 7 is genuinely difficult — the sequences are long and the colours flash quickly.

## Try This

- Change the "Correct!" message to flash: `FLASH 1;"Correct!"; FLASH 0`.
- Add a fourth tier: `95 IF sc>12 THEN PRINT AT 17,1; INK 7; BRIGHT 1;"LEGENDARY!"`.
- Add `BEEP 0.05,sc` after each correct round — the pitch rises with the score, building tension.

## What You've Learnt

- **Score tracking** — `LET sc=sc+1` after each completed round
- **Between-round feedback** — green border, BEEP, "Correct!" message
- **Game over screen** — red border, low BEEP, score display, rating tiers
- **IF/THEN tiers** — categorising the score into named bands with different colours
