The game works but it ends silently on a wrong answer. The player deserves feedback — a score that climbs, a celebration between rounds, and a proper game over with a rating.

## Round Complete

Red border, low BEEP, "Wrong! Game over." in bright red. The score and a rating tier — the same IF/THEN pattern from Lucky Number and Countdown.

## The Rating Tiers

| Score | Rating | Colour |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 0-3 | Keep practising! | Magenta |
| 4-7 | Good memory! | Yellow |
| 8+ | Incredible! | Green (bright) |

Design your own thresholds. On a real Spectrum, getting past 7 is genuinely difficult — the sequences are long and the colours flash quickly.

## Try This

- Change the "Correct!" message to flash: `FLASH 1;"Correct!"; FLASH 0`.
- Add a fourth tier: `95 IF sc>12 THEN PRINT AT 17,1; INK 7; BRIGHT 1;"LEGENDARY!"`.
- Add `BEEP 0.05,sc` after each correct round — the pitch rises with the score, building tension.

## What You've Learnt

- **Score tracking** — `LET sc=sc+1` after each completed round
- **Between-round feedback** — green border, BEEP, "Correct!" message
- **Game over screen** — red border, low BEEP, score display, rating tiers
- **IF/THEN tiers** — categorising the score into named bands with different colours
