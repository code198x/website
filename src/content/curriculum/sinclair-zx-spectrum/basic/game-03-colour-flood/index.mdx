The Spectrum's border floods with colour — a sequence of flashes, each with its own tone. Watch, memorise, repeat. Get it right and the sequence grows by one. Get it wrong and the game is over. How long can you remember?

![Game over with score and rating](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-colour-flood/unit-07-scoring-and-game-over/game-over.png)

## What You'll Learn

- **FOR/NEXT** — loops with an automatic counter and STEP
- **BORDER / PAPER / INK** — the Spectrum's three colour controls
- **BEEP** — per-colour sound design
- **BRIGHT** — doubling the palette to 16 shades
- **Strings as data** — storing a sequence in a string, reading it with VAL and LEN
- **String concatenation** — building a growing sequence with `s$=s$+STR$(n)`

## Prerequisites

Complete [Game 2: Countdown](/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-02-countdown/) first. You'll need CLS, INKEY$, PAUSE, and BEEP.
