The game you're building floods the border with colour — each colour accompanied by a tone. Watch and listen, then repeat the sequence. This unit builds the core visual: cycling through seven colours with sounds.

## Border + BEEP

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-colour-flood/unit-02/border-beep.bas" />

Type `RUN`. The border flashes through seven colours — blue, red, magenta, green, cyan, yellow, white — each with a rising tone. Between each flash, the border returns to black for a beat.

The loop variable `i` does triple duty:
- `BORDER i` — sets the border colour
- `BEEP 0.3,i*3` — plays a tone at pitch `i*3` (3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21 semitones)
- The gap between flashes (`BORDER 0`, `PAUSE 8`) makes each colour distinct

## The Seven Colours

| Number | Colour | Pitch (i×3) |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Blue | 3 |
| 2 | Red | 6 |
| 3 | Magenta | 9 |
| 4 | Green | 12 |
| 5 | Cyan | 15 |
| 6 | Yellow | 18 |
| 7 | White | 21 |

Colour 0 (black) is excluded — it's invisible against the black screen. Each remaining colour has a unique pitch, evenly spaced. After a few rounds of the game, you'll start recognising colours by their sound alone.

## Why the Border?

The border is the largest single-colour area on the Spectrum — it surrounds the entire screen. When it flashes green, you can't miss it, even in peripheral vision. That makes it perfect for a memory game: the signal is unmistakable.

## Try This

- Change `i*3` to `i*5`. The tones spread further apart — more dramatic.
- Change `PAUSE 10` to `PAUSE 25`. Each flash lingers longer. Change it to `PAUSE 3` — barely a flicker.
- Add `GO TO 10` after `NEXT i`. The rainbow cycles endlessly. Press BREAK to stop.
- Reverse the loop: `FOR i=7 TO 1 STEP -1`. The tones descend.

## What You've Learnt

- **BORDER with a loop** — `BORDER i` uses the counter as a colour number
- **Colour + sound pairing** — each colour has a pitch; `i*3` spaces them evenly
- **Flash pattern** — colour on, pause, colour off, pause — makes each flash distinct
- **Seven colours** — 1-7 are the playable colours; 0 is invisible on black
