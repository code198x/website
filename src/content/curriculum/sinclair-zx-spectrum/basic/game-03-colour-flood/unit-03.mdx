BORDER colours the edge. PAPER colours the background of each character cell. INK colours the text. Together they control everything you see.

## Flood the Screen

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-colour-flood/unit-03/paper-flood.bas" />

Type `RUN`. The entire screen turns red. `PAPER 2` sets the background colour to red, then `CLS` fills the whole screen with it. The text appears in white because `INK 7` overrides the default.

Try changing `PAPER 2` to `PAPER 1` (blue), `PAPER 4` (green), `PAPER 6` (yellow). Each one floods the screen with a different colour.

## The Colour Chart

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-colour-flood/unit-03/colour-chart.bas" />

Type `RUN`.

![All 16 Spectrum colours — 8 normal and 8 bright](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-colour-flood/unit-03-paper-and-ink/colour-chart.png)

Eight normal colours, then eight bright colours. `BRIGHT 1` makes each colour more vivid — blue becomes bright blue, red becomes bright red. That gives the Spectrum 16 distinct shades, all controlled by two attributes: a colour number (0-7) and a brightness flag (0 or 1).

The `INK 7-i` trick makes the text readable against each background — when the background is dark (low number), the text is bright (high number), and the other way round.

## PAPER vs INK vs BORDER

- **BORDER** — the area around the edge of the screen. One colour for the whole border.
- **PAPER** — the background colour of a character cell. Can be different for every cell on screen.
- **INK** — the foreground (text) colour of a character cell. Can also be different per cell.

When you print a coloured space (`PRINT PAPER 2;" "`), you're setting the PAPER colour for that one character cell. A row of 32 coloured spaces fills one screen row. That's how the header bars in Lucky Number and Countdown work — and it's how the patterns in the next units will fill the entire screen.

## Try This

- Set `PAPER 0: INK 0: CLS`. Black text on black background — the screen looks empty but text is there. Change `INK` to see it appear.
- Try `PAPER 3: INK 3: CLS`. Magenta on magenta — invisible text. Change one to see the other.
- Print text with `BRIGHT 1` and without. Can you see the difference on every colour?

## What You've Learnt

- **PAPER** — sets the background colour for character cells (0-7)
- **INK** — sets the text colour for character cells (0-7)
- **CLS** — fills the screen with the current PAPER colour
- **BRIGHT** — 0 for normal, 1 for bright; doubles the palette to 16 shades
- **Per-cell colour** — every character cell has its own PAPER and INK; the screen is a 24×32 grid of independently coloured cells
