The Caverns is a text adventure where you cannot see. Twenty rooms connected by tunnels. Somewhere in the dark: a bottomless pit, a creature that moves each turn, and three treasures you need to find.

This is the sixth game in Volume 2 — **Patterns of State**, and the most spatial: the cave's whole layout lives in data, and you reason about a place you cannot see. You navigate by compass direction — north, south, east, west. You survive by listening. A cold draft means a pit is one room away. A terrible smell means the creature is close. A faint glint means treasure is nearby. These clues are your only information. Every move is a decision under uncertainty.

**What you will learn:**

- Four parallel arrays as a navigable graph — data representing space
- DATA-driven world maps — twenty rooms defined as pure data
- `GO SUB` for reusable adjacency checks
- A moving hazard — the creature walks randomly each turn
- Sensory clues as indirect information — you never see danger, only sense it nearby

**11 units. About 12–16 hours.**
