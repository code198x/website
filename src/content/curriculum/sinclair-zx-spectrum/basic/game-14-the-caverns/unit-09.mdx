## Three ways out

Every game of The Caverns ends one of three ways: pit, creature, or escape. Unlike Three in a Row's three outcomes (win, lose, draw), these are not symmetric. Two endings are failures with different causes. Only one is a success.

The asymmetry is deliberate. The pit is your fault — you walked into a room despite the draft warning (or without one, because the pit was not adjacent). The creature is partially your fault and partially bad luck — you cannot fully control a randomly moving hazard. The win is pure achievement — you navigated a dangerous cave, interpreted clues correctly, and collected three treasures before either hazard caught you.

## The debug line

Line 360 still shows the hazard positions. In a real game, you would not know where anything is. The debug line lets you test that all three outcomes work — walk into the pit, walk into the creature, and collect all three treasures. Once you are satisfied the game works, the next units add atmosphere and remove the debug information.
