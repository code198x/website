## What You'll Build

Letters appear jumbled on screen. You type the word you think they spell. Ten rounds, each with a different word — short ones at the start, longer ones later. The game tracks your score and rates your performance.

![Word Scramble gameplay — unscramble the blue letters](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-05-word-scramble/gameplay.png)

## Why This Game?

Hot and Cold moved a cursor around the screen, but everything was single characters and numbers. Real games need words — player names, messages, menus, inventories. To work with text properly, you need to get inside strings.

Spectrum BASIC lets you slice strings apart, measure their length, and join them back together. These tools are essential for any program that handles text — and the scramble algorithm is a satisfying puzzle in its own right.

## What You'll Learn

- **String variables** — storing and comparing text with `LET w$ = "HELLO"`
- **LEN** — measuring the length of a string
- **String slicing** — extracting parts with `w$(2 TO 4)`
- **Concatenation** — joining strings with `+`
- **The scramble algorithm** — randomly reordering letters
- **DATA and READ** — storing word lists in your program
- **Colour and sound feedback** — `INK` for correct/wrong, `BEEP` for sound effects

## Prerequisites

Complete [Game 4: Hot and Cold](/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-04-hot-and-cold/) first. You'll need `PRINT AT`, `INKEY$`, `FOR/NEXT`, and colour commands.

## Time Investment

8 units at 45-75 minutes each. Roughly 4-6 hours total.
