The sealed chamber is hidden behind a wall in the chapel. Mrs Pemberton's clue revealed three symbols: moon, crown, star. When the player stands in the chapel and enters the right combination, the wall opens and the sealed chamber becomes accessible.

## Combination Puzzles

A combination puzzle requires the player to know something (from a clue) and do something (enter it). The challenge is information gathering, not reflexes. The player must find Mrs Pemberton, examine her, remember the symbols, go to the chapel, and enter them.

## How It Works

**Lines 1500-1550** handle a new ENTER verb. When the player types "ENTER MOON CROWN STAR" in the chapel (room 19), the code checks the combination. The three symbols are stored as a string: `cb$ = "MOON CROWN STAR"`. If the noun matches `cb$`, the sealed chamber opens.

**Opening the chamber** means two things: setting `lk(20) = 0` (unlocking room 20) and adding an exit from the chapel to the sealed chamber. `LET e(19) = 20` connects the chapel east to the sealed chamber. `LET w(20) = 19` connects back. The room that had no exits now has one.

**The feedback** is dramatic: "The wall groans and shifts. Stone grinds against stone as a section of wall slides back, revealing a dark passage." A sequence of BEEP sounds simulates the grinding of stone.

**Wrong combinations** produce: "Nothing happens." No hint about which symbols are wrong — the player must either find the clue or try all possibilities. With three symbols from a small set, brute force is possible but tedious. The clue makes it straightforward.

**The combination only works once.** After the chamber is open, entering the combination again produces: "The passage is already open."

## Try This

**Solve the combination.** Go to the chapel and type ENTER MOON CROWN STAR. The chamber should open. Walk east into the sealed chamber.

**Try wrong combinations.** Type ENTER STAR MOON CROWN (wrong order) and ENTER MOON MOON MOON (wrong symbols). Both should fail silently. The order matters.

**Find the amulet.** The sealed chamber contains the amulet (item 4). It was placed there during item setup but was unreachable. Now the player can take it. The puzzle chain continues.
