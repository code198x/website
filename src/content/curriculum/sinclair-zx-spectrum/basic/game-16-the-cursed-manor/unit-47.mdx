The curse is broken, but the game is not over. The player must leave the manor through the front door. Walking south from the entrance hall triggers the ending — but only after the ritual. Before the ritual, the front door is sealed (another locked exit). After the ritual, the door opens and the ending sequence plays.

## The Ending

The ending rewards the player for completing the game. It should feel like a conclusion — the tension releases, the manor is at peace, the guests are free. A short sequence of text, a final score, and a return to the title screen (or a STOP).

## How It Works

**The front door** is locked at the start of the game. `lk(0) = 1` represents the exit south from room 1. When the ritual completes (`rf = 1`), the lock clears and the player can leave.

**Lines 2400-2450** trigger the ending when the player goes south from room 1 and the ritual is complete. CLS clears the screen. The ending text plays:

"You step through the front door into the night air."
"Behind you, the manor glows with warm light for the first time in years."
"The guests follow, one by one, blinking in the moonlight."
"The Colonel tips his hat. Lady Ashworth nods. The Maid smiles."
"The curse of Elara Blackwood is broken."

**The score** displays after the ending text. The scoring system is detailed in the next unit, but the ending shows the final tally: rooms explored, items found, guests freed, puzzle steps completed.

**Lines 2460-2480** close the game. A final message — "Thank you for playing The Cursed Manor" — and a STOP command end the program. The player returns to the BASIC prompt.

**If the player tries to leave before the ritual:** "The front door will not budge. Something holds it shut — something that does not want you to leave." This reinforces the curse and the need to break it.

## Try This

**Complete the game.** Perform the ritual and walk to the entrance hall. Go south. Watch the ending. Does it feel satisfying? A good ending validates the player's effort.

**Try leaving before the ritual.** Go south from the entrance hall at the start of the game. The refusal message should be atmospheric, not mechanical.

**Time the ending.** The ending sequence should take about 20 seconds. Too short feels abrupt. Too long tests patience. Adjust PAUSE values to find the right rhythm.
