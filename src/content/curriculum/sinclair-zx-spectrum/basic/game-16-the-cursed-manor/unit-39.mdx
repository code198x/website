The grimoire requires "a flame that burns without heat, kindled by mortal hand." The tinderbox lights the lantern, but when lit near the amulet, the flame turns cold — blue-white instead of orange, casting light but no warmth. This is the cold fire the ritual demands.

## Combining Items

This puzzle requires two items used together: the tinderbox and the lantern. The command LIGHT LANTERN (or USE TINDERBOX) works when both items are carried. The lantern gains a "lit" state that affects its behaviour and description.

## How It Works

**Line 12** adds a lantern state variable: `LET lt = 0` (0 = unlit, 1 = lit with normal fire, 2 = lit with cold fire).

**Lines 1600-1650** handle the LIGHT verb. The code checks:
1. Is the noun "LANTERN"?
2. Is the player carrying the lantern (`ic(2) = 1`)?
3. Is the player carrying the tinderbox (`ic(5) = 1`)?

If all true, the lantern lights: `LET lt = 1`. The game prints "You strike the tinderbox and light the lantern. A warm orange flame springs to life."

**Lines 1660-1680** handle the cold fire transformation. If the lantern is lit (`lt = 1`) and the player is also carrying the amulet (`ic(4) = 1`), the flame changes: `LET lt = 2`. "The amulet pulses — and the lantern flame shifts from orange to blue-white. It still burns, but the heat fades. You hold your hand over it and feel nothing but cold."

**The transformation is automatic.** The player does not need a separate command. Lighting the lantern while carrying the amulet produces cold fire directly. If they light it without the amulet, they get normal fire. Picking up the amulet while the lantern is lit also triggers the change — checked during the NPC/event phase.

**The lantern's examine description** now varies with state: unlit, normal flame, or cold flame. Each version is a different string, selected by `lt`.

## Try This

**Light the lantern without the amulet.** The flame should be normal — warm and orange. Now pick up the amulet. The flame should turn cold.

**Light the lantern with the amulet already carried.** The flame should go directly to cold fire, skipping normal.

**Try LIGHT without the tinderbox.** The game should say "You have nothing to light it with." The tinderbox is consumed in the process — after lighting, consider whether it should remain in inventory or disappear.
