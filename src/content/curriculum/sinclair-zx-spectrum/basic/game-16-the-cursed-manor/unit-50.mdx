After the title screen, offer the player instructions. Not everyone knows how text adventures work. A clear, concise instructions page explains the command format, lists the key verbs, and describes the goal — without giving away any puzzles.

## Instructions Page

The instructions appear between the title and the game. The player can skip them with a keypress or read through them. They cover: how to type commands, what verbs the game understands, how to move, and the objective.

## How It Works

**Lines 55-59** ask "Would you like instructions? (Y/N)" after the title screen. If the player presses Y, the instructions display. If N, the game starts immediately. This respects experienced players' time.

**Lines 60-95** display the instructions on a clean screen. The format is structured:

**How to play:** "Type commands in plain English. Most commands are two words — a verb and a noun. For example: GO NORTH, TAKE KEY, EXAMINE PAINTING."

**Movement:** "GO followed by a direction: NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST, UP, DOWN."

**Actions:** "TAKE picks up items. DROP puts them down. EXAMINE looks closely at things. LOOK re-reads the room description. INVENTORY lists what you carry."

**Special:** "HELP shows commands during the game. SCORE shows your progress."

**Your goal:** "Five guests have been frozen by an ancient curse. Explore the manor, gather clues, and find a way to break the curse and free them all."

**Line 95** prints "Press any key to begin..." and waits. Then the game initialisation continues.

**The instructions do not mention specific puzzles, items, or solutions.** They tell the player what tools they have, not how to use them. Puzzle discovery is part of the game.

## Try This

**Read the instructions as a new player.** Do they make sense without any prior text adventure experience? If anything is unclear, rewrite it.

**Skip the instructions.** Press N at the prompt and confirm the game starts immediately. Re-players should not be forced through the instructions every time.

**Add a page break.** If the instructions are too long for one screen, split them across two pages with "Press any key for more..." between them.
