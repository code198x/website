The amulet is not just an item — it is a compass that guides the player toward the ritual location. When carried, it glows brighter in rooms closer to the drawing room (where the ritual will take place). This gives the player feedback without spelling out the answer.

## Dynamic Item Behaviour

Most items are static — they have a fixed description regardless of context. The amulet changes based on where the player is. This requires checking the room number during display and adjusting the amulet's description accordingly.

## How It Works

**Lines 275-278** add a special check during room display. If the player is carrying the amulet (`ic(4) = 1`), a message prints based on the current room:

- **Drawing room (room 7):** "The amulet blazes with cold light, almost too bright to look at."
- **Adjacent rooms (rooms 2, 5, 6, 8):** "The amulet pulses strongly in your hand."
- **Nearby rooms:** "The amulet glows steadily."
- **Distant rooms:** "The amulet glows faintly."

**The intensity scale** uses the room connections to approximate distance. Rooms directly connected to room 7 are "adjacent." Rooms connected to those are "nearby." Everything else is "distant." This is a rough measure but effective enough — the player notices the change as they move.

**The drawing room** is the ritual location. The amulet's reaction there is unmistakable. Combined with the grimoire's instruction about "the chamber of binding," the player can deduce that the drawing room is where the ritual must be performed.

**The glow message** appears after the room contents and before the exits. It uses bright cyan ink, consistent with the amulet's cold-light theme.

## Try This

**Walk with the amulet.** Carry it from the sealed chamber through the manor. Watch the glow description change as you get closer to and further from the drawing room. The feedback should feel like a compass needle.

**Drop and retake.** Drop the amulet, move rooms, and take it again. The glow should reflect the new location immediately. The check runs on display, not on take.

**Consider other dynamic items.** Could the lantern change behaviour when lit? Could the grimoire reveal different text in different rooms? Dynamic items make the world feel responsive. For now, only the amulet has this feature, but the pattern is reusable.
