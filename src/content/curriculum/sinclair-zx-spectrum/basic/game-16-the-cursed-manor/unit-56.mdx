The cellar should be dark. Without the lantern, the player cannot see room descriptions, items, or exits. They can still move (stumbling in the dark), but they cannot interact with anything useful. The lit lantern solves this — light reveals the room.

## Dark Rooms

A room flag `dk(r)` marks dark rooms. During display, if the room is dark and the player does not have a lit lantern, the description is replaced with "It is too dark to see." Items and exits are hidden. The player can still go back the way they came.

## How It Works

**Line 29** adds `DIM dk(20)` for darkness flags. `LET dk(15) = 1` marks the cellar as dark. The sealed chamber is also dark (`dk(20) = 1`).

**Lines 200-210** check darkness before display. If `dk(r) = 1` and the lantern is not lit (`lt = 0`), the room name prints as "Darkness" and the description as "It is pitch black. You cannot see a thing." No items, guests, or exits are listed. The illustration is skipped.

**Lines 205-208** check for the lit lantern. If `lt > 0` (normal or cold fire), the darkness is overcome and the room displays normally. "The lantern casts a steady glow, revealing..." precedes the normal description.

**Movement in darkness** is partially restricted. The player can go back the way they came (the game remembers their last direction) and can try other directions, but with a warning: "You stumble in the darkness." Wrong directions might produce "You walk into a wall." This is not dangerous — just unhelpful.

**The sealed chamber** is always dark. Even with the lantern, the description mentions the inadequacy of the light: "Your lantern pushes back the darkness, but the shadows cling to the corners." The cold fire version: "The blue-white flame reveals the chamber in stark detail."

## Try This

**Enter the cellar without the lantern.** The darkness message should appear. Try EXAMINE, TAKE, and LOOK — all should fail. Now light the lantern and return. The full room should appear.

**Drop the lantern in a dark room.** What happens? The room goes dark because the lantern is no longer carried. But the lantern is still here — the player can TAKE LANTERN in the dark (they know it is at their feet). Handle this edge case.

**Add more dark rooms.** Consider making some upstairs rooms dark as well — a windowless store room, for example. Darkness adds variety to exploration.
