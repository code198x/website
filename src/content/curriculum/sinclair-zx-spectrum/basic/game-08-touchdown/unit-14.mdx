## A flame when you thrust

Line 480 draws the thrust: `IF INKEY$ = " " AND fuel > 0 AND row < 20 THEN PRINT AT row + 1, 15; INK 6; CHR$ 145`. Whenever the player holds SPACE with fuel left, a flame — custom character 145 — appears in yellow just below the ship. It changes no physics; it is pure feedback. Holding SPACE now does something visible every frame, not just a number ticking down. The player can *see* the engine firing.

## A ship on the title

Line 110 draws the lander on the title screen with `PLOT` and `DRAW` — a chain of line segments tracing a rocket outline. The title already named the game; now it shows what you are about to fly. The starfield ship is a custom character; this one is drawn large with lines. Two ways to make the same shape, each suited to its size.

## The replay loop

Lines 730–750 close the loop: after the results, "Press any key to play again", `PAUSE 0`, and `GO TO 10` to restart from the title. The whole game — title, descent, outcome, results — wraps in an outer loop. Land, read your stats, and go again, chasing a cleaner landing or more fuel in the tank.

## What you have built

Touchdown is complete. Seventy-one lines: a title with a drawn ship, gravity, real-time thrust with a visible flame, limited fuel, an instrument dashboard, a fuel bar, a custom lander falling through a starfield, a colour-shifting border, three outcomes with distinct sounds, a results screen, and a replay loop. It is the most complex game in Volume 1 — a real-time physics game built from the same BASIC commands as a guessing game.
