Touchdown is a Lunar Lander game. The spacecraft descends. Gravity accelerates it. You fire thrusters to slow down — but fuel is finite. Land gently and you touch down. Land too fast and you crash.

This is the Volume 1 finale. Every tool you have learnt — variables, loops, decisions, INKEY$, PRINT AT, colour, sound — comes together in a program where the world runs continuously, whether you act or not. That is the fundamental shift from turn-based to real-time.

**What you will learn:**

- The continuous game loop — state updates every iteration
- Gravity and thrust as physics
- Finite resources (fuel depletion)
- PRINT AT with computed position (the lander moves on screen)
- Ambient feedback through BORDER and BEEP
- The design concept: **Descent** — the program runs whether you act or not

**13 units. About 10–14 hours.**
