## What changed

**Title screen (lines 20–80).** Name, rules, controls, and `PAUSE 0`. The same pattern from every game in V1 — by now it is second nature.

**Fuel bar (lines 250–270).** A row of green asterisks that shrinks as fuel burns. `FOR j = 1 TO fuel` prints asterisks; `FOR j = fuel + 1 TO 50` prints spaces to erase the rest. The bar is a visual resource gauge — the player sees fuel draining without reading the number.

**Three landing ratings (lines 450–470).** Speed 2 or less is "PERFECT LANDING!" in green. Speed 3–5 is "Bumpy but safe" in yellow. Speed over 5 is "CRASH!" in red. The middle band is new — it rewards a *good enough* landing alongside the perfect one.

**Landing stats (lines 900–910).** Speed at landing and fuel remaining, displayed after the outcome. The numbers tell the story: "I crashed at speed 8 with 3 fuel left — I should have started braking earlier."

**Replay (lines 920–940).** Press any key, go to line 10. Fresh game, fresh title.

## What you have built

Touchdown is about 50 lines of BASIC — the longest program in V1. It has:

- A continuous game loop that runs whether the player acts or not
- Two forces in balance (gravity vs thrust)
- A finite resource (fuel) that creates tension
- A moving character on screen (PRINT AT with computed position)
- Three channels of ambient feedback (border colour, fuel warning, thrust sound)
- A visual resource gauge (the fuel bar)
- Three landing outcomes with colour-coded ratings
- A title screen and replay loop

Every tool from Volume 1 is here: PRINT, LET, INPUT (on the title screen via PAUSE 0), IF/THEN, GO TO, FOR/NEXT, INKEY$, PRINT AT, RND (not used here — altitude is always 100, but the player's strategy is always different), BORDER, BEEP, INK, PAPER, BRIGHT (not used — reserved for Bright Spark).
