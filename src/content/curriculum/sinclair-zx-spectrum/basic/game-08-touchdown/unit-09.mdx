Lines 250–270 draw the fuel bar at row 2. Line 260 prints green asterisks for each unit of fuel remaining. Line 270 prints spaces for the rest — overwriting the bar positions that are now empty. Each frame, the bar shrinks by one asterisk when the player thrusts.

Line 160 now adds `BEEP 0.02, 15` when thrusting — a short, sharp sound that confirms the thrusters are firing. The player hears each burn.

## Two FOR loops, one bar

The bar is drawn with two FOR loops running back to back. The first loop prints `fuel` green asterisks. The second prints `50 - fuel` spaces. Together they always fill exactly 50 characters at row 2, regardless of how much fuel is left.

When fuel is 50, the first loop prints 50 asterisks and the second prints nothing. When fuel is 10, the first loop prints 10 and the second prints 40 spaces — erasing the old asterisks. When fuel is 0, the first loop runs zero times (FOR 1 TO 0 does nothing) and the second loop prints 50 spaces — a blank line.

The bar is a **visual metaphor**: length represents quantity. The player watches the green strip shrink with each thrust and instantly knows how much budget remains. The number "23" is abstract; a bar two-thirds gone is concrete.
