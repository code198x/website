## Speed and fuel as post-game analysis

The result screen shows two numbers: landing speed and fuel remaining. Speed determines the outcome (the player already knows this). But fuel remaining tells a different story — it is a measure of efficiency. Landing perfectly with 30 fuel left means the player barely used the thrusters. Landing perfectly with 1 fuel left means they cut it close. Both are perfect landings, but one shows more skill.

The player starts thinking about secondary goals: not just "can I land safely?" but "can I land safely with fuel to spare?" The game creates its own difficulty curve through the player's ambition.
