## Three endings, one structure

All three outcome blocks follow the same pattern: set the border colour, play a sound, print the message. The sounds tell the story: the perfect landing gets a celebratory ascending run of four notes. The bumpy landing gets two flat tones — neither triumph nor disaster. The crash gets a single low buzz. More notes for better results — the sound itself is a reward.

Lines 630 and 730 still end with `STOP`. The next unit replaces those with something better.
