## A front door and a way back

Lines 20–40 are the title — the name in colour, a small `PLOT`/`DRAW` emblem, and `PAUSE 0` to
wait. At the other end, line 480 asks "Play again?" and line 490 loops to the top on a yes, so
a completed quiz flows straight into a fresh one. A turn-based game can sit on its title as long
as the reader likes; the replay turns one quiz into a session.

## What you built

Quiz Master began as one printed question and grew into a structured quiz: a multiple-choice
round scored against an **answer-key array**, questions grouped into categories by `RESTORE`-ing
to sections of `DATA`, a colour-coded card, a **second array** tallying each subject, a rating,
and a replay. The new idea throughout was the array — `DIM`, and reaching values by index — and
once you have it, the rest of Volume 2 leans on it constantly.
