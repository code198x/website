## Look up a feature

These lessons explore individual parts of Sinclair BASIC, from entering keywords
to drawing, sound and debugging. Their examples are available for reference.

For a guided beginning, start with [Meet BASIC](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/).
Its first four lessons grow a greeting into a story and explain saving along the
way. You do not need to complete these reference lessons before starting it.
