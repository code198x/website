Restart and press S, S, E. You reach the Buried shrine with one treasure. The east tunnel has footsteps. Press Space: the creature arrives in your room. The warning gives you a turn to leave; press W to retreat to the Still pool.

## Position and place are different numbers

The eight DATA values at 9800 are room identifiers: 2, 3, 4, 8, 12, 11, 10, 6. DIM c(8) holds that circuit. The loop at 230 reads it after restoring to the separate patrol table.

ci means position **in the circuit**. cr means the creature's actual **room**. At the start ci is 3, so cr is c(3), room 4. Adding one to ci moves to the next entry. When ci reaches 9, it wraps to 1; line 470 then reads the corresponding room into cr.

Each consecutive pair is linked by a tunnel, including the final room 6 back to room 2. The creature follows this authored route independently of the player. It is patrolling, not hunting or choosing a shortest path towards you.

## Two ways to meet

Before moving the creature, line 420 checks whether the player's destination is its current room. Entering it loses. The previous screen's Footsteps clue identifies that occupied neighbouring room.

After moving the creature, line 480 checks whether it has arrived where the player now stands. This sets an urgent message instead of ending the game. The next valid action can leave. Waiting keeps dest equal to rm, which is now also cr, so line 420 catches it on that next turn.

The display prints Footsteps beside the creature's neighbouring room. If that room also has treasure, the final condition replaces the overlapping captions with Steps + glint. The clue must preserve both facts.

<Question prompt="Why not change cr by adding one directly?">

Room numbers are identifiers, not consecutive steps around the patrol. After room 4 comes room 8. Advancing ci and looking up c(ci) follows the authored route instead of assuming how rooms connect.

</Question>
