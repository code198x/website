Room one remains at DATA line 8000. Room two begins at 8100. The loader first selects 8000, then selects 8100 if `room = 2`. Each call starts with an explicit RESTORE, so restarting cannot accidentally continue reading after the previous map.

Line 135 accepts N only when `won = 1`. The routine at 800 advances `room`, wraps 3 back to 1 in this two-room version, then enters the existing rebuild at 20. R goes straight to 20 and leaves `room` alone. These are different actions even though they share the same rebuilding code.

The status messages now distinguish another room from a new game. Graphics are loaded once at the beginning; changing maps does not overwrite their DATA or character memory.

## Try the new layout

Solve room one, then press N. In room two the player starts at `(6,5)`, the crate at `(5,5)` and the target at `(3,5)`. A wall at `(4,5)` blocks the direct upward push. You need another approach.

<Figure src="/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/crates/teaching/two-rooms-room-2.png" width={352} alt="The second room, with an inner wall between the crate and its target." caption="The obvious route is blocked. Look for somewhere you can stand to push from another side." />

Try the puzzle before opening the route below. R restarts this room; it should not send you back to room one. N should do nothing until the room is complete.

<Question prompt="Can a push be allowed by the movement rules and still make the room impossible?">

Yes. The rule checks the destination of this push. It does not search every future move. In room two, a crate can enter an open square from which the player can never get behind it to push it towards the target.

</Question>

<details>
<summary>A legal trap, and one route that succeeds</summary>

From a fresh room two, press **J, I, L**. The last press pushes the crate right into `(5,6)`. The move is legal, but the wall above at `(4,6)` and wall to the right at `(5,7)` prevent the access needed to recover it. Press R.

One successful route is **L, I, J, K, J, I, I, J, I, L**. It takes ten successful steps and four pushes. The first push moves the crate left into the passage, where we can get underneath it. Later we get to its left to make the final rightward delivery.

This is a worked route, not a compulsory score to beat.

</details>

Finish room two and press N: the game returns to a fresh room one. Also check R after room-two success, and Q during play. If restart changes rooms, check whether it jumps to 800 rather than 20. If N skips an unfinished puzzle, check the `won = 1` condition at 135.
