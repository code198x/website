Lines 500–550 add the end-of-game screen. Line 512 handles collapse: if the population reached zero at any point, the game ends with "Your kingdom collapsed" in red. Lines 515–521 display the final state for completed games.

Lines 524–532 rate the player with a cascading IF chain. If population doubled to 200 or more: "Wise Ruler!" in green. 150+: "Prosperity!" 100+: "Steady Hand" in yellow. 50+: "Hard Times" in yellow. Below 50: "The People Suffer" in red. Each condition uses `GO TO 540` to skip lower ratings.

## The cascading IF

The rating chain at lines 524–532 checks from highest to lowest. If `pop >= 200`, it prints "Wise Ruler!" and jumps past the other checks. If not, it falls to the next line. This cascade ensures only one rating prints — the first one that matches.

This is the same technique used in Sonar's efficiency ratings and Locksmith's guess-count display. The pattern: test the best case first, jump on match, fall through to the next. By the time line 532 runs, all better ratings have been eliminated.

## Two endings, different weight

Collapse (line 512) and completion (lines 515–532) are structurally different. Collapse can happen at any point — year 3, year 7 — and the `GO TO 500` from line 240 jumps here immediately. Completion only happens after year 10 (line 355). Both land on the same screen, but collapse shows no stats, no rating. Just the fact of failure. The rating system only applies to rulers who survived. Making it to year 10 is the first achievement; the rating measures how well you did it.
