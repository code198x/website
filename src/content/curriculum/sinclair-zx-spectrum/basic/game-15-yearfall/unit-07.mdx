## Colour as causality

Red means loss. Green means gain. The player reads the report top to bottom: harvest (neutral), starvation (red or green), births (green or neutral). The colours answer the question "how did I do?" before the numbers explain why.

In Three in a Row, outcome screens used colour to mark win, lose, and draw. Here, colour marks cause and effect within the same screen. A year might show green for "nobody starved" and green for "3 newcomers arrived" — the second follows from the first. The colour connection reinforces the causality: feed well → no starvation → growth.

## The pause between years

Line 760 waits for a keypress before advancing. This forces the player to read the report — to sit with the consequences before planning the next year. Without the pause, the game would rush past the results. The pause is not a game mechanic; it is a teaching mechanic. It says: "Look at what happened. Then decide what to do about it."
