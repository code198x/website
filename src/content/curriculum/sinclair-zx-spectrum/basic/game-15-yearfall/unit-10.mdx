## What you have built

Yearfall is complete. About sixty lines of BASIC: a title screen, a three-variable kingdom (population, grain, land), three allocation decisions per year (trade, feed, plant) with constraint validation, starvation and harvest calculations, population growth from births, a 10-year simulation loop with a coloured year-end report, a five-tier rating system, collapse detection, and a replay loop.

This is the first game with no opponent, no puzzle, and no hidden state. Every number is visible. Every constraint is calculable. The challenge is entirely in the trade-offs: grain spent feeding cannot seed fields, land bought now costs grain that could feed people, and a good harvest this year does not guarantee one next year. The game teaches through compounding consequences — not the consequences of a single choice, but the way ten years of choices interact.

The V2 design concept is **Patterns of State**: turn-based games where the player reasons about structured information. Yearfall is the purest expression of this — three variables, three decisions, ten iterations, and a kingdom's fate in the balance.
