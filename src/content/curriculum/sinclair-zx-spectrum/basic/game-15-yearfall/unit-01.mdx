Every kingdom starts somewhere. This one starts with numbers.

Line 50 sets the population to 100 and the grain supply to 2800. Line 52 sets the land to 1000 acres. Lines 75–89 display these values as a dashboard.

Three numbers define the entire kingdom. Population is how many people depend on you. Grain is what you have to work with — food and seed in the same stockpile. Land is potential — acres you could plant if you had the grain and the workers.

## State as a snapshot

This is the game's starting state. Unlike The Caverns where the state was hidden, here everything is visible. The player sees exactly what they have before making any decisions. The challenge is not discovering information — it is managing it.

Every previous game had a moment-to-moment loop: guess a number, move a piece, enter a room. Yearfall operates on a larger timescale. Decisions are made once per year, and their effects compound across years. This dashboard is the lens through which the player reads those effects — and it will look different every time they return to it.
