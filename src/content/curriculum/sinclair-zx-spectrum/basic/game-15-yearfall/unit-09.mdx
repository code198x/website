Lines 10–90 add the title screen. `BORDER 0: PAPER 0: INK 7` sets black with white text — austere, not decorative. The rules are plain: "Feed your people. Plant crops. Grow or collapse."

Lines 230–240, 270, and 290–310 gain BEEP feedback for invalid inputs. A low buzz rejects impossible allocations without explanation — the dashboard shows the constraints. Line 680 adds a low tone when people starve. Line 1020 adds a fanfare on the end screen.

## Sound as feedback

The BEEP on invalid input is functional, not decorative. It tells the player "that value does not work" without explaining why. The constraints are visible on the dashboard: grain supply, land area, population × 10. The player must connect the rejection to the numbers themselves.

The starvation sound is different — it is not a rejection but an outcome. The low tone at line 680 marks a loss that the player caused by their own allocation. It fires after the decision is irreversible. Hearing it once teaches more than any warning.

## The austere palette

Yearfall uses black paper where most games use colour. There are no decorations, no border effects, no graphics. The austerity is deliberate: this is a game about scarcity. The screen reflects the theme. White text on black is legible, serious, and stripped of comfort.
