Yearfall is a kingdom you cannot save — only manage. One hundred people, two thousand eight hundred bushels of grain, a thousand acres of land. Ten years to prove you can keep them alive.

Each year you make three decisions: how much grain to feed your people, how many acres to plant, and whether to buy or sell land. Then the harvest comes — good or bad — and the consequences of your choices arrive. Underfeed and people starve. Overplant and you run out of seed grain. Hoard grain and your fields lie fallow.

**What you will learn:**

- Multi-variable state that persists across turns
- Allocation with constraints — you cannot spend more than you have
- Compound consequences — this year's choices shape next year's options
- Random variation on player decisions — the harvest is not in your hands
- A simulation loop where no single turn matters, but the pattern of ten does

**10 units. About 10–14 hours.**
