## Allocation with constraint

This is the first allocation mechanic in the curriculum. The player cannot feed more grain than they have. `IF feed > grain` catches overspending. But the harder constraint is implicit: feeding 2000 grain (full rations for 100 people) leaves only 800 for planting. Feeding 1000 keeps only 50 people alive but leaves 1800 for other uses.

The INPUT does not enforce wisdom — only solvency. You can legally feed 0 grain and starve everyone. The game lets you make terrible decisions and then shows you what happened. The starvation line at 600 is not a warning; it is a consequence.

## The starvation formula

`starved = pop - INT(feed / 20)`. Each 20 grain saves one person. If you feed 1500 grain to 100 people, `INT(1500/20) = 75` survive, and 25 starve. The integer truncation means partial rations save nobody — 19 grain feeds zero people. This is harsh but simple: the player can calculate exact survival from the numbers on screen.
