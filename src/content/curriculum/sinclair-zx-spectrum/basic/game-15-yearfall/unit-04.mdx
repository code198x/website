Lines 750–760 add a pause after each year's results. Lines 770–790 advance the year. Line 770 increments the counter. Line 780 checks if all 10 years are complete — if so, the game ends. Line 790 loops back to line 130 for the next year's dashboard.

The loop is not a FOR/NEXT — it is a manual counter with `GO TO`. This gives us control: if the population hits zero mid-game, we can break out at any point (added in a later unit). A FOR loop would run all 10 years regardless.

## Compounding decisions

The year loop is where Yearfall becomes a simulation rather than a puzzle. Each year's starting state is the result of every previous year's decisions. Feed too little in year 3 and the population drops, which means fewer workers in year 4, which means fewer planted acres, which means a smaller harvest in year 5.

The player cannot undo a bad year. They can only respond to its consequences. This is the design concept: choices land later. A decision that looks safe this year might create a crisis three years from now. The loop makes time visible — the year counter at the top tracks how many chances remain.
