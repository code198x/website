The counter says "You found it in 7 guesses." But 7 is just a number. Is that good? The player does not know. The program should tell them.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/lucky-number/unit-10/lucky-number.bas" />

Four new lines at the end. Line 330 prints a blank line. Lines 340–360 map the guess count to a rating:

- 5 or fewer: "Incredible!"
- 6 to 10: "Not bad!"
- Over 10: "Keep trying!"

The rating reframes the result — 7 guesses is not just a number, it is "Not bad!" The player feels judged (gently) and wants to do better.

## Make it yours

Change the thresholds. "Incredible" at 5 assumes a 1–100 range — change the range and the ratings should change too. Write ratings in your own voice: "Not bad for a human" or "The Oracle sees your future: more practice."

Change the title. Add your name. Add `INK 5` to make the title cyan. The program is yours.
