For the guided first game, start with [Give a useful clue](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/unit-05-give-a-useful-clue/)
and continue through [Feedback and another round](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/unit-08-feedback-and-another-round/).
This separate collection explores a larger range, distance-based border colours
and ratings. Its complete starting game uses input, comparisons, repetition
and random selection; the Meet BASIC sequence develops those ideas in smaller stages.

<Figure src="/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/lucky-number/unit-05/rating.png" alt="ZX Spectrum win screen with a green border, a guess count, and the rating Not bad!" caption="Where you are headed: a win, the guess count, and a verdict — framed in green." />

The game is there from Unit 1 — playable, complete. Every unit after that adds one layer
that makes it *feel* like a game, not just work like a program: colour, sound, an
offered start, a framed win, a rating.

**What you will build:**

- A border that runs cold blue to burning white, by `ABS` distance
- Directional sound — a low beep for "go higher", a high one for "come down"
- A win fanfare and a green winner's border
- An offered start with `PAUSE`, so the game feels invited, not inflicted
- A rating that turns a guess count into a verdict

The five units explore this variation. They are optional alongside the guided
[Meet BASIC game](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/unit-05-give-a-useful-clue/).
