## How BEEP works

`BEEP duration, pitch` plays a single tone.

- **Duration** is in seconds: `0.1` is a short click, `0.5` a held note.
- **Pitch** is in semitones from middle C: `0` is middle C, `12` one octave up, `-12` one octave down.

The directional trick: `BEEP 0.1, -5` plays below middle C — "go higher." `BEEP 0.1, 5` plays above — "come down." The player hears direction without reading text.

Line 300 plays four ascending tones as a fanfare: a quick celebration.

## Three channels

The game now has three feedback channels:

1. **Words** — "Too high!" / "Too low!" — explicit.
2. **Colour** — border shifts blue to white — peripheral.
3. **Sound** — directional tone — instant, even with eyes closed.

Each channel carries the same information in a different way. Together they create layered feedback: the game communicates through every sense it has.
