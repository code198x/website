A streak of 7 is just a number. "Impressive!" gives it meaning.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-07-hi-lo/unit-10/hi-lo.bas" />

Lines 470–510 add a rating to the game over screen. The IF chain tests from highest to lowest: 10 or more gets green "Unstoppable!", 5–9 gets cyan "Impressive!", under 5 gets yellow "Try again..." Line 510 resets `INK 7` so the replay prompt prints in white.

This is the same cascading-IF pattern from Bright Spark's ratings. The colour and the word work together — green for the best, yellow for encouragement. The rating gives the streak context. A streak of 4 says "Try again..." which sounds like a challenge, not a criticism.
