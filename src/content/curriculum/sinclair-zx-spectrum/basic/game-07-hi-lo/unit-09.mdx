Second, a rating: lines 500–540 judge the final streak below the score.

## Where you put a variable matters

`best` is initialised on line 90 — before the game loop. The replay you added last unit jumps back to line 180, skipping line 90, so `best` keeps its value across games. The streak resets every game (line 200), but the best score survives.

If `best` were initialised on line 200 alongside `streak`, it would reset every game and never grow. The position of the initialisation decides the variable's lifetime: lines before the replay point persist, lines inside the replay loop reset.

A status bar reading `S:3 B:7` is more motivating than the streak alone — it tells the player what they are capable of and gives them a number to beat.

## Ratings

Lines 510–530 turn the streak into a judgement. The IF chain tests from highest to lowest: 10 or more gets green "Unstoppable!", 5–9 gets cyan "Impressive!", under 5 gets yellow "Try again...". Line 540 resets `INK 7` so the replay prompt prints white again.

This is the cascading-IF pattern from Lucky Number and Bright Spark. The colour and the word work together — green for the best, yellow for encouragement. A streak of 4 reads "Try again...", which sounds like a challenge, not a criticism.
