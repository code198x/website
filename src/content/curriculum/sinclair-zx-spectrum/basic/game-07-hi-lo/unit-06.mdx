Lines 280–320 add multi-sensory feedback for a correct guess. Line 280 flashes the border green (`BORDER 4`) and plays a short high beep. Line 290 prints "Correct!" in green ink (`INK 4`). Line 300 pauses for 30 frames — just over half a second — so the player can see and hear the result. Lines 310–320 reset: the border goes black and the "Correct!" text is overwritten with spaces, ready for the next round.

## Feedback layers

Three channels fire at once: colour (green border), sound (high beep), and text ("Correct!"). The player registers the green flash before reading the word. The beep confirms the result even if they are not looking at the screen. Each channel reinforces the same message through a different sense.

This is the same principle as the red signal flash in Reflex — colour carries meaning faster than text. Here the pattern is richer: correct is green and high-pitched, wrong (which you will add next unit) is red and low-pitched. The player learns the sounds and colours before they consciously read the words.

Line 320 clears the "Correct!" message before the next round. Without it, the text would linger while the player makes their next guess — stale feedback from the last round mixed with fresh information for this one. Clean up after every event.
