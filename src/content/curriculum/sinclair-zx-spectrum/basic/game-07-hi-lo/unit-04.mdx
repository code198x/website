## The dashboard

Lines 60–70 use `PRINT AT` to place the current number and streak at fixed positions. Each round, these lines rewrite the values in place — no scrolling, no clutter. The player always sees the number they are betting on and the streak they are protecting.

## Colour feedback

Line 190: `BORDER 4` (green) on a correct guess. Line 300: `BORDER 2` (red) on a wrong guess. The border flashes the result — the same ambient feedback technique from Lucky Number.

## Ratings

Lines 330–350 rate the streak at game over. The familiar pattern: chained IF, colour-coded text. "Unstoppable!" in green for 10+, "Impressive!" in cyan for 5–9, "Try again..." in yellow for under 5.

The thresholds matter. With 13 possible values per draw, the probability of a correct guess is roughly 6/13 (about 46%) — slightly worse than a coin flip, because draws exist. A streak of 5 means winning five ~46% bets in a row: about a 2% chance. Ten in a row: about 0.04%. "Unstoppable" at 10 is genuinely earned.
