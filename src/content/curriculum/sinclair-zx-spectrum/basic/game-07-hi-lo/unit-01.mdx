Hi-Lo is built on one question: is the next number higher or lower than this one? Before asking the player, let us see the raw material.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-07-hi-lo/unit-01/two-numbers.bas" />

Two random numbers from 1 to 13 (the range of a playing card, ace through king). The program shows both and tells you which is higher.

Run it a few times. Sometimes the second is higher, sometimes lower, sometimes the same. There is no pattern — each pair is independent. But the *distribution* of higher vs lower is not 50/50, because "same" steals from both. With 13 possible values, the chance of an exact match is about 1 in 13.

This is the game's raw probability — the thing the player is betting against. No skill involved yet, just two numbers and a comparison.
