## What you have built

Sonar is complete. Eighty-two lines: a title screen, a randomly populated 8x8 grid, coordinate-based probing, hit detection, Manhattan distance calculation with `ABS`, colour-coded distance clues, a hit counter with win condition, duplicate-probe rejection, a 30-guess loss condition with target reveal, ratings, and a replay loop.

This game introduced two-dimensional arrays and spatial reasoning. The player learned to think in coordinates, interpret distance clues, and triangulate from multiple data points. The grid is a map, the distances are sonar pings, and the colour is the heat signature. The search itself is the game.
