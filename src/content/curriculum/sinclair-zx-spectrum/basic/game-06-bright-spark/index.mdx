Bright Spark is Simon on the Spectrum. Four coloured panels, each with a distinct tone. The computer plays a growing sequence; you repeat it from memory. Get one wrong and it is game over — your score is how long you lasted.

This is the most complex game so far. It combines everything you know — FOR/NEXT, INKEY$, strings, colour, sound — and adds two new tools: BRIGHT (the Spectrum's 15th colour) and GO SUB (reusable code blocks).

**What you will learn:**

- `BRIGHT` — the lighter variant of each colour
- Strings as sequences — growing, indexing, replaying
- `GO SUB`/`RETURN` — reusable subroutines
- `VAL` and `STR$` — converting between strings and numbers
- The design concept: **Pattern** — recognise, reproduce, extend

**6 units. About 7–9 hours.**
