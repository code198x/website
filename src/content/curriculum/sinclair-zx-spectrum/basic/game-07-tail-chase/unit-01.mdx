Unit 1: a character that moves by itself.

![A yellow @ moving across a black screen](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-07-tail-chase/unit-01-moving-character/moving.png)

Nine lines. A yellow `@` starts on the left and slides right across the screen. When it reaches column 30, it stops.

The pattern is familiar from Hot and Cold: draw, pause, erase, update, loop. But there's a crucial difference. In Hot and Cold, the cursor moved when you pressed a key. Here, **the character moves by itself**. Nobody is pressing anything. The `@` just goes.

This is the fundamental difference between a puzzle and an action game. In a puzzle, you think and then act. In an action game, the world moves whether you're ready or not. Every game from here on has something moving by itself — the snake, enemies, falling blocks, projectiles. The player reacts rather than initiates.

Right now, the character moves at whatever speed BASIC runs the loop. Some frames it's fast, some it's slow. PAUSE 15 gives a rough delay, but it's not precise. The next unit fixes this with hardware timing.

## What You've Built

- A character (`@`) that moves right automatically
- The erase-update-draw loop running continuously
- Automatic movement — the character moves without player input
