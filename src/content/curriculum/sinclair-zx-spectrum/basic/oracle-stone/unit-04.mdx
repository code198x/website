Six is a start. Ten is a personality.

Four new IF lines at 290–320. Line 200 changed from `RND * 6` to `RND * 10`. The new answers add character: "THE STARS SAY YES", "NOT ON A TUESDAY", "THE ORACLE IS UNSURE", "WITHOUT A DOUBT."

<Figure src="/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/oracle-stone/unit-04/screenshot.png" alt="Blue ZX Spectrum screen showing the Oracle's answer: THE STARS SAY YES" caption="Ten answers with more character — THE STARS SAY YES." />

The mix matters. Too many "yes" answers and the Oracle is a pushover. Too many "no" answers and it is hostile. The best set has a range — confident, evasive, cryptic, and at least one that makes you laugh.

## Content vs logic

The program's structure has not changed since unit 2 — it is still `RND` into chained `IF`. All that changed is the number of answers and what they say. This is the design concept **content vs logic**: the engine stays the same, the content grows. Most games work this way. More levels, more enemies, more dialogue — same engine, more content.
