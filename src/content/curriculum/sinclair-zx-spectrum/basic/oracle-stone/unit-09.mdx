The screen has been a blue chamber since the very first unit — `BORDER 1: PAPER 1` on line 10. What the Oracle has lacked is a voice of its own. Its answer prints in the same white as everything else around it.

Two new lines wrap the answer in colour. Line 220: `INK 5` switches the ink to cyan just before the answer prints. Line 330: `INK 7` switches back to white for the framing below. Whichever answer fires between them comes out cyan.

## Colour as meaning

The title and the framing are white. The answer is cyan. That single difference tells the player where to look — the cyan line is *the Oracle speaking*, distinct from the decoration around it.

`INK` sets the colour of everything printed after it, until the next `INK`. So `INK 5`, then the answer, then `INK 7` colours exactly the answer and nothing else. The same trick works with any of the eight colours: `INK 6` for yellow, `INK 2` for red.

The chamber has a voice now. Next, we make it look carved.
