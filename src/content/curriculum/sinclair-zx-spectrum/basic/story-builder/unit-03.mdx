## What changed

Lines 10 and 50 are untouched — the same setup and name question from unit 2. The new lines slot in at 60 and 70 (in the gap between 50 and 130). Line 130 changed from `PRINT "Hello, "; n$` to just `PRINT` (a blank line). Line 140 is new.

Three variables now: `n$` (name), `a$` (adjective), `b$` (animal). The semicolons in line 140 glue five pieces together: the name, fixed text, the adjective, a space, and the animal.

## Semicolons

`PRINT n$; " met a "; a$; " "; b$` joins text without starting a new line between pieces. Each `;` says "keep printing on the same line." Without semicolons, each piece would print on its own line.
