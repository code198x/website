Lines 950–990 handle a computer win: red "Computer wins!" with a low buzz. Lines 1000–1020 handle a draw: yellow "Draw!" with two flat tones.

Three outcomes, three colours, three sounds. The same parallel structure from Touchdown's landing screens. The player knows the result from the colour and sound before reading the text.

## Three outcomes, one game

Every game of Noughts and Crosses ends one of three ways. In previous games, outcomes were binary (win or lose) or tiered (perfect, bumpy, crash). Here there are three distinct endings with equal status. A draw is not a failure — it is what happens when both sides play well. The yellow colour and neutral tone reinforce this: a draw is an achievement, not a consolation.
