Nothing new here — `CLS` clears the screen, `PRINT` puts text on it. But the *tone* is different. Story Builder's opening was a greeting. This one is a challenge.

Notice the line numbers: 10, 50, 60, 70. There are gaps — room for new lines between 10 and 50, and between 70 and whatever comes next. You will fill those gaps in the next few units.
