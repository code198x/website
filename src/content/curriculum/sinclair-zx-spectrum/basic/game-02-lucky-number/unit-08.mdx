## How BEEP works

`BEEP duration, pitch` plays a single tone.

- **Duration** is in seconds: `0.1` is a tenth of a second — a short click. `0.5` is a half-second — a held note.
- **Pitch** is in semitones above or below middle C: `0` is middle C, `12` is one octave up, `-12` is one octave down.

The directional trick: `BEEP 0.1, -5` plays a tone five semitones below middle C. `BEEP 0.1, 5` plays one five semitones above. Low means "go up." High means "come down." The player hears the direction without reading the text.

## Three channels

The game now has three feedback channels:

1. **Words** — "Too high!" / "Too low!" — explicit, unambiguous.
2. **Colour** — border shifts blue to white — peripheral, intuitive.
3. **Sound** — directional tone — instant, even with eyes closed.

Each channel carries the same information in a different way. Together they create what the design concept calls **Response**: the game communicates through every sense it has available.

Close your eyes and play by ear. If you can tell "higher" from "lower" without seeing the screen, the audio feedback works.

## Try it

Change the pitches. `BEEP 0.1, (n - g) / 10` calculates the pitch from the actual distance — higher when you are far below, lower when you are far above, converging to middle C as you close in. More information in the sound, but harder to read. Which version do you prefer?
