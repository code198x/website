Story Builder is the first program you write on the Spectrum. No game logic, no scoring, no loops — just the computer speaking and listening.

<Figure src="/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-01-story-builder/unit-08/screenshot.png" alt="ZX Spectrum screen showing the finished mad-libs story printed in cyan" caption="Where you are headed: your answers, woven into a story and revealed in cyan." />

You start with a single line: `PRINT "Hello"`. By the end, you have a program that asks questions, remembers the answers, and weaves them into a ridiculous story. It is the kind of program you show someone.

**What you will learn:**

- `PRINT` — telling the computer what to display
- `CLS` — clearing the screen
- `INPUT` — asking the player to type something
- String variables (`n$`, `a$`) — remembering text
- Semicolons — joining text together on one line

**8 units. About 4–6 hours.**
