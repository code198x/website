Two new lines slotted into the gap: line 40 asks for an adjective, line 50 asks for an animal. Line 100 prints a blank line (spacing), and line 110 builds a sentence from all three answers.

Run it. Type "Dave", "enormous", "penguin". The screen shows:

```
Dave met a enormous penguin
```

## What changed

Lines 10 and 20 are untouched — the same CLS and name question from unit 2. The new lines slot in at 40 and 50 (in the gap between 20 and 100). Line 100 changed from `PRINT "Hello, "; n$` to just `PRINT` (a blank line). Line 110 is new.

Three variables now: `n$` (name), `a$` (adjective), `b$` (animal). The semicolons in line 110 glue five pieces together: the name, fixed text, the adjective, a space, and the animal.

## Semicolons

`PRINT n$; " met a "; a$; " "; b$` joins text without starting a new line between pieces. Each `;` says "keep printing on the same line." Without semicolons, each piece would print on its own line.
