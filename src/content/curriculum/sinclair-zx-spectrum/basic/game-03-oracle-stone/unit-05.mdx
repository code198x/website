The Oracle answers instantly. Real oracles take their time.

Four new lines between the question and the answer. Line 100 prints a blank line. Line 110 announces the pondering. Line 120 plays four descending tones — a thinking sound. Line 130 waits half a second.

Run it. Ask a question. The Oracle says "The Oracle ponders..." — four mystical tones play — a pause — then the answer appears. The delay is half a second, but it feels longer because the player is waiting.

## Dramatic timing

`PAUSE 25` waits half a second (50 frames per second on the Spectrum, so 25 frames = 0.5 seconds). The four `BEEP` notes take about 1.2 seconds. Together: roughly two seconds between question and answer.

That gap is the entire trick. An instant answer feels mechanical. A delayed answer feels considered. The Oracle is not actually thinking — but the player's brain fills the gap with meaning. "It is taking a moment because my question was hard." The ceremony creates the illusion.
