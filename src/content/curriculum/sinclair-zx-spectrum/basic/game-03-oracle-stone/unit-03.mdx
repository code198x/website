## Writing good answers

The best Oracle answers are vague enough to fit any question. "YES" works for everything. "ASK AGAIN LATER" works for everything. "THE SIGNS ARE UNCLEAR" works for everything.

Avoid specific answers like "YOU WILL GET A DOG" — they only make sense for one question. The Oracle's power comes from being confidently vague.
