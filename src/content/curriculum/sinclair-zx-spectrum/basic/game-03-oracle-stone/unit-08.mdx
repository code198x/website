The answer appears on a blank screen. It works, but it looks lonely. The answer needs framing.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-oracle-stone/unit-08/framed.bas" />

Seven new lines on the reveal screen. Lines 150–190 recreate the title and add decorative lines above the answer. Lines 340–350 add a matching decoration below. The answer sits between two rows of equals signs — a proclamation, not just a word.

<Figure src="/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-oracle-stone/unit-08/screenshot.png" alt="Blue ZX Spectrum reveal screen: the title, equals-sign rules, and WITHOUT A DOUBT between them" caption="Framed like a proclamation — WITHOUT A DOUBT." />

All seven lines are PRINT statements — no new commands. But the visual effect is significant: the answer screen now looks designed, not accidental.
