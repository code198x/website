The Oracle ponders, then the answer appears below the question. It works, but the question and answer share the screen. The answer deserves its own moment.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-oracle-stone/unit-06/reveal.bas" />

Two new lines. Line 140: `CLS` clears the screen after the thinking sequence. Line 210: `BEEP 0.1, 24` plays a short high tone — the reveal signal.

Run it. Ask a question. The Oracle ponders. The screen goes blank. A chime sounds. The answer appears alone on a clean screen.

The same idea as Story Builder's reveal (unit 5) — separate input from output. Here the gap between them is filled with ceremony: tones, a pause, a blank screen, then a chime. Each element adds a beat to the ritual.
