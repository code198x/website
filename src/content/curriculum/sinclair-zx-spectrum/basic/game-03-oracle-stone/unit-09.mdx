The Oracle speaks on a white screen. That is a computer. An Oracle should speak from somewhere darker.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-oracle-stone/unit-09/oracle-stone.bas" />

Line 10 gains `BORDER 1: PAPER 1: INK 7` — blue background, white text. The entire screen becomes the Oracle's chamber. Line 220 switches to `INK 5` (cyan) for the answer. Line 330 switches back to `INK 7` for the decoration below.

Three new lines, one modified. The program's behaviour is identical, but the atmosphere is transformed. Dark blue says "this is a place." Cyan says "this is the Oracle speaking." White framing says "the ritual is over."

## Make it yours

Write your own answers. Change the colours — `PAPER 0` (black) with `INK 6` (yellow) for a different mood. Add more answers. Change the prompt: "O Wise One, I ask thee:" or "Dear Oracle, please tell me:".

Show it to someone. Ask them to ask a question. Watch their face when the answer appears. The ceremony works — even when they know the answer is random.
