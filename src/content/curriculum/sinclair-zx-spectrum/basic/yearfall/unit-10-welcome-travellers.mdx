The first visit is scheduled for year three, four or five. After each decision, the next is three to five years away. That spacing makes the event occasional without rolling again every time the player presses an invalid key.

The offer contains three to six people. Welcoming costs six grain per person, separate from their annual food. The screen shows the fee, grain remaining, proposed population and total food requirement. Y accepts; N declines at no cost. They join immediately and can work in this year's fields if fed.

## Decide once, then initialise the plan

At the start of a year, clear welcome and admitted, choose the market price and compare yr with visit. The offer occurs before the default food and planting proposal is made. Returning to 220 therefore creates defaults for the population after the decision.

The can flag is one only when grain covers both the welcome fee and the whole settlement's food. It does not reserve seed or promise that acceptance is wise. When can is zero, Y does nothing and the screen explains the shortage. Q and R remain available.

Acceptance subtracts the fee, adds guests to pop and records admitted and welcome for the report. joined accumulates accepted travellers across years. Declining changes none of those resources. Both paths schedule the next visit once and leave the event screen.

<Question prompt="Five travellers arrive when 66 people have 737 grain. What does accepting mean?">

The fee is 30, leaving 707 grain. Population becomes 71, whose annual food costs 213. Those food costs have not yet been paid. If fed, the five arrivals add ten acres of working capacity, but provide no land or seed themselves.

</Question>

The report reconstructs the opening grain before the welcome, lists the fee separately and includes admitted people in its population change. This prevents an unexplained drop between the previous year's store and this year's account.

Play to an offer and try an irrelevant key before deciding. Nothing should change. Try declining in one run and welcoming in another. The random sequences and budgets can differ; those runs do not by themselves measure which policy is better. The ten-year summary now counts travellers welcomed, but still ends the run at this checkpoint.
