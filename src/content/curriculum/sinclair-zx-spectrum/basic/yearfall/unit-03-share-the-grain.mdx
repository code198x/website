## Three limits on planting

The balance is now `grain-feed-plant`. Planting must also fit the owned land and the available workforce. Each fed person can work two acres, so the labour limit is `2*fed`.

Use fed rather than pop here. With food for forty people, only eighty acres can be worked. Allowing all sixty opening residents to supply labour and then removing twenty through starvation would make the year's explanation inconsistent.

The land and labour checks are separate IF statements. They can fail for different reasons, even when grain remains. A screen with one warning is not a list of every problem: after repairing the displayed problem, another may become visible. `ok` remains zero if any check rejects the plan.

These rules define this game's economy. They are not agricultural measurements. Their purpose is to create relationships the player can read: less food reduces labour; fewer planted acres reduce the next harvest; more seed spends grain that could have remained in reserve.
