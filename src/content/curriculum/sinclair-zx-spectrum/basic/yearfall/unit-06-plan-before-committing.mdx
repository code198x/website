RUN. Food starts at the requirement, or all available grain if there is less. Planting starts with the available land, then is reduced to fit fed workers and the grain remaining after food. These are defaults, not commands to the player.

Press P and enter 101. The plan should reject planting more land than you own. Space must leave the year and resources unchanged. Enter 100 to repair it. Now try food 0: the existing planting proposal requires workers, so that combined plan is invalid. Set planting to 0 as well and the plan becomes possible, although committing it leaves nobody alive.

## Recalculate instead of patching the picture

Each accepted edit returns to 270. The display routine recomputes fed, left and ok from the current proposal. We do not adjust a number merely because a key was pressed; redrawing derives the picture from the same stored values used by the harvest.

The editor receives an edit selector: two means food, three means planting. That selects its label and the variable assigned at the end. The digit, deletion and cancellation behaviour stays shared. A string label is presentation; the selector decides the destination.

The opening defaults are applied only when setting up the run. Editing food does not silently rewrite planting. Keeping an invalid proposal visible lets the player choose which part to repair. The warning explains why Space currently does nothing.

<Question prompt="Why allow an unaffordable proposal to stay on screen?">

It is useful feedback while editing several related values. The safety boundary is the commitment: Space must reject the whole plan until every constraint passes. Silently replacing the player's other choices would hide the relationship they are trying to understand.

</Question>
