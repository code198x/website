RUN. The initial plan is valid, so the program records the opening values, spends its 280 grain and produces a harvest. The report should show 360 at the start, 180 food spent, 100 seed spent and a harvest between 200 and 500. The final grain is between 280 and 580.

## Copy before changing

The oldpop, oldgrain and oldland variables preserve the opening values for the report. They are snapshots, not extra resources. Line 410 commits left to grain. The starvation calculation removes `pop-fed` people and adds that number to lost.

The harvest uses `2+INT (RND*4)`. RND supplies a value from zero up to, but not including, one. Multiplying by four and rounding down gives 0, 1, 2 or 3; adding two produces a yield of 2–5. RANDOMIZE seeds the sequence from the machine's running clock. It does not promise a good year or guarantee a different result every run.

Multiply the yield by planted acres, then add that harvest to the remaining grain. These are separate changes, so the report can explain them. At this checkpoint newcomers stays zero: being fully fed does not yet make the population grow.

<Question prompt="A yield of two arrives after feeding 180 and planting 100. What remains?">

360−180−100 leaves 80 before harvest. The fields produce 100×2, adding 200. The store ends at 280. The harvest is an addition to the reserve, not a replacement for it.

</Question>

RUN several times and check that equation against each report. A small sample need not contain every yield. To inspect a rejected plan, temporarily change plant to 101 in line 240: the program must STOP on its planning screen before any resources change. Restore 100 afterwards. STOP after a valid report is also intentional; the next checkpoint will give the player a way to edit food and retry.
