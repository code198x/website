RUN and commit the opening plan. With everyone fed, three newcomers arrive: `INT (60/20)` is three. The first report should therefore show 63 people. Space continues to year two, where the proposed food requirement becomes 189. Grain carries over from the actual harvest; it does not return to 360.

## Follow the resolution order

Food and seed are spent first. Starvation removes people who lacked complete rations. The harvest is then added. Newcomers arrive only when nobody starved, at five per cent of the opening population rounded down. They join after the harvest, so they could not have worked those fields. They will need food next year.

The condition uses deaths equal to zero, rather than surplus food. Giving extra food does not increase growth. A well-fed population below twenty receives no newcomers under this rounded rule; we have not added a minimum of one.

After the report, test the ending before incrementing yr. An empty settlement ends immediately. Year ten ends after its harvest has been counted. Otherwise advance the year and construct a new affordable starting proposal from the retained resources.
