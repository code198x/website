Lines 900–1030 add the end-of-game screen. Line 920 handles collapse: if the population reached zero at any point, the game ends with "Your kingdom collapsed" in red. Lines 930–960 display the final state for completed games.

Lines 970–1010 rate the player with a cascading IF chain. You start with 100 people, so the thresholds measure growth against that baseline. 200+ means you doubled the population — genuinely hard — earning "Wise Ruler!" in green. 150+ means strong growth: "Prosperity!" 100+ means you held steady: "Steady Hand" in yellow. 50+ means you lost half: "Hard Times." Below 50, most are gone: "The People Suffer" in red. Each condition uses `GO TO 1030` to skip lower ratings.

## The cascading IF

The rating chain at lines 970–1010 checks from highest to lowest. If `pop >= 200`, it prints "Wise Ruler!" and jumps past the other checks. If not, it falls to the next line. This cascade ensures only one rating prints — the first one that matches.

This is the same technique used in Sonar's efficiency ratings and Locksmith's guess-count display. The pattern: test the best case first, jump on match, fall through to the next. By the time line 1010 runs, all better ratings have been eliminated.

## Two endings, different weight

Collapse (line 920) and completion (lines 930–1010) are structurally different. Collapse can happen at any point — year 3, year 7 — and the `GO TO 900` from line 500 jumps here immediately. Completion only happens after year 10 (line 780). Both land on the same screen, but collapse shows no stats, no rating. Just the fact of failure. The rating system only applies to rulers who survived. Making it to year 10 is the first achievement; the rating measures how well you did it.
