## From demo to experiment

Until now the roll count was baked into the program. Letting the player set it turns a fixed demo into an experiment they run themselves — and that one `INPUT` is the dial that reveals the whole point of the game.

Ask for 10 rolls and the totals come out jagged: randomness looks lawless. Ask for 5000 and they level off, every face landing near a sixth of the total. The fairness was always there — it was just hidden under small numbers. The player can now prove convergence at any scale they like, which makes the lesson theirs rather than yours.
