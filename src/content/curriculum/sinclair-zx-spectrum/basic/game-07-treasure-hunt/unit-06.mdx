## What's New

**Variable `v`** tracks lives, starting at 3.

**Place hazards (lines 600-660).** Three red `#` symbols placed randomly, using the same pattern as coin placement but with `INK 2` (red).

**Hazard collision (line 210).** `IF a = 58` checks for red ink on white paper (2 + 56 = 58). When the player hits a hazard, it calls the "lose life" subroutine.

**Lose life (lines 900-950).** Decreases `v` by 1, plays a low beep, updates the HUD, and resets the player to the centre of the screen.

**Game over (line 230).** When `v` reaches 0, the game jumps to the game-over screen.

## Collision by Colour

Two ATTR checks now happen on every move:

| ATTR value | Colour | Meaning |
|------------|--------|---------|
| 49 | Yellow on black | Coin — collect it |
| 58 | Red on white | Hazard — lose a life |

The pattern scales naturally. Add more colours, add more game objects.

## The Reset

When the player hits a hazard, they return to position (10, 15) — the centre of the screen. This gives them a moment of safety. Without this, they'd immediately hit the same hazard again.

## Try This

**Invincibility frames.** After losing a life, the player flashes for a moment. Add `FOR j = 1 TO 5: INK 4: PRINT AT r, c; "O": PAUSE 3: PRINT AT r, c; " ": PAUSE 3: NEXT j` after line 940.

**More hazards.** Change `FOR i = 1 TO 3` to `FOR i = 1 TO 8`. The screen becomes much more dangerous.

## What You've Learnt

- **Lives** — a counter that decreases on hazard contact
- **Multiple ATTR checks** — different colours mean different things
- **Player reset** — return to a safe position after taking damage
- **Game over** — end the game when lives reach zero
