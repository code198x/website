Hot and Cold doesn't end after one find. Five treasures, five rounds, total moves tracked. The entire game — from hiding the treasure to celebrating the find — runs inside a FOR loop:

```
90 FOR n=1 TO 5
...
590 NEXT n
```

Everything between lines 90 and 590 runs five times. Each pass: a new random position, the cursor resets to centre, the player hunts. After five rounds, the results screen appears with the total move count and a rating.

This is a **FOR loop around the whole game** — the biggest loop you've written. In Lucky Number, FOR loops drew panels. In Bright Spark, they played sequences. Here, the loop IS the game structure. Five rounds of hunting, wrapped in `FOR n=1 TO 5`.

**Scoring** in Hot and Cold is cumulative — fewer total moves = better. The rating tiers (`<=40`, `<=70`, `<=100`, `>100`) give the player a target to beat on their next play. This is **replayability through scoring** — the game is the same each time, but your performance changes. Can you find all five treasures in under 40 moves?

## What You've Learnt

- A **FOR loop around the game** creates multiple rounds
- **Cumulative scoring** (total moves) gives long-term motivation
- **Rating tiers** turn a number into a target to beat
- Loops scale from small (draw a row) to large (structure a whole game)
