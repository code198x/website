## What You'll Build

A treasure is hidden at a random position on screen. You move a cursor with Q/A/O/P and the border colour tells you how close you are — blue when cold, red when warm, white when you're on fire. Five rounds, fewest moves wins.

![Hot and Cold gameplay — blue border means cold](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-04-hot-and-cold/gameplay.png)

## Why This Game?

Colour Flood filled the screen with patterns, but everything was sequential — print from top to bottom. Games need to put things at specific positions and move them around.

`PRINT AT` is the command that unlocks this. It places text at any row and column on screen. Combined with variables for position, you can move a character around freely. This is the foundation of every game from here on.

## What You'll Learn

- **PRINT AT** — placing text at a specific row and column
- **The coordinate grid** — row 0-21, column 0-31
- **Position variables** — using `r` and `c` to track where something is
- **Four-direction movement** — Q/A/O/P to move up/down/left/right
- **Boundary checking** — keeping the cursor on screen
- **ABS** — calculating distance without caring about direction
- **The game loop** — read input, update state, draw

## Prerequisites

Complete [Game 3: Colour Flood](/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-03-colour-flood/) first. You'll need `FOR/NEXT`, `INK`, `PAPER`, `BORDER`, and `BRIGHT`.
