## Fixed positions

Every element has a fixed screen position:

- Title at row 1
- The word at row 4
- Lives at row 7
- Tried letters at row 9
- Input prompt at row 12
- Feedback message at row 14

`CLS` at the start of each loop clears the screen, then everything redraws in place. No scrolling, no leftover text.

## Colour-coded letters

Lines 190–200: revealed letters print in green (`INK 4`), underscores in white (`INK 7`). The player sees at a glance which letters they have found — green stands out against the dark background.

## Feedback

Line 370: wrong guesses show "Not in the word!" in red (`INK 2`). Line 380: correct guesses show "Yes!" in green. Colour + words + sound (BEEP from Unit 3) — three channels for the same information.

The screen is now a game board, not a terminal. Each element sits where the player expects it. The layout is a design decision — try moving the lives display to the bottom, or the tried letters to the top. Does the game feel different?
