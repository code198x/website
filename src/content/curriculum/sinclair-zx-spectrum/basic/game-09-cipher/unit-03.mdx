## The game loop

Line 210: `GO TO 70` sends the program back to redraw the screen and ask for another guess. The loop runs until one of two exit conditions:

- Line 190: `IF d$ = w$` — the display string matches the word. Every underscore has been replaced. The player wins.
- Line 200: `IF lives = 0` — no lives left. The player loses.

## Lives

`LET lives = 7` gives the player 7 wrong guesses. Each wrong letter costs one. The lives display shows how many remain. Seven is generous enough for short words but tight for longer ones — the word length *is* the difficulty.

## Revealing the answer

Both outcomes reveal the word: "The word was SPECTRUM." Win or lose, the player sees what they were working toward. Revealing on loss is important — it converts frustration into learning. The player thinks "oh, I should have tried P" instead of "I lost and don't know why."
