One word per game is not enough. The player should keep going — word after word, tracking wins and losses.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-09-cipher/unit-07/counters.bas" />

Line 140 initialises two counters: `wins` and `losses`, both at 0. Line 240 shows them at the top of the screen: `"Won: 3  Lost: 1"`.

Lines 500–510 change from `STOP` to `GO TO 150`. When the player wins, `wins` increments and the program jumps to line 150 — pick a new random word, build a fresh underscore string, reset lives and tried letters, start again. When the player loses, `losses` increments and the same thing happens.

## Two counters, one story

Hi-Lo tracked a single streak. Cipher tracks both wins and losses separately. The pair tells a richer story than either alone: "Won: 5, Lost: 2" means the player is good but not perfect. "Won: 3, Lost: 0" means they are on a clean run. "Won: 0, Lost: 3" means they should try more common letters first.

Both counters are initialised on line 140, before line 150 where the word-pick loop starts. The replay jumps to 150, skipping 140 — so the counters persist across words. This is the same position trick from Hi-Lo's `best` score: variables before the loop boundary survive; variables inside it reset.
