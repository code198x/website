One word is a round. A session is many rounds. Tracking wins and losses makes the player care about consistency, not just one result.

## Persistent score

Line 75: `LET wins = 0: LET losses = 0` — initialised once, before the game loop.

Lines 390–400: after each word, the appropriate counter increments.

Line 165: `PRINT AT 2, 6; "Won: "; wins; "  Lost: "; losses` — visible during play. The player sees their record building.

## Replay

Lines 530–550 and 630–650: "Press any key for next word" + `PAUSE 0` + `GO TO 80`. The program picks a new random word and starts a fresh round. The score persists because the `GO TO` jumps past the initialisation line.

The same replay-with-persistent-state pattern from Hi-Lo — jump past the init, keep the counters alive.

## The session effect

Without the score, each round is isolated. With it, the player thinks "I'm 4–1, let me keep going." The score transforms a series of independent rounds into a *session* — a stretch of play with a narrative arc. The player is not just guessing words; they are building a record.
