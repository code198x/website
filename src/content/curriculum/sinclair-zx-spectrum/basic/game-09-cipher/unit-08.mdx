## Parallel structure

The win screen and lose screen follow the same layout: clear, title, result message (green or red), the word, sound, totals, prompt. The structures are identical; only the colour, message, and sound differ. This parallel makes both screens feel like they belong to the same game — two outcomes of one system.

Revealing the word on both screens is important. When the player wins, seeing the completed word is satisfying. When they lose, seeing what they missed is educational — "oh, it was CASSETTE, I should have tried S earlier." The reveal turns failure into a learning moment, not just a dead end.
