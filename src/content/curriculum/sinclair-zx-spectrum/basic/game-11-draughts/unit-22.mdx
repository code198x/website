In draughts, a piece that captures must continue jumping if another capture is available from the landing square. A single turn can involve multiple jumps as the piece zig-zags across the board, removing opponent pieces along the way.

## How It Works

**Line 1059** calls `GO SUB 1750` after a capture is executed. This checks whether the piece that just landed can capture again.

**Lines 1750-1780** form the multi-jump check. It takes the piece's new position (u, v) and checks all four diagonal directions for another valid capture. The logic mirrors the capture scan from unit 20, but checks from a specific piece position rather than scanning the whole board.

**Line 1769** is the chain trigger. If another capture is available, it prints "Jump again!" and jumps back to the input prompt at line 110 using `GO TO`. The turn does not switch. The same player must enter another capture move.

## Looping Without Recursion

BASIC does not support recursion. A subroutine cannot cleanly call itself. Instead, the multi-jump uses `GO TO 110` to jump back to the input section of the main loop. This skips the turn-switch logic, keeping the same player active. When the chain ends (no more captures available), execution falls through to the normal turn switch.

This is a practical workaround. The piece stays active, the player keeps entering captures, and the loop continues until no more jumps are possible. It is not elegant by modern standards, but it works within the constraints of Spectrum BASIC.

## Multi-Jump Rules

During a multi-jump chain:

- The same piece must make all the jumps
- Each jump must be a valid capture (opponent piece in between, empty landing)
- The direction rules still apply (non-kings can only jump forward)
- Each captured piece is removed immediately
- If a piece reaches the back row during a chain, it is promoted to king and the chain continues with king rules

## Try This

**Set up a double jump.** Arrange three pieces in a line so that one piece can jump over two opponents in succession. Execute the chain and watch both opponent pieces disappear.

**Try to stop mid-chain.** When "Jump again!" appears, try entering a simple move instead of a capture. The validation should reject it (this will be enforced in unit 23).
