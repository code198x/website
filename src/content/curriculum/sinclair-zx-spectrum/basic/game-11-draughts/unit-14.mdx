When a capture happens, the player needs clear feedback: the jumped piece vanishes, a sound plays, and the captured piece count updates. This unit separates the capture display logic from the move execution so each part is clean and testable.

## How It Works

**Line 1035** checks the jump flag. If `j = 0`, this was a simple move and we skip the capture logic entirely, jumping to line 1060.

**Lines 1040-1050** handle the capture when `j = 1`. Line 1040 calculates the jumped square. Line 1045 clears it from the array. Line 1050 clears it from the screen by printing a space with PAPER 0.

**Line 1055** plays a BEEP at pitch 5, giving the capture a satisfying sound distinct from the move click.

**Lines 1057-1058** increment the correct counter. If Player 1 just captured, `g` increases. If Player 2, `h` increases.

**Line 835** displays both counters below the player indicator: "Captured: 2 - 1" with the numbers in the matching player colours.

## Sound as Feedback

The game now has two distinct sounds. A regular move plays a short click at pitch 10 (line 1060). A capture plays a deeper BEEP at pitch 5 (line 1055) followed by the move click. The player hears the difference without looking at the board.

Sound design in games is about communication. A capture is more important than a simple move, so it gets a more noticeable sound. As we add more features - king promotion, errors, wins - each will get its own audio signature.

## The Status Display

The captured piece count gives players a running score during the game. In draughts, captures are how you win. Seeing "Captured: 5 - 3" tells both players the game's trajectory at a glance. The INK colours match each player, so there is no ambiguity about which count belongs to whom.

## Try This

**Change the capture sound.** Try different pitch values. Lower values (like 0 or -5) sound more dramatic. Higher values (like 15 or 20) sound lighter. Find a pitch that feels satisfying when you jump an opponent's piece.

**Add a flash.** Before clearing the captured piece, briefly change its INK to white (INK 7) with a short PAUSE. This creates a flash effect that draws attention to the capture.
