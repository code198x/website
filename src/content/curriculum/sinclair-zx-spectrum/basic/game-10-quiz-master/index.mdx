Quiz Master is a category quiz. Four subjects, two questions each. Get one right and your score climbs. Get all eight and you earn the title — Quiz Master.

This is the second game in Volume 2 — **Patterns of State**. Where Cipher taught DATA and READ as a sequential list, Quiz Master teaches **indexed storage**: arrays that hold values you can access by position, and RESTORE to navigate between DATA sections.

By the end, the quiz has coloured category headers, a structured question card, per-category score tracking, ratings, and replay. The program grows from 9 lines to 50 — but the logic barely changes. What changes is how the data is stored, displayed, and reused.

**What you will learn:**

- `DIM` — creating arrays for indexed storage
- `RESTORE` — rewinding DATA to a specific line
- `PRINT AT` — structured screen layout (the question card)
- `BEEP` — audio feedback for correct and wrong answers
- Colour-as-category — each subject gets its own PAPER colour
- Ratings as identity — the score becomes a title

**10 units. About 10–14 hours.**
