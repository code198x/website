The player answers but gets no immediate feedback beyond text. Sound makes the result feel instant.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-10-quiz-master/unit-07/sound.bas" />

Lines 270–280 add BEEP to each outcome: `BEEP 0.2,12` for correct (high, short), `BEEP 0.3,-5` for wrong (low, longer). The player hears the result before reading the text. This is the same high/low feedback pattern from Hi-Lo and Cipher — by now the player instinctively knows what each sound means.

Lines 350–370 add a results screen after the quiz ends. Line 350 clears the screen. Line 360 prints "Quiz complete!" Line 370 shows the final score. The quiz no longer just stops after the last question — it wraps up properly.

The results screen is separate from the question cards. `CLS` wipes the last question and gives the score its own moment — the same principle as `CLS` before the story in Story Builder and `CLS` before the game over in Bright Spark.
