A score of 6 out of 8 is information. "Expert" is an identity. Ratings turn a number into something the player wants to earn.

Lines 350–390 map the score to a rating. The checks run top-down: 8 is "Quiz Master!", 6 or 7 is "Expert", 4 or 5 is "Not bad", anything below is "Keep trying." Each IF ends with `GO TO 390` to skip the remaining checks — without it, a score of 8 would trigger *every* condition (since 8 is also ≥ 6 and ≥ 4).

Each rating prints in a different colour: green for Quiz Master, cyan for Expert, yellow for Not bad, red for Keep trying. The colour reinforces the meaning before the player reads the word.

## Ratings as identity

This is the embedded concept for Quiz Master: **a rating reframes a number as an identity.** "Quiz Master" isn't just 8/8 — it's a title the player feels they've earned. It's the same score, but it *means* something different when it has a name.

Every game with a high-score table, a rank, or a title screen uses this trick. The number is the mechanism. The name is the reward.
