## Finite resources create tension

Without fuel, the game has no stakes. Hold SPACE, land slowly, done. With fuel, the player must make choices: brake now and spend fuel early, or save it and hope to slow down in time? The resource forces a strategy.

Fifty fuel units is a tight budget. Gravity adds 1 speed per frame, and each thrust removes 2. If the player holds SPACE continuously, they burn through the fuel fast and still have altitude left when the tank runs dry. The game teaches itself: spam the button and you run out; manage your burns and you land safely.

This is the design concept behind every resource-management game: **a finite resource transforms "can I do this?" into "when should I do this?"** The mechanic is simple — one subtraction on one line — but it changes the entire feel of the game.
