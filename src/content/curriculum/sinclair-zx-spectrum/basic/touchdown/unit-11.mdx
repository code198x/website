The crash outcome says "CRASH!" and stops. It should feel like a crash.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/touchdown/unit-11/crash.bas" />

Line 470 changes from an inline STOP to `GO TO 800`. Lines 800–820 build the crash screen: line 800 turns the border red. Line 810 plays a low buzz — the impact sound. Line 820 prints "CRASH!" in red ink.

The crash is the first outcome to get its own section because it is the most common one. New players will crash many times before they learn to manage thrust and fuel. The crash screen is their most frequent feedback — it should be distinctive.

The other two outcomes (perfect and bumpy) still use inline STOP. They get their own screens next unit. Building them one at a time lets you see how each outcome takes shape independently before they all connect.
