Combat costs HP. Without a way to heal, the player slowly bleeds to death across multiple fights — even victories leave them weaker. Health potions solve this. Found in chests, carried in limited supply, and used with a single keypress during exploration. They add a resource management layer: when to use a potion, when to save it, and whether the next floor will offer more.

## How It Works

**Line 26** initialises the potion count `p` to 0. The player starts with no potions.

**Line 170** in the main loop checks for the H key. When pressed, the potion subroutine at line 700 is called.

**Lines 700-714** handle potion use. Line 702 checks whether the player has any potions — if `p` is 0, a red message "No potions!" appears and the subroutine returns. Otherwise, the potion count decrements, HP increases by 10, and a cap ensures HP never exceeds maximum (`IF h > z THEN LET h = z`). A brief rising tone confirms the heal, and a green message shows the new HP.

**Line 756** in the chest subroutine handles finding a potion. The check `IF i = 1 AND p < 3` ensures the player can carry a maximum of 3 potions. If already carrying 3, the chest falls through to the gold fallback.

## Resource Management

The potion system introduces scarcity. Three potions is enough to survive a few bad fights, but not enough to heal through every encounter without care. The player must decide: use a potion now to enter the next fight at full strength, or save it for an emergency? This decision is more interesting than having unlimited healing.

The carry limit of 3 creates additional tension around chests. Finding a potion when already carrying 3 wastes the chest — the gold fallback is less valuable than a potion on a deep floor. Managing potion count before opening a chest becomes part of the strategy.

## The Healing Cap

`IF h > z THEN LET h = z` prevents overhealing. A potion always restores exactly 10 HP, but current HP never exceeds maximum HP. Using a potion when only 3 HP below maximum wastes 7 points of healing. This encourages waiting until the player has taken significant damage before healing — another small decision that adds depth.

## Status Display

The status panel (line 512) shows the potion count as "PT:" followed by the number. The player can always see how many potions they carry. This is essential information for making combat decisions — knowing you have a potion in reserve changes whether you stand and fight or flee.

## Try This

**Change the heal amount.** Try 5 HP or 20 HP per potion. A smaller heal makes potions less decisive, requiring multiple uses to recover from a tough fight. A larger heal makes each potion more precious.

**Add a potion hotkey in combat.** Currently potions can only be used during exploration. What would change if the player could heal mid-fight? Would that make combat too easy?
