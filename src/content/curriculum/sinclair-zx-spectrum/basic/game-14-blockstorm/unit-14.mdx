Until now, the player is invulnerable. Enemies descend and the formation can invade, but nothing directly damages the ship. This unit adds a life system: three lives, a death animation when hit, a brief respawn pause, and a game over screen when all lives are spent.

## How It Works

**Line 20** sets the starting lives: `LET li = 3`. The HUD already displays this value.

**Lines 530-549** handle the player death sequence. When the ship is hit (by a bomb or by the formation reaching the bottom row), the routine:

1. Decreases `li` by 1.
2. Replaces the ship UDG with the explosion UDG (CHR$ 149) in red at the ship position.
3. Plays a descending death sound — two low BEEPs.
4. PAUSEs for 25 frames (half a second) so the player can register what happened.
5. Clears the explosion.
6. If `li` is 0 or below, jumps to the game over screen.
7. Otherwise, resets the ship to centre position and redraws.

**Lines 700-745** display the game over screen. CLS clears everything, then the final score, wave reached and high score are shown. PAUSE 0 waits for any key press. Then the game resets: lives back to 3, score to 0, wave to 1, and execution jumps back to the beginning.

## The Death-Respawn Cycle

The pause after death is important. Without it, the player loses a life and immediately resumes — they might not even notice. The explosion animation, the sound, the half-second freeze, and the ship reappearing at centre all communicate: something bad happened, here is your second chance.

Recentring the ship after death gives the player a moment of safety. They know where they are. They can get their bearings before the next bullet flies.

## Game Over Flow

When lives reach zero, the game does not simply stop. It shows a results screen — score, wave reached, high score — and waits. This gives the player closure. They can see how they did, process the result, and choose to play again. The game loops back to the start rather than ending the program.

The high score persists across games within the same session. If the player beats their best score, `hi` updates. It resets only when the program is restarted with RUN.

## Try This

**Invincibility mode.** Comment out the line that decreases `li`. Play through without the risk of death. Does the game feel different? The threat of losing lives is what makes dodging enemies meaningful.

**Add a continue countdown.** After game over, instead of waiting for any key, count down from 5 and auto-restart. Use a FOR/NEXT loop with PAUSE 50 between each number. This mimics the arcade "INSERT COIN" countdown.
