The game starts with a board appearing. It should start with an explanation of who plays what.

<CodeFromFile src="sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-13-three-in-a-row/unit-10/titled.bas" />

Lines 10–70 add the title screen. Three lines of rules: "You are X. Computer is O." establishes the roles. "Get three in a row to win." states the goal. "Pick a position: 1 to 9." explains the controls.

The role assignment matters. In previous two-player games (Locksmith, Sonar), the player acted alone against hidden state. Here the player acts against an *opponent* — the computer has a turn, makes choices, and tries to win. The title screen frames this relationship clearly: you are X, the computer is O, one of you will win or neither will.
