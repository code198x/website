## The dumb opponent

The random AI has no strategy. It picks any empty square with equal probability. Play against it and you will win easily — it does not block your winning moves, does not take its own, and does not prefer any position over another.

That is the point. The AI starts dumb so you can see each layer of intelligence added in the next three units. Random → reactive → proactive → strategic. Each layer makes the opponent harder to beat, and the learner sees exactly which lines of code created the improvement.
