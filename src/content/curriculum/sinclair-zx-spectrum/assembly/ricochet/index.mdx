## What you'll build

Bat, ball and a wall of bricks. The ball moves smoothly with pre-shifted sprites — no jerky cell-stepping — and bounces with pixel-accurate collision and bat-angle deflection.

## Skills you'll master

- Pre-shifted sprites
- Smooth pixel motion
- Bounding-box collision
- Bat-and-ball physics
