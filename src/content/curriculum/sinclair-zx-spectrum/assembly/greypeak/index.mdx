## What you'll build

An isometric arcade-adventure in the Knight Lore tradition. Rooms drawn in 3/4 perspective with objects layered correctly back-to-front — the hardest rendering on the Spectrum, reached only once masking and tiles are owned.

## Skills you'll master

- Isometric projection
- Y-sort depth ordering
- Rooms and state
- Puzzle navigation
