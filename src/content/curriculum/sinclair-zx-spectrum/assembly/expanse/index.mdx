## What You'll Build

Epic scrolling platformer. Vast worlds to explore with multiple themed areas, bosses, and commercial-quality polish.

The single screen is gone. Now the world scrolls as you run, jump, and explore. This combines everything you learned in Cavern with the scrolling from Blitz — the foundation for the kind of platformer that defined the Spectrum's later years.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Scrolling platformer** — combined scrolling and physics
- **Camera systems** — dead zones and smooth tracking
- **Level compression** — fitting large worlds into memory
- **Level streaming** — loading as you go
- **Slope physics** — angled surfaces
- **Moving platforms** — dynamic level elements
- **Boss encounters** — per-world guardians
- **AY-3-8912 music** — 128K soundtrack
- **Save/password systems** — progress persistence
- **48K and 128K versions** — dual-target development
