## What you'll build

A keep on a dark hill, and a hooded thief who walks its halls by night. You explore it room by room — the screen flicking from hall to hall as you cross each threshold — lighting the dark, gathering what glints in it, and learning the shape of a place that grows as you go.

*Gloaming* was a single screen — and across *The Long Night* you made that screen *deep*: light and mood, a hunting dark that bore grudges, a five-watch night with a voice. Shadowkeep makes the leap the night couldn't from inside one square: from a screen to a **place**. A multi-room keep you explore, in the lineage of Ultimate's flick-screen adventures — *Atic Atac*, *Sabre Wulf*, *Knight Lore* — the 1984 bar for an ambitious bedroom-coded game on the 48K Spectrum.

## It builds on The Long Night

Shadowkeep doesn't start from a blank file. It picks up the engine you finished in *The Long Night* — the cell sprite and its save-and-restore, the wall collision, the frame-locked game loop, the title-to-play-to-end state machine — and grows it into a keep. The early units are deliberately an *extension* of what you have: the same machine, given a new face and a new place.

The genuinely new work is **scale** — a world of many rooms you flick between, a keep too big for one screen. Light, mood and sound return too, but you meet them fresh, as new instruments for skills you already own: where The Long Night lit its ground by *recolouring attributes* into glow pools, the keep lights by *dither density*, chosen by distance from a flame; where the night sang in musical *phrases* — the dusk bells, the dawn — the keep speaks in *place* — footsteps on stone, the groan of a door. Old confidence, new instruments, a room at a time.

That's the spiral: you don't relearn the foundations, you build a bigger thing on top of them.

## Where it sits

After *Gloaming* and *The Long Night* — one game you built, then deepened, across two modules. Shadowkeep is the next, and the first you don't finish in an afternoon: a place to keep coming back to. It's being built and published unit by unit; the first units are live now, with more arriving as the keep takes shape.
