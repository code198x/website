## What You'll Build

Run 'n' gun action. Contra-style platforming with multi-directional shooting, multiple weapons, epic bosses.

Run. Jump. Shoot in eight directions. Collect weapon power-ups. Fight through waves of enemies. Face massive bosses. This combines platformer and shooter mechanics into pure arcade action.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Platformer + shooter** — combined mechanics
- **8-way firing** — multi-directional shooting
- **Shoot while moving** — concurrent actions
- **Enemy gunfire** — enemies shoot back
- **Weapon power-ups** — spread, laser, homing
- **Boss patterns** — multi-phase encounters
- **Stage themes** — varied environments
- **Two-player co-op** — simultaneous play
- **Continues** — checkpoint respawning
