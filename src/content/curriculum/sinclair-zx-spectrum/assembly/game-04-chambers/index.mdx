## What You'll Build

Flip-screen top-down exploration. Navigate rooms, collect items, avoid enemies. Atic Atac territory.

Explore a mansion of interconnected rooms. Find keys to unlock doors. Avoid ghosts and ghouls. Reach the exit. This teaches room-based world design and the state management needed to remember what's where.

## Skills You'll Master

- **4-way movement** — top-down controls
- **Room data structures** — level storage
- **Screen transitions** — flip-screen movement
- **Persistent state** — remembering across rooms
- **Simple inventory** — keys and items
- **Enemy chase AI** — following behaviour
- **Locked doors** — key colours and matching

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 3: Cavern** first.

## Time Investment

128 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 128-192 hours total.
