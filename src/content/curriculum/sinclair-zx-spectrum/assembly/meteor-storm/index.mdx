Race through a scattered meteor storm. One hit ends the run. Stars tempt you away from a safe path; holding Space doubles your progress and their value. Cross one storm and the next begins: the voyage is five storms long, and each is faster, denser and hotter than the last. Finish each storm faster for a bigger bonus, multiplied by the storm's number, then try to beat your fastest storm and your furthest voyage. Every star, boost, arrival and impact has its own sound, a hit breaks the ship apart before the result, and meteors change colour as they fall towards you.

## Build it in small steps

We start with a pixel crossing a byte boundary and finish with a game you can load from tape. The target is a stock **48K PAL ZX Spectrum**, using original Z80 instructions. [Meet Assembly](/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/assembly/meet-assembly/) introduces the registers, memory, loops, calls, keyboard input and debugger we use here; immediate ideas are recalled as they become useful. BASIC is not a prerequisite.

Every lesson includes runnable source, an experiment and a way to inspect the real machine. Companions containing artwork and course data are editable and downloadable too. The browser starts code directly so you can spend time changing the program; the final lesson separately checks a fresh tape load. Tick **Sound** beside an experiment's buttons to hear the machine; it starts off, and once ticked it stays on for the other lessons. [Asm198x](https://asm198x.github.io/) is the recommended local assembler, with upstream Pasmo as an alternative.
