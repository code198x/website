## Choose an origin

The crossing of the axes is our origin: (0, 0). Coordinates are written (x, y), with the horizontal value first. A point at (120, 90) lies 120 units right of the origin and 90 units above it. These are world units; resizing the picture does not change the point’s position.

Make X negative while leaving Y at 90. The point crosses to the left, but stays at the same height. Now make Y negative too. The signs tell us which side of each axis contains the point.

## Position and movement

A position is where something is. A displacement is the change from one position to another. To get from (20, 30) to (120, 90), subtract start from finish: Δx = 120 − 20 = 100 and Δy = 90 − 30 = 60. The symbol Δ means “change in”.

<Question prompt="From (120, 90), move 30 units left and 50 units down. Where will the point end up?">

(90, 40): add −30 to X and −50 to Y. A negative change does not necessarily produce a negative position.
