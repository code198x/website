A ship can respond immediately, slide past its target or take time to gather speed.
Compare one rule at a time in **Controls and response**, or try the
[three-ship playground](/experiments/game-feel/index.html) first.

Each lesson explains its rule in plain language and pseudocode. You can also inspect
the JavaScript the browser runs; knowing JavaScript is not required to explore the
experiment. Choose a rule for the experience you want, then test what it changes.
