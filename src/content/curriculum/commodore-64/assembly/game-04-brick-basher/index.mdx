## What You'll Build

Breakout clone. Paddle, ball, bricks. Clear the screen.

Bounce the ball, smash the bricks, don't let it fall. Simple to explain, satisfying to play, and technically rich. Ball physics, paddle control, grid collision, power-ups — all the elements of a polished arcade game.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Ball physics** — velocity vectors, reflection angles
- **Paddle control** — responsive movement
- **Brick grid collision** — efficient detection
- **Power-up systems** — multi-ball, laser, catch
- **Increasing difficulty** — speed and complexity
- **Level design variety** — brick patterns

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 4: Platform Panic** first.

## Time Investment

64 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 64-96 hours total.
