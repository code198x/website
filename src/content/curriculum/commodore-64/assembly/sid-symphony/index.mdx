## What you'll build

A game with a real soundtrack — a music player that ticks on the raster interrupt, playing under the action instead of blocking it. This is the Hubbard paradigm: three voices of SID music and sound effects running while the game keeps going, the moment your games stop falling silent when something moves.

## Skills you'll master

- Interrupt-driven SID music
- A player on the raster IRQ
- A visualiser
- Input under a running player
