## What You'll Build

Single-screen platformer. Jump between platforms, avoid hazards, reach the exit.

Guide your character through screens filled with platforms, ladders, and dangers. Master the jump. Time your moves. Reach the exit. This is the foundation of every platformer — and you'll build it from scratch.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Gravity and jump physics** — realistic falling and jumping
- **Platform collision** — landing on platforms, bumping heads
- **Animation frames** — walk cycles and jump states
- **Hazard timing** — moving dangers
- **Level progression** — multiple screens
- **Ladders and moving platforms** — traversal variety
- **Enemy patterns** — obstacle AI

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 3: Maze Raider** first.

## Time Investment

64 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 64-96 hours total.
