## What You'll Build

Advanced side-scrolling shooter with multi-layer parallax. R-Type/Gradius style.

Three layers of scrolling. Four weapon types with upgrades. Option drones flying in formation. Charge shots that devastate. Seven complete stages with epic bosses. This is the C64 shooter pushed to its limits.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Multi-layer parallax** — 3+ scrolling layers
- **Advanced weapons** — 4 types with upgrade paths
- **Option drones** — formations and behaviours
- **Charge shots** — variable-power attacks
- **7 complete stages** — unique themes and enemies
- **Multi-phase bosses** — epic encounters
- **Level compression** — RLE for large levels
- **Checkpoint system** — progress saving
- **Two-player co-op** — simultaneous play
