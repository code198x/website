## What You'll Build

One-on-one fighting game. Four fighters, health bars, special moves, combos.

Street Fighter on the C64. Complex animation state machines track every frame. Hitboxes determine what connects. Input buffering catches your commands. Motion inputs unlock special moves. This is fighting game technology from first principles.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Animation state machines** — complex character states
- **Hitbox/hurtbox collision** — fighting game detection
- **Frame data** — startup, active, recovery frames
- **Input buffering** — catching player commands
- **Motion inputs** — quarter-circle, dragon punch
- **Special moves** — super meter mechanics
- **AI opponents** — difficulty scaling
- **Character variety** — 4 distinct archetypes
