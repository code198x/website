## What you'll build

A dungeon explorer across a world far larger than the screen. Scroll through connected catacombs, fight the things that live there, and manage the items you collect — all with an interrupt-driven soundtrack playing underneath. This is where smooth scrolling, sprite multiplexing and SID music first run together in a real game.

## Skills you'll master

- A large scrolling world
- Item management
- Tiles and enemies
- Music under play
