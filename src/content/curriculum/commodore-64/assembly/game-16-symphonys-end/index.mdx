## What You'll Build

Commercial-quality capstone. A complete, polished action-adventure combining everything learnt with demo-scene techniques.

The curriculum ends where it began — with the SID. But now you command every aspect of the machine. Demo-scene effects like FLD, DYCP, and AGSP. Advanced SID techniques. Direct 1541 drive programming. Custom fast loaders. Everything you've learned, synthesised into one masterpiece.

## Skills You'll Master

- **All previous techniques** — mastery level integration
- **Demo-scene effects** — FLD, DYCP, AGSP
- **Advanced SID** — filters, ring modulation, multi-speed
- **IRQ loader** — music during disk loads
- **Direct 1541 programming** — the drive's own 6502
- **Custom fast loader** — bypassing Kernal, GCR decoding
- **Turbo tape loader** — cassette distribution
- **Professional mastering** — disk and tape releases

## Prerequisites

Complete **Games 1-15** first. This is the capstone.

## Time Investment

256 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 256-384 hours total.
