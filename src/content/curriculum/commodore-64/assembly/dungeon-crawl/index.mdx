## What You'll Build

Top-down adventure. Explore rooms, collect keys, unlock doors, solve puzzles, defeat enemies.

A complete adventure game with multiple interconnected systems. Inventory management. NPC dialogue. Quest tracking. Combat. And for the first time — saving your game to disk. This is where games become worlds.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Multi-room maps** — connected world architecture
- **Room transitions** — seamless movement
- **Inventory system** — items and equipment
- **NPC dialogue** — conversation trees
- **Quest framework** — objectives and tracking
- **Combat system** — fighting mechanics
- **Environmental puzzles** — interactive world
- **Disk I/O** — Kernal routines for save/load
- **D64 creation** — disk image mastering

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 10: Raster Rider** first.
