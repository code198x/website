## What You'll Build

Side-scrolling platformer. A scrolling world built in AMOS — seeing both capabilities and limitations.

The world scrolls as you run. Enemies populate the landscape. Platforms challenge your timing. This is AMOS at its most ambitious — and where you'll discover why 68000 assembly exists. The performance ceiling becomes visible.

## Skills You'll Master

- **AMOS scrolling** — SCREEN OFFSET command
- **Large level storage** — efficient data
- **Camera following** — player tracking
- **Parallax attempts** — layered scrolling
- **Enemies in scrolling world** — moving targets
- **Performance limitations** — speed ceiling
- **Why assembly is needed** — motivation
- **Transition preparation** — ready for 68000
