## What you'll build

An arena defence game in the Chaos Engine mould: hold your ground as waves of enemies close in. This is the game that gives sound its own stage — SAM sampled audio on every event and an AMOS Music soundtrack underneath, designed as a system rather than a single beep.

## Skills you'll master

- SAM sampled audio
- AMOS Music as a system
- Wave survival
- Audio cues on events
