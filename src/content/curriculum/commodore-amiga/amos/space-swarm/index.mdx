## What You'll Build

Fixed-screen shooter. Player ship versus descending enemies. Classic arcade action.

Enemy waves descend from above in menacing formations. Your ship fires upward. Defeat each wave to face the next, harder challenge. This game teaches projectile management, multiple collision types, and wave-based difficulty scaling.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Player shooting** — projectile spawning
- **Projectile management** — sprite arrays
- **Enemy formations** — coordinated movement
- **Multiple collision types** — bullet-enemy, enemy-player
- **Wave progression** — level advancement
- **Difficulty scaling** — increasing challenge
- **High score persistence** — saved records
- **Sound effects variety** — audio feedback
