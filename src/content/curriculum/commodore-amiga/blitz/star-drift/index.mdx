## What you'll build

A horizontal shooter that reaches past the bitmap into the chips: hardware sprites laid over a Copper-split background, with tables driving the sprites and the colour list. Where Blitz stops drawing the picture and starts steering the hardware.

## Skills you'll master

- Hardware sprites (GetaSprite/ShowSprite)
- A Copper split
- Many objects at speed
- Sprite and Copper tables
