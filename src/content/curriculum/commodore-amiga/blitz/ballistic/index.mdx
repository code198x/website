## What you'll build

A turn-based artillery duel in the Scorched Earth tradition: lob shells across a landscape that deforms where they land. The destructible terrain *is* the lesson — the Blitter doing bulk pixel work as the core game mechanic, reached through Blitz's own Blit and Shapes verbs.

## Skills you'll master

- The Blitter as the mechanic
- Destructible terrain
- Projectile arcs and wind
- Shapes and collision
