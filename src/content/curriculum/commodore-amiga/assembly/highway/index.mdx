## What You'll Build

Pseudo-3D racing. The road stretches to the horizon, rendered line by line with Copper assistance. Lotus-style speed.

Feel the acceleration as the road rushes toward you. Corners challenge your reflexes. Hills obscure oncoming traffic. Other vehicles weave ahead. This game teaches perspective projection, Copper-based road rendering, and the sprite scaling simulation that creates the illusion of depth.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Perspective projection** — 3D mathematics
- **Copper road rendering** — scanline colours
- **Sprite scaling simulation** — size-based depth
- **Speed simulation** — acceleration feel
- **Traffic and obstacles** — hazard spawning
- **Road curves** — Copper-generated bends
- **Hill rendering** — vertical variation
- **Split-screen two-player** — competitive racing
- **Championship modes** — multi-race progression
