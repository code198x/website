## What You'll Build

Breakout clone with Copper gradients and sampled sound.

Paddle at the bottom. Ball bouncing. Bricks shattering above. Classic Breakout gameplay — but with Amiga polish. The Copper paints gradient skies. Paula plays satisfying thwacks and crashes. The Blitter clears bricks instantly.

## Why This Game?

After Signal's shooting action, Shatter Point focuses on **physics and precision**. The ball must bounce correctly, the paddle must feel responsive, and the effects must satisfy. You'll learn:

- Implement **ball physics** with fixed-point maths
- Use **Blitter fills** for brick graphics
- Create **Copper gradient backgrounds** that look stunning
- Play **Paula samples** for audio feedback
- Handle **brick destruction** with Blitter clear
- Build **power-up systems** that modify gameplay
- Achieve **smooth animation** with double buffering

By the end, you'll have a polished Breakout with Amiga flair.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Ball physics** — fixed-point maths for precision
- **Blitter fills** — fast brick graphics
- **Copper gradients** — beautiful backgrounds
- **Paula samples** — satisfying audio feedback
- **Brick destruction** — Blitter clear operations
- **Power-ups** — gameplay modifiers
- **Smooth animation** — double buffering techniques

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 2: Signal** first. You'll need the Blitter and collision skills from that game.
