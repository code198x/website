## What You'll Build

Single-screen platformer. Climb upward through hazards, reach the top. Platform collision, gravity, and jump physics with Blitter objects.

Navigate vertically through increasingly challenging platforms. Enemies patrol. Hazards threaten. Your destination is above. This is where platforming fundamentals meet Amiga hardware — cookie-cut BOBs, gravity physics, and collision detection against bitplane backgrounds.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Gravity and jump physics** — natural-feeling platforming
- **Platform collision** — bitplane detection
- **Player animation** — BOB sequences
- **Blitter cookie-cut masking** — proper sprite compositing
- **Enemy patterns** — patrol behaviours
- **Level data structures** — tile-based design
- **Hazard collision** — damage and death

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 3: Shatter Point** first.

## Time Investment

A full-length game, built unit by unit — a substantial undertaking, sized to the techniques it teaches rather than to a fixed unit count.
