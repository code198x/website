## What you'll build

A between-games interval you *build* rather than read: a small demo that drives the Copper directly. Step out of the game you just finished and watch the Amiga's display co-processor paint the screen one scanline at a time — WAIT for a line, MOVE a colour, and see the gradient appear. It pays off the Copper splits you used in Shatter Point by showing exactly how the DMA list works underneath.

## Skills you'll master

- The Copper as a co-processor
- Copper DMA and the display list
- WAIT and MOVE instructions
- Colour by scanline
