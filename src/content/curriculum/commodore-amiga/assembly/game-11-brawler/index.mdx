## What You'll Build

Side-scrolling beat 'em up. Walk right, punch enemies, clean up the streets. Y-axis depth sorting creates the illusion of walking into the scene.

Enemies approach from all directions on the playfield. Y-position determines drawing order — characters closer to the bottom appear in front. Combo systems reward skilled play. Weapons can be picked up. Bosses punctuate each stage. Bring a friend for two-player action.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Y-axis depth sorting** — proper draw order
- **Large sprite animation** — detailed characters
- **Melee combat mechanics** — punches, kicks, throws
- **Combo systems** — chained attacks
- **Multiple enemy coordination** — crowd management
- **Health and damage** — combat systems
- **Weapon pickups** — found weapons
- **Boss fights** — stage bosses
- **Two-player cooperative** — simultaneous play
