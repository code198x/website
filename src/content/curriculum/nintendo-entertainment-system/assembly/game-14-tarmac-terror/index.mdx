## What You'll Build

Pseudo-3D racing. Road rushing toward you, dodge traffic, survive the terror of the tarmac.

Feel the speed as the road stretches to the horizon. Other cars weave ahead. Curves challenge your reflexes. This is Out Run on the NES — achieved through clever sprite scaling and road rendering.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Pseudo-3D road rendering** — perspective illusion
- **Sprite scaling simulation** — size-based depth
- **Speed variation** — acceleration, braking
- **Obstacle spawning** — traffic management
- **Collision at speed** — crash handling
- **Road curves and hills** — terrain variation
- **Distance-based scoring** — progress tracking
- **Split-screen two-player** — competitive racing

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 13: Pavement Justice** first.
