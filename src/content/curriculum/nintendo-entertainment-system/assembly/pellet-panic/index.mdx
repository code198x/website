## What You'll Build

Pac-Man style maze game. Collect pellets, avoid ghosts, power pills turn the tables. Frantic energy.

Navigate corridors. Eat every pellet. Avoid the ghosts — each with their own personality. Eat a power pill and hunt them down. Understanding ghost AI is the key to mastering maze games.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Tile-based mazes** — constrained movement
- **Corridor navigation** — direction changing
- **Ghost AI personalities** — distinct behaviours
- **Power-ups** — state changes
- **Wrap-around edges** — tunnel mechanics
- **Difficulty progression** — speed increases
- **Scatter/chase modes** — AI states

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 3: Fracture Point** first.

## Time Investment

A full-length game, built unit by unit — a substantial undertaking, sized to the techniques it teaches rather than to a fixed unit count.
