## What You'll Build

Breakout clone. Paddle, ball, bricks. The tension builds to the breaking point.

Bounce the ball. Break the bricks. Don't let it fall. This classic teaches ball physics and — crucially — how to modify background tiles during gameplay when bricks shatter.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Ball physics** — velocity and angles
- **Paddle control** — responsive movement
- **Reflection mechanics** — bounce calculations
- **Brick destruction** — updating background tiles
- **APU sound design** — satisfying audio
- **Score and lives** — game state tracking
- **Power-up bricks** — special effects

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 2: Crate Escape** first.

## Time Investment

A full-length game, built unit by unit — a substantial undertaking, sized to the techniques it teaches rather than to a fixed unit count.
