## What You'll Build

Side-scrolling platformer. The NES's signature genre — Crate Escape's mechanics in a larger, scrolling world.

This is Super Mario Bros. territory. The world scrolls smoothly thanks to NES hardware. The camera follows you through expansive levels. This is where NES development truly shines.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Hardware scrolling** — NES scroll registers
- **Nametable management** — seaming tables together
- **Camera following** — tracking the player
- **Large level data** — extended world storage
- **Scrolling collision** — detection in moving view
- **Enemies in scrolling world** — relative positioning

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 7: Gridlock** first.
