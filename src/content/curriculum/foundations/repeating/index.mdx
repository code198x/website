## What this is

A **loop** does a piece of work over and over, and you write the work once.

We use three loop forms in this module. By the end
of the second one you will have turned the guessing game from **Decisions** into something
you can play.

## Who it's for

You will use stored values and yes/no comparisons. We recall the question as we
use it. [Decisions](/foundations/decisions/) offers more practice; prior attendance
is not required. BASIC and assembly remain independent entry points.

## The shape

- **Doing it again** — the counted loop, and a counter you can use as it goes.
- **Until you get it** — test after the work, use `RANDOM`, and follow a small
  game-loop model.
- **Checking before you start** — the loop that asks first, so the work can happen no times
  at all.
