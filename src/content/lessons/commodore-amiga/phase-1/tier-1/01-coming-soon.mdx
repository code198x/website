# Amiga Assembly Programming Coming Soon!

We're developing comprehensive 68000 assembly programming lessons for the Commodore Amiga. Soon you'll harness the power of the most advanced home computer of the 1980s!

## What's Coming

- **68000 Assembly** - Master the powerful Motorola 68000 processor
- **Custom Chipset** - Direct programming of Agnus, Denise, and Paula
- **Copper Programming** - Create stunning visual effects with the coprocessor
- **Blitter Mastery** - Fast graphics operations and effects
- **Audio Programming** - Four-channel PCM audio with Paula
- **Multitasking** - Work with AmigaOS and system-friendly code

## Amiga Architecture We'll Cover

- Chip RAM vs Fast RAM
- Planar graphics organization
- Hardware sprites and dual playfield
- Copper wait and move instructions
- DMA channels and priorities
- Interrupt handling

## Legendary Techniques You'll Learn

- Copper bars and rainbow effects
- Unlimited bobs using the Blitter
- Module playback (ProTracker format)
- Smooth scrolling with hardware registers
- HAM mode for photorealistic images
- Vector balls and 3D transformations

## Why Amiga Assembly Matters

The Amiga's custom chips were years ahead of their time. Games like Shadow of the Beast, demos by The Deathstar, and applications like Deluxe Paint showed what was possible when programmers truly understood the hardware.

## Development Timeline

The Amiga deserves comprehensive coverage of its unique architecture. We're building lessons that capture the magic of this revolutionary machine.
