## Compute daily prices

<TryItNow>
```basic
10 REM SPACE TRADER - DAILY PRICES
20 DIM TODAY(5)
30 PACK = RISK*16 + ECON : REM from previous lesson
40 ECON = PACK AND 15 : RISK = INT(PACK/16)
50 SEED = INT(TI/10)
60 RND(-SEED) : REM deterministic shuffle per day
70 FOR I=0 TO 5
80 BASE = PRICE(I) * ECON / 10
90 DRIFT = INT((RND(1)-.5)*RISK*2)
100 TODAY(I) = BASE + DRIFT
110 IF TODAY(I) < 1 THEN TODAY(I) = 1
120 NEXT
130 PRINT "TODAY'S MARKET"
140 FOR I=0 TO 5
150 PRINT GOODS$(I);TAB(14);TODAY(I);" CR"
160 NEXT
```
</TryItNow>

Seeding `RND` with a negative value ensures each in-game day produces the same prices until midnight (or whenever you advance the clock). Risk scales the volatility around the economic baseline.

<InfoBox>
Store `TODAY()` for selling calculations and for inclusion in save files. You’ll also use it for economic simulations in the next lesson.
</InfoBox>
