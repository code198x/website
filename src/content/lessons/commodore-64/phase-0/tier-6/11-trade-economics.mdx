# Trade Economics

**Let every deal ripple through the local economy.**

<PrerequisiteBox system="commodore-64" phase={0} lessonNumber={91} />

## Update supply and metrics

<TryItNow>
```basic
10 REM SPACE TRADER - ECONOMY SHIFT
20 GOOD = 2 : REM food
30 UNITS = 3
40 IF TODAY(GOOD) * UNITS > CREDITS THEN PRINT "NOT ENOUGH CREDITS" : END
50 CREDITS = CREDITS - TODAY(GOOD) * UNITS
60 CARGO(GOOD) = CARGO(GOOD) - UNITS
70 IF CARGO(GOOD) < 0 THEN CARGO(GOOD) = 0
80 ECON = ECON + INT(UNITS/2)
90 IF ECON > 15 THEN ECON = 15
100 RISK = RISK + SGN(UNITS - 2)
110 IF RISK > 7 THEN RISK = 7
120 PRINT "NEW ECON:";ECON/10;" RISK:";RISK
130 PRINT "STOCK LEFT:";CARGO(GOOD)
```
</TryItNow>

As the player buys goods, supply drops. Margins tighten (`ECON` increases), while large purchases raise risk. Selling could reverse the adjustments—use negative `UNITS` to replenish stock and lower prices.

<InfoBox>
Pack the updated `ECON`/`RISK` back into a byte (Lesson 89) before saving the game so changes persist across planets.
</InfoBox>
