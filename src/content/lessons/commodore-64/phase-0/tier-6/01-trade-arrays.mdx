# Trade Arrays

**Build the backbone of your trading ledger with tidy arrays.**

<PrerequisiteBox system="commodore-64" phase={0} lessonNumber={81} />

## Shape the market table

<TryItNow>
```basic
10 REM SPACE TRADER - MARKET LIST
20 DIM GOODS$(5),PRICE(5),CARGO(5)
30 DATA "LYTHIUM",80,2,"WATER",12,1,"FOOD",18,1
40 DATA "TECH",160,3,"MEDS",55,1,"SPICE",110,2
50 FOR I=0 TO 5
60 READ GOODS$(I),PRICE(I),CARGO(I)
70 NEXT
80 PRINT "GOOD        CR  UNIT"
90 PRINT "----------------------"
100 FOR I=0 TO 5
110 PRINT GOODS$(I);TAB(12);PRICE(I);TAB(17);CARGO(I)
120 NEXT
```
</TryItNow>

The DATA block feeds three arrays in parallel: the commodity name, base credit price, and cargo space required per unit. Printing them together creates the first market interface for Space Trader.

<InfoBox>
The C64’s `TAB()` function helps align columns quickly. Later we’ll swap this for custom formatting to match the in-game HUD.
</InfoBox>
