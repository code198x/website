## Optimize the render pass

<TryItNow>
```basic
10 REM BASIC LOOP OPTIMIZATION
20 DIM TABLE(39)
30 FOR I=0 TO 39: TABLE(I)=INT(SIN(I*.157)*64+64) : NEXT
40 TI$="000000"
50 FOR ROOM=0 TO 99
60 IDX = ROOM AND 15
70 COLOR = TABLE(IDX)
80 POKE 55296+ROOM,COLOR
90 POKE 1024+ROOM,81
100 NEXT
110 PRINT "TABLE LOOKUP:";VAL(TI$)
120 TI$="000000"
130 FOR ROOM=0 TO 99
140 POKE 55296+ROOM,INT(SIN((ROOM AND 15)*.157)*64+64)
150 POKE 1024+ROOM,81
160 NEXT
170 PRINT "INLINE MATH:";VAL(TI$)
```
</TryItNow>

The first loop uses a precomputed `TABLE()` array of sine values; the second recalculates sine each iteration. Timing shows the precomputed approach wins. You can squeeze even more speed by unrolling short loops, replacing `FOR/NEXT` with sequential statements when the body is tiny.

<InfoBox>
SID-style vibrato, particle effects, and combat AI all benefit from lookup tables. Build them once during initialization, then reuse them every frame to keep the game responsive.
</InfoBox>
