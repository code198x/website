# First Note

**Flip the SID switch and hear your Commodore sing.**

<PrerequisiteBox system="commodore-64" phase={0} lessonNumber={65} />

## Gate a single tone

<TryItNow>
```basic
10 REM SID WARM-UP
20 POKE 54296,15           : REM full volume
30 POKE 54277,6            : REM attack=0, decay=6
40 POKE 54278,9            : REM sustain=9, release=9
50 POKE 54273,34           : REM freq low  (C4 ≈ 1000 decimal)
60 POKE 54272,3            : REM freq high
70 POKE 54272,17           : REM triangle + gate on
80 FOR T=1 TO 2000 : NEXT
90 POKE 54272,16           : REM gate off
100 POKE 54272,0           : REM silence
```
</TryItNow>

Lines 20-40 set global volume and envelope. Lines 50-70 load the frequency/ control registers and open the gate. A short delay keeps the note audible before closing the gate again.

<InfoBox>
SID addresses: voice 1 starts at `$D400` (decimal 54272). Frequency uses two bytes: low first, high second. Control bit 0 gates the sound when set.
</InfoBox>
