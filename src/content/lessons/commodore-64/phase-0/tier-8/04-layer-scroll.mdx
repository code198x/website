# Layer Scroll

**Make the stars glide by while the action stays razor sharp.**

<PrerequisiteBox system="commodore-64" phase={0} lessonNumber={116} />

## Scroll background + HUD layers

<TryItNow>
```basic
10 REM PARALLAX DEMO
20 BITMAP=12288 : SCREEN=8192
30 BACKSPEED=1 : FORESPD=2
40 DO
50 CALLSHIFT(BITMAP,BACKSPEED)
60 FOREX=(FOREX+FORESPD) AND 7
70 POKE 53270,(PEEK(53270) AND 248) OR FOREX
80 LOOP

100 REM SHIFT BITMAP BY DELTA PIXELS (8-pixel chunks)
110 SUB CALLSHIFT(ADDR,DELTA)
120 BYTES=40*25
130 SHIFT=SGN(DELTA)
140 STEPS=ABS(DELTA)
150 FOR S=1 TO STEPS
160 IF SHIFT>0 THEN FOR I=0 TO BYTES-1: POKE ADDR+I,PEEK(ADDR+I+1): NEXT
170 IF SHIFT<0 THEN FOR I=BYTES-1 TO 1 STEP -1: POKE ADDR+I,PEEK(ADDR+I-1): NEXT
180 NEXT
190 END SUB
```
</TryItNow>

The background bitmap shifts one byte at a time, creating slow star drift. The foreground character layer uses the fine-scroll register (`$D016`) to move independently. Adjust speeds dynamically when the player boosts or when the boss slows time.

<InfoBox>
For smoother parallax, pair this coarse shift with an 8-step fine scroll for the bitmap as well: once you shift eight fine steps, rotate the bitmap bytes and reset the fine scroll.
</InfoBox>

Be sure to reset scroll registers when pausing or entering menus so text remains readable.
