## Quick juice toolkit

<TryItNow>
```basic
10 REM JUICE EFFECTS
20 SUB HITFLASH
30 FOR I=1 TO 4
40 POKE 53280,2 : POKE 53281,1
50 FOR D=1 TO 50:NEXT
60 POKE 53280,0 : POKE 53281,0
70 FOR D=1 TO 50:NEXT
80 NEXT
90 END SUB

100 SUB SCREENSHAKE
110 FOR SH=4 TO 0 STEP -1
120 POKE 53265,(PEEK(53265) AND 248) OR SH
130 FOR D=1 TO 80:NEXT
140 NEXT
150 POKE 53265,PEEK(53265) AND 248
160 END SUB

200 SUB SIDBURST
210 POKE 54273,120 : POKE 54272,0
220 POKE 54277,0*16+2 : POKE 54278,8*16+3
230 POKE 54272,65
240 FOR T=1 TO 30: POKE 54273,120+T*2: NEXT
250 POKE 54272,64
260 END SUB
```
</TryItNow>

Call `HITFLASH` on enemy explosion, `SCREENSHAKE` when the boss slams the colony, and `SIDBURST` when overcharge activates. Chain multiple effects for bigger moments.

<InfoBox>
Throttle juice: use small random offsets so screenshake doesn’t induce motion sickness, and reset palettes after flashes so HUD readability remains high.
</InfoBox>

Catalog each effect in `docs/JUICE.md` with triggers so future contributors maintain consistent style.
