## Plan the wave ramp

<TryItNow>
```basic
10 REM DIFFICULTY TABLE
20 DATA 1,6,1,12, 2,7,2,15, 3,8,3,18, 4,9,4,22, 5,10,5,26
30 FOR W=1 TO 5
40 READ WAVE,COUNT,SPEED,HP
50 ENEMYCOUNT(W)=COUNT
60 ENEMYSPEED(W)=SPEED
70 ENEMYHP(W)=HP
80 NEXT
90 PRINT "WAVE";" COUNT";" SPEED";" HP"
100 FOR W=1 TO 5
110 PRINT W;TAB(5);ENEMYCOUNT(W);TAB(12);ENEMYSPEED(W);TAB(20);ENEMYHP(W)
120 NEXT
```
</TryItNow>

Use tables to make wave adjustments easy. Combine with live telemetry from Tier 8 playtests: if players fail wave 4 repeatedly, reduce speed or add extra shield pickups after respawn.

<InfoBox>
Implement a mercy system: after two consecutive failures, lower enemy speed by 1 or spawn a high-energy pickup early in the wave. Reset to standard difficulty once the player clears the wave.
</InfoBox>

Document final tuning decisions in `docs/balance-notes.md` for future iteration.
