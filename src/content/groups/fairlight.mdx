## The Legend Lives On

Fairlight (FLT) is one of the most storied groups in demoscene history. Founded in Sweden in 1987, they have maintained an active presence for over three decades, releasing groundbreaking demos and maintaining high standards in both the cracking and demo scenes.

Fairlight emerged from the ashes of West Coast Crackers and Judges, bringing together Sweden's best crackers and coders. Their name comes from the Fairlight CMI synthesizer, reflecting their commitment to both technical excellence and artistic expression.
