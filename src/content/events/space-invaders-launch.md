Space Invaders, created by Tomohiro Nishikado and released by Taito in June 1978, became the first blockbuster arcade game and launched the golden age of arcade gaming.

## Revolutionary Impact

- **Financial Success**: Caused a coin shortage in Japan due to its popularity
- **Cultural Phenomenon**: First game to become a household name
- **Design Innovation**: Introduced concepts like high scores, lives, and increasing difficulty
- **Industry Growth**: Inspired countless companies to enter the video game business

## Influence on Home Computing

Space Invaders' massive success directly influenced the home computer revolution:

- Proved that games could drive hardware sales
- Inspired ports to every major home computer platform
- Established gaming as a primary use case for home computers
- Demonstrated the commercial potential of software

The game's simple but addictive gameplay became a template for countless home computer games throughout the 1980s.
