On 26th April 1986, reactor 4 at the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant in Ukraine exploded during a safety test, releasing massive amounts of radioactive material into the atmosphere.

## Technology Connections

While not directly related to personal computing, Chernobyl had significant implications for computer technology:

- **Safety Systems**: Highlighted the need for reliable computer-controlled safety systems
- **Fault Tolerance**: Accelerated research into fault-tolerant computing
- **Simulation**: Increased demand for computer simulations of complex systems
- **Information Flow**: Demonstrated how lack of information (and computers to process it) could worsen disasters

## Cultural Impact

The disaster occurred during the height of the home computer revolution:

- Many computer games addressed nuclear war and disaster themes
- Public interest in technology's risks as well as benefits increased
- Emphasis on the importance of proper system design and testing
- Influenced a generation's view of technology's potential dangers

The Chernobyl disaster served as a stark reminder that as society became more dependent on technology, the consequences of technological failures became more severe.
