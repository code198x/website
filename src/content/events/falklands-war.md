On 2nd April 1982, Argentina invaded the Falkland Islands, beginning a 74-day conflict with the United Kingdom that would test both nations' military capabilities and resolve.

## Technology and War

The Falklands conflict was notable for its use of modern technology:

- Satellite communications for command and control
- Computer-assisted weapons systems
- Electronic warfare capabilities
- Advanced logistics management

## Home Front Innovation

Remarkably, the British home computer industry continued to flourish during the conflict:

- The ZX Spectrum launched on 23rd April 1982, during the war
- British software development continued unabated
- The conflict didn't dampen enthusiasm for home computing
- Technology was increasingly seen as key to national competitiveness

The war's end in June 1982 coincided with the beginning of the ZX Spectrum's phenomenal success, symbolising Britain's transition from military to technological prominence.
