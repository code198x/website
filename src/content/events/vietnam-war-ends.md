On 30th April 1975, the Fall of Saigon marked the end of the Vietnam War, fundamentally reshaping American society and global politics.

## Technology Context

The end of the Vietnam War coincided with crucial developments in computing:
- Military technology and expertise began transitioning to civilian applications
- Defence contractors shifted focus to commercial electronics
- Veterans with technical training entered the civilian workforce
- Government funding increasingly supported civilian technology research

## Cultural Shift

The post-Vietnam era saw a cultural transformation that embraced personal empowerment and individual creativity—values that would be embodied in the personal computer revolution just beginning with the Altair 8800 and the formation of the Homebrew Computer Club in 1975.
