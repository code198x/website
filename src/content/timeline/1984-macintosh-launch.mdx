---

The Macintosh launched on January 24, 1984, two days after Ridley Scott's "1984" commercial aired during the Super Bowl. The ad positioned Apple as a liberator against IBM's corporate dominance.

The Mac brought the graphical user interface—developed at Xerox PARC—to a mass market. Its mouse-driven, icon-based system seemed revolutionary compared to command-line interfaces.

While expensive and initially limited, the Macintosh demonstrated a future where computers would be accessible to everyone, not just programmers and enthusiasts.
