Princess Diana died in a car crash in Paris on August 31, 1997. The news spread instantly via television, radio, and the still-young internet, triggering an outpouring of grief.

An estimated 2.5 billion people watched her funeral—the largest television audience for any event in history at that time. The intensity of public mourning surprised many observers.

The event demonstrated how global media could create shared emotional experiences across cultures and borders.
