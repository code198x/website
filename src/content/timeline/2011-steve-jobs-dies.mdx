summary: "Apple's co-founder and visionary leader dies of pancreatic cancer at 56, ending an era of personal computing."
---

Jobs had co-founded Apple, been forced out, returned to save it from bankruptcy, and transformed it into the world's most valuable company. The iPhone, iPad, and iTunes store reshaped multiple industries.

His death marked a generational shift. The pioneers who built the personal computer industry—Jobs, Gates's generation—were passing the torch to a new era of founders.
