title: "Minecraft begins its blocky conquest"
year: 2009
month: 5
category: "technology"
summary: "Markus 'Notch' Persson releases an early version of Minecraft, a game that will become one of the best-selling of all time."
---

Minecraft proved that graphics weren't everything. Its blocky aesthetic enabled infinite creativity—players built everything from simple houses to working computers within the game.

The game's success demonstrated that indie developers could compete with major studios. Microsoft eventually bought Mojang for $2.5 billion, validating the indie model while raising questions about corporate acquisition of creativity.
