---
title: "Nintendo Switch blurs home and portable gaming"
year: 2017
month: 3
category: "technology"
summary: "Nintendo's hybrid console succeeds where Wii U failed, proving there's still a market for dedicated gaming hardware."
---

After the Wii U's failure, some questioned whether Nintendo could survive in hardware. The Switch answered emphatically—a home console you could take anywhere resonated with players.

Breath of the Wild launched alongside it, reinventing Zelda for a new generation. The Switch proved that innovative hardware combined with strong software could still compete against smartphones and streaming.
