title: "September 11 attacks reshape the world"
year: 2001
month: 9
day: 11
category: "politics"
summary: "Terrorist attacks on the United States trigger wars, surveillance expansion, and a fundamental shift in global politics and technology."
---

The attacks killed nearly 3,000 people and launched two decades of conflict in Afghanistan and Iraq. The response transformed technology: mass surveillance, biometrics, and data collection became normalised.

The tech industry would build the tools of the surveillance state while simultaneously creating platforms for global communication and organisation that governments struggled to control.
