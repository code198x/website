year: 1939

category: "politics"
summary: "Germany invades Poland, triggering a global conflict that will accelerate computing research through military necessity."
---

The war drove massive investment in computing. Codebreaking demanded machines like Colossus and the Bombe. Ballistics calculations led to ENIAC. Radar and communications required electronic innovation.

Many pioneers of computing—Turing, von Neumann, Zuse—did their crucial work during or immediately after the war. Military funding created the research infrastructure that would birth the computer age.
