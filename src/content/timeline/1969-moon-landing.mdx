On July 20, 1969, Apollo 11's lunar module Eagle landed on the Moon's Sea of Tranquility. Neil Armstrong's first steps on the lunar surface, watched live on television by hundreds of millions, represented humanity's greatest technological achievement.

The mission fulfilled President Kennedy's 1961 challenge and demonstrated what concentrated engineering effort could accomplish. The Apollo Guidance Computer, with just 74KB of memory, helped inspire a generation of programmers.

The Moon landing occurred just eight years after Gagarin's first spaceflight—a pace of progress that seemed to promise limitless technological advancement.
