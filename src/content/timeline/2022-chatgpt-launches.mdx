ChatGPT reached 100 million users faster than any application in history. Suddenly, AI could write, code, and converse—capabilities that seemed years away arrived overnight.

The implications for game development were immediate: AI-assisted coding, procedural content generation, dynamic NPCs. The tools that built the retro era were being reimagined for an AI-augmented future.
