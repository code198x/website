---
title: "Colossus begins codebreaking at Bletchley Park"
year: 1943
month: 12
category: "technology"
summary: "The world's first programmable electronic computer enters service breaking Nazi codes, though its existence remains secret for decades."
---

Built by Tommy Flowers, Colossus used 1,500 vacuum tubes to break the German Lorenz cipher. It was programmable—operators could change its function by rewiring plugboards and setting switches.

Colossus remained classified until the 1970s, so ENIAC received credit as the first electronic computer. But Colossus came first, and its success proved that electronic computation could solve problems impossible for humans alone.
