---
title: "Berlin Wall falls"
year: 1989
month: 11
day: 9
category: "politics"
summary: "East Germany opens the Berlin Wall, symbolically ending the Cold War division of Europe and beginning German reunification."
---

On November 9, 1989, the East German government announced citizens could cross the border freely. Thousands gathered at checkpoints, and jubilant crowds began dismantling the wall that had divided Berlin since 1961.

The fall of the Berlin Wall symbolised the collapse of Soviet communism in Eastern Europe. Within two years, the Soviet Union itself would dissolve.

The event opened new markets for Western technology companies and began the integration of Eastern European programmers into the global software industry.
