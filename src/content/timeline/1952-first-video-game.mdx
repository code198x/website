---
title: "OXO: the first graphical video game"
year: 1952
category: "technology"
summary: "Alexander Douglas creates OXO (noughts and crosses) for his Cambridge PhD thesis, the earliest known game with a visual display."
---

OXO ran on the EDSAC computer at Cambridge, using a cathode ray tube to display the game board. Players used a rotary telephone controller to make moves against the computer.

Though primitive, OXO demonstrated that computers could be interactive and visual. It was a game—perhaps the first video game—created to explore human-computer interaction.
