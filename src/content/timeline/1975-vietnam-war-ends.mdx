title: "Fall of Saigon ends Vietnam War"
year: 1975
month: 4
day: 30
category: "politics"
summary: "North Vietnamese forces capture Saigon, ending two decades of American involvement in Vietnam and reshaping US foreign policy."
---

The fall of Saigon on April 30, 1975, marked the end of the Vietnam War. Iconic images of helicopters evacuating the US embassy symbolised America's first major military defeat.

The war had divided American society and consumed enormous resources. Its end allowed redirection of attention and funding toward other priorities, including technology research.

The conflict also drove computing advances: ARPANET, the precursor to the internet, was developed partly to create resilient military communications.
