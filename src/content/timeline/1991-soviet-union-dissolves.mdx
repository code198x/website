title: "Soviet Union dissolves"
year: 1991
month: 12
day: 26
category: "politics"
summary: "The Soviet Union officially dissolves after 69 years, ending the Cold War and reshaping global politics and economics."
---

On December 26, 1991, the Soviet Union officially ceased to exist. The hammer and sickle flag was lowered over the Kremlin for the last time, replaced by the Russian tricolour.

The dissolution ended the Cold War that had shaped global politics since 1945. It also opened vast new markets and labour pools to Western technology companies.

Many talented Soviet programmers and engineers, previously isolated behind the Iron Curtain, would soon contribute to the global technology industry.
