---
title: "Kickstarter transforms game funding"
year: 2012
category: "technology"
summary: "Double Fine Adventure raises $3.3 million on Kickstarter, proving crowdfunding can revive genres abandoned by publishers."
---

Tim Schafer's adventure game campaign showed that audiences would fund games directly. Suddenly, niche genres and retro revivals became viable again.

Projects like Wasteland 2, Pillars of Eternity, and Shovel Knight followed. Crowdfunding gave developers independence from publishers—and gave players a voice in what got made.
