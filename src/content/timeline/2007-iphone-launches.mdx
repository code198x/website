The iPhone wasn't the first smartphone, but it was the first that ordinary people wanted to use. Its multitouch interface, app ecosystem, and seamless design made pocket computers mainstream.

Within a decade, smartphones became essential infrastructure. Mobile gaming exploded, creating a market larger than consoles and PCs combined. The always-connected world had arrived.
