---
title: "OPEC oil embargo triggers energy crisis"
year: 1973
month: 10
category: "economics"
summary: "Arab oil producers embargo Western nations, quadrupling oil prices and triggering a global recession that reshapes the world economy."
---

In October 1973, OPEC's Arab members announced an oil embargo against nations supporting Israel in the Yom Kippur War. Oil prices quadrupled almost overnight, from $3 to $12 per barrel.

The resulting economic shock ended the post-war boom. Long queues at petrol stations became a defining image of the decade. Inflation and unemployment rose together, creating "stagflation."

The crisis accelerated interest in electronic efficiency and miniaturisation. It also demonstrated how interconnected the global economy had become—and how vulnerable.
