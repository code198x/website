---
title: "IBM PC launched"
year: 1981
month: 8
day: 12
category: "technology"
summary: "IBM's entry into personal computing legitimises the market and establishes the architecture that dominates business computing for decades."
---

IBM announced its Personal Computer on August 12, 1981, entering a market previously dominated by hobbyist machines. The IBM PC's open architecture and business credibility transformed the industry.

Using off-the-shelf components and licensing its operating system from Microsoft, IBM created a platform others could clone. This decision would ultimately commoditise PC hardware and make Microsoft dominant.

While home computers like the C64 and Spectrum dominated gaming and hobbyist markets, the IBM PC architecture would eventually absorb them all.
