Space Shuttle Challenger disintegrated on January 28, 1986, just 73 seconds after launch. All seven crew members died, including teacher Christa McAuliffe, who was to be the first civilian in space.

The disaster was broadcast live to millions of schoolchildren who had tuned in to watch McAuliffe's historic mission. The investigation revealed NASA had ignored engineer warnings about launching in cold weather.

Challenger reminded the world that space travel remained dangerous and that institutional pressures could override safety concerns.
