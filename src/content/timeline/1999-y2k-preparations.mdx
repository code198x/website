summary: "The world prepares for potential computer chaos as the year 2000 approaches, highlighting society's dependence on software."
---

As 1999 drew to a close, the "Y2K bug" dominated headlines. Would computers that stored years as two digits crash when "99" became "00"? Billions were spent on remediation.

The Y2K scare highlighted how deeply computers had penetrated every aspect of modern life. Banking, utilities, transportation—all depended on software that might fail.

Midnight came and went with minimal disruption, partly due to intensive preparation. But Y2K had demonstrated that society now ran on code.
