---
title: "World War II ends"
year: 1945
month: 9
category: "politics"
summary: "The deadliest conflict in human history concludes, leaving behind technological advances that will reshape civilian life."
---

The war's end released scientists and engineers back into civilian research. Technologies developed for military purposes—radar, computing, jet propulsion—would be adapted for peacetime.

The post-war economic boom, particularly in America, created conditions for technological optimism. The space race, television, and eventually personal computing all grew from seeds planted during the war.
