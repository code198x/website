---
title: "Intel releases the 4004 microprocessor"
year: 1971
month: 11
day: 15
category: "technology"
summary: "The first commercially available microprocessor launches, putting a complete CPU on a single chip and making personal computing possible."
---

Intel's 4004 was the first general-purpose programmable processor available as a single integrated circuit. Designed by Federico Faggin, Ted Hoff, and Stanley Mazor, it packed 2,300 transistors into a chip smaller than a fingernail.

Originally created for a Japanese calculator company, the 4004's general-purpose design opened possibilities far beyond arithmetic. It could be programmed for any computational task.

The microprocessor revolution had begun. Within a decade, far more powerful chips like the 6502 and Z80 would power the home computer revolution.
