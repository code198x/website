title: "Pokémon GO sends millions into the streets"
year: 2016

category: "culture"
summary: "Niantic's augmented reality game becomes a global phenomenon, briefly making mobile gaming a shared outdoor experience."
---

Pokémon GO merged smartphone gaming with physical movement. Parks filled with players hunting virtual creatures. For a summer, gaming was social, active, and inescapable.

The craze faded, but the game proved augmented reality could work at scale. It also demonstrated nostalgia's power—a 1990s franchise became 2016's biggest cultural moment.
