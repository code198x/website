The crisis destroyed wealth, ended careers, and shook faith in institutions. In its aftermath, Bitcoin emerged—a technological response to distrust of banks and governments.

The gaming industry proved surprisingly resilient. People needed escapism, and games were cheaper than most entertainment. Mobile gaming's rise accelerated as smartphones spread.
