title: "Tiananmen Square protests suppressed"
year: 1989
month: 6
day: 4
category: "politics"
summary: "Chinese military forces suppress pro-democracy protests in Beijing, with the iconic 'Tank Man' image broadcast worldwide."
---

On June 4, 1989, Chinese military forces moved to clear Tiananmen Square of pro-democracy protesters who had occupied it for weeks. The crackdown resulted in hundreds, possibly thousands, of deaths.

The image of a lone man standing before a column of tanks became one of the most iconic photographs of the century, broadcast globally despite Chinese censorship efforts.

The event highlighted both the power and limits of global media. Information could spread rapidly, but authoritarian governments could still suppress dissent through force.
