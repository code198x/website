Star Wars opened on May 25, 1977, and changed entertainment forever. Its unprecedented success proved audiences hungered for escapist fantasy after a decade of gritty realism.

The film's groundbreaking special effects, created by Industrial Light & Magic, demonstrated what technology could achieve in service of storytelling. Its influence on video games was immediate and lasting.

From arcade cabinets to home computers, Star Wars-inspired games became a staple of 1980s gaming. The film's universe of space battles, lightsabers, and the Force provided endless inspiration.
