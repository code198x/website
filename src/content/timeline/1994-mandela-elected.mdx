On May 10, 1994, Nelson Mandela was inaugurated as President of South Africa. After 27 years of imprisonment and a lifetime fighting apartheid, he led his country's transition to democracy.

The election was broadcast worldwide, with millions watching Mandela take the oath of office. His message of reconciliation over revenge offered hope for peaceful resolution of seemingly intractable conflicts.

South Africa's transformation demonstrated that change was possible even in deeply divided societies.
