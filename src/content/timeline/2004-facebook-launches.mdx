title: "Facebook launches from a Harvard dorm room"
year: 2004
month: 2
category: "technology"
summary: "Mark Zuckerberg's social network begins its rise to become the dominant platform connecting billions of people worldwide."
---

What started as a college directory became the world's largest social network. Facebook pioneered the attention economy, turning user data into advertising revenue and reshaping how people communicate.

The platform's impact extended far beyond socialising—it influenced elections, spread misinformation, and raised fundamental questions about privacy, monopoly, and the power of technology companies.
