John Lennon was shot and killed outside the Dakota apartment building in New York City on December 8, 1980. The news spread rapidly via television and radio, creating one of the first global real-time mourning events.

Lennon had been preparing a musical comeback after five years of retirement. His death at 40 seemed to close the door on the idealism of the 1960s.

The event demonstrated media's power to create shared experience across borders—a capability that home computers and networks would soon expand dramatically.
