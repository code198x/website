On July 13, 1985, Live Aid staged simultaneous concerts at Wembley Stadium and JFK Stadium, connected by satellite. The event raised over £100 million for famine relief and became the most-watched live broadcast in history.

Organised by Bob Geldof following Band Aid's "Do They Know It's Christmas?", Live Aid demonstrated global television's power to mobilise action. Nearly 40% of the world's population watched some portion.

The event showed technology's potential to connect humanity across borders—a vision that networked computers would soon enable in new ways.
