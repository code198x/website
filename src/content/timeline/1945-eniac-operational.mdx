---
title: "ENIAC becomes operational"
year: 1945
month: 12
category: "technology"
summary: "The Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer, the first general-purpose electronic computer, begins operation at the University of Pennsylvania."
---

ENIAC filled a 50-foot room, weighed 30 tons, and used 18,000 vacuum tubes. It could perform 5,000 additions per second—revolutionary for its time, though a modern calculator far exceeds it.

Originally designed to calculate artillery firing tables, ENIAC demonstrated that electronic computation was practical. The machine's designers, John Mauchly and J. Presper Eckert, would go on to build UNIVAC, the first commercial computer.
