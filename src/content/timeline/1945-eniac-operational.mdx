ENIAC ran a major calculation for Los Alamos in December 1945. Its formal dedication at the University of Pennsylvania's Moore School followed on 15 February 1946. Operation and public introduction were separate stages in the machine's development.

John Mauchly and J. Presper Eckert led its hardware design. The [original programming group](/vault/groups/eniac-women-programmers) comprised Kathleen McNulty Mauchly Antonelli, Jean Jennings Bartik, Betty Snyder Holberton, Marlyn Wescoff Meltzer, Frances Bilas Spence and Ruth Lichterman Teitelbaum. Configuring calculations required understanding the machine's units and setting up switches and cable connections. Their work forms part of the history of [women's contributions to computing](/vault/culture/women-in-computing#programming-an-electronic-machine).
