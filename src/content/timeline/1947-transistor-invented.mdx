---
title: "The transistor is invented at Bell Labs"
year: 1947
month: 12
category: "technology"
summary: "John Bardeen, Walter Brattain, and William Shockley invent the transistor, the tiny switch that will replace vacuum tubes and enable modern electronics."
---

The transistor did what vacuum tubes did—amplify and switch electronic signals—but smaller, cooler, cheaper, and more reliably. This invention made portable radios possible in the 1950s and personal computers possible in the 1970s.

Without the transistor, there would be no microprocessors, no memory chips, no digital revolution. Every electronic device today contains billions of transistors, descendants of that first crude prototype at Bell Labs.
