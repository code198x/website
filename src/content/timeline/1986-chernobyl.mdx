On April 26, 1986, Reactor No. 4 at the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant exploded during a safety test. The resulting release of radioactive material was 400 times greater than the Hiroshima bombing.

The Soviet government's initial secrecy gave way to unprecedented openness as the scale of the disaster became clear. Chernobyl accelerated glasnost and exposed systemic failures in Soviet governance.

The disaster reminded the world that technology's power required respect and transparency—lessons applicable far beyond nuclear power.
