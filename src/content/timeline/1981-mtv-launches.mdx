MTV launched at 12:01 AM on August 1, 1981, with the Buggles' "Video Killed the Radio Star." The 24-hour music video channel created an entirely new form of entertainment.

The channel's rapid-fire visual style influenced everything from advertising to game design. Its emphasis on style and sensation over substance reflected broader cultural shifts.

MTV proved that audiences would watch the same content repeatedly if presented compellingly—a lesson the games industry would embrace with replayable, visually striking titles.
