---
title: "Retro gaming reaches mainstream acceptance"
year: 2023
category: "culture"
summary: "Classic gaming moves from niche hobby to mainstream culture, with official re-releases, preservation efforts, and record auction prices."
---

Nintendo's online services offered NES, SNES, N64, and Game Boy libraries. Analogue's FPGA consoles sold out instantly. Sealed games fetched six-figure auction prices (amid controversy).

The generation that grew up with 8-bit and 16-bit games now had disposable income and nostalgia. Retro gaming was no longer about poverty or limitations—it was about appreciation and preservation.
