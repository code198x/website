year: 1979

summary: "The portable cassette player creates a new category of personal electronics and demonstrates the appeal of technology you carry with you."
---

The Sony Walkman, released on July 1, 1979, was initially considered a risky product—a tape player that couldn't record seemed pointless to many. It became one of the most successful consumer electronics products ever made.

The Walkman proved that personal, portable technology had mass appeal. It let people create their own soundtracks to daily life, transforming the relationship between technology and lifestyle.

This shift toward personal electronics presaged the home computer revolution. If people would carry their music, they might also want their own computers.
