---
title: "The integrated circuit is invented"
year: 1958
category: "technology"
summary: "Jack Kilby at Texas Instruments creates the first integrated circuit, putting multiple transistors on a single chip and launching the microelectronics revolution."
---

Kilby's integrated circuit combined transistors, resistors, and capacitors on a single piece of germanium. Months later, Robert Noyce at Fairchild developed a more practical silicon version.

This invention made miniaturisation possible. Instead of wiring individual components together, engineers could now pack thousands—eventually billions—of transistors onto a chip the size of a fingernail.
