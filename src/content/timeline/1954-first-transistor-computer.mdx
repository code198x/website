---
title: "First fully transistorised computer built"
year: 1954
category: "technology"
summary: "The TRADIC, built by Bell Labs for the US Air Force, becomes the first computer to use transistors instead of vacuum tubes throughout."
---

TRADIC used 800 transistors and consumed far less power than its vacuum tube predecessors. It proved that transistor-based computers were not just possible but practical.

The transition from tubes to transistors would take a decade, but this machine showed the future. Computers would become smaller, more reliable, and eventually cheap enough for ordinary people to own.
