summary: "Tim Berners-Lee's World Wide Web project goes public, laying the foundation for the internet as we know it today."
---

On August 6, 1991, Tim Berners-Lee posted a summary of his World Wide Web project to the alt.hypertext newsgroup, inviting collaboration. The web was now public.

Developed at CERN to help physicists share research, the web combined hypertext, the internet, and a simple addressing system into something transformative. Anyone could publish; anyone could read.

The web would eventually absorb much of what made home computing special—games, communities, creativity—while reaching billions more people than 8-bit machines ever could.
