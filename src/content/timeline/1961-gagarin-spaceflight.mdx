On April 12, 1961, Yuri Gagarin became the first human to journey into outer space. His single orbit of Earth aboard the Vostok 1 spacecraft took just 108 minutes, but its impact on human history was immeasurable.

The flight represented a stunning Soviet achievement in the Space Race against the United States. It demonstrated that human spaceflight was possible and set the stage for the decade's ambitious lunar program.

Gagarin became an international celebrity and symbol of Soviet technological prowess. His famous words upon seeing Earth from space—"The Earth is blue... how wonderful"—captured humanity's first view of our planet from beyond.
