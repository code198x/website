title: "COVID-19 pandemic transforms daily life"
year: 2020
month: 3
category: "politics"
summary: "A global pandemic forces billions indoors, accelerating digital transformation and making gaming more central to social connection."
---

Lockdowns drove unprecedented gaming engagement. Animal Crossing: New Horizons became a social lifeline. Twitch viewership exploded. The games industry boomed while other entertainment struggled.

The pandemic accelerated trends already underway: remote work, streaming, digital distribution. It also created supply chain chaos that made new consoles nearly impossible to buy for years.
