# Fujitsu FM Towns

_Coming Soon_

The FM Towns was Japan's vision of the multimedia future - a 32-bit computer with built-in CD-ROM drive in 1989, years before multimedia PCs became common. It pioneered CD-ROM gaming and digital audio integration.

## Why Learn FM Towns Programming?

The FM Towns bridges 16-bit and 32-bit computing with sophisticated multimedia capabilities. Its 80386 processor and advanced graphics make it ideal for learning early 32-bit programming and CD-ROM development.

## What Makes the FM Towns Special

- **CD-ROM Standard**: Built-in from day one in 1989
- **32-bit Power**: 80386 processor years before PCs
- **True Color**: 32,768 colors in high resolution
- **Sound Excellence**: FM synthesis plus PCM audio

The FM Towns showed the future of computing - multimedia-rich, CD-ROM based systems with powerful graphics and sound. While it never left Japan, its influence on multimedia computing and CD-ROM gaming was profound.
