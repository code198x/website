# Tandy Color Computer 3

*Coming Soon*

The CoCo 3 was Radio Shack's masterpiece - an 8-bit computer that punched far above its weight. With the powerful 6809E processor, GIME graphics chip, and the optional OS-9 operating system, it offered capabilities that wouldn't be matched by other 8-bit systems.

## Why Learn CoCo 3 Programming?

The 6809 is widely considered the most elegant 8-bit processor ever designed. Combined with OS-9's Unix-like multitasking and the GIME's advanced graphics, the CoCo 3 teaches concepts that bridge 8-bit and 16-bit computing.

## What Makes the CoCo 3 Special

- **6809E Excellence**: Most advanced 8-bit CPU architecture
- **OS-9 Level II**: Real multitasking on an 8-bit machine
- **GIME Graphics**: Hardware scrolling and 640×225 resolution
- **Radio Shack**: Available in every mall in America

## The CoCo 3 Legacy

The CoCo 3 proved that 8-bit computers could be serious machines. Its devoted community continues to develop new hardware and software, with modern additions like CoCoSDC drives and network adapters keeping it relevant today.
