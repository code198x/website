# Dragon 32

*Historical Reference*

The Dragon 32 was Wales's entry into the home computer boom. Built around the powerful Motorola 6809E processor and largely compatible with the Tandy Color Computer, it offered superior processing power but couldn't compete with the Spectrum's price and software library.

## Historical Significance

The Dragon represented Britain's alternative approach to home computing - choosing the advanced 6809 over the more common 6502 or Z80. Manufactured in Wales, it was a source of national pride and briefly outsold the BBC Micro.

## Technical Features

- **6809E Power**: Most advanced 8-bit processor
- **CoCo Compatible**: Could run much TRS-80 Color Computer software
- **Microsoft BASIC**: Extended Color BASIC built-in
- **Welsh Manufacturing**: Built in Port Talbot, Wales

## The Welsh Dragon's Fate

Despite initial success, Dragon Data couldn't compete with Sinclair's prices or software support. The company went bankrupt in 1984, though the design lived on briefly as the Dragon 64 and in Spain as the Eurohard Dragon 200.

## Legacy

The Dragon proved that technical superiority doesn't guarantee success. Its 6809 processor was more powerful than the Spectrum's Z80, but lack of software and higher prices doomed it. The Dragon community remains active, appreciating what might have been.
