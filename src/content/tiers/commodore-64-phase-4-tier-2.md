# Tier 2: SID Chip Fundamentals & Sound Synthesis

Bring your games to life with sound! With **32 comprehensive lessons**, you'll learn to program the legendary SID chip, creating rich audio experiences that complement your advanced graphics with equally impressive sound.

## What You'll Build

Enhance **Neon Runner** with professional audio including dynamic background music, responsive sound effects, environmental audio, musical cues, and rich synthesis that creates an immersive audiovisual experience.

## Tier Overview

**Lessons 1-8: SID Oscillator Programming**
- Understanding SID architecture
- Programming oscillators and frequencies
- Waveform selection and control
- Oscillator synchronization

**Lessons 9-16: ADSR Envelope Control**
- Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release programming
- Creating expressive instrument sounds
- Envelope timing and optimization
- Musical expression techniques

**Lessons 17-24: Filter Programming**
- Low-pass, high-pass, and band-pass filters
- Filter frequency and resonance control
- Audio processing effects
- Creative filter usage

**Lessons 25-32: Sound Design & Optimization**
- Sound effect creation techniques
- Musical instrument synthesis
- Real-time audio performance
- Memory-efficient audio

## Key Concepts

This tier brings professional audio to your games:

- **Sound Synthesis** - Creating rich, expressive audio
- **Musical Programming** - Implementing music systems
- **Audio Effects** - Processing sound for impact
- **Real-Time Performance** - Responsive audio systems
- **Creative Expression** - Using sound artistically

These SID programming skills create memorable audio experiences!
