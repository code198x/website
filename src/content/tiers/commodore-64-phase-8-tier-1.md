# Tier 1: Mastery Projects & Professional Portfolio

Prove your C64 programming mastery! With **32 comprehensive lessons**, you'll create ambitious commercial-quality projects that demonstrate expert-level skills and build a professional portfolio that showcases your complete command of Commodore 64 development.

Create **Legacy Collection** - your professional portfolio featuring three major projects: a commercial-quality RPG with a 16-hour storyline, a cutting-edge demo that pushes the C64 to its limits, and a sophisticated productivity application that demonstrates your versatility as a master programmer.

**Lessons 9-16: Commercial RPG Development**
- Implementing complex game mechanics and storytelling
- Advanced data compression and streaming systems
- Sophisticated AI and procedural content generation

**Lessons 17-24: Demo Scene & Hardware Pushing**

**Lessons 25-32: Portfolio Completion & Professional Presentation**
- Finalizing and polishing all major projects
- Creating professional project documentation
- Building your portfolio website and technical showcase
- Preparing for professional opportunities and collaboration
