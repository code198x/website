# Tier 7: Strategy Games: Tactical Thinking on the NES!

Time to engage your brain! With **32 strategic lessons**, you'll create thinking-person's games that proved the NES could handle more than just jumping and shooting - where every move counts and victory comes from planning.

## What You'll Build

Create **PIXEL TACTICS** - a strategy game featuring:
- Grid-based battlefields with terrain effects
- Multiple unit types with unique abilities
- AI opponents that actually use strategy
- Campaign mode with persistent armies
- Multiplayer battles via controller passing!

## Tier Overview

**Lessons 1-8: "The Battlefield" - Grid Systems**
- Creating tactical grids that work with NES tiles
- Movement ranges and line-of-sight calculations
- Terrain types that affect gameplay
- Clear visual communication of complex states

**Lessons 9-16: "Art of War" - Combat Mechanics**
- Turn-based combat resolution
- Unit strengths, weaknesses, and counters
- Special abilities and resource management
- Making math fun (seriously!)

**Lessons 17-24: "Strategic Minds" - AI Programming**
- AI that thinks several moves ahead
- Different AI personalities and strategies
- Difficulty that scales without cheating
- Making the computer fun to outsmart

**Lessons 25-32: "Campaign Glory" - The Full War**
- Mission progression and story elements
- Army management between battles
- Victory conditions beyond "defeat all enemies"
- The satisfaction of a well-executed plan

## Key Concepts

This tier teaches you strategy game essentials:

- **Information Design** - Showing complex data clearly
- **Game Balance** - Making every choice meaningful
- **AI Strategy** - Opponents that think, not just react
- **Interface Excellence** - Complex actions, simple controls
- **Tactical Depth** - Easy to play, hard to master

By the end, you'll be thinking three moves ahead! ♟️
