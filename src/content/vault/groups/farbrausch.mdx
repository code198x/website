## Overview

Farbrausch took the demo scene's size constraints and made them art. Their 64K intros contained more content than most full games. Their 96KB .kkrieger was a complete first-person shooter. They proved that procedural generation and clever compression could achieve the seemingly impossible.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 2000, Germany.
- **Speciality:** Size-limited productions.
- **Famous for:** Procedural generation, compression.
- **Influence:** Modern game engines.

## Legendary productions

| Production | Size | Achievement |
|------------|------|-------------|
| fr-08: .the" product | 64KB | Full 3D engine, procedural everything |
| .kkrieger | 96KB | Complete FPS with levels |
| fr-041: debris | 177KB | Stunning visuals |

## Technical innovations

Farbrausch pioneered:
- Procedural texture synthesis
- Algorithmic music generation
- Extreme executable compression
- Real-time content generation

## Industry influence

Their techniques influenced:
- Procedural game generation
- Asset compression
- Shader programming
- Indie game development

Members joined companies like Crytek, bringing size-coding discipline to AAA development.
