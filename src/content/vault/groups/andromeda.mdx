Andromeda were one of the Danish scene's strongest Amiga groups, hitting their peak as the AGA chipset opened new headroom on the A1200 and A4000. Their 1994 demo [Nexus 7](/vault/demos/nexus-7) took first place at [The Party](/vault/events/the-party) — the same Danish event that had hosted Kefrens' *Desert Dream* a year earlier — and remains a touchstone of mid-90s Amiga design.

The group's work sat at the confident, polished end of the scene, where strong art direction mattered as much as raw effects.

## Fast facts

- **Active:** early-to-mid 1990s, Denmark.
- **Platform:** Amiga (AGA).
- **Best known for:** *[Nexus 7](/vault/demos/nexus-7)* (1994), winner at The Party 1994.

## Why Andromeda mattered

Andromeda helped show what the AGA generation of Amigas was for. *Nexus 7* used the newer chipset's extra colour and power in service of design, not just spectacle, and stands among the most fondly remembered productions of its moment.
