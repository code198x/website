At the end of December 1994 the Amiga demo competition at The Party, the Danish scene gathering held between Christmas and New Year, was won by a landslide. *[Nexus 7](/vault/demos/nexus-7)*, by the Norwegian group Andromeda, scored 1,443 points in the official results. The runner-up, Virtual Dreams' *Psychedelic*, scored 710, and Bomb's *Motion* came third with 501. *Nexus 7* was written for the AGA Amigas, the A1200 and A4000, and within months British public-domain libraries were selling it on floppy disk.

## Fast facts

- **From:** Norway.
- **Active:** from 1990; still releasing in the 2020s.
- **Platforms:** Amiga, and the PC from 2007.
- **Best known for:** *[Nexus 7](/vault/demos/nexus-7)*, winner of the Amiga demo competition at [The Party](/vault/events/the-party) 1994.
- **Other placings:** *D.O.S.*, second at [The Gathering](/vault/events/the-gathering) 1992; *Noumenon*, second in the PC demo competition at Breakpoint 2007.

## On the Norwegian scene

Andromeda began in 1990, and its first releases, listed by the scene database Demozoo, were the usual mix for a young Amiga group: demos, intros, music disks and crack intros. By 1992 it was competing at home. At The Gathering, held in Norway in April 1992, its demo *D.O.S.* came second to [Spaceballs](/vault/groups/spaceballs)' *Wayfarer*, and its second entry, *Multica*, finished tenth. More demos followed, among them *Mind Riot* and *Surrealism* in 1993 and *Sequential* in 1994, all for the older Amiga chipset.

## Nexus 7

*Nexus 7* is the first production for the AGA chipset in Andromeda's Demozoo list. AGA let the A1200 and A4000 show 256 colours at once, chosen from 16.7 million. The Party's results list it under Dr. Jekyll of Andromeda. Demozoo credits the code to Dr. Jekyll, Mr. Hyde and View, the graphics to Archmage and the music to Interphace.

Its winning margin stands out in the results: 1,443 points to 710 for second place, against twenty-one other demos, including entries from [Sanity](/vault/groups/sanity), [Oxyron](/vault/groups/oxyron) and Polka Brothers.

British Amiga owners could buy it within a few months. Public-domain libraries listed "Nexus 7 AGA Demo By Andromeda" in *Amiga Shopper* in March 1995, and one library's advertisement in *Amiga User International* that May called it "the latest stunning AGA demo from Andromeda". Another, advertising in *CU Amiga* in August 1995, recommended it as "voted 1st at the Party 94! Absolute must".

## Later work

Andromeda's next productions came thirteen years later, on the PC. *Noumenon* came second in the PC demo competition at Breakpoint in Germany in April 2007, behind [farbrausch](/vault/groups/farbrausch)'s *debris.*. The group made further PC demos up to 2009 and went back to the Amiga with new demos in 2021 and 2024.
