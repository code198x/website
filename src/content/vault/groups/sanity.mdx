## Overview

For a few intense years at the start of the 1990s, Sanity were close to the top of the Amiga scene. The German group built a reputation for finish — demos where the effects, the music, and the design all pulled in the same direction. *Arte* (1993) is the production most often named as their high-water mark, but their catalogue was deep and consistently admired.

Like many of the strongest groups, Sanity burned bright and relatively briefly; several members carried their discipline into professional games and tools work as the scene's first wave matured.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** around 1990, Germany.
- **Platform:** Amiga.
- **Best known for:** *Arte* (1993).
- **Reputation:** Benchmark-setting polish.

## Why Sanity mattered

Sanity proved that the Amiga scene's best work could be as composed and deliberate as it was technically daring. They raised the expectation of what a finished demo should feel like, and their peers had to meet it.
