## Overview

Genesis Project — GP — is one of the great survivors of the scene. Formed in 1986 (its name lifted from *Star Trek*'s terraforming "Project Genesis"), it was a Commodore 64 cracking group that, like the best of them, treated the intro fronting each release as a craft in its own right.

What sets GP apart is endurance. Where most cracking crews burned out in a few intense years, GP kept going — through line-up changes, a long-lived PC branch, and a full C64 reboot in 2009 — and remains active on the C64 and beyond, a living thread back to the cracking scene's heyday.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1986.
- **Platform:** Commodore 64 (later multiplatform).
- **Status:** Still active.
- **Trait:** Exceptional longevity for a cracking group.

## Intros across the eras

Because GP spans so much of the C64's life, its intros are almost a timeline of the form — from the terse early signatures through the competitive, music-and-scroller showcases that pushed crackers and demo coders alike. The group is proof that the cracking scene's traditions never fully stopped; they just kept being practised.
