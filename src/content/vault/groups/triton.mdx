Triton are one of the clearest answers to the question "what happened to the scene's best people?" The Swedish group built a strong reputation with productions like *Crystal Dream* — fast, confident demos that showed an instinct for design as much as effects. Then they made the leap a lot of groups only talked about: they became a commercial studio.

That studio was [Starbreeze](/vault/companies/starbreeze), later known worldwide for games such as *The Chronicles of Riddick: Escape from Butcher Bay* and *Brothers: A Tale of Two Sons*. The line from demo compo to retail shelf runs straight through Triton.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** early 1990s, Sweden.
- **Platforms:** Amiga, PC.
- **Best known for:** *[Crystal Dream](/vault/demos/crystal-dream)* and its sequel.
- **Became:** Starbreeze Studios.

## Scene to studio

Triton's transformation is one of the demoscene's signature success stories. The discipline that made a good demo — tight code, strong art direction, finishing under deadline at a party — is exactly the discipline a games studio needs, and Triton carried it across the threshold intact.
