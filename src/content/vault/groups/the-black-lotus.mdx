## Overview

The Black Lotus — TBL — are the group most responsible for the idea that the Amiga demoscene never grew old. Formed in the mid-1990s, they kept producing showpiece demos long after the wider industry had moved on, each one a statement that the platform still had headroom nobody else was reaching.

Their 2006 production [Starstruck](/vault/demos/starstruck), premiered at Assembly, is the one most often held up as proof — a demo so polished it forces a double-take about which machine it's running on.

## Fast facts

- **Formed:** mid-1990s.
- **Platform:** Amiga.
- **Best known for:** *Starstruck* (2006).
- **Role:** Flag-bearer of the modern Amiga scene.

## Why TBL matter

Where many groups dispersed as the Amiga faded commercially, TBL treated its limits as an open question and kept answering it. They are to the Amiga what Booze Design are to the C64: living proof that a platform's scene is a tradition, not a memory.
