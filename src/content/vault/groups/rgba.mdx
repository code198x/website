## Overview

Rgba are a small group with an outsized influence on one of the demoscene's hardest disciplines: the 4K intro, a complete real-time production in just four kilobytes. Working with their collaborators TBC, Rgba's [Elevated](/vault/demos/elevated) (2009) showed that 4K need not mean crude — it could mean landscapes, lighting, and motion that rivalled full demos.

The engine behind that was real-time raymarching of signed distance fields, a technique Rgba's iq (Íñigo Quílez) did more than almost anyone to popularise — first in intros, then far beyond the scene.

## Fast facts

- **Platform:** PC.
- **Speciality:** 4K intros, real-time raymarching.
- **Best known for:** *Elevated* (2009, with TBC).
- **Notable member:** iq (Íñigo Quílez).

## Scene to the wider world

iq went on to co-create Shadertoy — the browser playground where a generation learned shader programming — and to work in film and graphics research. The raymarching ideas Rgba sharpened in 4K intros now turn up in tutorials, tools, and production renderers everywhere: another case of scene technique escaping into the mainstream.
