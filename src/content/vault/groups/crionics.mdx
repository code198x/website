Crionics earned their place in scene history through one extraordinary collaboration. Teaming with [The Silents](/vault/groups/the-silents), they co-produced [Hardwired](/vault/demos/hardwired) in 1991 — a demo that swapped the usual effect-after-effect parade for pacing, mood, and direction, and helped redefine what an Amiga production could aspire to.

A capable group in their own right, Crionics are most often remembered for that landmark, and for the cross-group cooperation that made it possible — a reminder that the scene's biggest leaps often came from crews pooling their best people.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** around 1989.
- **Platform:** Amiga.
- **Best known for:** *[Hardwired](/vault/demos/hardwired)* (1991), with The Silents.
- **Legacy:** Co-authors of an early cinematic demo landmark.

## Why Crionics mattered

*Hardwired* is one of the productions people cite when arguing the demo is an art form. Crionics' share in it — and the model of two strong groups combining forces — left a mark on how ambitious scene projects were assembled.
