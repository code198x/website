## Overview

Booze Design are the answer to anyone who assumes the C64 scene is a nostalgia act. The group's 2008 demo [Edge of Disgrace](/vault/demos/edge-of-disgrace) was a statement — a long, lavish, technically ferocious production that pushed the 1982 machine well past what most thought it could still do, and reminded the wider world that the C64 scene never stopped innovating.

Working decades after the hardware's commercial death, Booze Design carry the lineage of groups like Censor and Oxyron into the present.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** around 2000.
- **Platform:** Commodore 64.
- **Best known for:** *Edge of Disgrace* (2008).
- **Role:** Modern flag-bearers for the C64 scene.

## Why Booze Design matter

*Edge of Disgrace* is one of the strongest arguments that the demoscene is a living tradition. Booze Design demonstrate that the C64's limits are still being found — and that the chain of technique running back to the 1980s is unbroken and still advancing.
