## Overview

Sweden produced several legendary scene groups, and Triad ranks among the greatest. Founded in 1986, they focused primarily on the C64, releasing hundreds of cracks with intros that rivalled standalone demos. Their consistency and quality made them essential to C64 scene history.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1986, Sweden.
- **Platform:** Primarily C64.
- **Status:** Still active in demo scene.
- **Output:** Hundreds of releases.

## Scene contribution

Triad exemplified the C64 scene's golden age:
- Elaborate crack intros
- Original music and graphics
- Competition with peer groups
- Community building through releases

## Demo transition

As cracking's moral complications became clearer, many Triad members transitioned to the demo scene—using the same skills for legal creative expression.

## Legacy

Triad members went on to careers in games, music, and software. Their intros trained a generation of programmers and artists.
