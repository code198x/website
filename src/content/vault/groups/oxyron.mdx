## Overview

Oxyron are a coders' group above all. The German C64 outfit built a reputation for technical invention — finding new ways to bend the VIC-II and the CPU past their documented limits, and packaging the results in demos like the *Coma Light* series. Tricks first seen in Oxyron productions filtered out across the scene to become common vocabulary.

Long-running and stubbornly committed to the platform, Oxyron embody the C64 scene's defining trait: the conviction that the machine always has one more secret to give up.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** around 1989, Germany.
- **Platform:** Commodore 64.
- **Best known for:** the *Coma Light* demos; technical firsts.
- **Reputation:** A pioneering coders' group.

## Why Oxyron mattered

A great deal of "standard" advanced C64 technique was, at some point, an Oxyron discovery. By sharing those breakthroughs through their demos, they pushed the whole scene's ceiling higher — the engine that keeps the C64 producing astonishing work decades on.
