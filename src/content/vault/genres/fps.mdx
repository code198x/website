## Overview

**First-person shooters** (FPS) put the camera behind the protagonist's eyes—and usually behind a weapon. The genre began with *Wolfenstein 3D* (1992) and *Doom* (1993), creating both a visual grammar and competitive culture. Technical innovation drove early development; multiplayer communities shaped later evolution. FPS remains one of gaming's most commercially significant genres.

## Legacy

The first-person perspective proved uniquely suited to conveying presence and agency. FPS techniques—the bobbing viewmodel, the crosshair, the minimap—became gaming's visual language. Whether through competitive multiplayer or narrative campaigns, FPS remains central to the medium.
