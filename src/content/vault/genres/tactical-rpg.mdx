Tactical RPGs place parties on grid-based battlefields where positioning, terrain, and character abilities determine victory. Unlike pure strategy games, characters persist between battles with RPG-style progression. The genre split between Japanese traditions (*Fire Emblem*, *Final Fantasy Tactics*) and Western approaches (*XCOM*, *Jagged Alliance*), each emphasising different elements.

## Fast facts

- **Core elements:** grid combat + character progression.
- **Japanese lineage:** *[Fire Emblem](/vault/games/fire-emblem)*, *[Final Fantasy Tactics](/vault/games/final-fantasy-tactics)*, *Disgaea*.
- **Western lineage:** *XCOM*, *Jagged Alliance*.
- **Combat style:** turn-based, position-dependent.
- **Narrative:** often story-heavy with developed characters.

## Design elements

What defines tactical RPGs:
- **Grid movement:** tiles with movement costs.
- **Terrain effects:** cover, elevation, hazards.
- **Character classes:** roles with distinct abilities.
- **Permadeath:** often permanent character death (optional in some).

## Genre split

Eastern versus Western approaches:
- **JRPG tactics:** story focus, anime aesthetics, deterministic.
- **Western tactics:** simulation-adjacent, percentage-based outcomes.
- **Modern convergence:** elements blend across traditions.
