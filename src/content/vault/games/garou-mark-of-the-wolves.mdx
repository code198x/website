## Overview

SNK's fighting game apex. Garou stripped away gimmicks to deliver pure, refined combat. The new generation cast—Rock Howard, B. Jenet, Gato—brought fresh energy to Fatal Fury's universe. The T.O.P. system rewarded aggressive play. Just Defend offered frame-perfect parry rewards. Released as SNK faced bankruptcy, it stands as their fighting game pinnacle.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** SNK.
- **Year:** 1999.
- **Platform:** Neo Geo MVS/AES.
- **Status:** Considered SNK's finest.
