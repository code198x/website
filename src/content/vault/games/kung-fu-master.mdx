Thomas must fight through five floors of Mr. X's pagoda to save his girlfriend Sylvia. *Kung-Fu Master* (known as *Spartan X* in Japan) created the template that *Double Dragon* and *Final Fight* would later perfect: walk right, punch enemies, defeat boss, repeat.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Irem](/vault/companies/irem).
- **Release:** 1984.
- **Inspiration:** Jackie Chan's *Wheels on Meals*.
- **Alternate title:** Spartan X (Japan).
