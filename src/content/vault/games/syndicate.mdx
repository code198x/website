*Syndicate* dropped players into a dark future where megacorporations waged covert war through cybernetically enhanced agents. Bullfrog's isometric tactics game combined strategic territory control with brutal real-time missions. Persuadertron-equipped agents turned civilians into human shields. Nothing was off limits.
