*Centipede* was different. The trackball allowed precise movement. The descending centipede weaved through a mushroom field that players could reshape. Designer Dona Bailey, one of gaming's few female programmers, created something accessible yet deep. It found an audience beyond the typical arcade demographic.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Atari](/vault/companies/atari).
- **Designers:** [Dona Bailey](/vault/people/dona-bailey), [Ed Logg](/vault/people/ed-logg).
- **Release:** 1981.
- **Control:** Trackball.

## Dona Bailey

Rare female game designer:
- One of few women in arcade development
- Brought different design sensibility
- Created accessible gameplay
- Attracted broader audience
