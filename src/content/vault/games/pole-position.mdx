*Pole Position* put players behind the wheel with a view from behind their car—a perspective that became racing game standard. Namco's sprite scaling created the illusion of speed as the Fuji Speedway rushed toward you. Qualify, then race: the formula seems obvious now because *Pole Position* invented it.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Namco](/vault/companies/namco).
- **Release:** 1982.
- **Track:** Fuji Speedway.
- **Innovation:** Rear-view racing perspective.
