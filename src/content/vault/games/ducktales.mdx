## Overview

A licensed game that was actually great. DuckTales proved Disney properties could produce excellent gameplay when paired with capable developers. Capcom crafted tight controls around Scrooge McDuck's cane-bouncing pogo attack, while non-linear stage selection and hidden treasures encouraged exploration. The Moon theme became one of gaming's most beloved compositions.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Capcom.
- **Source:** Disney TV series.
- **Innovation:** Pogo cane mechanic.
- **Music:** Legendary Moon stage theme.
