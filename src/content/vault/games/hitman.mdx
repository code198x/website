Murder as problem-solving. Hitman dropped Agent 47 into complex environments with targets to eliminate and countless methods to accomplish the job. Disguise as a chef, poison the soup. Impersonate a guard, get close. Arrange an "accident" and walk away clean. Each level became a puzzle box where creativity and patience outweighed reflexes.
