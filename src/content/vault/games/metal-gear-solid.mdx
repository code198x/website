Hideo Kojima's vision finally had hardware to match. Metal Gear Solid transformed the MSX stealth games into a cinematic experience, blending long codec conversations, elaborate boss battles, and meta moments that acknowledged the player's existence. Solid Snake's infiltration of Shadow Moses became gaming's definitive stealth-action template.
