## Overview

Keep eating or die. Adventure Island challenged players with constant momentum and a stamina bar that never stopped draining. Master Higgins sprinted through islands, grabbing fruit to survive, throwing axes at snakes, and riding skateboards for speed. Based on Wonder Boy but evolved into its own distinct series.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Hudson Soft.
- **Protagonist:** Master Higgins.
- **Origin:** Wonder Boy reskin.
- **Mechanic:** Stamina management.
