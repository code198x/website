## Overview

Bloodborne (2015) is an action RPG developed by FromSoftware exclusively for PlayStation 4. Set in the Victorian Gothic city of Yharnam, it trades the defensive shield-based combat of Dark Souls for aggressive, fast-paced action with firearms and transforming weapons.

## Key Features

- **Rally system:** Regain health by attacking after taking damage
- **Trick weapons:** Weapons transform between two forms
- **Chalice dungeons:** Procedurally generated optional areas
- **Lovecraftian horror:** Cosmic horror themes emerge as the game progresses

## Critical Reception

Bloodborne received widespread acclaim for its atmosphere, combat system, and world design. The aggressive combat style influenced subsequent FromSoftware titles and the broader action RPG genre.
