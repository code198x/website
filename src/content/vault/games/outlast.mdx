## Overview

Record everything, survive nothing. Outlast sent journalist Miles Upshur into Mount Massive Asylum with only a camcorder. Night vision drained batteries. Enemies couldn't be fought—only fled or hidden from. The found-footage aesthetic and relentless intensity made it ideal for streaming, turning reactions into content and cementing its cultural presence.

## Fast facts

- **Protagonist:** Miles Upshur.
