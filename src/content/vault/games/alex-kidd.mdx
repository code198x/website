Before the blue hedgehog, there was a boy with big ears and a powerful punch. Alex Kidd became Sega's answer to Mario, built into the Master System's ROM in some regions. His adventures featured tight platforming, motorcycle and boat sequences, and the memorable Janken (rock-paper-scissors) boss battles that could end a run on bad luck.
