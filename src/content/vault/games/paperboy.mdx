*Paperboy* took the mundane job of newspaper delivery and made it chaotic. Players cycled down suburban streets, hurling papers at subscriber mailboxes while avoiding obstacles and, optionally, destroying the property of non-subscribers. The isometric perspective and unique premise made it instantly memorable.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Atari Games](/vault/companies/atari-games).
- **Release:** 1985.
- **Perspective:** Isometric.
- **Control:** Handlebar controller (arcade).
