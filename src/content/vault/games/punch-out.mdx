Little Mac is tiny. His opponents are giants. Punch-Out!! succeeded not through simulation but pattern recognition—each boxer telegraphed their attacks with distinctive tells. Learn the pattern, counter at the right moment, work your way to Mike Tyson (or Mr. Dream). The transparent protagonist sprite let players see incoming punches clearly.
