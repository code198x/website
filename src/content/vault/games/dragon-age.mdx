## Overview

BioWare's spiritual successor to Baldur's Gate. Dragon Age: Origins embraced dark fantasy—blood magic, political betrayal, morally compromised companions—while reviving tactical party combat. Six distinct origin stories meant your character's background shaped dialogue options and NPC reactions throughout. The Grey Wardens' desperate fight against the Blight anchored a world worth saving.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** BioWare.
- **Year:** 2009.
- **Setting:** Thedas (Ferelden).
- **Threat:** The Blight.
