## Overview

Bloodstained: Ritual of the Night (2019) is an action-adventure game directed by Koji Igarashi, the former Castlevania series producer at Konami. Funded through Kickstarter, it raised over $5.5 million, becoming one of the most successful video game crowdfunding campaigns.

## Design Philosophy

The game deliberately recreates the "Igavania" style:
- **Exploration-based progression:** Abilities gate access to new areas
- **RPG elements:** Equipment, levels, and crafting
- **Gothic atmosphere:** Dark castle setting
- **Shard system:** Absorb enemy abilities

## Development Story

After leaving Konami, Igarashi turned to crowdfunding to create the Metroidvania game fans wanted. The campaign's success demonstrated demand for the classic formula.
