## Overview

PlayStation found its image. WipEout wasn't just fast—it was fashionable. Designers Republic's visual identity, licensed electronic music from Chemical Brothers and Orbital, and sleek anti-gravity craft positioned gaming as club culture. The difficulty was punishing, but the aesthetic was irresistible. Sony marketed the game in nightclubs, reaching audiences who'd never considered consoles.
