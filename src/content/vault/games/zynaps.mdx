## Being the other one

This is a shape the Vault should record, because it describes most games.

*Zynaps* is good. It charted for nine months, which few titles managed, and it did so against a stablemate from its own publisher that kept taking the top spot. There is no failure here — and there is also no story of the kind that gets a game remembered, because remembering runs on firsts and bests.

Hewson could sustain two competing shooters at once precisely because its identity was **the programmer, not the property**. Robinson and Cecco were not fighting over a licence; they were two named authors doing similar work for the same label, and the label was happy for both to sell.

That arrangement is rare. A publisher built on licences cannot have two *Batman*s.
