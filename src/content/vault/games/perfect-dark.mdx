## Overview

Everything GoldenEye did, but more. Perfect Dark added bots to multiplayer, letting solo players experience deathmatch. It added secondary fire modes to every weapon. It added Counter-Operative mode where a second player controlled enemies. The Expansion Pak requirement limited its audience, but those who played found Rare's ambition exceeded the hardware.
