## Overview

The DevTeam thinks of everything. NetHack evolved from Hack (itself a Rogue descendant) into perhaps gaming's most systemically deep experience. Dip a poisoned corpse in a fountain? The game knows what happens. Polymorph into a cockatrice and lay eggs? Valid strategy. Thirty-plus years of development created a simulation where player creativity is rewarded with interaction depth no modern game approaches.
