*Asteroids* was elegance itself. A triangular ship. Drifting rocks. Newtonian physics. Atari's vector display drew crisp white lines against blackness while players learned that in space, momentum never stops. The game earned more than *Space Invaders* in American arcades.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Atari](/vault/companies/atari).
- **Designer:** Lyle Rains, Ed Logg.
- **Release:** 1979.
- **Display:** Vector (XY monitor).

## Physics model

Newtonian movement:
- Thrust adds velocity
- No friction in space
- Momentum conserved
- Wraparound screen edges

Learning to control drift was the game.
