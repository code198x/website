## Overview

*The Chaos Engine* dropped two mercenaries into a steampunk nightmare where a time-manipulating machine had twisted Victorian England. Six playable characters with different abilities, sixteen levels of maze-like exploration, and relentless enemies created the Bitmap Brothers' most ambitious title.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** The Bitmap Brothers.
