Dave Theurer wanted to create a *Space Invaders* nightmare—enemies climbing out of the screen toward you. *Tempest* realised that vision with vector graphics, a rotary controller, and tube-shaped playfields. The result was intense, hypnotic, and unlike anything else in arcades.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Atari.
- **Designer:** Dave Theurer.
- **Release:** 1981.
- **Display:** Colour vector.
