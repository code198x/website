## Overview

Pit rises from the Underworld. Kid Icarus mixed vertical scrolling stages with horizontal fortresses, layering RPG progression over demanding platforming. The angel protagonist earned hearts as currency, purchased items, and grew stronger—if players could survive the brutal early game before upgrades made Pit powerful.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Nintendo R&D1.
- **Director:** Yoshio Sakamoto.
- **Engine:** Shared with Metroid.
- **Protagonist:** Pit.
