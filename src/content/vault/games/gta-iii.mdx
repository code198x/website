## Overview

*Grand Theft Auto III* turned a controversial top-down series into an industry-defining 3D experience. Liberty City became a living, breathing criminal playground. Players could follow story missions or simply explore, steal cars, and cause chaos. The freedom was unprecedented, the controversy predictable, and the influence permanent.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** DMA Design (became Rockstar North).
- **Publisher:** Rockstar Games.
- **Engine:** RenderWare.
- **Setting:** Liberty City (New York inspired).
- **Sales:** 17+ million copies.

## From 2D to 3D

Transformation from earlier games:

| GTA 1/2 | GTA III |
|---------|---------|
| Top-down view | Third-person 3D |
| Sprite characters | Polygon models |
| Abstract cities | Detailed environments |
| Score-focused | Story-driven |

## Gameplay systems

| System | Function |
|--------|----------|
| Vehicles | Cars, boats, tanks stealable |
| Weapons | Expanding arsenal |
| Missions | Story and side activities |
| Wanted level | Police escalation |
| Radio stations | Licensed music, talk shows |

## Liberty City

Three interconnected islands:
- **Portland:** Industrial, Mafia territory.
- **Staunton Island:** Commercial centre.
- **Shoreside Vale:** Suburban, airport.

Islands unlocked through story progression.

## Narrative approach

Silent protagonist (Claude):
- Voice acting for all other characters.
- Cutscenes drive story.
- Betrayal and revenge arc.
- Mob, cartel, Yakuza factions.

## Open world innovation

What GTA III established:
- Persistent, explorable city.
- Freedom between missions.
- Emergent gameplay opportunities.
- Living world simulation.
- Side activities (hidden packages, rampages).

## Technical achievement

On PlayStation 2:
- Streaming world (minimal loading).
- Day/night cycle.
- Weather systems.
- Pedestrian and traffic AI.
- Draw distance limitations.

## Controversy

Predictable outrage:
- Violence against civilians.
- Ability to interact with sex workers.
- Gang themes.
- Australia banned initially.
- US political scrutiny.

## Commercial impact

Sales and influence:
- Fastest-selling game at release.
- Established PlayStation 2 dominance.
- Created template for competitors.
- Launched Rockstar to industry leader status.

## Sequel quality

Followed by:
- *Vice City* (2002): 1980s Miami.
- *San Andreas* (2004): Expanded scope.
- *GTA IV* (2008): Next generation.
- *GTA V* (2013): Three protagonists.

## Industry influence

Games following GTA III template:
- Saints Row series.
- Sleeping Dogs.
- Watch Dogs.
- Countless open-world games.

## Legacy

*GTA III* proved games could offer film-like narrative within player-driven freedom. The sandbox crime genre it created remains one of gaming's most popular and profitable.
