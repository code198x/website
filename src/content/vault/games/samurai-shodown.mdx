## Overview

Patience over aggression. Samurai Shodown rejected the combo-heavy trend of its era for deliberate, high-damage exchanges. A single well-timed slash could turn a match. The feudal Japanese setting provided visual distinction from Street Fighter's globe-trotting. SNK's sprite work captured the weight and danger of blade combat with artistic flair.
