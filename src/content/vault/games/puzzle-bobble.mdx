Point, shoot, match. Puzzle Bobble took Bub and Bob from platforming to puzzling, launching coloured bubbles at clusters overhead. Match three or more colours to clear them; let bubbles reach the bottom and lose. The aiming mechanic felt physical and satisfying. Known as Bust-A-Move in North America, it became one of gaming's most imitated puzzle formats.
