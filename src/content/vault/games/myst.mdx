No inventory. No dialogue trees. Just you, an island, and the quiet certainty that everything means something. Myst's contemplative puzzle design and pre-rendered visuals made it the best-selling PC game of its era, bringing adventure gaming to audiences who'd never touched the genre. Its meditative pace divided players but sold millions.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Cyan](/vault/companies/cyan).
- **Creators:** Rand and Robyn Miller.
- **Sales:** Best-selling PC game (until The Sims).
