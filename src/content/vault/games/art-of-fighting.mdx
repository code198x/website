Technical ambition on Neo Geo. Art of Fighting showcased SNK's hardware with massive scaling sprites that zoomed dramatically during combat. The spirit gauge limited special move spam, forcing resource management. Ryo Sakazaki and Robert Garcia became permanent SNK fixtures, crossing into King of Fighters and representing the Kyokugen fighting style.
