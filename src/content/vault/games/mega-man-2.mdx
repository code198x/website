## Overview

*Mega Man 2* was made on borrowed time—Capcom nearly cancelled the series after modest initial sales. The development team worked nights and weekends to prove the concept. They succeeded beyond imagination, creating the definitive NES action platformer and ensuring the Blue Bomber's future.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Capcom.
- **Release:** 1988 (Japan), 1989 (West).
- **Director:** Akira Kitamura.
- **Composer:** Takashi Tateishi.
