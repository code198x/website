## Overview

Snowboarding as impossible spectacle. SSX launched with PlayStation 2, showcasing the hardware through massive mountains, impossible tricks, and characters with actual personality. The boost system—build meter through tricks, spend it on speed—created risk-reward decisions mid-race. EA Sports Big found its flagship.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** EA Canada.
- **Year:** 2000.
- **Platform:** PS2 launch title.
- **Label:** EA Sports Big.
