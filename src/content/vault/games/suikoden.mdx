## Overview

Gather your stars. Suikoden drew from the Chinese classic Water Margin—108 heroes uniting against corrupt power. Konami's JRPG translated this into recruitment gameplay; players built a headquarters that grew with each ally, culminating in army-scale strategic battles. The series' interconnected world and character transfers between entries created a saga spanning generations before Konami's neglect ended it.

## Fast facts
