Shoot clouds, juggle bells. TwinBee wrapped vertical shooting in candy colours and cheerful design, pioneering what would become the "cute-em-up" aesthetic. The distinctive bell power-up system—shoot bells to change their colour and effect—added strategic depth, while two players could link their ships for combined attacks.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Konami](/vault/companies/konami).
- **Genre:** Cute-em-up shooter.
- **Innovation:** Bell juggling power-ups.
- **Players:** Two simultaneous.
