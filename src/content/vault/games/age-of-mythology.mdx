Call upon the gods. Age of Mythology took Ensemble's proven formula into legendary territory—Zeus's lightning bolts, Ra's blessings, Thor's dwarven forges. Each civilisation worshipped a pantheon, granting divine powers and mythological creatures. Cyclopes, sphinxes, and frost giants joined historical units. The campaign followed Atlantean hero Arkantos across mythologies in a story more ambitious than typical RTS fare.
