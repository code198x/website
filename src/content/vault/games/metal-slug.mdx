Every frame a work of art. Metal Slug elevated run-and-gun gameplay with animation quality that remained unmatched—soldiers reacted expressively, vehicles transformed fluidly, explosions cascaded beautifully. The Nazca/SNK team crafted tight action around the "Super Vehicle-001" tank, creating a series that defined Neo Geo's late-era appeal.
