## Overview

Everyone dies. Not everyone gets a good travel package. Manny Calavera works in the Land of the Dead, selling premium afterlife journeys to souls who earned them—except someone's stealing his best clients. Tim Schafer's magnum opus blended Mexican folklore, art deco design, and hardboiled detective fiction into gaming's most stylish adventure.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** LucasArts.
- **Director:** Tim Schafer.
- **Setting:** Land of the Dead.
- **Engine:** GrimE (3D).
