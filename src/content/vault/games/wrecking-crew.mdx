## Overview

Mario swings a hammer, but this isn't combat—it's demolition. Wrecking Crew tasked players with destroying every wall, ladder, and pillar in 100 single-screen stages. The catch: destroy things in the wrong order and you'd trap yourself. Enemies patrolled constantly, forcing strategic destruction under pressure.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Nintendo R&D1.
- **Protagonist:** Mario (demolition worker).
- **Stages:** 100 levels.
- **Feature:** Level editor.
