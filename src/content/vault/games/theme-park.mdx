*Theme Park* let players build the amusement park of their dreams—then manage its finances, staff, and increasingly demanding visitors. Bullfrog's business simulation balanced creative construction with economic reality, wrapped in characteristic British humour.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Bullfrog Productions](/vault/companies/bullfrog).
- **Publisher:** [Electronic Arts](/vault/companies/electronic-arts).
- **Designer:** Peter Molyneux (producer).
- **Lead programmer:** Demis Hassabis.
