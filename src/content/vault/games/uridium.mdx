## What the press gave it

The Spectrum conversion arrived in late 1986 and did well: **90% in *CRASH*** in December, **5/5 in *Sinclair User*** in November, 9/10 in *Your Sinclair*. *Sinclair User* was still giving it 90% for a budget re-release in 1988.

⚠ The original is a C64 game — [Braybrook](/vault/people/andrew-braybrook) wrote it there, as he had *[Paradroid](/vault/games/paradroid)* — and its famous scrolling belongs to that machine. The scores above are the conversion's.

## Overview
Released in 1986 by Hewson Consultants, *Uridium* tasks pilots with strafing enemy dreadnoughts, dodging towers, and landing for reactor destruction. Its smooth scroll and crisp controls made it a C64 showcase.

## Fast facts
- **Developer:** Andrew Braybrook, following the success of *Paradroid*.  
- **Tech:** 50 Hz scrolling achieved through careful CPU budget and sprite multiplexing.  
- **Ports:** Spectrum and Amstrad conversions reimagined the action with clever compromises.
