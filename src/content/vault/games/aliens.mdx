## ⚠ What this entry got wrong

The first version of this page claimed *Aliens* reached **#1 in August and November 1988**, a year after release, and built an argument about licences behaving unusually.

**Every one of those rows was a different medium.** *CRASH* printed a **video rental chart** — films on VHS — and *ZZAP!64* ran a readers' poll page stacking "MY FAVOURITE PIECE" (music), "MY FAVOURITE ARCADE" (coin-ops) and "MY FAVOURITE VIDEO" (films). The chart extractor finds charts by looking for a run of consecutive rank numbers, which those are.

The giveaway was in the data all along:

```
1 (1)  PLATOON               RCA/COLUMBIA
2 (3)  ALIENS                CBS/FOX
3 ( )  LETHAL WEAPON         WARNER
```

RCA/Columbia, CBS/Fox and Warner are **video distributors**. And in the ZZAP! poll, *Aliens* sits above *Blade Runner*, *Ferris Bueller's Day Off* and *Beverly Hills Cop II* — a list of films, not games.

The extractor now drops any chart page carrying two video distributors or a "MY FAVOURITE" heading, and *Aliens* falls back to the two rows above.

## Why the correction is kept

Because the failure is instructive and the original error was confident. The page reasoned carefully from bad data to a wrong conclusion, and nothing about the reasoning would have revealed it — the numbers were real, the dates were real, and only the **medium** was wrong.

A title string carries no medium, no platform and no version. Everything else in this Vault that reads a chart position now depends on that being checked first.
