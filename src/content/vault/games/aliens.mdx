## ⚠ Not every *Aliens* chart row is a games chart

*Aliens* also appears at **#1** in *CRASH* in August 1988 and in *ZZAP!64* in November 1988 — a year after the game's run had ended, and neither figure belongs to the game.

**Both are a different medium.** *CRASH* printed a **video rental chart** — films on VHS. *ZZAP!64* ran a readers' poll page stacking "MY FAVOURITE PIECE" (music), "MY FAVOURITE ARCADE" (coin-ops) and "MY FAVOURITE VIDEO" (films). Both look like games charts at a glance: a title, a rank, a run of consecutive numbers.

The giveaway is in the rows themselves:

```
1 (1)  PLATOON               RCA/COLUMBIA
2 (3)  ALIENS                CBS/FOX
3 ( )  LETHAL WEAPON         WARNER
```

RCA/Columbia, CBS/Fox and Warner are **video distributors**. And in the *ZZAP!64* poll, *Aliens* sits above *Blade Runner*, *Ferris Bueller's Day Off* and *Beverly Hills Cop II* — a list of films.

The wider lesson holds for every film and arcade licence of the period. A title string carries no medium, no platform and no version, and a magazine that reviewed games also printed charts for films, music and coin-ops. Check what is being ranked before reading a position.
