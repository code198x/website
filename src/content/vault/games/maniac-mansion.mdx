*Maniac Mansion* rescued adventure games from parser frustration. Ron Gilbert and Gary Winnick created SCUMM—a verb-based interface that let players click instead of type. Three teenagers infiltrating a mad scientist's mansion could die (unlike later LucasArts games), but the puzzles were fair and the humour was sharp.
