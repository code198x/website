## Overview

*Super Metroid* opens with one of gaming's great introductions: "The last Metroid is in captivity. The galaxy is at peace." Minutes later, Ridley steals the infant Metroid, and Samus pursues to planet Zebes. What follows is exploration-driven action perfected—interconnected areas unlocked through ability acquisition, atmospheric isolation, and a final sequence that remains emotionally powerful decades later.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Nintendo R&D1 / Intelligent Systems.
- **Director:** Yoshio Sakamoto.
- **Platform:** Super Nintendo.
- **Map size:** massive interconnected world.
- **Abilities:** morph ball, grapple beam, super missiles, and more.
- **Influence:** defined the Metroidvania genre.

## Design perfection

Why it endures:
- **Guided freedom:** subtle environmental cues suggest paths.
- **Ability gating:** new powers open new areas.
- **Atmosphere:** isolation through visual and audio design.
- **Sequence breaking:** skilled players find shortcuts.

## The ending

Emotional impact:
- **Baby Metroid:** returns to assist Samus.
- **Sacrifice:** creature dies saving the player.
- **Mother Brain:** final confrontation with series nemesis.
- **Escape:** timed exit as planet explodes.
