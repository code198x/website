## Overview

Drive in, rescue soldiers, drive out. Jackal combined vehicular combat with rescue missions as players navigated jeeps through hostile territory. Free prisoners from camps, load them into your jeep, deliver them to helicopters, and upgrade your weapons. Two-player cooperative play doubled the chaos and the firepower.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Konami.
- **Also known as:** Top Gunner (US arcade).
- **Players:** Two simultaneous.
- **Style:** Vertical scrolling shooter.
