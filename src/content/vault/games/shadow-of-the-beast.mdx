## Overview

*Shadow of the Beast* was designed to sell Amigas. Its deep parallax scrolling, ethereal soundtrack by David Whittaker and alien landscapes made other systems look primitive. That the gameplay was brutally difficult and sometimes unfair mattered less than the technical showcase it provided.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Reflections. (The studio was credited as Reflections in 1989; "Reflections Interactive" is a later name.)
- **Publisher:** Psygnosis.
- **Programmer:** Paul Howarth. **Design and art:** Martin Edmondson, who is often miscredited as the programmer.
- **Artist:** Roger Dean (cover), internal team.

## Technical showcase

| Feature | Achievement |

| Parallax layers | 12 or 13 in the outdoor sections, depending on what you count as a layer. Psygnosis marketed 13; technical breakdowns since have counted 12 |
| Colour palette | Up to 128 on screen |
