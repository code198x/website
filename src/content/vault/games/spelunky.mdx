One more run. Derek Yu's Spelunky translated roguelike principles into platform action—procedurally generated caves, permadeath, and systemic interactions, but requiring reflexes alongside strategy. The 2008 freeware release inspired countless imitators; the 2012 remake polished it to brilliance. Every death teaches; every run offers new combinations. The roguelite explosion that followed owes its existence to Spelunky's proof of concept.
