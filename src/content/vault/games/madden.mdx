## Overview

*Madden NFL* began in 1988 with John Madden's insistence on realistic 11-vs-11 gameplay despite hardware limitations. The franchise grew into annual releases that became American football's video game representation. Through exclusive NFL licensing, strategic depth, and consistent refinement, *Madden* became both a gaming institution and a cultural touchstone.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** EA Tiburon (and predecessors).
- **Publisher:** Electronic Arts.
- **Named for:** John Madden, legendary coach and broadcaster.
- **First release:** *John Madden Football* (1988).
- **NFL exclusivity:** exclusive licence since 2004.
- **Sales:** among the best-selling annual franchises.

## John Madden's influence

The namesake's demands:
- **11-on-11:** insisted on full team simulation.
- **Realism:** accurate football strategy.
- **Authenticity:** NFL teams, players, playbooks.
- **Commentary:** voice work until the 2000s.

## Annual tradition

The Madden cycle:
- **August release:** coinciding with NFL season start.
- **Cover athlete:** "Madden Curse" superstition.
- **Roster updates:** reflect real-world trades.
- **Ultimate Team:** modern microtransaction mode.
