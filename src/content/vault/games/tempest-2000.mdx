Commissioned by Atari, *Tempest 2000* updates the 1981 arcade game with polygonal webs, particle storms, and a techno soundtrack. Jeff Minter layered in bonus modes and power-ups while preserving the original’s frantic, tube-based shooting.

## Fast facts
- **Original platform:** Atari Jaguar, later ported to PC, Saturn, PlayStation, and modern collections.  
- **Soundtrack:** composed with Yak’s signature trance influences; often cited as one of the Jaguar’s best.  
- **Legacy:** set the tone for later Llamasoft revivals like *TxK* and *Polybius*.
