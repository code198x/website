Capcom's original mascot got his own game. Captain Commando led an unlikely squad through Metro City's criminal underworld in classic beat-em-up style. The four-player action, rideable mechs, and eclectic character roster made it memorable, even as it refined rather than revolutionised the Final Fight template.
