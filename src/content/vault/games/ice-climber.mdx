## Overview

Climb the mountain. Break the ice. Ice Climber scrolled vertically as players hammered through ice platforms from below, racing upward while avoiding polar bears, birds, and the ever-present danger of falling off the bottom of the screen. Two-player cooperative mode let Popo and Nana climb together—or sabotage each other.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Nintendo R&D1.
- **Players:** One or two simultaneous.
- **Characters:** Popo (blue), Nana (pink).
- **Smash Bros:** Playable fighters.
