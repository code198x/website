Be the monster. Rampage inverted the typical dynamic—instead of fighting creatures, players became them. George the gorilla, Lizzie the lizard, and Ralph the wolf climbed buildings, smashed windows, ate civilians, and reduced cities to rubble. Three-player simultaneous action turned destruction into chaotic cooperative fun.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Bally Midway.
- **Players:** Up to three simultaneous.
- **Monsters:** George, Lizzie, Ralph.
- **Goal:** Destroy every building.
