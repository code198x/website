Beyond Good & Evil (2003), designed by Michel Ancel at Ubisoft, is an action-adventure game following photojournalist Jade as she uncovers a conspiracy on the alien-occupied planet Hillys. Despite critical acclaim, it sold poorly at launch but developed a devoted following.

## Distinctive Features

- **Photography mechanic:** Documenting wildlife and evidence
- **Varied gameplay:** Combat, stealth, hovercraft racing, puzzles
- **Strong protagonist:** Jade became an iconic female lead
- **Art style:** Unique blend of sci-fi and fantasy aesthetics

## Cultural impact

The game's cult status led to:
- Multiple re-releases and HD remasters
- A long-delayed sequel (Beyond Good & Evil 2)
- Recognition as an important work of video game art
