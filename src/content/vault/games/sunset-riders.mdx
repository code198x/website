## Overview

Konami took their run-and-gun expertise west. Sunset Riders delivered four-player arcade action across frontier settings—saloons, stampedes, trains, and mining towns. The colourful Western aesthetic and satisfying shooting made it an arcade favourite, while home conversions brought the bounty hunting to living rooms.
