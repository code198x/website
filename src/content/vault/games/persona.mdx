I am thou, thou art I. Persona asked what happens when teenagers confront their shadow selves—the Jungian concept made literal through demon summoning. The series evolved from cult SMT spin-off to cultural phenomenon; Persona 3 introduced social links that made relationships mechanically meaningful; Persona 5's stylish rebellion captured mainstream attention. Identity, friendship, and facing inner darkness define the franchise.
