*Battletoads* answered the question: what if the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles were amphibians, and their game was impossibly hard? Rare created a technical showcase with varied levels, impressive graphics, and difficulty that traumatised a generation. The Turbo Tunnel remains gaming's most infamous obstacle course.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Rare](/vault/companies/rare).
- **Publisher:** Tradewest.
- **Platforms:** NES (original), multiple ports.
- **Difficulty:** Legendary.

## The Turbo Tunnel

Level 3's speeder bikes:
- Memorisation required
- Instant death obstacles
- Increasing speed
- Two-player nearly impossible

This level alone defined the game's reputation.
