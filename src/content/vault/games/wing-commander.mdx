Chris Roberts didn't just want players to fly spaceships—he wanted them to live a space opera. Wing Commander wrapped intense dogfighting in branching narratives, wingman relationships, and cinematic presentation that made players feel like action movie protagonists. The formula spawned sequels with ever-increasing production values.
