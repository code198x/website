## Overview

*Track & Field* asked a simple question: how fast can you press buttons? Konami's Olympic-style game turned athletics into endurance tests of button mashing. Arcades installed metal plates to handle the abuse. Friendships were forged and destroyed over split-second victories in the 100-metre dash.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Konami.
- **Release:** 1983.
- **Controls:** Run buttons + action.
- **Damage:** Notorious joystick destroyer.
