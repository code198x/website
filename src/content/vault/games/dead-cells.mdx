In *Dead Cells*, the thing you control can survive the loss of its body. The body cannot. A failed attempt sends you back to the island's dungeon to begin another journey, but permanent unlocks can give that journey different possibilities.

Motion Twin described this mixture as “RogueVania”: the repeated attempts of a [roguelike](/vault/genres/roguelike), combined with the gradually opened routes of a [Metroidvania](/vault/genres/metroidvania). The name describes the combination. It need not imply that *Dead Cells* invented every part of it.

The game was publicly playable in Early Access in 2017, before its full release in August 2018. Its original developer was Motion Twin, the French cooperative; Evil Empire later collaborated on its continuing content.

## Surviving the next fight

Combat depends on positioning and recognising enemy attacks. In his launch advice, producer Steve Filby explained that rolling lets the character pass through an enemy and avoid damage during the roll. There is a recovery period before another roll, so repeatedly pressing the button does not provide continuous protection.

The player also equips weapons and separate skills such as traps, turrets and grenades. These skills recharge after use. Filby emphasised learning attack timings and choosing when to fight; charging into every enemy is not a requirement.

That responsiveness was developed through changes, rather than a universal absence of animation restrictions. The first major update's notes, for example, credit a community suggestion for allowing active skills to interrupt weapon attacks, including throwing a grenade during a broadsword attack.

## What persists

Permanent abilities open new routes, letting later attempts take different paths through the island. Unlocking more equipment and abilities expands the available choices. This differs from carrying the entire state of a defeated character into the next run.

Death is also no longer an unchangeable rule for every player. The 2022 accessibility update introduced Assist Mode, including an option to restart from the beginning of the current area after dying. It also added adjustable enemy and trap settings. The standard run structure and the optional assistance should be described separately.

## Content and maintenance

Post-release additions included the free *Rise of the Giant* and paid expansions such as *The Bad Seed*, *Fatal Falls*, *The Queen and the Sea* and *Return to Castlevania*. Motion Twin's February 2024 announcement credited five years of collaboration with Evil Empire and identified update 35 as the end of new content development.

That update, *The End is Near*, arrived in August 2024 with additions centred on curses and further accessibility options. Maintenance continued: the official changelog records version 35.9 on 15 June 2026, with crash fixes, font corrections and Linux controller work. Ending major content additions did not mean that all updates stopped.
