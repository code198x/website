## Overview

Will Wright wanted to build cities, not destroy them. *SimCity* emerged from a level editor for a different game and became a genre. Players zoned land, laid infrastructure, and watched cities grow organically—or crumble under mismanagement. There was no winning, only the satisfaction of creation.
