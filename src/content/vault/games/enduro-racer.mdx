## Why an arcade name was not enough

*Enduro Racer* had the thing *Cobra* had — a recognisable name from outside the home — and it behaved completely differently, which is the useful comparison.

A **film** licence has a fixed window: the film is in cinemas, then it is not, and the name stops working. An **arcade** licence does not decay the same way, because the cabinet stays in the arcade being played, advertising the home version continuously and for free, for as long as it earns its keep.

That is why arcade conversions dominate the British charts of these years and why publishers paid so much for them. The marketing was already installed in every seaside town in the country.
