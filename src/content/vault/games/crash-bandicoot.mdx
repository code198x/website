## Overview

Sony needed a mascot. Naughty Dog delivered a genetically enhanced bandicoot who spun through crates. Crash Bandicoot's corridor-style 3D platforming solved camera problems that plagued other 3D platformers, offering precise jumping challenges in linear stages packed with collectibles and secrets.
