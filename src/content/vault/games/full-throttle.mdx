Ben leads the Polecats. Someone killed Malcolm Corley. Ben's been framed. Tim Schafer's biker adventure traded puzzle density for cinematic style, creating a shorter but more atmospheric experience. The dusty highways, rumbling engines, and Gone Jackals soundtrack defined a world where being cool mattered as much as being clever.
