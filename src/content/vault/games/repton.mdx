## Overview

*Repton* emerged from the BBC Micro scene as a *Boulder Dash*-style puzzle game with distinctive character. The reptilian protagonist navigated caves, collecting diamonds while avoiding falling rocks and eggs that hatched into monsters. The series' level editor encouraged user-created content, and its sequels (*Repton 2*, *Repton 3*, *Around the World in 40 Screens*) became BBC Micro staples.
