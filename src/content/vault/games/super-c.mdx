## Overview

The aliens returned. Super C (Super Contra in arcades) picked up where Contra left off with more weapons, new overhead stages, and the same brutal difficulty. Two players fought through alien-infested military bases, coordinating fire and sharing limited lives. The Konami Code still helped—you'd need it.
