## Overview

The Force is strong with this one. Knights of the Old Republic set its story four thousand years before the films, freeing BioWare from movie canon to tell an original tale. The light side/dark side morality system let players genuinely roleplay—help the oppressed or crush them, redeem fallen Jedi or corrupt the innocent. The twist revelation rivals anything in the films themselves.
