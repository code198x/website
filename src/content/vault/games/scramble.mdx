Before *Gradius*, before *R-Type*, there was *Scramble*. Konami's 1981 arcade game established side-scrolling shooter conventions: horizontally scrolling terrain, bombs for ground targets, lasers for air threats, and fuel management adding strategic depth. Every side-scroller that followed owes it a debt.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Konami.
- **Release:** 1981.
- **Genre:** Side-scrolling shooter.
- **Innovation:** First side-scrolling shooter with forced scrolling.

## Core mechanics

| Element | Function |
|---------|----------|
| Laser | Destroy air targets |
| Bombs | Destroy ground targets |
| Fuel | Depletes constantly |
| Terrain | Collision kills |

## Fuel system

Revolutionary resource management:
- Fuel depletes continuously
- Destroy fuel tanks to refill
- Adds urgency beyond enemies
- Strategic target prioritisation
