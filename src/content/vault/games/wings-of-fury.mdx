## Overview

Launch from the carrier. Attack the island. Return and land. Wings of Fury distilled Pacific War carrier aviation into intense action—dive-bomb enemy bunkers, strafe ground troops, dogfight Japanese fighters, then nail the carrier landing. Brøderbund's flight combat achieved an addictive balance between simulation and arcade action.
