*Renegade*'s flatness is what a game looks like when it has neither a launch spike to burn off nor a hit single to bounce back on: **steady demand, met steadily**, for most of a year.

## What arrived with it

*Renegade* is where the **beat-em-up** reaches the British home charts in force — not a martial-arts tournament like *International Karate*, but street fighting, on a scrolling city street, against a gang.

The conversion problem is the one [Andrew Deakin](/vault/people/andrew-deakin)'s *Operation Wolf* entry describes: arcade hardware throwing many large animated sprites at once, reproduced on a machine with no sprites at all and one colour pair per character cell. Lamb's answer was the one Ocean's Spectrum team generally took — keep the pace, accept the palette, and make sure the thing feels right under the hands.
