## Objects with weight

*Fairlight* is in the family [Ultimate](/vault/companies/ultimate)'s *Knight Lore* opened, and its contribution is **physical objects**.

Things have weight. They can be stacked, pushed, carried in limited quantity and dropped where they will be useful later. A door you cannot reach is solved by finding something to stand on — not by finding the key that the designer decided opens it.

That is a different kind of puzzle from almost everything around it in 1985. A key-and-lock puzzle has one solution and the designer knows it. **A physics puzzle has as many solutions as the objects allow**, and the player gets credit for the one they found.

Getting that onto a 48K Spectrum means describing rooms as geometry and objects as things with properties, then drawing filled, overlapping, correctly ordered solids fast enough to play — in a display with [attribute colour constraints](/vault/techniques/colour-clash). It is a rendering problem, and one that later industries solved with dedicated hardware.

## Written in Sweden

Worth restating because the 8-bit years get narrated as a national story: *Fairlight* is a Swedish programmer's game, published from Covent Garden, reviewed as British, and scored 96% by a magazine in Shropshire.
