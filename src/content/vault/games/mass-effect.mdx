## Overview

Your Shepard, your story. Mass Effect created an original science fiction universe dense with alien cultures, political intrigue, and existential threat. The conversation wheel let players craft Shepard's personality through moment-to-moment choices. Romance options, squad loyalty, and moral decisions persisted across three games—your Mass Effect 1 choices echoed in Mass Effect 3's finale.
