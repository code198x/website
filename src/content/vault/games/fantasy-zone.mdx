Not all shooters need to be dark. Fantasy Zone sent the sentient spaceship Opa-Opa through candy-coloured worlds, destroying enemy bases and shopping for weapon upgrades. The bright palette and whimsical enemies created a shooter anyone could enjoy, establishing the "cute-em-up" aesthetic that would influence countless Japanese games.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Sega.
- **Protagonist:** Opa-Opa (spaceship).
- **Innovation:** Shop system in shooters.
