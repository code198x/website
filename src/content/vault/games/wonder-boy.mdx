## Overview

Tom-Tom races to save his girlfriend Tina. Wonder Boy introduced a vitality system that constantly drained—stop collecting fruit and you'd die of hunger. Grab a skateboard for speed, throw axes at enemies, and navigate hazards across vibrant jungle landscapes. The series would evolve through many forms, but this original defined momentum-based platforming.
