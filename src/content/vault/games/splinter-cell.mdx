## Overview

Hide in the shadows, strike from darkness. Splinter Cell's light meter made visibility a quantifiable resource. Sam Fisher's tri-focal goggles became iconic. The tension of waiting in darkness while guards passed inches away defined a generation of stealth gameplay. Ubisoft's answer to Metal Gear Solid found its own identity through tactical precision.
