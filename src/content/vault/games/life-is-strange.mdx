## Overview

*Life is Strange* gave players power over time and asked them to consider consequences. Max Caulfield could rewind moments, exploring different dialogue choices before committing. The episodic structure built tension over months as players discussed theories and dreaded outcomes. Its emotional storytelling resonated particularly with audiences underserved by mainstream gaming.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Dontnod Entertainment.
- **Publisher:** Square Enix.
- **Format:** Five episodes.
- **Setting:** Arcadia Bay, Oregon.
- **Protagonist:** Max Caulfield.
