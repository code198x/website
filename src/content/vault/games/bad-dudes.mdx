## Overview

"The President has been kidnapped by ninjas. Are you a bad enough dude to rescue the President?" Data East's arcade brawler combined side-scrolling combat with that unforgettable premise. Players fought through waves of colour-coded ninjas, climbed trucks, and proved their badness across multiple stages of urban mayhem.
