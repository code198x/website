## Overview

Football as it should feel. PES (evolved from International Superstar Soccer) made licensing secondary to gameplay. Real player names were fake, but the weight of passes, the timing of through balls, the satisfaction of a well-worked goal—these felt authentic. While FIFA dominated commercially, PES earned devotion from players who prioritised substance over presentation.
