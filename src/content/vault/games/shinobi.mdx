Joe Musashi is Shinobi. The criminal organisation Zeed has kidnapped the children of the Oboro ninja clan. Across five missions, Shinobi combined precise platforming with varied weaponry—shurikens for distance, sword for close combat, and devastating ninja magic that obliterated everything on screen.
