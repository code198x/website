Jason's frog fell down a hole. Following it, he found a tank called SOPHIA III. Blaster Master mixed vehicular side-scrolling with top-down dungeon exploration—drive the tank through large areas, then exit on foot to explore caves and battle bosses. The bizarre premise belied a carefully crafted action adventure.
