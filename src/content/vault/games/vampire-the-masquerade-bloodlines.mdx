A tragedy in three acts: brilliant design, disastrous launch, fan resurrection. Troika's final game captured the World of Darkness perfectly—Los Angeles as vampire playground, clan politics, the eternal Masquerade hiding monsters among mortals. The Malkavian playthrough alone (where your insanity grants prophetic dialogue) demonstrated unmatched writing ambition. Community patches eventually fixed what Activision's rushed release broke.
