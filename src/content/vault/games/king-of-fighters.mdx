SNK's crossover became its flagship. King of Fighters gathered characters from Fatal Fury, Art of Fighting, and Ikari Warriors into three-on-three team battles. Annual releases refined the formula while advancing an ongoing storyline. The series developed its own identity—faster, more technical, with a devoted following that sustained SNK through corporate upheaval.
