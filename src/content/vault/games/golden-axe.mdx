## Overview

*Golden Axe* transported beat-em-up action to a Conan-inspired fantasy world. Three warriors sought revenge against Death Adder, hacking through hordes of enemies while riding fire-breathing dragons and casting devastating magic. Sega's arcade hit became a Genesis system seller.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Sega (Team Shinobi).

- **Genre:** Fantasy beat-em-up.
- **Players:** 1-2 cooperative.
