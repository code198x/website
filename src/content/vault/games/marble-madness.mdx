## Overview

*Marble Madness* asked a simple question: can you roll a marble down a hill? The answer was much harder than it seemed. Mark Cerny's isometric courses featured narrow paths, aggressive enemies, and devious obstacles. The trackball controls required delicate precision. Two players could race simultaneously.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Atari Games.
- **Designer:** Mark Cerny.
- **Release:** 1984.
- **Innovation:** First game with FM synthesis audio.
