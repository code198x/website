The impossible transition made real. Ocarina of Time took Zelda's top-down dungeons and open exploration into three dimensions without losing what made the series special. Z-targeting solved 3D combat. Context-sensitive buttons handled complex interactions simply. The result wasn't just a good Zelda game—it redefined what 3D action-adventure could be.
