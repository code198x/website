*Alien Breed* made no secret of its inspiration. Ripley's nightmare became a two-player cooperative shooter where ammunition was scarce, aliens were plentiful, and self-destruct countdowns added urgency. Team17's demoscene roots showed in the polished presentation that became their trademark.
