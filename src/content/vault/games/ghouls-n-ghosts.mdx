## Overview

Sir Arthur faces the demon realm again. Ghouls 'n Ghosts expanded on its predecessor with smoother controls, more weapons, and vertical attacks. The difficulty remained brutal—two hits and you're down to underwear, two more and you're dead. And completing the game once? That only earns a trip back to the start to do it all again.
