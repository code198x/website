"Rise from your grave!" Zeus resurrects a fallen Roman centurion to rescue his daughter Athena from the demon god Neff. Altered Beast packed visual spectacle into its transformation mechanic—collect enough power-ups and morph into a werewolf, dragon, bear, or tiger. It defined the Genesis launch and introduced a generation to 16-bit gaming.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Sega.
