When Obsidian Entertainment launched a Kickstarter for a spiritual successor to *Baldur's Gate*, backers pledged $4 million. *Pillars of Eternity* delivered on the promise: an original fantasy world with deep lore, party-based combat, meaningful choices, and walls of text. The CRPG genre, seemingly extinct, proved it had only been dormant.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** [Obsidian Entertainment](/vault/companies/obsidian-entertainment).
- **Publisher:** Paradox Interactive.
- **Director:** Josh Sawyer.
- **Crowdfunding:** $4.16 million via Kickstarter (2012).
- **Engine:** Unity (custom isometric).
- **Sequel:** *Pillars of Eternity II: Deadfire* (2018).

## Design philosophy

Honouring the classics:
- **Isometric perspective:** top-down party management.
- **Real-time with pause:** *Baldur's Gate* combat model.
- **Text-heavy:** extensive dialogue and lore.
- **Choices matter:** decisions affect world and endings.

## The setting

Eora's unique elements:
- **Souls:** measurable, transferable spiritual essence.
- **Reincarnation:** souls return in new bodies.
- **Gods:** demonstrably real, actively interventionist.
- **Colonial themes:** exploration and exploitation.
