Thief meets superpowers. Dishonored gave players Corvo Attano's revenge quest alongside the Outsider's supernatural gifts—Blink teleportation, possession, time manipulation. Each assassination target offered lethal and non-lethal options. The chaos system tracked violence, affecting world state and ending. Arkane proved immersive sims still had commercial potential.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Arkane Studios.
- **Directors:** Raphael Colantonio, Harvey Smith.
- **Year:** 2012.
- **Setting:** Dunwall (steampunk empire).
