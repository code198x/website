## Overview

BARF! River City Ransom broke beat-em-up conventions by adding RPG elements—defeated enemies dropped coins, shops sold food that increased stats, and books taught new fighting techniques. Alex and Ryan fought through River City's gangs with fists, weapons, and eventually, purchased martial arts mastery.
