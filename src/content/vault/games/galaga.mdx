*Galaga* took *Galaxian*'s foundation and built something timeless. Aliens swooped in formation patterns that demanded memorisation. The Boss Galaga could capture your ship—but reclaim it and you'd fight with double firepower. This risk/reward mechanic elevated simple shooting into strategic depth.
