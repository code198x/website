## Overview

"Can't let you do that, Star Fox." Nintendo's space combat game featured full voice acting—a rarity for the era—and every line became memorable. The Rumble Pak launched alongside it, adding physical feedback to explosions and hits. Branching paths based on performance gave replay value. From Corneria to Venom, the Arwing's journey remained thrilling through countless playthroughs.
