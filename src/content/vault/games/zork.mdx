## Overview

Before graphics dominated gaming, *Zork* proved that words alone could create compelling worlds. Its parser understood complex sentences. Its puzzles demanded lateral thinking. Its humour—"It is pitch black. You are likely to be eaten by a grue"—entered gaming culture. Infocom built an empire on its foundation.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Infocom.
- **Original authors:** Marc Blank, Dave Lebling, et al.
- **Origin:** MIT mainframe (late 1970s).
- **Commercial release:** 1980.
