## Overview

He's on fire! NBA Jam rejected simulation for spectacle. Two-on-two basketball with no fouls, impossible acrobatics, and players literally catching fire after three consecutive baskets. Tim Kitzrow's commentary—"Boomshakalaka!", "From downtown!"—became cultural shorthand. The game proved sports games could be pure entertainment without pretending to be realistic.
