## Overview

Hit the turbo. Watch the temperature. Excitebike delivered satisfying motocross racing with a critical resource management twist—overuse the turbo and your engine overheats, forcing a trackside cooldown. The included track editor let players design their own courses, pioneering user-generated content on the NES.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Nintendo R&D1.
- **Designer:** Shigeru Miyamoto.
- **Innovation:** Built-in track editor.
