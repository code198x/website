## Overview

She could run, jump, climb, and shoot—all in full 3D. Lara Croft became gaming's first mainstream icon, but Tomb Raider succeeded because of its gameplay: vast underground spaces to explore, environmental puzzles to solve, and platforming that rewarded precision. Core Design created a template that launched a franchise and countless imitators.
