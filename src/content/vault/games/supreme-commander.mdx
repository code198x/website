## Overview

See everything. Supreme Commander's strategic zoom transformed RTS interface design—one mousewheel scroll took players from watching a single unit to viewing the entire battlefield as icons. Chris Taylor's vision of grand-scale warfare finally had the technology to match. Thousand-unit battles, experimental superweapons, and multi-front conflicts demanded the macro-level perspective that zoom provided.
