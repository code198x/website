## Overview

Radio-controlled car racing with weapons. R.C. Pro-Am presented isometric tracks where tiny vehicles competed for position while collecting missiles, bombs, and speed upgrades. Rare's technical mastery created smooth diagonal scrolling and detailed sprites that pushed the NES hardware, establishing patterns they'd refine in future classics.

## Fast facts

- **Developer:** Rare.
- **Perspective:** Isometric.
- **Innovation:** Combat racing on NES.
- **Technical:** Impressive NES graphics.
