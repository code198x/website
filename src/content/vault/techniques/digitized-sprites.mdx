## Overview

Real people in your game. Digitised sprites photographed actors performing moves, then converted those images into game graphics. The result looked startlingly real compared to hand-drawn sprites—and distinctly uncanny. Mortal Kombat made the technique famous; the violence looked more visceral with realistic figures. The style faded as 3D graphics offered better realism.

## Fast facts

- **Peak era:** 1990-1995.
- **Notable use:** Mortal Kombat series.
- **Process:** Photo capture to pixels.
- **Successor:** 3D polygonal graphics.
