## Overview

The art of not being seen. Stealth mechanics invert traditional action game power dynamics—enemies become threats to avoid rather than obstacles to destroy. From Castle Wolfenstein's guard evasion to Thief's light gem, designers developed increasingly sophisticated systems for modelling awareness. The genre peaked technically with Splinter Cell's light meter and Hitman's social stealth, creating gameplay where patience and observation outweigh twitch reflexes.

## Fast facts

- **Origins:** Castle Wolfenstein (1981).
- **Breakthrough:** Metal Gear Solid (1998).
- **Refinement:** Thief, Splinter Cell series.
- **Core loop:** Observe, plan, execute.
