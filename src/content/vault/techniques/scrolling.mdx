## Overview

Single screens limited game worlds. Scrolling broke that barrier, letting players explore environments larger than the display. Different platforms handled scrolling differently—some with dedicated hardware, others requiring clever programming. The technique became fundamental to platformers, shooters, and countless other genres.

## Fast facts

- **Purpose:** Display worlds larger than screen.
- **Methods:** Hardware or software.
- **Directions:** Horizontal, vertical, multi-directional.
