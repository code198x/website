## Overview

Between composer and chip sat the driver. Sound drivers translated music data into hardware commands, managing the complex timing of audio chips. Each driver had personality—some prioritised memory efficiency, others sound quality, others ease of composition. Famous drivers like the one Rob Hubbard wrote for C64 became as celebrated as the music itself.

## Fast facts

- **Purpose:** Control audio hardware.
- **Function:** Play music and sound effects.
- **Variety:** Platform and composer-specific.
- **Legacy:** Defined platform sound.
