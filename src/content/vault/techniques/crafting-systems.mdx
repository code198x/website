## Overview

Gather, combine, create. Crafting systems transform collected resources into useful items through recipes or freeform combination. Minecraft's grid-based crafting spawned an entire genre; survival games made crafting essential to progression. The mechanic creates satisfying loops—exploration finds resources, crafting creates tools, better tools enable deeper exploration.

## Fast facts

- **Popularised by:** Minecraft (2011).
- **Core loop:** Gather → Craft → Use.
- **Appeal:** Creative agency.
- **Risk:** Inventory management tedium.
