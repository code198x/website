If Datastorm is the Amiga's loyalist party, X is the Commodore 64's. Held in the Netherlands and running since the mid-1990s, X is the largest and most prestigious C64-only event — a biennial gathering where the 8-bit scene's most ambitious demos are saved up and unveiled.

The C64 scene is famously, stubbornly alive, and X is its showcase. New productions premiered there routinely redefine what the 1982 machine is thought capable of, decades after Commodore stopped making it.

## Fast facts

- **First held:** mid-1990s, Netherlands.
- **Focus:** Commodore 64 only.
- **Cadence:** Biennial.
- **Status:** The flagship C64 party.

## Why it matters

X concentrates the C64 scene's best work into a single weekend, and the prestige of an X premiere pushes groups to hold back their strongest demos for it. The result is a steady stream of productions that keep extending the limits of forty-year-old hardware.
