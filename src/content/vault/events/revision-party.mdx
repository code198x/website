Breakpoint 2010 was the last of its series, and for the German demo scene that left a gap at Easter. Since the late 1990s the holiday weekend had meant a party: [Mekka & Symposium](/vault/events/mekka-symposium) in Fallingbostel until 2002, then Breakpoint in Bingen am Rhein. A team of experienced organisers decided that "Easter without a proper party wouldn't be what it's meant to be", and in April 2011 they opened Revision in Saarbrücken. They were careful about how they described it: "Revision isn't just Breakpoint with a different name or a successor. It's a completely new event." It has been held every Easter since.

## Fast facts

- **Held:** every Easter since 2011, from Good Friday to Easter Monday; online only in 2020, 2021 and 2022.
- **Where:** the E Werk, an events venue in Saarbrücken, the capital of the Saarland in south-west Germany.
- **Run by:** a team whose members had organised or were organising Breakpoint, Mekka & Symposium, the Ultimate Meeting, Rewired and 0a000h.
- **Competitions:** demos and intros for the PC and the Amiga, "oldskool" competitions for older machines such as the Commodore 64, music, graphics and more.

## A new Easter party

The first Revision ran from 22 to 25 April 2011. Its website promised "4 days of pure demoscene fun, competitions & international socializing", with competitions for the PC, the Amiga and the C64. Quite a few of the former Breakpoint team were involved, the organisers wrote, but "this is a new beginning!"

The PC demo competition was close. PandaCube's *Flux* won with 822 points, 22 ahead of Nuance's *Sommerville*, with *Frozen*, by [farbrausch](/vault/groups/farbrausch) and Rebels, third on 758. Ghostown and Loonies' *Human Traffic* won the Amiga demo competition, Bauknecht and The Electronic Knights' *States United* the C64 one, and Brain Control's *Pandora* the PC 64K intro competition. The "wild" competition, for anything that fitted no other category, went to *T42 - Tennis For Two 2011*.

## Winners and old machines

Revision has kept a place for the machines the scene grew up on. [The Black Lotus](/vault/groups/the-black-lotus), which had won the Amiga demo competition at Mekka & Symposium and at Breakpoint, won it at Revision too, with *Rift* in 2014 and *Eon*, written for the original Amiga chipset, in 2019. In 2014 [Red Sector Inc](/vault/groups/red-sector-inc)'s *10 Orbyte*, a Commodore 64 production that the group says was sent to and run on a tiny satellite, won the wild competition. In 2024 [Spaceballs](/vault/groups/spaceballs)' Amiga intro *Disiplin* came second in its competition by 16 points, 1,329 to 1,345.

The PC demo competition has gone to Fairlight and Cloudkicker's *five faces* in 2013, Cocoon's *Observatory* in 2014 and *ziphead*, a joint production by CNCD and [Fairlight](/vault/groups/fairlight), in 2015, among others.

## Online years

During the COVID-19 pandemic Revision moved online. Demozoo lists Revision Online 2020, "Welcome Home", and Revision Online 2021, "Together", and the 2022 party, "Satellites", held online from 15 to 18 April 2022, when Futuris's *Miyagi* won the PC demo competition. In 2023 the party's motto was "Back to the E-Werk".

## Revision 2026

Revision 2026, "The Power Within", ran from 3 to 6 April 2026 at the E Werk. Its website called it "the world's biggest pure Demoscene event" and invited people who could not travel to Saarbrücken to host a "satellite" event of their own. The PC demo competition went to [Razor 1911](/vault/groups/razor-1911)'s *Razor1911* with 1,621 points to 1,259 for Gaspode's *Golden Egg*, and the same demo topped the crowd favourite vote. Desire and The Twitch Elite's *Second Nature* won the Amiga demo competition with 1,795 points, and Otomata Labs' *Triplet* the oldskool demo competition. Farbrausch's 8K intro *fr-079: Zero Distance* came fifth in its competition.
