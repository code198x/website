## Overview

When Breakpoint ended in 2010, the scene needed a successor. Revision filled that role, launching in 2011 at Saarbrücken and maintaining the tradition of Germany's Easter demo party. Today it's considered the scene's most important annual gathering—where new productions premiere and old friendships renew.

## Fast facts

- **First event:** 2011.
- **Location:** Saarbrücken, Germany.
- **Timing:** Easter weekend.
- **Predecessor:** Breakpoint (2003-2010).

## Why Germany?

Germany has hosted major demo parties since the 1990s:
- Geographic centre of European scene
- Strong local scene presence
- Established organisational traditions
- Accessible from across Europe
