Mekka & Symposium — "MS" — was born in 1996 from the merger of two existing German parties, Mekka and Symposium, and ran each Easter through 2002. For those years it was a centre of gravity for the European scene, especially strong on the Amiga and PC sides.

Its real significance, though, is dynastic. When MS ended, the German Easter slot passed to Breakpoint (2003–2010), and when that closed, to Revision (2011 onward). Trace today's flagship Easter party back and the line runs through MS.

## Fast facts

- **Ran:** 1996–2002, Germany.
- **When:** Easter, annually.
- **Origin:** Merger of the Mekka and Symposium parties.
- **Successors:** Breakpoint, then Revision.

## Notable moments

MS sat at the hinge between the 16-bit scene's first generation and the modern PC-centred party circuit, carrying the Easter tradition through a period when many older parties were folding.
