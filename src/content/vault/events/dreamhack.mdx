## Overview

**DreamHack** is a Swedish digital festival that grew from a small LAN party in 1994 to become the world's largest, holding the Guinness World Record for most computers connected to a single LAN (22,810 in 2013). It combines LAN gaming, esports tournaments, demo competitions, and tech exhibitions into a massive multi-day event that defined the LAN party concept for a generation.

## Fast Facts

- **Founded:** 1994
- **Location:** Jönköping, Sweden (main)
- **Peak attendance:** 26,000+ participants
- **World record:** 22,810 connected computers
- **Evolution:** LAN party → esports festival
- **Global:** Now held in multiple countries
