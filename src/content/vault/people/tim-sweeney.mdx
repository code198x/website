## Overview

**Tim Sweeney** is the founder of Epic Games and creator of Unreal Engine, one of the game industry's most successful entrepreneurs. Starting with **ZZT** in 1991—a shareware game with a built-in level editor—Sweeney built Epic from a bedroom operation into a major force through technical excellence and understanding that user creation tools drive engagement.

## Fast Facts

- **Born:** 1970
- **Founded:** Epic MegaGames (1991)
- **First game:** ZZT (1991)
- **Major creation:** Unreal Engine
- **Current role:** Epic Games CEO
- **Net worth:** Billions

## The ZZT Beginning

Sweeney's first game revealed his philosophy:

| Feature | Significance |
|---------|-------------|
| **Level editor** | Users create content |
| **Simple tools** | Accessible creation |
| **Community** | Thousands of user levels |
| **Shareware** | Free distribution |

## Business Philosophy

Sweeney's consistent themes:

- **Tools matter** - Empower creators
- **Free drives paid** - Shareware to free engine
- **Technical excellence** - Best technology wins
- **Long-term thinking** - Build ecosystems

## Modern Epic

Under Sweeney's leadership:

- Unreal Engine licensing
- Fortnite's massive success
- Epic Games Store
- Industry acquisitions
- Legal battles (Apple)

## Legacy

Tim Sweeney's trajectory—from coding ZZT in his parents' house to running a company worth billions—represents the ultimate realisation of the shareware dream. His consistent philosophy of empowering creators through tools has shaped the modern game industry.
