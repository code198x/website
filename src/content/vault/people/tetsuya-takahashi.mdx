## Overview

Ambition incarnate. Tetsuya Takahashi's career is a continuous attempt to create the ultimate JRPG—philosophical depth, massive scale, and emotional resonance combined. Xenogears exceeded Square's resources; Xenosaga fragmented across publishers; Xenoblade Chronicles finally delivered the vision at scale. Each project pushed boundaries, accepting commercial risk for artistic ambition.

## Fast facts

- **Company:** Monolith Soft (founder/CEO).
- **Previous:** Square.
- **Signature series:** Xeno franchise.
- **Themes:** Philosophy, religion, identity.
