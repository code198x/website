**byuu** (later known as **Near**) was an emulator developer who revolutionised the field by proving that cycle-accurate emulation was both possible and practical. Their bsnes (later higan) emulator achieved near-perfect SNES emulation by documenting and implementing every hardware quirk, setting a new standard for accuracy that influenced the entire emulation community.

Tragically, Near died in 2021.

## Fast facts

- **Born:** 1983
- **Died:** 2021
- **Projects:** bsnes, higan, ares
- **Focus:** Cycle-accurate emulation
- **Philosophy:** Accuracy over speed
- **Legacy:** Changed emulation standards
