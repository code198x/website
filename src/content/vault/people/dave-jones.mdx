## Overview

A bedroom coder who built an industry. Dave Jones founded DMA Design in Dundee at 22, and made games that defined genres: *Lemmings* became a puzzle phenomenon, and *Grand Theft Auto* originated under his direction before he left. His career — Amiga demos to industry-shaping franchises — is the clearest single example of a British bedroom coder building something enormous.

## Fast facts

## Lemmings

Jones and artist [Mike Dailly](/vault/people/mike-dailly) made *Lemmings* out of an animation test. Dailly drew tiny walking figures; Jones saw a game in them. The puzzle mechanics — guiding creatures who will not stop walking, by changing the world, not by commanding them — came partly out of technical constraint, and turned into one of the era's most-copied designs.

## Grand Theft Auto origins

*GTA* began as "Race'n'Chase", a cops-and-robbers concept, and Jones directed the project through its change into an open-world crime game. He left DMA before *GTA III* moved the series into 3D, but the foundation was his.

## Dundee

Jones established Dundee as a development centre. DMA's presence drew talent, the University of Abertay launched Europe's first games degree, and studios including 4J and Ruffian grew from the same ground.
