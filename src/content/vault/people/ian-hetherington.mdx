**Ian Hetherington** was a director of [Imagine Software](/vault/companies/imagine-software)
and, after its collapse, a founder of [Psygnosis](/vault/companies/psygnosis) — the company
that carried Liverpool's games industry from the wreckage of the 8-bit era into the Amiga
years and eventually into Sony's.

*ACE* recorded the transition plainly at the end of 1987: Hetherington and
[David Lawson](/vault/people/david-lawson), former Imagine directors, were by then running a
16-bit software house in Psygnosis.

## The decision that mattered

Interviewed by *ACE* as company founder in September 1989, Hetherington described the point
at which Psygnosis stopped doing what everyone else did.

In the mid-eighties, he explained, a publisher could release on the Atari ST and the Amiga
simultaneously and ship near-identical products — because games were being **written down**
to stay ST-compatible. Psygnosis looked at the Amiga, liked what it saw, and decided to give
it special attention instead.

That is a commercial decision rather than a technical one, and it is the origin of the
company's reputation. Everything Psygnosis is remembered for visually — the parallax
showcases, the technical ambition, the premium £24.95 positioning — follows from choosing to
build for the better machine rather than the common denominator.

⚠ Worth noting what it cost. Writing for the Amiga specifically meant ST owners got a
lesser version rather than an equal one, which is the same trade-off in reverse.

## What he said about the early games

The same interview supplies dates the [Psygnosis](/vault/companies/psygnosis) entry had
wrong. *Brataccas*, the company's first game, reached the Atari ST **before the machine was
on sale in Britain**. *Barbarian* followed in the **summer of 1986**, on Amiga and ST at
once.

## Afterwards

Hetherington's career continued past Psygnosis's absorption into Sony. A 2002 retrospective
records him working with **Martin Kenwright** — formerly of the flight-simulation house
Digital Image Design — on a new studio, **Evolution Studios**, whose debut was the *World
Rally Championship* series.

⚠ That comes from a single non-English source and is recorded here as reported.
