## Overview

From Northern Ireland to gaming stardom. David Perry began programming on home computers, moved to the UK industry, then emigrated to America where he created some of the Mega Drive's most distinctive games. Earthworm Jim's irreverent humour and Aladdin's animation quality showcased his ambition. Later, he pioneered game streaming with Gaikai.

## Fast facts

- **Born:** 1967, Northern Ireland.
- **Companies:** Shiny Entertainment, Gaikai.
- **Known for:** Earthworm Jim, Aladdin.
- **Later work:** Game streaming pioneer.
