## What "known in the scene" looked like in 1991

The hobbyist-to-professional arc gets asserted far more often than it gets evidenced. For
Richter it is visible in the back pages of British Amiga magazines, two years
before Babylon 5 aired.

PD libraries advertising in *Amiga Format* through 1991 stocked his work by
name — "Agatron 8: Fab Tobias Richter pics!", "Agatron 6: Stunning ray traced
slides!", "Tobias Richter's Star Trek (2)", "AG.03 Star Trek (Agatron)... Tobias
Richter". Disks sold by post for well under a pound.

Being named in a PD catalogue advert, rather than just numbered in it, is what
reputation looked like on this platform: the libraries knew buyers would
recognise him. Note also the channel — in 1991 this circulated on physical disk
through commercial libraries, not on Aminet, which did not yet exist.
