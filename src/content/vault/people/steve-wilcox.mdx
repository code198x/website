## Overview

**Steve Wilcox** ran [Elite Systems](/vault/companies/elite-systems), which *8000 Plus*
introduced in 1986 as a house best known for a string of arcade hits on the Commodore and
Spectrum. Elite's business was licensed conversions, and it was good at it — its
*Ghosts 'n' Goblins* placed fifth in *CRASH*'s readers' vote for the best game of 1986.

## On a new machine, cautiously

Wilcox appears in *8000 Plus*'s first issue being asked whether Elite would support the
Amstrad PCW — the word-processor machine that had arrived with an unexpected games audience.

His answer is a good example of how a publisher of that period talked about risk. It was "**obviously an extension to the market**", he said, but they would have to **suck it and see**
— while confirming Elite had games lined up for the autumn, probably including *Bombjack* and
*Commando*.

Not enthusiasm, and not dismissal: a publisher committing two proven conversions to an
unproven platform and waiting to see what came back.
