**Rob Northen** was the British programmer who created **Copylock**, the dominant disk protection system for the Amiga and Atari ST throughout the late 1980s and early 1990s. His protection schemes were used on hundreds of commercial games, making him the primary antagonist in the cracking scene's eternal arms race. Northen's work represented the most sophisticated protection technology of the home computer era.

## Fast facts

- **Known for:** Copylock protection system
- **Company:** Rob Northen Computing
- **Platforms:** Amiga, Atari ST
- **Clients:** Hundreds of publishers
- **Era:** Late 1980s-1990s
- **Legacy:** Defined protection standards

## Technical approach

Copylock used multiple techniques:

| Technique | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Track timing** | Detect original disk |
| **Weak bits** | Unreproducible data |
| **Encryption** | Hide check code |
| **Anti-debugging** | Detect crackers |
