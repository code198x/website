## Overview

Al Lowe proved adult games could be clever. Before Leisure Suit Larry, he composed music and designed educational software for Sierra. The lounge lizard adventure combined genuine puzzle design with innuendo-laden comedy, finding an audience that appreciated both the humour and the challenge. His jazz background infused the series with distinctive audio.
