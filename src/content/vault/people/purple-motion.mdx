## Overview

Purple Motion — Jonne Valtonen, the handle a nod to Deep Purple — was the other half of [Future Crew](/vault/groups/future-crew)'s musical firepower alongside [Skaven](/vault/people/skaven). His tracker compositions, including the soundtrack to [Second Reality](/vault/demos/second-reality), are among the most fondly remembered the scene produced.

His career since is one of the demoscene's great glow-ups: Valtonen became a professional composer, arranger, and orchestrator, scoring and re-scoring video-game music for full orchestras at concerts around the world. The kid writing modules for a PC demo grew up to conduct his music in concert halls.

## Tracker to orchestra

Valtonen's path is the most dramatic illustration of how far scene music could carry someone — from four-channel modules squeezed into a demo to full symphonic arrangements performed live. The melodic instinct was always there; the canvas just kept getting bigger.
