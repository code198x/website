## Overview

While Ed Boon handled programming, John Tobias created Mortal Kombat's visual identity and mythology. His character designs—Scorpion, Sub-Zero, Raiden, Liu Kang—became icons. The fictional realms, tournaments, and backstories he developed gave the series narrative depth that sustained it beyond its initial shock value.
