David Wise joined Rare in 1985 and stayed for nearly 30 years, scoring dozens of games. His *Donkey Kong Country* soundtrack—ambient, melodic, and deeply atmospheric—elevated SNES audio to new heights. Wise's work combined memorable melodies with technical mastery, creating soundscapes that defined Rare's golden era.

## Fast facts

- **Start:** joined Rare at 18, initially as a musician and sound programmer.
- **Breakthrough:** *[Donkey Kong Country](/vault/games/donkey-kong-country)* (1994) brought worldwide recognition.
- **Technique:** pioneered use of sampled real instruments within hardware constraints.
- **Later work:** returned to compose *Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze* (2014).

## Atmospheric mastery

Wise's *DKC* work stands apart:
- **Ambient textures:** subtle environmental sounds layered with melody.
- **Emotional range:** from energetic mine cart themes to contemplative underwater passages.
- **Sample quality:** made the SNES sound richer than contemporaries.

## Technical approach

Working within tight memory limits:
- Created custom instruments from small samples.
- Used reverb and delay creatively.
- Composed around the hardware rather than against it.
