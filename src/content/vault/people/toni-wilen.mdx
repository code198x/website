## Overview

**Toni Wilen** is the Finnish programmer who has maintained WinUAE since 2000. His decades of dedicated work transformed the Amiga emulator into the definitive platform for running Amiga software, with comprehensive accuracy and feature support.

## Fast Facts

- **Nationality:** Finnish
- **Project:** WinUAE
- **Maintainer since:** 2000
- **Focus:** Amiga emulation
- **Status:** Actively developing
