## Overview
Rob Hewson is [Andrew Hewson](/vault/people/andrew-hewson)'s son, and the reason
[Hewson Consultants](/vault/companies/hewson-consultants) is a going concern
rather than only a historical entry.

He came to it from the modern industry rather than from nostalgia: before the
revival he was a game director at **TT Fusion**, the LEGO studio.

## What he does
- **Co-author** of *Hints & Tips for Videogame Pioneers* with his father, funded on Kickstarter in 2013 — the project that brought the Hewson name back.
- **Chief executive and creative director** of the reformed Hewson Consultants, when the book's success led to the games division being relaunched.
- **Huey Games**, his own studio, which has taken UK Games Fund backing and makes games openly modelled on the Hewson catalogue of the 1980s and early 1990s.

## Why this is in the Vault
Most of this Vault's British companies end. Hewson is the clearest case of one
that did not stay ended, and of the specific mechanism: a book about the period,
written with the people who were there, which turned out to be a viable business
proposition and pulled the label back with it.

It is also a genuine second generation. The 1980s British industry was young
enough that almost none of it has passed to anyone's children; here the son runs
the company, the father is chairman, and the games being made now are
deliberately in the line of *Uridium* and *Paradroid* rather than incidentally
so.
