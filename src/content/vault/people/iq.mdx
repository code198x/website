## Overview

iq — Íñigo Quílez — is the rare scener whose techniques escaped the scene entirely and reshaped how a generation thinks about real-time graphics. With [Rgba](/vault/groups/rgba) he made some of the most admired 4K intros ever, including [Elevated](/vault/demos/elevated), rendering whole lit landscapes from a few thousand bytes by raymarching signed distance fields.

He then took those ideas mainstream. Quílez co-created Shadertoy, the in-browser playground where countless people first learned shader programming, and has worked in film and graphics research. The maths he sharpened to fit a demo in 4 kilobytes now turns up in tutorials, tools, and production renderers everywhere.

## Why iq matters

Quílez is the clearest single example of demoscene technique becoming general knowledge. The size-coding discipline forced elegant, procedural solutions, and those solutions — distance fields, procedural texturing, compact lighting — proved far too useful to stay locked inside 4K intros.
