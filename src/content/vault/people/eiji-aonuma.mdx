## Overview

From Marvelous designer to Zelda steward. Aonuma joined Ocarina of Time's development and took over the series, directing Majora's Mask and Wind Waker before becoming producer. Under his guidance, Zelda experimented with art styles, mechanics, and structures while preserving the exploration and puzzle-solving that defined it.
