## Overview

The look of Nintendo's future. Imamura joined Nintendo as an artist and became the visual identity behind Star Fox and F-Zero. Fox McCloud, Captain Falcon, the Blue Falcon racer—his designs defined Nintendo's science fiction. He also contributed to Zelda as art director before retiring in 2021.
