## Overview

Designer, artist, community builder. Derek Yu's influence extends beyond Spelunky—he co-founded TIGSource, the forum that incubated 2000s indie gaming, and wrote "Spelunky" for Boss Fight Books, documenting game design thinking with unusual clarity. His pixel art background informed Spelunky's visual design; his design philosophy prioritised emergence and player creativity over authored experience.

## Fast facts

- **Education:** UCLA.
- **Background:** Pixel artist, comic creator.
- **Community:** TIGSource co-founder.
- **Book:** "Spelunky" (Boss Fight Books).
