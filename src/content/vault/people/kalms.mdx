Kalms — Mikael Kalms — is one of the coders behind [The Black Lotus](/vault/groups/the-black-lotus)'s reputation, with credits including [Ocean Machine](/vault/demos/ocean-machine) and a deep specialism in wringing performance from the Amiga. Coding the Atari ST and Amiga since 1990, he became known for exactly the kind of hardware-level optimisation the demoscene prized — and still gives talks on high-performance coding for the Amiga 500.

That craft transferred directly into industry. Kalms joined [DICE](/vault/companies/dice-studio) in 1998 as a graphics programmer working on software-rendering backends, and later co-founded the Swedish studio Fall Damage. His path is another clean line from Amiga demo coder to professional graphics engineer.
