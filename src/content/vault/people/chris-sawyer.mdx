One person, assembly language, millions of fans. Sawyer represented the vanishing figure of the solo developer creating commercial hits. Transport Tycoon demonstrated his simulation ambitions; RollerCoaster Tycoon perfected them. Writing nearly everything in assembly language, he achieved performance that let complex simulations run smoothly. His games sold tens of millions through quality, not marketing.
