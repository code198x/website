Eric Chahi spent two years creating *Another World* alone, handling programming, art, animation, and design. The result revolutionised game storytelling—no text, no HUD, just pure visual narrative. His rotoscoping technique and commitment to atmosphere over mechanics influenced generations of developers from *Flashback* to *Limbo*.

## Fast facts

- **Born:** 1967, France.
- **Key work:** *Another World* (1991).
- **Development time:** 2 years solo.
- **Tools:** Custom polygon animation system.

## Another World (1991)

| Aspect | Innovation |
|--------|------------|
| Visual style | Vector-based rotoscoped characters |
| Narrative | No dialogue, pure visual storytelling |
| Cinematics | Seamless cutscene integration |
| Atmosphere | Mood over gameplay mechanics |

### Development approach

Chahi created everything:
- Custom game engine
- Rotoscoped protagonist from video reference
- Polygon-based graphics for animation flexibility
- Sound design and music integration

### Technical innovation

The polygon system allowed:
- Smooth character animation
- Cinematic camera angles
- Memory-efficient storage
- Consistent visual style
