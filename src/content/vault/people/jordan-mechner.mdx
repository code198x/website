Jordan Mechner filmed his brother running and jumping, then traced the footage frame by frame. The result was *Prince of Persia*'s unprecedented animation fluidity. His earlier *Karateka* proved the concept; *Prince* perfected it. Mechner then largely left games for screenwriting before returning to oversee the franchise's evolution.

## Fast facts

- **Born:** 1964, New York, USA.
- **Education:** Yale University.
- **Key games:** [Karateka](/vault/games/karateka), [Prince of Persia](/vault/games/prince-of-persia).
