If the cracking scene had a press, Jeff Smart was one of its editors. He ran [Illegal](/vault/magazines/illegal), the Commodore 64 diskmag that, in association with [Triad](/vault/groups/triad), carried the scene's news, charts, rumours, and feuds — including the famous final-issue verdict that crowned [Ikari](/vault/groups/ikari) the world's best cracking group.

In a community that mostly spoke in scrolltexts, the person editing the magazine held real influence: they set the agenda, ran the polls, and shaped how the scene argued about itself. Jeff Smart was one of those people.

## Fast facts

- **Scene name:** Jeff Smart.
- **Known for:** Editing *Illegal*, the C64 cracking scene's leading diskmag.
- **Associated group:** [Triad](/vault/groups/triad).
- **Role:** Editor, chronicler, agenda-setter.

## The editor's influence

Crack intros announced; diskmags discussed. By steering *Illegal*, Jeff Smart sat at the centre of the cracking scene's self-understanding — and, unintentionally, became one of its most valuable archivists. The opinions and charts he published are now part of the historical record of how the scene actually worked.
