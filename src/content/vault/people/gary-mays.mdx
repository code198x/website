**Gary Mays** co-founded [PSS](/vault/companies/pss) in Coventry with
[Richard Cockayne](/vault/people/richard-cockayne). *Amstrad Action* visited them in March
1986 and identified Mays as co-founder and partner in the company.

## The name

The best thing Mays said to *Amstrad Action* was about his own company. Asked about the name
PSS, the magazine records him looking pained, sighing, and calling it **the company's first
and biggest mistake** — offering in mitigation that they had done their own early publicity.

That is an unusually candid thing for a founder to say to a magazine, and it is a fair
description of the problem. A house selling wargames and strategy titles had given itself a
set of initials that told a browsing customer nothing at all.

## What he thought of the competition

The same interview asks Mays what had impressed him lately, and his answer points across this
Vault: he named **[Gargoyle](/vault/companies/gargoyle-games)**'s work as the most impressive
thing he had seen recently.

Two Midlands houses, both making games for adults rather than the teenage market, one
publicly admiring the other. It is a small piece of evidence that the British industry of
1986 was small enough for its principals to be each other's audience.
