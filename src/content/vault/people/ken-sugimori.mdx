## Overview

The look of Pokémon. Sugimori's watercolour artwork defined how generations saw Pikachu, Charizard, and the original 151. His designs balanced cute appeal with creature logic—believable animals that happened to be fantastic. As art director, he maintained visual consistency as Pokémon grew from 151 to over 1000 creatures.

## Fast facts

- **Born:** 1966.
- **Company:** Game Freak (co-founder).
- **Role:** Character designer, art director.
- **Style:** Watercolour illustration.
