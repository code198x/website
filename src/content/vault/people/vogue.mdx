## Overview

Vogue — Magnus Högdahl — is the handle behind a studio. A coder in [Triton](/vault/groups/triton) known for tech demos, he founded [Starbreeze Studios](/vault/companies/starbreeze) in 1998, carrying the group's demoscene craft directly into commercial games. The line from [Crystal Dream](/vault/demos/crystal-dream) to *The Chronicles of Riddick* runs through him.

He is part of a remarkably productive Swedish lineage: alongside Triton and Starbreeze, alumni of that orbit went on to found MachineGames (of *Wolfenstein*), making the cluster one of the demoscene's most fruitful exports to the industry.

Högdahl's story is the Triton-to-Starbreeze pipeline made personal: the same drive to push hardware in a demo, redirected into a studio with the discipline to ship retail games. It is one of the clearest single-person illustrations of the scene-to-industry path.
