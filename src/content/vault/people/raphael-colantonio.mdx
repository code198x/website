Keeping the immersive sim alive. Colantonio founded Arkane Studios in France and spent two decades championing a genre most publishers considered uncommercial. Dark Messiah showed combat potential; Dishonored proved mainstream viability; Prey (2017) delivered System Shock's spiritual successor. He departed Arkane in 2017 to found WolfEye Studios, continuing his design philosophy independently.
