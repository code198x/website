Skaven — Peter Hajba — is one of the most recognisable names in tracker music, half of the musical engine (with [Purple Motion](/vault/people/purple-motion)) that gave [Future Crew](/vault/groups/future-crew) their sound. His work on demos like [Second Reality](/vault/demos/second-reality) reached far beyond the scene; for many, an .S3M file with "Skaven" in the credits was a guarantee of quality.

When Future Crew members founded [Remedy Entertainment](/vault/companies/remedy-entertainment), Hajba's audio and visual work went with them, another strand of the scene-to-studio pipeline made personal.

## Fast facts

- **Handle:** Skaven.
- **Group:** Future Crew.
- **Discipline:** Tracker music (and visual art).
- **Later:** Remedy Entertainment.

## From .mod to the studio

The tracker — a music tool where samples are sequenced in a grid — was the scene's instrument, and Skaven was one of its virtuosos. The discipline of writing memorable, tightly-produced music inside tight technical limits transferred directly to game audio, and Hajba's career is one of the clearest lines from demo soundtrack to professional studio.
