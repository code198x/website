## Overview

**Henk Nieborg** is a Dutch pixel artist whose meticulous, vibrant work defined the visual peak of 16-bit gaming. His detailed sprites, rich backgrounds, and fluid animation on titles like Lionheart, The Misadventures of Flink, and Lomax demonstrated what dedicated artistry could achieve within platform constraints.
