## Overview

Jester — Volker Tripp — wrote some of the Amiga demoscene's most fondly remembered tracker music. Active from roughly 1989 to 1994, his modules powered productions by [Sanity](/vault/groups/sanity) and Pygmy Projects, sitting at the heart of an era when the four-channel .mod was the scene's defining instrument.

His tunes have outlived their demos: like the best scene music, they circulate as standalone files, covered and remixed long after the productions that first carried them.

## The module as instrument

Jester belongs to the cohort of Amiga musicians who proved the tracker was a real compositional tool, not just a playback trick. Working within four channels and a handful of samples, he wrote melodies memorable enough to stand on their own — exactly the craft that, in other hands, carried scene musicians into professional game audio.
