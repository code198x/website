## Overview
Rob Hubbard’s C64 soundtracks—*Monty on the Run*, *Commando*, *International Karate*—pushed the SID chip to its musical limits. A classically trained musician turned programmer, Hubbard composed and coded his own playback routines, blending arpeggios, filters, and pulse-width modulation into unforgettable hooks.

## Fast facts
- **Background:** studied music and worked as a teacher before moving into game audio.  
- **Technique:** wrote custom drivers to squeeze more channels and effects out of the SID.  
- **Career arc:** later composed for Electronic Arts and worked on audio tools for game studios.
