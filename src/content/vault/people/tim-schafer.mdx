Tim Schafer writes games people quote. From the surreal humour of Day of the Tentacle through Grim Fandango's noir poetry to Psychonauts' emotional depth, his work combines sharp comedy with genuine heart. His LucasArts adventures defined the genre's peak; Double Fine continues exploring new territory with the same distinctive voice.
