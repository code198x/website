The PlayStation 2 achieved unprecedented success through smart positioning and an overwhelming game library. Launching as an affordable DVD player when standalone units cost more, it entered homes beyond gaming enthusiasts. Sony's third-party relationships delivered exclusive content while backwards compatibility preserved PlayStation 1 libraries. No console before or since has matched its 155 million sales.

## Fast facts

- **Manufacturer:** Sony Computer Entertainment.
- **Release:** March 2000 (Japan), October 2000 (NA/EU).
- **Units sold:** 155+ million (best-selling console ever).
- **Library:** 4,000+ titles.
- **Production ended:** 2013.

## The DVD advantage

Trojan horse strategy:
- DVD players cost 300+ dollars in 2000.
- PS2 launched at 299 dollars with DVD playback.
- Families bought it for films.
- Gaming became secondary benefit for some.
- Accelerated DVD adoption.
