The Sega Saturn launched with dual CPUs and powerful 2D capabilities designed for arcade ports. Unfortunately, 3D gaming was emerging as the standard, and Saturn's architecture made 3D development challenging. The console found success in Japan with its 2D fighting game library but struggled against PlayStation in Western markets.

## Fast facts

- **Manufacturer:** [Sega](/vault/companies/sega).
- **CPUs:** dual Hitachi SH-2 at 28.6MHz.
- **VDPs:** two, with different jobs — VDP1 renders quads, VDP2 composites the layers by priority and transparency.
- **RAM:** 2MB main, 1.5MB video.
- **Media:** [CD-ROM](/vault/hardware/cd-rom) (2x speed).
- **Strength:** 2D sprite handling, arcade ports.
- **Audio:** SCSP driving a Motorola 68000 of its own, with 512 KiB of dedicated RAM.

## Architecture challenges

Why 3D was difficult:
- **Dual CPU:** complex to program effectively — and the two SH-2s shared a bus, so "one had to wait for the other if both needed to access either to RAM or a peripheral". A shared 4 KiB cache "had little impact on the bottleneck".
- **Quad-based rendering:** VDP1 drew quads "distorted in screen space to poorly fake perspective", and its forward texture-mapping suited sprites far better than magnified 3D textures.
- **Eight chips to coordinate,** documented across "eight voluminous manuals".
- **Unfamiliar concurrency:** mutexes and semaphores were "uncommon concepts for game console programmers" in 1995.

How hard this was in practice is best put by a developer at the time. Mick West's
1995 Saturn development journal: "Currently we only use the Master SH2, the slave
SH2 will be used when we get around to figuring out how."

**A correction.** This entry previously listed "no hardware transparency" among
the difficulties. That is not right: VDP2 composited its layers "according to
their priority and transparency settings". The hard part was the quad renderer
and the two-CPU bus, not an absence of transparency.

## Strengths in 2D

Where Saturn excelled:
- **Fighting games:** *Virtua Fighter 2*, *Street Fighter* series.
- **2D shooters:** arcade-perfect ports.
- **Sprite capabilities:** massive sprite counts.
- **Japanese library:** strong RPG and niche support.
