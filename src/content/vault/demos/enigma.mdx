If you ask older Amiga sceners to name the demos that hooked them, [Phenomena](/vault/groups/phenomena)'s *Enigma* comes up again and again. Released in 1991, it arrived as the Amiga scene was maturing fast, and it set a bar that few productions of its moment reached: effects, design, and music all operating at the top of their game at once, in a package that held together as a whole.

Its soundtrack in particular became scene-famous, the kind of music people sought out and kept long after the demo itself.

- **Reputation:** A perennial "best early Amiga demo" pick.

## Why it endures

*Enigma* is one of the productions people point to when explaining what "complete" meant on the Amiga — not a single dazzling trick, but coding, art, and sound pulling together. It helped define the standard the platform's golden age was measured against.
