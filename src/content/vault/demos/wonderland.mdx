The *Wonderland* series is [Censor Design](/vault/groups/censor-design)'s monument on the Commodore 64. Across its installments it became a byword for top-tier C64 work — productions where the coding, the graphics, and the SID soundtrack all hit the same high level, pulling effects from the 1982 machine that seemed to exceed its budget.

For many, the *Wonderland* demos are the answer to anyone who assumes the 8-bit C64 couldn't keep pace with 16-bit machines for sheer craft. The series helped fix Censor at the very top of the C64 scene.

## Fast facts

- **Group:** Censor Design.
- **Platform:** [Commodore 64](/vault/systems/commodore-64).
- **Form:** A celebrated multi-part demo series.
- **Standing:** Among the most revered C64 productions.

## Why it matters

*Wonderland* set a ceiling for what a C64 demo could be, and later groups — [Oxyron](/vault/groups/oxyron), [Booze Design](/vault/groups/booze-design) and others — measured themselves against it. It is a cornerstone of the argument that the C64 scene's best work rivals any platform's.
