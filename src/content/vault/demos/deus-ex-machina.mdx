When two of the C64 scene's strongest groups pool their best people, you get something like *Deus Ex Machina*. A collaboration between [Crest](/vault/groups/crest) and [Oxyron](/vault/groups/oxyron), it took first place at [Mekka & Symposium](/vault/events/mekka-symposium) 2000 and has sat near the top of "greatest C64 demo" lists ever since.

Code by Crossbow, graphics by Deekay, music by Jeff — a lineup of scene heavyweights — and a production that pushed the 1982 machine into territory that still makes people double-check what they're watching.

## Fast facts

- **Groups:** Crest + Oxyron.
- **Platform:** [Commodore 64](/vault/systems/commodore-64).
- **Released:** 2000 — first place at Mekka & Symposium.
- **Standing:** A perennial "best C64 demo" pick.

## Why it matters

*Deus Ex Machina* is the C64 cross-group collaboration at its peak — proof, like the Amiga's *Hardwired* before it, that the scene's biggest leaps often came from rival crews combining forces. It remains a cornerstone of the case that the C64 scene rivals any platform's.
