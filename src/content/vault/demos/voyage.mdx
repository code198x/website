Voyage is the demo that defines [Razor 1911](/vault/groups/razor-1911)'s Amiga legacy — and a neat reminder that the line between the cracking and demo scenes ran right through the same groups. Razor were a legendary cracking crew, but with Voyage they made one of the platform's landmark demos: real-time texture mapping running smoothly on a stock Amiga, an effect that seemed almost impossible on hardware never designed for it.

Released at the end of 1991, it took third place at The Party. Its soundtrack — a four-channel module by Codex — is still cited among the best the Amiga ever had.

## Fast facts

- **Group:** [Razor 1911](/vault/groups/razor-1911).
- **Year:** 1991 (3rd, [The Party](/vault/events/the-party) 1991).
- **Famous for:** Texture mapping on stock Amiga hardware.
- **Credits:** Sim (code), Codex & Tip (music), Drake & Murdock (graphics).

## Crackers making demos

Voyage shows the two scenes were never really separate. The same competitive drive that made Razor 1911 race to crack a game first pushed them to wring impossible effects from the Amiga in a demo — and many of the members who built Voyage carried those skills onward as the group moved to the PC.
