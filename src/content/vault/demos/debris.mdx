If [fr-08](/vault/demos/fr-08-the-product) proved [Farbrausch](/vault/groups/farbrausch) could fit a demo in 64 kilobytes, *Debris* (2007) proved they could make one of the most spectacular demos of its era at a fraction of a normal production's size. Winner at the Breakpoint party, it is a continuous, escalating sequence of procedurally generated geometry — streets and structures assembling and tearing apart — set to a driving soundtrack.

It is Farbrausch's procedural philosophy turned up to full volume: enormous visual density from a tiny executable.

## Fast facts

- **Group:** Farbrausch.
- **Platform:** PC (Windows).
- **Released:** 2007 — winner at Breakpoint.
- **Hallmark:** Procedurally generated city destruction.

## Why it matters

*Debris* is a landmark of the late-2000s PC scene, showing how far the procedural techniques pioneered in *fr-08* could be pushed. It remains a go-to example when explaining what the modern demoscene is capable of.
