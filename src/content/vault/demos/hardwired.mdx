*Hardwired* (1991), a collaboration between Crionics and The Silents, is one of the demos people cite when they argue the form is an art. It has the effects you would expect of a top Amiga production, but it spends them differently — choreographed to the music, sequenced for rhythm and mood rather than simply paraded one after another.

That shift mattered. *Hardwired* helped move the demo away from "look what the hardware can do" toward "look what we can make you feel", and its sense of direction influenced productions for years afterward.

## Fast facts

- **Groups:** Crionics and The Silents.
- **Platform:** Amiga.
- **Released:** 1991.
- **Significance:** An early high point of cinematic, music-led demo design.

## Why it endures

Where many contemporaries read as technical sampler reels, *Hardwired* reads as a piece with intent. It treated timing, transitions, and theme as first-class concerns — the vocabulary the best demos still work in today.
