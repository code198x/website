## Overview

*The Lyra II*, by [ESI](/vault/groups/esi), is one of the productions that announced the ZX Spectrum demoscene as a serious creative force. Appearing in the early 1990s, it was a megademo in the full sense — a run of effects, music, and design pushing a machine that, on paper, had far less to give than the Amiga or C64.

That was always the Spectrum scene's point of pride. With a display built from 768 character blocks and a single colour pair per block, every effect demanded ingenuity, and *The Lyra II* showed how much could be wrung from so little.

## The Spectrum challenge

The Spectrum's "multicolour" tricks — racing the beam to change colour attributes faster than the hardware intends — became the scene's signature, and demos like *The Lyra II* helped establish that vocabulary. The Eastern European scene, much of it on Pentagon clones, carried it furthest.
