*Signal Part 3* (1991), by the Polish group Flash Inc and coded by Mikropol, is one of the productions that show the ZX Spectrum demoscene was never a single-country story. Where Russia gave the scene groups like [ESI](/vault/groups/esi), Poland had its own thriving community, and *Signal Part 3* is among its fondly remembered entries — a confident piece of Spectrum craft wringing motion and effect from the machine's stubborn display.

It belongs to the same tradition as the rest of the Eastern European scene: a refusal to treat the Spectrum as a lesser canvas.

## Fast facts

- **Group:** Flash Inc (coded by Mikropol).
- **Platform:** ZX Spectrum.
- **Released:** 1991.
- **Origin:** Poland.

## How the West met the scene

The Eastern European Spectrum scene moved on disks, BBSs, and party halls — channels most Western owners never touched. For them, the scene arrived secondhand, through the magazines: titles like [Your Sinclair](/vault/magazines/your-sinclair) covered the public-domain and demo worlds, and for a lot of British Spectrum fans that coverage was the doorway in.
