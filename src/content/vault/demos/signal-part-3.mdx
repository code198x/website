"MIKROPOL PRESENTS SIGNAL PART 3" fills the screen in tall black letters on a green crosshatched panel, while a line of small text moves across the top. *Signal Part 3* came from Poland in the spring of 1991, two years after his *Signal 1*. It was made by a small team: Mikropol wrote the code, Ewa drew the graphics and The Spy wrote the music.

## Fast facts

- **By:** Mikropol, from Bydgoszcz, Poland.
- **Credits:** code by Mikropol; graphics by Ewa; music by The Spy (credits as listed on Demozoo).
- **Platform:** ZX Spectrum, on tape.
- **Released:** 6 April 1991, according to Demozoo; the loader says 1991.

## The Signal series

The demo was released under Mikropol's own name, not a group's. Its loader calls it "Signal 3", and Demozoo lists an earlier *Signal 1* from 1989, as well as a *Soon Demo* from 1991. The whole of *Signal Part 3* loads from tape as one block of about 31 KB, then waits on a "PRESS SPACE" screen before the title appears. The Spy's music for it is also listed separately on Demozoo, as *Signal 3-4*. Public sources do not describe how the demo's effects were programmed.

## After the Spectrum

Mikropol moved on to the Amiga. Demozoo credits him, as Mikie, with the music for *Sigh*, a musicdisk by the group Old Bulls, in November 1991, and with music for an Amiga cracktro in 1996. The Spy kept writing for the Spectrum: he wrote the music for *Speed 2*, a 1995 demo by Technium 220, the group whose member Reb converted *[Jesus on E's](/vault/demos/jesus-on-es-spectrum)* to the Spectrum the next year.
