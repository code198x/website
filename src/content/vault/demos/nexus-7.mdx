*Nexus 7*, by [Andromeda](/vault/groups/andromeda), is one of the demos people reach for when describing the Amiga's AGA generation at its best. Released at the end of 1994 and winning first place at [The Party](/vault/events/the-party), it used the newer chipset's extra colour and muscle in the service of a coherent, confident piece of design rather than a parade of disconnected effects.

It arrived a year after Kefrens' *Desert Dream* had set the bar at the same event, and it cleared it — evidence that the Amiga scene kept raising its own game even as the platform aged.

## Why it endures

*Nexus 7* showed the AGA Amiga could host work as composed and design-led as the platform's finest OCS-era productions. It remains a fixture in conversations about the Amiga's best demos.
