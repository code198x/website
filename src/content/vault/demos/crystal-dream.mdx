## Overview

*Crystal Dream* is the production that made [Triton](/vault/groups/triton)'s name, and in hindsight it reads like a studio's portfolio piece before the studio existed. Fast, clean, and confidently designed, it showed the qualities that separated the scene's professionals-in-waiting from the hobbyists: tight code, coherent art direction, and the discipline to finish.

Triton followed it with a sequel on PC and then made the leap so few groups managed — becoming [Starbreeze Studios](/vault/companies/starbreeze). *Crystal Dream* is where that trajectory becomes visible.

## Fast facts

- **Group:** Triton.
- **Platform:** Amiga (a sequel followed on PC).
- **Released:** 1992.
- **Significance:** Early career-defining work by future Starbreeze founders.

## Why it matters

*Crystal Dream* is a clean example of the scene-to-studio pipeline caught in the act. The instincts on display — performance, presentation, finishing under deadline — are exactly the ones a commercial studio runs on, and Triton carried them straight across.
