## Overview

*Shock Megademo* is one of [ESI](/vault/groups/esi)'s landmark productions, released in 1992 alongside their work on [The Lyra II](/vault/demos/lyra-ii). Together they mark the moment the ZX Spectrum scene hit full stride — megademos that strung effect after effect across a machine the West had largely stopped taking seriously.

For the Eastern European scene, productions like *Shock Megademo* were a statement of intent: the Spectrum was not a lesser platform to be apologised for, but a canvas worth mastering.

## The Spectrum scene's ambition

What makes Spectrum megademos remarkable is the constraint they overcome. With colour locked to one pair per character block, every smooth effect is a small act of defiance against the hardware — and *Shock Megademo* is full of them. It remains a fondly remembered piece of the platform's demoscene canon.
