## Overview

*Starstruck*, by [The Black Lotus](/vault/groups/the-black-lotus), premiered at Assembly 2006 and immediately became the reference point for what the Amiga could still do decades after release. It is the kind of demo that prompts disbelief — viewers checking, and re-checking, that they really are watching original Amiga hardware.

It belongs to the same spirit as the C64's *Edge of Disgrace*: a modern production whose whole argument is that the old machine's ceiling was never as low as people assumed.

## Fast facts

- **Group:** The Black Lotus.
- **Platform:** Amiga (AGA).
- **Released:** 2006, at Assembly.
- **Reputation:** A benchmark of the modern Amiga scene.

## Why it matters

*Starstruck* is one of the strongest single arguments that the demoscene is a living craft. By extracting results like these from aged hardware, TBL kept the Amiga scene's frontier moving — and gave newcomers a reason to keep learning the machine.
