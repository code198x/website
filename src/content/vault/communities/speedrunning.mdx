Speedrunning asks: how fast can this game be completed? What began with *Doom* recordings evolved into a global community with standardised rules, live events, and charitable marathons. Players discover glitches, optimise routes, and compete for world records measured in frames.

## Fast facts

- **Origins:** Doom demos (1993).
- **Major site:** [Speedrun.com](/vault/culture/speedrun-com).
- **Events:** AGDQ, SGDQ.
- **Community:** Global, collaborative.
