## Overview

Before the World Wide Web, there were BBSes—Bulletin Board Systems. Dial a phone number with your modem, and you connected to another computer. Download software, post messages, play door games, chat with the sysop. BBSes created online communities and distributed the software that fueled the home computer revolution.

## Fast facts

- **First BBS:** CBBS (1978).
- **Technology:** Dial-up modem.
- **Peak:** Late 1980s to mid-1990s.
- **Decline:** Internet access growth.

## How it worked

| Step | Process |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Configure terminal software |
| 2 | Dial BBS phone number |
| 3 | Modem handshake |
| 4 | Login to system |
| 5 | Navigate menus |
