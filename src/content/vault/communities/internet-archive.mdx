## Overview

The **Internet Archive** is a non-profit digital library founded by Brewster Kahle in 1996, dedicated to providing "universal access to all knowledge." For gaming, it hosts the Software Library and Console Living Room - collections of thousands of playable games running in browser-based emulators, along with scanned magazines, manuals, and documentation. It's become essential infrastructure for game preservation.

## In-Browser Emulation

The revolutionary feature:

- **DOSBox** - DOS games playable in browser
- **JSMESS** - Console emulation via JavaScript
- **MAME** - Arcade games online
- **No download** - Click and play
- **Preservation** - Games accessible forever
