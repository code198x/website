## Overview

**DefleMask** is a multi-system chiptune tracker that supports numerous vintage sound chips from a single, unified interface. Composers can create authentic music for Sega Genesis, NES, Game Boy, Commodore 64, and many other platforms, exporting to formats playable on real hardware or in emulators.
