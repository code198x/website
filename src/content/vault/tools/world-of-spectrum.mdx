## Overview

World of Spectrum began as a hobby project and became the essential resource for ZX Spectrum preservation. Housing over 25,000 titles with documentation, screenshots, and historical information, WoS ensured that the Spectrum's software library would survive the death of physical media.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1995.
- **Founder:** Martijn van der Heide.
- **Content:** 25,000+ titles.
- **Focus:** Complete Spectrum preservation.
