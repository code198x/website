## Overview

GameBase combined database and emulator frontend into a unified preservation system. Click a game, and it launched in VICE or WinUAE automatically. With comprehensive metadata, screenshots, and historical information, GameBase made exploring Commodore gaming history effortless.

## Fast facts

- **Platform coverage:** C64, Amiga, VIC-20, Plus/4.
- **Integration:** Direct emulator launching.
- **Content:** Thousands of catalogued titles.
- **Format:** Windows application with database.
