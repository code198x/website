## Overview

**ZZAP!64** was the defining Commodore 64 gaming magazine. Issue 1 carried a May 1985 cover date and reached shops on 11 April 1985; the original run ended with issue 90 in November 1992. Newsfield published it for most of that time, but not all of it — after Newsfield collapsed in 1991 the title continued under **Europress Impact**, whose name appears throughout the 1992 issues.

📘 The title has also been revived more than once since. A **March 2002** issue picks up the numbering at 107, decades after issue 90. Its enthusiastic coverage, detailed review system, and iconic Gold Medal awards made it essential reading for C64 owners. Sister publication to CRASH, ZZAP!64 brought the same passion and personality to Commodore coverage.

## Fast Facts

- **Publisher:** Newsfield, then Europress Impact from 1991
- **Run:** 1985-1992
- **Cover tape:** Megatape (from 1988)
- **Notable staff:** issue 1's masthead lists Chris Anderson as editor, Bob Wade, and Gary Penn and Julian Rignall as the reviewing pair — under joke titles: "Slavedriver", "Tape collector", "Joystick junkies". **Oliver Frey** supplied the art, as he did for *CRASH*, and **Jeff Minter** wrote a column ("Hairiest columnist in galaxy"). Steve Jarratt and Gordon Houghton came later; Jarratt went on to edit.
- **Innovation:** Gold Medal awards, detailed scoring

## The Review System

ZZAP!64 pioneered thorough game evaluation:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Multiple reviewers** | Different perspectives |
| **Detailed scores** | Presentation, graphics, sound, playability, lastability |
| **Gold Medal** | Introduced in **issue 2**, June 1985, and defined there in the magazine's own words: *"our biggest rave of the month"*. ⚠ Never a fixed percentage — *Elite* and *Dropzone* took Gold at 95%, *Paradroid* at 97%. The Sizzler predates it, running from issue 1, and normally wanted 80%+ for value |
| **Screenshots** | Generous visual coverage |

## The Megatape

From 1988, ZZAP!64 included cover-mounted cassettes:

- Exclusive material

## Cultural Impact

ZZAP!64's influence extended beyond reviews:

- Set standards for C64 journalism
- Gold Medal became sales guarantee
- Staff moved to game development and industry roles
- Archived the golden age of C64 gaming

## Legacy

ZZAP!64 remains the benchmark for platform-specific gaming coverage. Its passionate writing, thorough reviews, and community focus created a template that modern gaming media still follows.
