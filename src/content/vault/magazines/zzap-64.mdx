## Overview

**ZZAP!64** was the defining Commodore 64 gaming magazine. Issue 1 carried a May 1985 cover date and reached shops on 11 April 1985; the original run ended with issue 90 in November 1992. Newsfield published it for most of that time, but not all of it — after Newsfield collapsed in 1991 the title continued under **Europress Impact**. Its enthusiastic coverage, detailed review system, and iconic Gold Medal awards made it essential reading for C64 owners. Sister publication to CRASH, ZZAP!64 brought the same passion and personality to Commodore coverage.

## Fast Facts

- **Publisher:** Newsfield, then Europress Impact from 1991
- **Run:** 1985-1992
- **Cover tape:** Megatape (from 1988)
- **Notable staff:** the launch reviewing team was Bob Wade, Julian Rignall and Gary Penn, under founding editor Chris Anderson. Steve Jarratt and Gordon Houghton joined later; Jarratt went on to edit.
- **Innovation:** Gold Medal awards, detailed scoring

## The Review System

ZZAP!64 pioneered thorough game evaluation:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Multiple reviewers** | Different perspectives |
| **Detailed scores** | Presentation, graphics, sound, playability, lastability |
| **Gold Medal** | The magazine's "biggest rave of the month". ⚠ No fixed percentage — *Elite* and *Dropzone* took Gold at 95%, *Paradroid* at 97%. A Sizzler, by contrast, normally needed 80%+ for value |
| **Screenshots** | Generous visual coverage |

## Cultural Impact

ZZAP!64's influence extended beyond reviews:

- Set standards for C64 journalism
- Gold Medal became sales guarantee
- Staff moved to game development and industry roles
- Archived the golden age of C64 gaming

## Legacy

ZZAP!64 remains the benchmark for platform-specific gaming coverage. Its passionate writing, thorough reviews, and community focus created a template that modern gaming media still follows.
