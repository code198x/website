**Your Commodore** ran from **October 1984** to **October 1991**, published by **Argus Specialist Publications Ltd**, No. 1 Golden Square, London W1R 3AB. Ninety-two items are held here, spanning the VIC-20, the [Commodore 64](/vault/systems/commodore-64) and eventually the [Amiga](/vault/systems/commodore-amiga).

## Launched out of another magazine

Three months before issue 1, Argus bound a free Commodore supplement into *Personal Computing Today*, one of its existing titles — VIC-20 and CBM 64 type-in listings, with the same editorial staff named on it. A publisher testing a market inside a magazine it already owned, then launching into it.

It launched in **October**, which by this point is unsurprising: [eleven of thirty-nine British computer titles](/vault/culture/magazine-distribution) launching between 1981 and 1993 did the same, against five for the next-best month.

⚠ This entry is built from the issues held and their colophons. This library holds *Your Commodore* in quantity but no account of its editorial history, so nothing is claimed about how it changed across seven years.
