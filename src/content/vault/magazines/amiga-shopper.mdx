**Amiga Shopper** ran 108 issues, **April 1991 to January 1997**, from [Future Publishing](/vault/companies/future-publishing).

It was Future's **third** [Amiga](/vault/systems/commodore-amiga) title, alongside *[Amiga Format](/vault/magazines/amiga-format)* and *[Amiga Power](/vault/magazines/amiga-power)*, and it existed because the three were aimed at different questions: what to play, whether it was any good, and what to buy.

## Made on the machine it covered

Issue 1 describes its own production, which is the sort of thing only a magazine of this kind would print. Pages were laid out in **Professional Page**; the Future Publishing logo was scanned with a **Golden Image hand scanner**, touched up in **DPaint**, and imported. A whole page, it reckons, took about thirty minutes to prepare.

*[Deluxe Paint](/vault/systems/commodore-amiga)* was [Electronic Arts](/vault/companies/electronic-arts)' Amiga art program, and here it is being used to make the magazine about the Amiga, on an Amiga.
