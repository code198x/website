**Atari ST User** ran from **March 1986** to **October 1994**, published by **Database Publications Ltd** — part of [Europress](/vault/companies/europress) — from Europa House, Hazel Grove, Stockport.

## One title per machine

Set beside its stablemates, it completes a pattern that is invisible from any single entry:

| Title | From | Machine |
|---|---|---|
| *[The Micro User](/vault/magazines/micro-user)* | 1983 | [BBC Micro](/vault/systems/bbc-micro) |
| **Atari ST User** | **1986** | [Atari ST](/vault/systems/atari-st) |
| *[Amiga Computing](/vault/magazines/amiga-computing)* | 1988 | [Amiga](/vault/systems/commodore-amiga) |

Database launched one magazine per platform as each became worth covering, roughly every two to three years, from a single building in Cheshire. That is a different strategy from [Future](/vault/companies/future-publishing)'s — which split *ST/Amiga Format* into two titles and then added *[Amiga Shopper](/vault/magazines/amiga-shopper)* as a third for the same machine — and different again from [EMAP](/vault/companies/emap)'s, which renamed *Commodore User* into *[CU Amiga](/vault/magazines/cu-amiga)* and carried the readership across.

Three houses, three answers to the same question about what to do when the machines change.

⚠ The address moved. This colophon gives Europa House at **Hazel Grove, Stockport**; *Amiga Computing*'s gives Europa House at **Adlington Park, Macclesfield**. The building name travelled with the company.
