**ST Format** ran from **July 1989** to **September 1996**, published by [Future Publishing](/vault/companies/future-publishing) from **4 Queen Street, Bath**.

## The other half

*ST Format* and *[Amiga Format](/vault/magazines/amiga-format)* are the two halves of one earlier magazine. Future published *ST/Amiga Format* — a single title covering both 16-bit machines — for eighteen issues from July 1988, and split it in mid-1989. *ST Format* begins in **July 1989**; *Amiga Format* in **August**.

So the two are not rivals that grew apart. They are a deliberate division of one readership at the moment each machine could support a magazine of its own.

## An address worth noting

The Bath address in issue 1 dates Future's move. Its first title, *[Amstrad Action](/vault/magazines/amstrad-action)*, was published from **Somerton, Somerset** in 1985 and still gave Somerton in early 1986. By July 1989 the colophon says **4 Queen Street, Bath** — the city Future is usually associated with, and which it had not yet reached when it started.
