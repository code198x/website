**Amiga Joker** was a German [Amiga](/vault/systems/commodore-amiga) magazine
published by **Joker Verlag** of Untere Parkstraße 67, **Haar**, outside Munich.
This library holds 78 issues, **April 1990** to **October 1996**.

It is the first German entry in this Vault, which had eight entries on British
Amiga magazines and none on a German one — for a machine that sold heavily in
Germany.

## A schedule, and a way to check it

Magazine entries usually take a publishing schedule on trust, because the only
witness is the magazine saying what it intends. Here both halves are present.

The April 1990 Impressum states it exactly:

> "AMIGA JOKER erscheint **monatlich, zum jeweils letzten Freitag des
> Vormonats**. Doppelausgaben: **6/7 und 8/9**."

Monthly, on the last Friday of the *preceding* month, with June/July and
August/September combined — so ten issues a year, which is what the subscription
line charges for: *Jahrespreis (10 Ausgaben): DM 60,-*.

The holdings then say whether that is what happened. The cover months held for
**1992, 1993, 1994 and 1995** are identical in all four years:

> 01 02 03 04 05 06 08 10 11 12

Ten issues, July and September missing, exactly as a 6/7 and 8/9 double-issue
scheme produces. Four consecutive years matching a stated schedule month for
month is about as close as this library gets to confirming a publishing pattern
rather than repeating one.

⚠ The two earliest years do not fit, and it is not safe to say why. 1990 and
1991 hold different month sets, and an absent month in this library may mean an
issue was never published or merely that it is not held. The four matching years
are evidence; the two that differ are not evidence of a change.

## The market was a language, not a country

The Impressum names **Verlagsunion of Wiesbaden** as distributor "für Inland
(Groß-, Einzel- und Bahnhofsbuchhandel), Österreich, Schweiz" — for Germany
through wholesale, retail and station bookshops, plus **Austria and
Switzerland**.

Which is a useful correction to the territory suffix this library files it
under. *Amiga Joker* was sold into the German-speaking market across three
countries, and its subscription price is quoted twice, DM 60 at home and DM 72
abroad. A magazine's territory and its language are not the same boundary, and
in this case the language is the larger one.

## Readers on the page

The issue held here carries an appeal that places the magazine near the
[demoscene](/vault/communities/demo-scene) rather than merely reporting on it.
The editor announces that a *Galerie* page will alternate with the magazine's
established *Girl* page, and asks readers to send work in:

> "Was immer Euch einfällt, seien es **Demos**, Gedichte, Zeichnungen oder
> andere Wunder der schönen Künste."

Whatever occurs to you — demos, poems, drawings. Reader-submitted demos treated
as gallery material alongside poetry and drawing is a different editorial
posture from the British Amiga press of the same year, which mostly covered the
scene from outside.

⚠ That is one issue's editorial, not a description of six years. This library
holds *Amiga Joker* in quantity and no secondary account of it, so nothing here
describes how it reviewed, what it scored, or how German readers regarded it.
