## Overview
Spun out of *Your Spectrum*, *Your Sinclair* (YS) leaned into comedic writing, pranks, and reader interaction while still delivering thorough reviews, tips, and tech features for Spectrum owners.

## Fast facts
- **Editorial tone:** irreverent columns, made-up alter egos, and running jokes (“Newsfield staff invasions”).  
- **Content mix:** game reviews, “YS Tipshop,” technical articles, and cover-mounted tapes in later years.  
- **Community:** letters section and competitions encouraged readers to submit art, code, and cheats.

## Legacy
YS helped preserve Spectrum culture during the late 80s slump, maintaining enthusiasm until the platform’s twilight. Modern retro publications still echo its mix of satire and sincerity.
