**MC-microcomputer** was an Italian computing monthly published by **Technimedia** of Rome. Issue 1 is dated **September 1981** and priced at 3,000 lire; the last issue held here is from **June 2001**. Two hundred and twenty-two issues span those twenty years.

It is the **largest single publication in this project's reference library** — 60,950 pages, ahead of *Byte*, *Compute!* and *Computer Gaming World* — and this is the first entry the Vault has written about anything Italian.

## Founded on a deadline

The editorial in issue 1, by *direttore* **Paolo Nuti**, is not the sort of thing a launch usually opens with. He describes packing a bag for a week's windsurfing — wetsuit, sail, mast foot — telephoning his *condirettore* **Marco Marinacci** to read him the editorial down the line because "this co-editor has something to say about everything", leaving it on Giovanna Molinari's desk, and going away to rest after "**due mesi e dieci giorni di lavoro frenetico**" — two months and ten days of frantic work.

The arithmetic in that sentence is the useful part. The same paragraph dates the publisher: "**Technimedia è stata fondata il 21 maggio 1981**." Founded 21 May, first issue September. Two months and ten days is exactly right.

## Why it is here
