**Mean Machines** ran from **October 1990** to **September 1992**, published by **[EMAP](/vault/companies/emap)** — the same house as *[Computer and Video Games](/vault/magazines/computer-and-video-games)*, *[Sinclair User](/vault/magazines/sinclair-user)* and *[CU Amiga](/vault/magazines/cu-amiga)*.

Its subscription coupons route cheques to **EMAP plc, PO Box 300, Peterborough** — a different address from the Clerkenwell one the eighties titles used, and a reminder that by 1990 EMAP was a public company running magazines from more than one place.

## The timing is the point

*Mean Machines* launched in **October 1990** into a British market that had spent a decade on home computers. EMAP had covered the [Spectrum](/vault/systems/zx-spectrum), the [C64](/vault/systems/commodore-64) and the [Amiga](/vault/systems/commodore-amiga) through the eighties; this was the same publisher deciding that consoles now needed a magazine of their own.

It is also — inevitably by now — an **October** launch, which [eleven of thirty-nine](/vault/culture/magazine-distribution) British computer titles of the period share.

⚠ The run held here ends in September 1992. That is the extent of this library's holding and not necessarily the magazine's end; *Mean Machines* continued in successor titles beyond what is held.
