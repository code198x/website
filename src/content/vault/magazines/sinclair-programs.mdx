**Sinclair Programs** ran 52 issues, **June 1983 to September 1985**, from **ECC Publications** — the company that had launched *[Sinclair User](/vault/magazines/sinclair-user)* the previous year and that [EMAP](/vault/companies/emap) later absorbed.

Its premise was the opposite of the review magazines. Where *[CRASH](/vault/magazines/crash-magazine)* told you what to buy, *Sinclair Programs* printed programs to type in — the practice covered at [type-in listings](/vault/distribution/type-in-listings).

## Why this Vault cites it

Its May 1985 issue ran a feature organising the British software trade by how long each company had been going — a "fourth year", "fifth year" listing — and that survey is one of the few contemporary sources that dates these companies at all rather than describing them.

Two entries here rest on it: [Hewson Consultants](/vault/companies/hewson-consultants), for the account of Andrew Hewson being talked round about the ZX80 over tea in a Wimpy in Stratford-upon-Avon, and [the record companies' move into software](/vault/phenomena/record-companies-in-software), for Creative Sparks as "Thorn EMI's contender in the software publishing market. Started in April 1984."

A listings magazine turns out to be a better business source than most of the review press, because it had a reason to ask companies how old they were.
