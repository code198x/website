## Overview

Before the web, the demoscene communicated through diskmags—magazines distributed on floppy disks. They featured custom interfaces, music, and graphics alongside news, interviews, charts, and tutorials. Written by sceners for sceners, they documented a culture that existed largely outside mainstream awareness.

## Fast facts

- **Format:** Floppy disk distribution.
- **Content:** News, interviews, tutorials, charts.
- **Creators:** Scene groups.
- **Peak:** Late 1980s through 1990s.
