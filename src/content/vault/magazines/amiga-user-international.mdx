**Amiga User International** was published by **Croftward Limited** from the Finsbury Business Centre, 40 Bowling Green Lane, London **EC1R** — and distributed by Comag.

Seventeen issues are held here, running **March 1988 to April 1996**. The earliest of them is **volume 2, number 3**, which means the magazine started before this library can see it.

⚠ `founded: 1987` is therefore an inference from that volume number rather than a date read off an issue. This is the thinnest holding of any British title in this Vault — seventeen issues across an eight-year span — and the entry is correspondingly cautious.

## Clerkenwell again

Bowling Green Lane is a few minutes' walk from Priory Court, Farringdon Lane, where [EMAP](/vault/companies/emap) produced *[Sinclair User](/vault/magazines/sinclair-user)*, *[CU Amiga](/vault/magazines/cu-amiga)* and the rest, and from Herbal Hill, the address *[C&VG](/vault/magazines/computer-and-video-games)* gave in 1981. Three publishers, one postcode district.

That is not a conspiracy, it is print. **EC1R** is Clerkenwell, historically London's printing and publishing quarter, and magazines were made near the people who typeset and printed them.

## What it was up against

*Amiga User International* was independent, like *[Amiga Action](/vault/magazines/amiga-action)* out of Chichester, in a market otherwise divided between [Future](/vault/companies/future-publishing), [EMAP](/vault/companies/emap) and [Europress](/vault/companies/europress). Of the seven British Amiga magazines this Vault now covers, five belonged to one of those three houses and two did not.
