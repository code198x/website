**Amstrad Action** ran for **117 issues**, from **October 1985** to **June 1995** — a
decade covering a machine that was discontinued partway through it. This library holds the
full run.

It was **Future Publishing**'s first magazine, published from Somerton in Somerset. The
company that would go on to print *ACE*, *ST/Amiga Format*, *8000 Plus*, *PC Plus* and *New
Computer Express* began with a magazine for a machine that had been on sale for eighteen
months.

## The Mastergame was there from issue one

Most magazines settle their award after a few issues. *Amstrad Action* ran **Mastergame**
from the first, awarding it in October 1985 to Melbourne House's *Way of the Exploding
Fist* — "our action-packed Mastergame". The name appears on 197 pages of the run held here.

## Fast facts

- **Publisher:** Future Publishing Ltd, Somerton, Somerset.
- **Run:** issue 1 (October 1985) to issue 117 (June 1995).
- **Award:** **Mastergame**, in use from issue 1.
- **Machine:** the [Amstrad CPC](/vault/systems/amstrad-cpc), throughout.
- **Sibling titles:** *ACE*, *8000 Plus*, *PC Plus*, *New Computer Express*, *ST/Amiga Format*.

## Outlasting the machine

The CPC stopped being a current machine long before 1995, and the magazine kept going
anyway — which makes its later issues a rare thing: sustained coverage of a platform after
the industry had moved on. Its final issue describes itself as "a magazine of over ten
years' standing", and records the cover price rising with the cost of paper and printing.
