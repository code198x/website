**Amiga Action** ran from **October 1989** to **June 1995**. It was launched by **Gollner Publishing Ltd** of Latham House, Quarry Lane, **Chichester**, under publisher **Hugh Gollner** — and it did not stay there.

It is the only title in this Vault's magazine set to begin at that house, and it began as one of the few British computer magazines here published by neither [Future](/vault/companies/future-publishing), [EMAP](/vault/companies/emap), [Europress](/vault/companies/europress) nor [Newsfield](/vault/companies/newsfield). It ended owned by the successor to one of them.

## Into the busiest market there was

October 1989 was a hard month to launch an [Amiga](/vault/systems/commodore-amiga) games magazine. Already on the shelf were *[CU Amiga](/vault/magazines/cu-amiga)* (EMAP), *[Amiga Format](/vault/magazines/amiga-format)* (Future, two months old), *[Amiga Computing](/vault/magazines/amiga-computing)* (Europress) and *[Amiga User International](/vault/magazines/amiga-user-international)*. Within two years *[Amiga Power](/vault/magazines/amiga-power)* and *[Amiga Shopper](/vault/magazines/amiga-shopper)* would join them, both from Future.

*Amiga Action* lasted five and a half years in that company, which is the fact worth recording about it.

It is also, once again, an **October** launch — [eleven of thirty-nine](/vault/culture/magazine-distribution) British titles of the period share the month.

⚠ This entry is built from the issues held and their colophons. This library holds no account of Gollner Publishing beyond what appears in its own magazine.

## It did not stay with Gollner

The colophon moves twice. By **February 1992** readers' letters go to "Amiga
Action, **Europress Interactive Ltd., Europa House, Adlington Park, Adlington,
Macclesfield SK10 4NP**" — the Chichester magazine had been bought by
[Europress](/vault/companies/europress) and moved to Cheshire, with both names
appearing side by side through the second half of 1991.

By **March 1995** the credit block reads "CREATED BY... **IDG Media Ltd,
Adlington Park, Macclesfield**". *Amiga Action* was among the eleven titles
Europress sold to [IDG](/vault/companies/idg) at the end of 1994, so its last
few months belong to a third publisher again.

⚠ Not to be confused with the *Amiga Action* supplement bound into the Italian
edition of *The Games Machine* by [Xenia Edizioni](/vault/companies/xenia-edizioni)
in 1992-93 — a 13-to-32-page section, not a magazine in its own right.
