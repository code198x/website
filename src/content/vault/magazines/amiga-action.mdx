**Amiga Action** ran from **October 1989** to **June 1995**, published by **Gollner Publishing Ltd** of Latham House, Quarry Lane, **Chichester**, under publisher **Hugh Gollner**.

It is the only title in this Vault's magazine set from that house, and one of the few British computer magazines here published by neither [Future](/vault/companies/future-publishing), [EMAP](/vault/companies/emap), [Europress](/vault/companies/europress) nor [Newsfield](/vault/companies/newsfield).

## Into the busiest market there was

October 1989 was a hard month to launch an [Amiga](/vault/systems/commodore-amiga) games magazine. Already on the shelf were *[CU Amiga](/vault/magazines/cu-amiga)* (EMAP), *[Amiga Format](/vault/magazines/amiga-format)* (Future, two months old), *[Amiga Computing](/vault/magazines/amiga-computing)* (Europress) and *Amiga User International*. Within two years *[Amiga Power](/vault/magazines/amiga-power)* and *[Amiga Shopper](/vault/magazines/amiga-shopper)* would join them, both from Future.

*Amiga Action* lasted five and a half years in that company, which is the fact worth recording about it.

It is also, once again, an **October** launch — [eleven of thirty-nine](/vault/culture/magazine-distribution) British titles of the period share the month.
