**Nintendo Power** was Nintendo of America's official magazine, published from 1988 to 2012. As a house publication, it provided exclusive coverage, detailed strategy guides, and promotional content that made it essential reading for Nintendo owners, despite its inherently positive editorial stance.

## Fast facts

- **Publisher:** [Nintendo](/vault/companies/nintendo) of America
- **Run:** 1988-2012
- **Focus:** Nintendo platforms exclusively
- **Tone:** Positive, promotional
- **Extras:** Posters, trading cards, maps

Despite promotional bias, Nintendo Power offered:

## Cultural impact

Nintendo Power shaped gaming culture:

- Created strategy guide expectations
- Established Nintendo brand loyalty
- Influenced magazine extra standards
- Built gaming community before internet

## Legacy

Nintendo Power demonstrated that house magazines could provide genuine value despite inherent conflicts of interest. Its strategy content and physical extras created lasting appeal that pure advertising couldn't achieve.
