**Your Spectrum** ran for 21 issues, **January 1984 to December 1985**, published by **Sportscene Specialist Press Ltd** of 14 Rathbone Place, London W1P 1DE.

It did not close. *[Your Sinclair](/vault/magazines/your-sinclair)* issue 1 is dated **January 1986** — the month after *Your Spectrum*'s last — from the same house, with the numbering restarted.

## Why it has its own entry

Because the join is invisible from either side if you only read one of them. *Your Sinclair*'s issue numbers begin at 1 in 1986, which reads as a launch; *Your Spectrum* stops at 21, which reads as a closure. Together they are one magazine running from January 1984, and the two years before the rename are held here in full.

This is the third such case in this Vault, alongside *[Amiga Format](/vault/magazines/amiga-format)* out of *ST/Amiga Format* and *[CU Amiga](/vault/magazines/cu-amiga)* out of *Commodore User*. A `founded` date on a British magazine is about as likely to mark a relaunch as a beginning, and a reset issue number is what hides it.
