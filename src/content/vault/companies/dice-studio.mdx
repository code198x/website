## Overview

**DICE** (Digital Illusions Creative Entertainment) is a Swedish game studio founded in 1992 with roots in the Scandinavian demo scene. Known for the Battlefield series and Mirror's Edge, DICE exemplifies how Swedish demo scene culture produced world-class technical talent.

## Fast Facts

- **Founded:** 1992
- **Location:** Stockholm, Sweden
- **Origin:** Swedish demo scene
- **Owner:** Electronic Arts (2006+)
- **Known for:** Battlefield, Mirror's Edge, Frostbite
