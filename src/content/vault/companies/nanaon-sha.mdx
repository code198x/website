Music and games as one. NanaOn-Sha formed around Masaya Matsuura's vision of games that were genuinely musical, not just games with music. PaRappa the Rapper delivered that vision with style. The studio continued exploring rhythm gameplay through Um Jammer Lammy and the procedurally generated Vib-Ribbon. Small but influential, they helped define a genre.
