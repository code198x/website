Nazca Corporation existed briefly but left an enormous impact. Founded by former Irem staff who had worked on games like In the Hunt, they brought their expertise in detailed 2D animation to SNK's Neo Geo hardware. Metal Slug, their signature achievement, showcased what dedicated artists could accomplish with the platform's generous sprite capabilities.
