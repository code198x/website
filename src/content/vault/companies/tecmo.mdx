Challenge as identity. Tecmo built a reputation on demanding games—NES Ninja Gaiden's precision platforming, Rygar's action-RPG combat, Dead or Alive's technical fighting. Team Ninja became their premier development studio. The 2010 merger with Koei created a larger entity, but Tecmo's action game legacy continued through Team Ninja's output.
