## Overview

Too ambitious to live. Troika Games assembled Fallout veterans determined to push RPG boundaries. Arcanum blended steampunk with fantasy; Temple of Elemental Evil implemented perfect D&D 3.5 rules; Vampire: Bloodlines captured the World of Darkness. Each game launched broken, found devoted audiences, and failed commercially. The studio closed in 2005, its legacy preserved by modders and cult fans.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1998.
- **Founders:** Tim Cain, Leonard Boyarsky, Jason Anderson.
- **Location:** Irvine, California.
- **Closed:** 2005.
