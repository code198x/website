## Overview

Football management as lifestyle. Sports Interactive began developing Championship Manager with Eidos, creating an obsession-inducing simulation. When the partnership dissolved, they kept the engine and database, launching Football Manager under Sega. The game consumes hundreds of hours per save, tracking real-world players with data scouted globally. For many, it's not a game—it's a second job.
