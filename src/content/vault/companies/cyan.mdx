Brothers Rand and Robyn Miller built worlds meant for exploration, not conquest. Cyan's Myst became the best-selling PC game by trusting players to observe, think, and discover without hand-holding. Their contemplative design philosophy—beautiful worlds, logical puzzles, minimal instruction—created a distinct genre that continues with new projects.
