## Overview

IO Interactive built their reputation on assassination. The *Hitman* series, beginning in 2000, established IO as masters of the stealth sandbox—games where elaborate levels become playgrounds for creative problem-solving. After years under Eidos and Square Enix, IO achieved independence and continues developing the franchise they made famous.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1998 in Copenhagen, Denmark.
- **Debut title:** *Hitman: Codename 47* (2000).
- **Publishers:** Eidos (2000-2009), Square Enix (2009-2017), independent (2017-present).
- **Main franchise:** *Hitman* series (eight mainline entries).
- **Other titles:** *Kane & Lynch*, *Mini Ninjas*, *Freedom Fighters*.

## The Hitman formula

What makes the series distinctive:
- **Sandbox levels:** multiple approaches to every target.
- **Disguises:** blending in through costume changes.
- **Environmental kills:** creative assassination methods.
- **Replayability:** designed for repeated playthroughs.
- **Dark humour:** absurdist undertones beneath serious presentation.

## Independence

IO's journey to freedom:
- **Square Enix ownership:** 2009-2017.
- **Management buyout:** 2017, retaining Hitman rights.
- **World of Assassination:** episodic trilogy (2016-2021).
- **New projects:** including James Bond game.
