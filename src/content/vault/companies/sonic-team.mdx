More than just Sonic. While the hedgehog defined Sega's identity, Sonic Team's output ranged widely: the dreamlike flight of NiGHTS, the pioneering online RPG Phantasy Star Online, the frantic puzzle action of Chu Chu Rocket. Under Yuji Naka's leadership, they combined technical excellence with distinctive design sensibilities.
