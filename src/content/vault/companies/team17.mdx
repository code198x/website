Formed from the merger of 17-Bit Software and Team 7, Team17 emerged from the Amiga scene with technical excellence and business savvy. Games like *Alien Breed* and *Superfrog* established their reputation; *Worms* (1995) created a franchise that sustains the company decades later.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1990, Wakefield, UK.
- **Origins:** [demo scene](/vault/communities/demo-scene) and Amiga game development.
- **Breakthrough:** *[Worms](/vault/games/worms)* (1995) became global phenomenon.
- **Status:** publicly traded, now also a publisher.

## The Ocean deal, 1995

*Worms* did not reach 16 formats on its own. *CU Amiga* reported the reason in **May 1995**:

> Team 17 have signed a deal with **Ocean Software to exclusively distribute their games on all formats**… both Ocean and Team 17 retain their own separate identities… will increase the Team's software distribution from their current **18 countries to 64**… games which fall under the deal will be branded with **both the Ocean and Team 17 logos**.

The magazine put the obvious question to them — "Will Team 17 be swallowed up though?" — and PR manager **Alan Bunker** answered that the branding would be maintained and "we are still 100% independent".

⚠ **This is the hinge of Team17's history and it goes unnoticed**, because the company kept its name, its logo and its staff. A studio that had reached 18 countries under its own steam was, from 1995, inside [Ocean](/vault/companies/ocean-software)'s distribution machine with Ocean's cash behind it — which is what made *Worms* a global franchise instead of a well-liked Amiga game.

It also marks a change in how the company behaved. The Team17 of 1991 is the demo-scene studio running two-person development duos, one of them [Andreas Tadic](/vault/people/andreas-tadic)'s in Sweden. The Team17 of 1996 is conducting a public dispute with *Amiga Power* through legal threats and Usenet, as the [Amiga Power versus Team17](/vault/culture/amiga-power-versus-team17) entry sets out. Same company, same name, different commercial conditions — and dating the deal is what keeps those two readings straight.
