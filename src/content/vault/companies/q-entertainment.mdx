Mizuguchi's independent vision. Q Entertainment formed when Tetsuya Mizuguchi left Sega, continuing his exploration of games as synaesthetic experiences. Lumines launched the PSP with puzzle perfection. Every Extend Extra turned chain reactions into music. The studio delivered consistent quality until Mizuguchi moved on to Enhance, Inc.
