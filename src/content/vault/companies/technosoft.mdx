## Overview

Technosoft earned respect through technical excellence. Their Thunder Force series showcased what dedicated developers could achieve on Sega hardware, with Thunder Force IV standing as one of the Mega Drive's most impressive technical achievements. Beyond shooters, they demonstrated versatility across genres while maintaining consistently high production values.
