**Future Publishing** (later Future plc) is a UK media company founded in 1985 that built an empire of gaming magazines. From Amiga Format to PC Gamer, Future became one of the world's largest gaming media publishers, defining enthusiast coverage across multiple platforms and generations.


## The founder arrived from ZZAP!64

Future's first title appeared in October 1985, and its founder had spent that summer launching somebody else's magazine.

*CRASH* introduced him in March 1985, as [Newsfield](/vault/companies/newsfield) assembled the team for *[ZZAP!64](/vault/magazines/zzap-64)*: "Heading up the editorial team is **Chris Anderson**, who was editor of *PCG* until he heard about *ZZAP! 64*." *ZZAP!64* issue 1 carries his name as editor and is dated May 1985. *[Amstrad Action](/vault/magazines/amstrad-action)* issue 1 is dated **October 1985**.

So the gap between launching *ZZAP!64* in Ludlow for Newsfield and launching *Amstrad Action* in Somerset for himself is about five months.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1985, in **Somerton, Somerset** — not Bath, which came later. Issue 1 of *Amstrad Action* and the order forms in issue 2 both give Somerton, TA11 7PS, and that address holds through early 1986.
- **First title:** *[Amstrad Action](/vault/magazines/amstrad-action)*, October 1985.
- **Key titles:** [Amiga Format](/vault/magazines/amiga-format), PC Gamer, Edge
- **Model:** Platform-specific enthusiast magazines
- **Status:** Still operating (as Future plc)
