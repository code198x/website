**Future Publishing** (later Future plc) is a UK media company founded in 1985 that built an empire of gaming magazines. From Amiga Format to PC Gamer, Future became one of the world's largest gaming media publishers, defining enthusiast coverage across multiple platforms and generations.


## The founder arrived from ZZAP!64

Future's first title appeared in October 1985, and its founder had spent that summer launching somebody else's magazine.

*CRASH* introduced him in March 1985, as [Newsfield](/vault/companies/newsfield) assembled the team for *[ZZAP!64](/vault/magazines/zzap-64)*: "Heading up the editorial team is **Chris Anderson**, who was editor of *PCG* until he heard about *ZZAP! 64*." *ZZAP!64* issue 1 carries his name as editor and is dated May 1985. *[Amstrad Action](/vault/magazines/amstrad-action)* issue 1 is dated **October 1985**.

So the gap between launching *ZZAP!64* in Ludlow for Newsfield and launching *Amstrad Action* in Somerset for himself is about five months.

## Measured from outside, in 1999

Most descriptions of a publisher's size come from the publisher. This one does
not.

When Future bought the German monthly *[PC Player](/vault/magazines/pc-player)*
in September 1999, the magazine explained the buyer to its readers on the
assumption they had not heard of it — *"Wem dieser Name nichts sagen sollte"*,
for anyone to whom the name means nothing:

> "Future ist eines der führenden Unternehmen in der Spielezeitschriftenbranche
> und veröffentlicht alles in allem **108 Magazine**, unter anderem die
> Marktführer in **Großbritannien, Italien, Frankreich und USA**."

108 magazines and the market leader in four countries — Italy among them, which
is worth noting beside this Vault's Italian entries, where the British press
appears as a licensor rather than an owner.

The purchase itself came in at the last moment. *PC Player*'s note says the news
arrived *"praktisch direkt vor dem Belichten unserer Seitenfilme"* — practically
right before the page films went to exposure — and that the magazine had passed
from the **WEKA-Verlagsgruppe** to **Future Verlag GmbH** of Poing, outside
Munich.

Fourteen years after one man left *ZZAP!64* to start a magazine in Somerton, the
company was buying the largest PC games monthly in Germany.
