Starbreeze Studios emerged from the Swedish demo scene and proved that licensed games didn't have to be shovelware. *The Chronicles of Riddick: Escape from Butcher Bay* received acclaim that rivalled its source film. The studio later found success with the cooperative heist series *Payday*, though financial difficulties would follow expansion attempts.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1998 in Uppsala, Sweden (from demo group [Triton](/vault/groups/triton)).
- **Demo scene origins:** members from Triton and other groups.
- **Breakout title:** *The Chronicles of Riddick: Escape from Butcher Bay* (2004).
- **Franchise:** *Payday* series (2011-present).
- **Recent history:** financial restructuring (2019).

## Riddick: the exception

A licensed game done right:
- **Quality surprise:** exceeded expectations for tie-in game.
- **First-person immersion:** Vin Diesel-voiced protagonist.
- **Critical acclaim:** scored higher than the film it promoted.
- **Technical achievement:** impressive visuals for original Xbox.

## Payday success

- **GaaS model:** ongoing content updates.
