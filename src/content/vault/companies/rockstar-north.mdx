**Rockstar North** is the Edinburgh-based development studio responsible for the Grand Theft Auto series. Originally founded as DMA Design in Dundee (1988), the company created Lemmings before pioneering open-world crime games with the original GTA (1997). Acquired by Take-Two in 1999 and rebranded as Rockstar North in 2001, it became one of gaming's most influential studios.

## From Dundee to Edinburgh

DMA Design moved from Dundee to Edinburgh as the team grew. The Dundee connection remained significant—the city's game development scene partly exists because DMA proved world-class games could come from Scotland.
