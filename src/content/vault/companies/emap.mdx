**EMAP** — East Midland Allied Press, trading in this period as *EMAP Business and Computer Publications Ltd* — published a remarkable proportion of the British computer press from a single postcode in Clerkenwell.

## What it published

| Magazine | From |
|---|---|
| *[Computer and Video Games](/vault/magazines/computer-and-video-games)* | 1981 |
| *[Sinclair User](/vault/magazines/sinclair-user)* | 1982 |
| *Commodore User*, later *[CU Amiga](/vault/magazines/cu-amiga)* | 1983 |
| *[ACE](/vault/magazines/ace-magazine)* | 1987 |
| *[The One](/vault/magazines/the-one)* | 1988 |

That list is five of this Vault's magazine entries plus a sixth, and until now none of them said whose they were.

The evidence for the earliest is characteristically indirect. *Computer and Video Games* issue 1, November 1981, does not print a colophon this library can read — but it prints competition rules, and they are open to "anyone except **EMAP** employees and their relatives". A company that has to exclude its own staff from a prize draw has named itself.

## One building, several rivalries

By 1984 the small print gives the address: **Priory Court, 30-32 Farringdon Lane, London EC1R 3AU**, with the earlier C&VG contact a few streets away at Durrant House, Herbal Hill, in the same EC1R postcode.

So *Sinclair User* and *Commodore User* — magazines for two machines whose owners spent the decade arguing about which was better — were produced by one company in one building in Clerkenwell. So, at the top of the market, was *C&VG*, which reviewed both.

⚠ Sharing a publisher is not sharing an editorial line, and nothing here suggests the titles were run as one. The point is narrower: the rivalry the readers felt was, at the corporate level, a portfolio.

## What this entry does not cover

EMAP was a large general publisher long before and long after it sold computer magazines, and this library holds its computing titles rather than its history. `founded: 1981` above is **the year it enters this Vault's story**, not the year the company was formed.
