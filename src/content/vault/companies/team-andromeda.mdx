## Overview

Four years, one franchise, lasting impact. Team Andromeda existed solely to create Panzer Dragoon games for the Saturn. Their trilogy—shooter, refined shooter, full RPG—demonstrated remarkable evolution within a consistent artistic vision. When Saga shipped in 1998, the team dissolved, but their world-building influenced games for decades.
