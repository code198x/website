**Futuremark** is the other studio that fell out of [Future Crew](/vault/groups/future-crew). Where some of the group's members founded [Remedy](/vault/companies/remedy-entertainment) to make games, others built Futuremark to make benchmarks — and there is no cleaner expression of the scene's DNA. A benchmark like *3DMark* is, at heart, a demo: a tightly authored real-time showcase of graphics effects, pushed to wring the most from the hardware. Futuremark simply added a score.

3DMark and its sibling PCMark became the reference tools the whole PC industry used to measure performance, run on millions of machines far beyond the scene that birthed them.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1997, Finland.
- **Origin:** [Future Crew](/vault/groups/future-crew) members.
- **Best known for:** the 3DMark and PCMark benchmark suites.
- **Sibling studio:** Remedy (same scene roots).

## Scene to benchmark

A demo proves what hardware can do; a benchmark measures it. Futuremark's products kept the demoscene's defining skills — real-time effects, ruthless optimisation, visual spectacle on a deadline — and pointed them at a different goal. For years, the way the PC world quantified "fast" was a Future Crew production with a number attached.
