## Overview

Few studios have reinvented themselves as successfully as Naughty Dog. From 16-bit obscurity through PlayStation mascot platformers to cinematic narrative experiences, they've defined each PlayStation generation. Crash, Jak, Drake, Joel—each era brought new characters and new technical achievements.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1984 (as JAM Software).
- **Location:** Santa Monica, California.
- **Owner:** Sony (acquired 2001).
- **Known for:** Crash, Uncharted, Last of Us.
