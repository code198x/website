**Compupress** was a Greek magazine publisher in Athens. Both Greek titles in
this project's reference library are its, and they are not small: together they
account for **21,480 pages**, more than the library holds from France, Hungary,
Spain and Russia combined.

## What it published

*Computer για Όλους* (*Computer for Everyone*) came first. This library does not
hold it, but [*Pixel*](/vault/magazines/pixel) documents it: *Pixel* was spun out
of it in **October 1983** because a questionnaire in its fifth issue found
readers wanted more type-in listings.

*Pixel* ran to **June 1996** — 133 issues. *PC Master* followed in **September
1989** and ran to December 1993, 46 issues, with a cover disk from the first
issue.

The same name signs both. **Νίκος Μανούσος** (Nikos Manousos) is *ΕΚΔΟΤΗΣ*
(publisher) on *Pixel*'s masthead in 1984 and *ΕΚΔΟΤΗΣ-ΔΙΕΥΘΥΝΤΗΣ*
(publisher-director) on *PC Master*'s in 1989. **Βαγγέλης Παπαλιός** appears on
both too, in advertising and public relations on the first and as deputy
director on the second.
