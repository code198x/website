Games with attitude. Devolver Digital emerged as indie publishing's most distinctive voice—their E3 presentations mock corporate gaming; their catalogue favours style, violence, and creative risk. From Hotline Miami's neon ultraviolence to Inscryption's meta-horror, Devolver champions games that wouldn't exist under traditional publishing. The company proves marketing can have personality.
