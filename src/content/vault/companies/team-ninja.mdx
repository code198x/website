## Overview

Tecmo's action specialists. Team Ninja formed around Dead or Alive development and became synonymous with technical excellence and demanding difficulty. Under Itagaki's direction, they revived Ninja Gaiden to critical acclaim. Post-Itagaki, the studio expanded beyond their core franchises, collaborating on Nioh, Fire Emblem Warriors, and other projects.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1995.
- **Parent:** Tecmo (now Koei Tecmo).
- **Founder:** Tomonobu Itagaki.
- **Post-Itagaki:** Yosuke Hayashi.
