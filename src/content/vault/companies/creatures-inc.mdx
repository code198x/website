## Overview

The third owner of Pokémon. Creatures emerged from Ape Inc. and became essential to the franchise's multimedia empire. They co-developed early Pokémon games, created the Stadium series, and have managed the trading card game since its inception. Their one-third ownership stake makes them permanent partners in Pokémon's success.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1995.
- **Origin:** Ape Inc. reorganisation.
- **Role:** Pokémon co-owner (33%).
- **Key product:** Pokémon Trading Card Game.
