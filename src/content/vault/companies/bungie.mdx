## Overview

Bungie began making Mac games before creating the Xbox's flagship franchise. Marathon established their FPS credentials on Apple hardware. Halo: Combat Evolved launched with the original Xbox and defined console shooters for a generation. Destiny pioneered the shared-world shooter concept, blending MMO progression with FPS action.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1991 in Chicago by Alex Seropian.
- **Key figure:** Jason Jones (co-founder, lead designer).
- **Acquired:** By Microsoft in 2000, independent again in 2007.
- **Acquired again:** By Sony in 2022.
- **Notable games:** Marathon, Halo trilogy, Destiny series.

## Marathon era (1990s)

Before Halo:
- **Pathways into Darkness** (1993): Mac-only shooter.
- **Marathon** (1994): Sci-fi FPS with deep lore.
- **Marathon 2: Durandal** (1995): Expanded universe.
- **Myth** series (1997-1998): Real-time tactics.

Marathon pioneered dual-wielding weapons and network multiplayer on Mac.

## Microsoft acquisition

Bungie's acquisition in 2000:
- Originally developing Halo for Mac and PC.
- Microsoft bought company for Xbox launch title.
- Halo became console FPS template.
- Three mainline Halo games under Microsoft.

## Independence and Destiny

After splitting from Microsoft:
- Retained Halo IP with 343 Industries.
- Partnered with Activision for Destiny (2014).
- Created shared-world shooter genre.
- Left Activision in 2019 for self-publishing.
