## Overview

When the Mega Drive needed games to compete with the SNES and emerging 32-bit systems, BlueSky Software delivered. Their pre-rendered visual style in Vectorman proved the ageing hardware could still impress, while licensed titles demonstrated consistent technical competence across Sega's platforms.
