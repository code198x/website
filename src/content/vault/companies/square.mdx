## Overview

Final Fantasy saved Square from bankruptcy and launched a dynasty. The company pushed RPG presentation forward with each entry—Mode 7 airships, pre-rendered backgrounds, full-motion video, orchestral scores. From NES origins to PlayStation dominance, Square defined what Japanese RPGs could achieve before merging with Enix in 2003.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1986.
- **Breakthrough:** Final Fantasy (1987).
