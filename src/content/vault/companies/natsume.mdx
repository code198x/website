## Overview

From action games to farming life. Natsume developed diverse titles before Harvest Moon changed everything—a farming simulation where players grew crops, raised animals, and built relationships. The gentle gameplay loop influenced countless successors including Stardew Valley, establishing an entire genre from what seemed an unlikely concept.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1987.
- **Location:** Japan (dev), US (publishing).
- **Key franchise:** Harvest Moon.
- **Genre created:** Farming simulation.
