## Overview

Turn-based excellence. Intelligent Systems became Nintendo's strategy game specialists, creating Fire Emblem's permadeath tactical combat, Advance Wars' accessible warfare, and Paper Mario's charming RPG adventures. Their games combined deep systems with Nintendo's polish, building devoted fan bases for genres often considered niche.
