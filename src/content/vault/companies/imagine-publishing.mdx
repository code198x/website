**Imagine Publishing** was a specialist magazine house in **Bournemouth**, incorporated on
**23 February 2005** and publishing from **Richmond House, 33 Richmond Hill**. Its first
appearance in this library is a masthead, and it introduces itself:

> Thank you for supporting Imagine Publishing — a new independent specialist magazine
> publisher where quality, innovation and excellence are paramount.

That notice runs in *[Retro Gamer](/vault/magazines/retro-gamer)* **issue 19**, and the
reason it is there is that the magazine had very nearly stopped.

## Fast facts

- **Incorporated:** 23 February 2005; the operating board — Damian Butt, Steven Boyd, Mark Kendrick, Hareesh Dhand and Clifford Jakes — appointed 14 May 2005.
- **Address:** Richmond House, 33 Richmond Hill, Bournemouth, Dorset BH2 6EQ.
- **Registered number:** 05374037.
- **Acquired *Retro Gamer*:** October 2005, from Live Publishing's receivers.
- **Acquired by:** [Future](/vault/companies/future-publishing), October 2016.
- **Dissolved:** 20 March 2018.

## Buying a magazine out of a collapse

*Retro Gamer* launched in January 2004 under **Live Publishing** of Nantwich, and Live's
name is on the masthead through **issue 18**. In August 2005 the company went under. Its
founding editor Martyn Carroll set out what that looked like from the inside in the
magazine's own fiftieth issue:

> Live didn't have enough cash to print the finished issue 19, so the pages just sat there,
> waiting to be uploaded. [...] Live was bankrupt and the receivers had been called in.
> [...] The only good came in October 2005, when Imagine Publishing announced that it had
> acquired the magazine from the receivers.

Freelancers went unpaid, subscribers lost issues they had bought, and the staff lost their
jobs. What Imagine bought was a title with a completed issue that had never been printed and
a readership of roughly eight thousand. **Issue 19 is the first Imagine issue**, and the
break in the masthead is exactly the break in the company.

## Why this Vault cares

*Retro Gamer* is one of the most-cited publications in this library. Its developer interviews
and making-of features are, for a great many British games, the only place a first-hand
account was ever recorded — and every one of those from **issue 19 to 2016** was published by
Imagine. When an entry here rests on a *Retro Gamer* piece, this is the house that
commissioned it.

Imagine ran far more than one title, across technology, photography, film and history, but
that is the part of its output the Vault draws on.

## Absorbed into Future

The end is legible in the public record without needing a press report. On **21 October 2016**
three serving directors of [Future Publishing](/vault/companies/future-publishing) Limited —
Zillah Byng-Thorne, Penelope Ladkin-Brand and Oliver Foster — were appointed to Imagine's
board. Three weeks later, on **11 November 2016**, the registered office moved from Richmond
Hill in Bournemouth to **Quay House, The Ambury, Bath**: Future's own address.

The founders left over the following months, the last of them on **1 January 2017**, and the
company was dissolved on **20 March 2018**. *Retro Gamer* continues under Future, which is
why a masthead from 2005 and a masthead from today name different publishers for the same
magazine.
