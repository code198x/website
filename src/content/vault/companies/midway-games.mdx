## Overview

America's arcade powerhouse. Midway emerged from pinball to dominate 1990s arcades with games built for spectacle and controversy. Mortal Kombat's violence drove sales and Senate hearings. NBA Jam's over-the-top basketball became cultural shorthand. Financial mismanagement ended the company, but their fighting game legacy continued through NetherRealm Studios.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1958 (as Midway Manufacturing).
- **Headquarters:** Chicago, Illinois.
- **Bankruptcy:** 2009.
- **Legacy:** NetherRealm Studios.
