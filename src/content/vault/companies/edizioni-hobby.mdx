**Edizioni Hobby s.r.l.** was a Milanese magazine publisher, of **Via della
Spiga 20, 20121 Milano**, which held the Italian licences to two of
[Newsfield](/vault/companies/newsfield)'s magazines.

It published [*Zzap!*](/vault/magazines/zzap-64) in Italy from **May 1986** and
[*The Games Machine*](/vault/magazines/the-games-machine-italy) from **September
1988**. Both carried the same line in the colophon — **"Su licenza di NEWSFIELD
PUBLICATION"** — and both were registered at the Tribunale di Milano, *Zzap!* as
no. 251 on 10 May 1986 and TGM as no. 587 on 19 September 1988.

## A relationship, not a transaction

TGM's first Italian editorial establishes that the second licence followed the
first. Signed by **Roberto Ferri**, it credits "gli amici della Newsfield che
**ancora una volta** hanno creduto nelle nostre capacità" — the friends at
Newsfield who *once again* believed in our abilities. Two years of publishing
*Zzap!* had earned the next title.

The Italian editions carried translated Newsfield material — Andrew Braybrook's
development diaries, Julian Rignall's reportage, Gary Penn and Gary Liddon — set
around Italian editorial staff and an Italian letters page. Readers wrote in to
argue about *Zzap!*'s scores, in Italian, about British reviews.

## The 1991 handover

Both titles moved to [Xenia Edizioni](/vault/companies/xenia-edizioni) in
**September 1991**, the year Newsfield went into liquidation, and moved in the
same month and the same manner: the September issue of each names Xenia as
publisher while still printing the Newsfield licence line, and from October the
licence line is gone from both. The tribunal registrations, printer, distributor
and advertising agency continued unchanged.

⚠ What the colophons record is that the titles changed hands. Whether Edizioni
Hobby was wound up, renamed, absorbed, or simply sold two magazines and carried
on is not established here, and no Italian corporate record has been consulted.
