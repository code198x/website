**Xenia Edizioni** was a Milanese publisher that took over the two Italian
magazines [Edizioni Hobby](/vault/companies/edizioni-hobby) had been publishing
under licence from [Newsfield](/vault/companies/newsfield) — *Zzap!* and *The
Games Machine* — in **September 1991**.

The handover is legible in the colophons because everything except the publisher
stayed still. The tribunal registration numbers, the printer at Cernusco sul
Naviglio, the distributor on Via Famagosta, the advertising agency at Piazzale
Archinto and the *direttore responsabile* **Roberto Ferri** all carried straight
across. Only two things changed: the name of the *editore*, and — one month
later — the disappearance of the line **"Su licenza di NEWSFIELD PUBLICATION"**.

That timing is not a coincidence. Newsfield went into liquidation in 1991. From
October 1991 the Italian editions stopped crediting a company that no longer
existed, and became Italian magazines outright.

## What it did with them

*Zzap!* ran under Xenia until **December 1993**, ending a year after the British
*Zzap!64* and shortly before *Commodore Force* closed.

[*The Games Machine*](/vault/magazines/the-games-machine-italy) ran until **May
1999** — nearly nine years after the British edition's last issue. Under Xenia
it acquired supplements of its own, including seasonal specials and a bound-in
Amiga section.

⚠ That Amiga supplement was titled *Amiga Action*, which is also the name of an
unrelated British monthly from Gollner Publishing. They are separate
publications: the Italian one is a 13-to-32-page section inside TGM, the British
one a 90-to-160-page magazine in its own right. See
[Amiga Action](/vault/magazines/amiga-action) for the British title.
