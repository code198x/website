## Overview

Insomniac made PlayStation platforms sing. From Spyro's colourful worlds through Ratchet's weapon-filled adventures to Spider-Man's web-swinging, they've consistently delivered polished action games. Unlike sister studio Naughty Dog, they remained independent longer, eventually joining Sony in 2019.

## Fast facts

- **Founded:** 1994.
- **Founder:** Ted Price.
- **Location:** Burbank, California.
- **Owner:** Sony (acquired 2019).
