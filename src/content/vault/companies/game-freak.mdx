## Overview

From fanzine to phenomenon. Game Freak began as a handmade video game magazine created by Satoshi Tajiri, evolved into a development studio, and created Pokémon—inspired by Tajiri's childhood insect collecting. The Game Boy title's six years of development nearly bankrupted them; its success created the most profitable media franchise in history.
