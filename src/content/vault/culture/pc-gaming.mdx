## Overview

PC gaming grew alongside personal computing itself. Unlike closed console ecosystems, the PC's openness enabled modding, user-generated content, and hardware customisation. From text adventures on the IBM PC to modern esports, PC gaming has continuously pushed technical boundaries while fostering unique game genres and communities.

## Fast facts

- **Origins:** IBM PC (1981), but earlier on Apple II, C64.
- **Defining moment:** Doom shareware distribution (1993).
- **Modern market:** Billions in annual revenue.
- **Key platform:** Steam (launched 2003).

## Early PC gaming (1981-1990)

The IBM PC era:
- CGA graphics (4 colours, 320x200).
- PC speaker audio (beeps only).
- Business machine repurposed for games.
- Text adventures dominated (Infocom).

Key games:
- King's Quest (1984): Graphic adventure.
- Flight Simulator (1982): Simulation genre.
- Ultima series: RPG foundation.

## Sound and graphics revolution (1987-1995)

Hardware advancement:
- **Sound Blaster (1989):** FM synthesis, digitised audio.
- **VGA (1987):** 256 colours, 320x200.
- **SVGA:** Higher resolutions emerge.
- **CD-ROM:** FMV and large datasets.

Enabling games:
- Wing Commander (1990): Cinematic action.
- Wolfenstein 3D (1992): FPS genre birth.
- Doom (1993): Industry-defining.

## Shareware distribution

Pre-internet game delivery:
- First episode free via BBS/disks.
- Players share copies.
- Pay for complete game.
- id Software perfected this model.

## 3D acceleration era (1996-2000)

Graphics cards transform gaming:
- 3dfx Voodoo (1996): Hardware 3D.
- Quake (1996): True 3D engine.
- Half-Life (1998): Narrative FPS.
- Online multiplayer via internet.

## PC vs console dynamics

PC advantages:
- Upgradeable hardware.
- Mouse and keyboard precision.
- Modding and user content.
- Backward compatibility.

Console advantages:
- Standardised hardware.
- Lower entry cost.
- Couch gaming.
- Exclusive titles.

## Digital distribution revolution

Steam's impact (2003 onwards):
- Eliminated physical media.
- Enabled indie development.
- Created sales culture.
- Centralised community features.

Other platforms: GOG, Epic Games Store, itch.io.

## PC-first genres

Genres that thrive on PC:

| Genre | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| RTS | Mouse precision essential |
| MMO | Keyboard shortcuts, long sessions |
| Simulation | Complex interfaces |
| Grand strategy | Deep menus, data |
| Modding games | Open file systems |

## Modding culture

PC gaming's unique strength:
- Total conversions (Counter-Strike from Half-Life).
- User maps and levels.
- Community patches.
- Extended game lifespans.

## Modern PC gaming

Current landscape:
- Esports dominance.
- VR development platform.
- Ray tracing graphics.
- Cloud gaming integration.
- Steam Deck portability.

## Legacy

PC gaming proved that open platforms foster innovation. The culture of modding, user content, and community-driven development created genres and practices that influenced all of gaming.
