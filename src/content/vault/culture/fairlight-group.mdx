## Overview
Fairlight began as a Swedish cracking crew before pivoting to demos that pushed hardware to its limits. Their productions showcased polished graphics, custom music, and a signature sense of mischief.

## Fast facts
- **Founders:** Strider (Tony Krvaric) and Black Shadow (Fredrik Segerfalk).  
- **Milestones:** legendary crack intros, Amiga demos like *Elysium*, C64 megademos such as *Edge of Disgrace*, and PC works including *Agenda Circling Forth*.  
- **Style:** high production values, hand-drawn art, and playful scrolltext.

## Legacy
Fairlight remains active, earning Scene.org and Revision awards while mentoring new demosceners. Their transition from piracy to artistry mirrors the pathway many hobbyists take from tinkering to creation.
