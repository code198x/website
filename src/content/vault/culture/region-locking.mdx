Your Japanese cartridge won't fit. Region locking divided the world into territories—games and consoles from one region couldn't work together. Physical cartridge shapes differed. Lockout chips checked region codes. The result: import culture, mod chips, and frustration when desired games never received local releases.

## Fast facts

- **Purpose:** Territorial market control.
- **Methods:** Physical shape, lockout chips.
- **Result:** Import culture, modifications.
- **Modern trend:** Decreasing restrictions.
