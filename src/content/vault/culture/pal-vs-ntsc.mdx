**PAL vs NTSC** describes the regional video standard differences that affected gaming. European PAL systems ran at 50Hz versus NTSC's 60Hz, and where a game's logic was tied to the display rate the result was roughly 17% slower play, wrong-pitched music and black borders unless the conversion was done properly.

⚠ **The 17% is the display difference, not automatically the game's.** *Edge*'s letters page made the distinction in March 1994, arguing that the UK Jaguar was "not 17% slower" because "the game update runs independently of" the 50Hz display, with the visible difference coming from having fewer lines onscreen; the example it gave "runs only 3-5% slower". Whether a PAL conversion lost a sixth of its speed depended on whether it was frame-locked. Many were. Not all were.

## Fast facts

- **NTSC:** 60Hz, 525 lines (USA, Japan)
- **PAL:** 50Hz, 625 lines (Europe, Australia)
- **Speed difference:** up to ~17% where the game is frame-locked; less, sometimes much less, where it is not
- **Optimised PAL:** [Rare](/vault/companies/rare), required extra work
- **Impact:** Music pitch, gameplay speed
