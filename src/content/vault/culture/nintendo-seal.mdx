## Overview

After the 1983 crash, Nintendo controlled everything. The "Official Nintendo Seal of Quality" certified that games met Nintendo's standards—but also ensured Nintendo controlled what appeared on their platform. Publishers needed approval, paid licensing fees, and accepted manufacturing limits. Quality improved; so did Nintendo's profits and power.

## Fast facts

- **Introduced:** 1985 (US NES launch).
- **Purpose:** Quality assurance, platform control.
- **Requirements:** Licensing agreement.
- **Evolution:** Later "Official Nintendo Seal."
