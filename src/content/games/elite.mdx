# Elite

**Elite** was a revolutionary space trading and combat simulator that introduced open-world gameplay, 3D graphics, and procedural generation to home computers in 1984.

## Technical Marvel

Elite achieved the impossible on 8-bit machines:

- Real-time 3D wireframe graphics
- Eight galaxies with 256 star systems each
- All fitting in just 32KB of memory
- Procedural generation creating a vast universe

## Gameplay Innovation

Elite pioneered concepts that define gaming today:

- Open-world exploration
- Non-linear gameplay
- Trading economy
- Moral choices (legal vs. piracy)
- Ship upgrades and progression

## Cultural Impact

Elite inspired a generation of game developers and established the space trading genre. Its influence can be seen in everything from Wing Commander to Eve Online.

## Legacy

The Elite series continues today with Elite Dangerous, but the original's achievement of creating an entire universe in 32KB remains one of programming's greatest accomplishments.
