# Rhythm Engine

**Rhythm Engine** unlocks the full power of the NES's sophisticated 5-channel audio system, teaching advanced sound programming through interactive music creation and rhythm gameplay.

## The Game Concept

Create a complete music laboratory and rhythm game:

- **5-Channel Composition**: Use all NES audio channels simultaneously
- **Rhythm Challenges**: Musical games that test timing and coordination
- **Sound Synthesis**: Create and manipulate audio waveforms
- **Visual Music**: See sound waves and musical patterns
- **Performance Mode**: Real-time musical performance and improvisation

## Why This Game for Tiers 5-8?

**Rhythm Engine** teaches sophisticated NES programming concepts:

- **Audio Programming**: Learn the NES's advanced sound capabilities
- **Real-Time Systems**: Create responsive, precisely-timed applications
- **Data Compression**: Store complex musical data efficiently
- **Multi-Channel Coordination**: Manage multiple simultaneous systems
- **Performance Optimization**: Maintain smooth operation during complex audio

## NES Audio System Mastery

Leverages the complete NES APU (Audio Processing Unit):

- **Pulse Wave Channels (2)**: Melodic lines and harmony
- **Triangle Wave Channel**: Bass lines and percussion
- **Noise Channel**: Drums and sound effects
- **DMC Channel**: Digital samples and voice
- **Advanced Techniques**: Duty cycles, sweeps, and envelope control
