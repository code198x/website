## What You'll Build

Falling blocks puzzle. Tetris-style rotation, stacking, and line clearing. Fill up the grid until you're locked.

Pieces fall. Rotate them. Stack them to form lines. Lines clear, you score. The speed increases. Classic puzzle gaming that teaches rotation algorithms and grid collision.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Piece rotation** — 4 orientations per piece
- **Falling timers** — gravity mechanics
- **Grid collision** — fit detection
- **Line detection** — complete row finding
- **Line clearing** — row removal
- **Next piece preview** — look-ahead
- **Speed progression** — increasing difficulty
- **Ghost piece** — drop preview

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 7: Fangs for the Memory** first.
