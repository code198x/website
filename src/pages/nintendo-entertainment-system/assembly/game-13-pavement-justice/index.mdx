## What You'll Build

Side-scrolling beat 'em up. Walk right, punch enemies, clean up the streets. Vigilante action with Y-depth sorting.

Walk into the screen. Enemies approach from all directions. Punch, kick, throw them. Pick up weapons. Fight bosses. The Y-axis depth sorting creates the illusion of walking into the scene.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Y-axis depth sorting** — proper drawing order
- **Melee combat** — punches, kicks, throws
- **Combo systems** — chained attacks
- **Multiple enemies** — crowd management
- **Health and damage** — combat systems
- **Walking "into" screen** — pseudo-3D movement
- **Stage progression** — advancing through levels
- **Boss battles** — unique encounters

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 12: Iron Assault** first.
