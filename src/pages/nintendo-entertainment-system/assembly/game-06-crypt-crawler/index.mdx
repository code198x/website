## What You'll Build

Flip-screen top-down exploration. Navigate dungeon rooms, collect items, avoid enemies. The ancestor to Zelda-style games.

Explore dark crypts room by room. Find keys to unlock doors. Avoid or fight enemies. This teaches the room-based design that would evolve into action-adventure classics.

## Skills You'll Master

- **4-way or 8-way movement** — top-down controls
- **Room data structures** — level storage
- **Screen transitions** — flip-screen design
- **Persistent state** — remembering room states
- **Simple inventory** — keys and items
- **Enemy chase AI** — following behaviour
- **Key/door mechanics** — locked progression

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 5: Pellet Panic** first.
