## What You'll Build

Side-scrolling shooter. Gradius territory — horizontal scroll, shoot enemies, navigate terrain. Leave a trail of ions in your wake.

The world scrolls. Enemies attack. You fire back. Collect power-ups to upgrade your weapons. This combines scrolling with combat, completing your foundation in NES development.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Horizontal scrolling shooter** — combined mechanics
- **Player in scrolling playfield** — relative positioning
- **Projectiles in scrolling world** — bullet management
- **Enemy waves** — attack patterns
- **Terrain collision** — avoiding walls
- **Power-ups** — weapon upgrades
