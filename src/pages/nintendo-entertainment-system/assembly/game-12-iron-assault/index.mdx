## What You'll Build

Run 'n' gun action. Contra-style platforming with multi-directional shooting. Heavy metal assault.

Run. Jump. Shoot in eight directions while doing both. Enemies attack from everywhere. Grab weapon power-ups. Fight bosses. Bring a friend for two-player co-op. Pure arcade action.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Platformer with shooting** — combined mechanics
- **8-way firing** — multi-directional shooting
- **Shoot while moving** — concurrent actions
- **Enemy gunfire** — enemies shoot back
- **Weapon power-ups** — upgrades
- **Boss encounters** — multi-phase fights
- **Two-player co-op** — simultaneous play
- **Advanced sprite management** — many objects
