## What You'll Build

Advanced mapper game. The MMC5 is Nintendo's most powerful NES mapper — used in Castlevania III (US version) and Just Breed. Push beyond standard NES limitations with extended graphics and audio capabilities.

This post-capstone game teaches the MMC5's unique features: extended nametables, 8×8 attribute mode for finer colour control, an extra sound channel, and split-screen effects. Techniques for the most ambitious homebrew projects.

## Skills You'll Master

- **MMC5 mapper programming** — Nintendo's advanced mapper
- **Extended nametables** — additional background layers
- **8×8 attribute mode** — per-tile colour palettes
- **Extra sound channel** — expanded audio
- **Split-screen effects** — status bar techniques
- **Advanced homebrew techniques** — pushing NES limits
