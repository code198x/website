## What You'll Build

Vertical scrolling shooter. 1942-style planes and formations. The thunder of engines and gunfire.

The sky scrolls vertically. Enemy planes dive in formations. Ground installations fire up at you. Boss planes challenge you at the end of each stage. And now you're using the MMC1 mapper for more graphics.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Vertical scrolling** — new scroll direction
- **Formation patterns** — coordinated enemies
- **Bullet patterns** — enemy fire
- **Ground and air targets** — mixed enemies
- **Power-up collection** — upgrades
- **Boss encounters** — large enemies
- **MMC1 mapper basics** — bank switching
- **Extended CHR banks** — more graphics
