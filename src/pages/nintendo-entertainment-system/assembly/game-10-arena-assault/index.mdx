## What You'll Build

Arena combat. Waves of enemies. Multiple weapons. Survive as long as you can. The arena fights back.

A multi-directional combat game where enemies flood in from all sides. Choose your weapons, manage your ammo, and fight through increasingly brutal waves. This is where your NES skills come together before tackling mapper-based games.

## Why This Game?

Arena Assault bridges the gap between foundation games and mapper games. You've built scrollers, puzzles, and maze games. Now combine it all into a combat arena that pushes the base NES hardware to its limits:

- **Multi-directional movement** — 8-way player control
- **Weapon systems** — multiple fire modes
- **Wave management** — spawning and difficulty curves
- **Score attack** — high score tables and rankings
- **Arena design** — environmental hazards
- **Performance** — many sprites, tight timing

## Skills You'll Master

- **Arena combat** — multi-directional action
- **Wave survival** — enemy spawning systems
- **Weapon systems** — multiple projectile types
- **Score attack** — leaderboards and rankings
- **Sprite management** — handling many objects
- **Performance optimisation** — pushing NROM limits
