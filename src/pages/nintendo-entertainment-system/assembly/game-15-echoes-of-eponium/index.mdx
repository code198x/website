## What You'll Build

Metroidvania. Connected world, ability-gating, backtracking, upgrades. Explore the mysterious ruins of Eponium, where echoes of the past guide your way.

A vast connected world where progress is gated by abilities. Gain new powers from boss battles. Backtrack to explore previously inaccessible areas. This is the formula that defined Metroid and Castlevania.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Large connected world** — seamless map design
- **Non-linear progression** — multiple paths
- **Ability-gating** — powers unlock areas
- **Backtracking** — returning with new abilities
- **Player upgrades** — health, weapons, abilities
- **Save/password system** — progress persistence
- **Boss encounters** — ability-granting fights
- **World design** — pacing and exploration
- **MMC3 mapper** — scanline counter, banking
