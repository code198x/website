## What You'll Build

Audio expansion game. Using Konami's VRC6 mapper — the same chip that powered Castlevania III (Akumajou Densetsu) in Japan — to create music beyond the standard NES sound hardware.

The VRC6 adds three additional sound channels to the NES: two pulse waves and a sawtooth wave. This post-capstone game teaches you to compose and implement enhanced audio for homebrew releases.

## Skills You'll Master

- **VRC6 mapper programming** — Konami's expansion chip
- **Two additional pulse channels** — extended melody options
- **Sawtooth wave channel** — distinctive VRC6 sound
- **Enhanced music composition** — richer arrangements
- **Audio driver development** — multi-chip sound engine
- **Japanese cart compatibility** — Famicom expansion audio
