## Overview
Released by Codemasters in 1986, *Super Robin Hood* set players loose in Nottingham Castle to rescue Maid Marian. The game’s crunchy jumps and inventory-based puzzles previewed the Dizzy series that followed.

## Fast facts
- **Development:** built by the Oliver Twins while they juggled school exams.  
- **Reception:** strong magazine reviews and high chart placements on multiple 8-bit platforms.  
- **Legacy:** proved the twins could deliver polished action at budget prices, leading to a long Codemasters partnership.
