## Overview
Jeff Minter’s *Attack of the Mutant Camels* mixes fast horizontal shooting with giant, laser-spewing ungulates. The game riffs on *Empire Strikes Back*’s AT-AT battles while leaning hard into Llamasoft’s trademark surrealism.

## Fast facts
- **Platforms:** VIC-20 original, C64 and Atari 8-bit conversions.  
- **Structure:** defend your base from waves of colossal camels while dodging enemy fire.  
- **Tech highlight:** sprite multiplexing keeps the screen busy with enemies beyond the hardware’s basic limits.
