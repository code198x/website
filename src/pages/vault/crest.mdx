## Overview
Austrian group Crest earned respect for polished aesthetics and deep technical experiments. Their demos pushed sprite multiplexing, bitmap effects, and music sync beyond what many believed possible on the C64.

## Fast facts
- **Founders:** Crossbow (Hannes Seifert) and Orcan.  
- **Highlights:** *Krestology*, *Royal Arte*, *Deus Ex Machina* collaboration.  
- **Signature:** smooth transitions, advanced bitmap plasmas, and music from composers like Thomas Detert and DeeKay.

## Lesson connections
- Raster Tricks 101 cites Crest routines for multi-layer colour effects.  
- Assembly Acts II–IV use Crest demos when discussing stable raster and cycle-count budgeting.  
- Encourages learners to study demo code as a masterclass in optimisation.

## Legacy
Many Crest members transitioned into professional game and audio careers while still releasing periodic productions. Their work remains a benchmark for modern C64 demo quality.
