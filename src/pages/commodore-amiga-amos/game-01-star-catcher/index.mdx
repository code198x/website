## What You'll Build

Catch falling stars while avoiding hazards. Simple action game showcasing AMOS sprite handling.

Stars drift down from the night sky. Move your basket to catch them. Avoid the meteors that threaten to end your run. This introduces AMOS — the friendly face of Amiga game development. Sprites, sound, and smooth action from line one.

## Skills You'll Master

- **AMOS environment** — editor and commands
- **Screen setup** — display modes
- **Sprite commands** — SPRITE, GET SPRITE
- **Sprite collision** — SPRITE HIT, SPRITE COL
- **Input handling** — JOY, INKEY$
- **Sound playback** — BOOM, SHOOT, SAM PLAY
- **Score display** — on-screen text
- **Menu screens** — game states

## Prerequisites

None — this is your first game!
