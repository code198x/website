You've created games with sound effects. You've played with music sequences. Commercial C64 arcade games have background music that continues smoothly while gunshots, explosions, and power-ups play simultaneously.

**This lesson reveals BASIC's audio channel conflict**—SID voice management. The C64's SID chip has only three voices. Commercial games use interrupt-driven music players that intelligently share voices between music and effects. When you attempt background music plus sound effects in BASIC, you'll discover why it fails: BASIC executes sequentially, so triggering a sound effect POKEs new values to SID voices, overwriting music data and causing the music to stutter or go silent.

The key discovery: **Smooth audio mixing requires interrupt-driven coordination**. BASIC can play music OR effects, but not both simultaneously without conflicts. Every sound effect call clobbers music voices, and restarting music creates audible gaps. This isn't an optimisation problem—it's a fundamental single-threaded execution boundary.

## The One-Minute Tour

**SID Architecture:**
- **Three voices** (Voice 1, Voice 2, Voice 3)
- **Each voice** has frequency, waveform, ADSR envelope registers
- **Voice conflicts** setting Voice 1 frequency for gunshot overwrites music melody
- **No buffering** whatever POKE happens last is what plays

**Music + Effects Goals:**
- **Background music** 3-voice melody using all SID voices
- **Gunshot effect** needs Voice 1 (high-frequency noise)
- **Explosion effect** needs Voices 1, 2, 3 (low rumble + mid boom + high crack)
- **Simultaneous playback** music continues while effects play

**The Conflict Problem:**
- **Music plays** Voice 1 melody, Voice 2 harmony, Voice 3 bass
- **Gunshot fires** POKEs Voice 1 with noise frequency (overwrites melody)
- **Music continues** next note POKEs Voice 1 with melody frequency (cuts gunshot short)
- **Result:** Music stutters, gunshot truncated, neither sounds good

**What You'll Hear:**
- **Music plays normally** until first sound effect
- **Gunshot fires** music Voice 1 goes silent or distorted
- **Music "hiccups"** gaps and glitches when restarting voices
- **Explosion fires** all three music voices go silent simultaneously
- **Unprofessional audio** worse than music-only or effects-only

## Understanding SID Voice Conflicts

**What smooth audio mixing needs:**
```
Frame 1: Music updates Voice 1 (melody note C)
         Music updates Voice 2 (harmony note E)
         Music updates Voice 3 (bass note G)

Frame 2: Music continues (next note)
         Gunshot fires on Voice 1 (HIGH PRIORITY)
         Music system: "Voice 1 busy, skip melody this note"
         Voice 2 and 3 continue harmony/bass

Frame 3: Gunshot complete
         Music system: "Voice 1 free, restore melody"
         All three voices playing again

Result: Smooth music with overlaid sound effect
```

**What BASIC does:**
```
Frame 1: GOSUB 4000 (music)
         POKE 54272,262 (Voice 1 melody = C)
         POKE 54279,330 (Voice 2 harmony = E)
         POKE 54286,196 (Voice 3 bass = G)

Frame 2: GOSUB 1000 (input)
         Player presses SPACE
         GOSUB 5000 (gunshot)
         POKE 54272,200 (Voice 1 gunshot = OVERWRITES MELODY)
         POKE 54276,129 (Voice 1 noise = OVERWRITES WAVEFORM)
         FOR I=1 TO 10 (delay = MUSIC UPDATE BLOCKED)
         GOSUB 4000 (music)
         POKE 54272,294 (Voice 1 next note = OVERWRITES GUNSHOT)
         POKE 54276,33 (Voice 1 pulse = CUTS GUNSHOT SHORT)

Result: Gunshot plays for 10 frames then cut off
        Music melody missing during gunshot
        Audible glitch when music restarts
```

**The fundamental problem:** BASIC has no "voice priority" or "voice busy" concept. Every POKE overwrites the previous value. Music and effects fight for the same hardware registers.

## The Three Voice Limitation

**SID chip architecture:**
```
Voice 1: Registers $D400-$D406 (54272-54278)
Voice 2: Registers $D407-$D40D (54279-54285)
Voice 3: Registers $D408-$D413 (54286-54292)

Each voice has:
- Frequency (2 bytes: low, high)
- Pulse width (2 bytes)
- Control register (waveform, gate, sync, ring)
- Attack/Decay (1 byte)
- Sustain/Release (1 byte)
```

**Music using all three voices:**
```basic
Voice 1: Melody (main tune)
Voice 2: Harmony (chords)
Voice 3: Bass line (rhythm)

Total: 3 voices (SID maximum)
```

**Sound effects needing voices:**
```basic
Gunshot:  1 voice  (high-frequency noise)
Explosion: 3 voices (low rumble + mid boom + high crack)
Power-up: 2 voices (rising sweep + arpeggio)
Jump:     1 voice  (quick chirp)
```

**The conflict:**
```
Music: 3 voices (all used)
Gunshot: 1 voice needed
Result: Must steal Voice 1 from music
        Melody goes silent during gunshot

Music: 3 voices (all used)
Explosion: 3 voices needed
Result: Must steal all voices from music
        Complete music silence during explosion
```

**No workaround exists in BASIC:** You have 3 hardware voices. Both music and effects need them. Something must give.

## Sequential Execution Problem

**Assembly interrupt-driven music:**
```assembly
; Pseudocode (assembly)
; Raster interrupt every frame (50 Hz)
IRQ_MUSIC:
  ; Check if Voice 1 busy with effect
  LDA VOICE1_BUSY
  BNE SKIP_VOICE1
  ; Voice 1 free, update music melody
  LDA MELODY_FREQ_LO
  STA $D400
  LDA MELODY_FREQ_HI
  STA $D401

SKIP_VOICE1:
  ; Always update Voice 2 (harmony)
  LDA HARMONY_FREQ_LO
  STA $D407
  ; ... continue for Voice 3
  RTI

PLAY_GUNSHOT:
  ; Mark Voice 1 busy
  LDA #30
  STA VOICE1_BUSY   ; Busy for 30 frames
  ; Set gunshot frequency
  LDA #200
  STA $D400
  LDA #10
  STA $D401
  RTS

; Each frame, VOICE1_BUSY decrements
; When it hits 0, Voice 1 returns to music
```

**BASIC sequential execution:**
```basic
4000 REM UPDATE MUSIC
4010 REM UPDATE ALL THREE VOICES
4060 POKE 54272,F1:POKE 54273,F1/256  ← Voice 1
4090 POKE 54279,F2:POKE 54280,F2/256  ← Voice 2
4120 POKE 54286,F3:POKE 54287,F3/256  ← Voice 3
4160 RETURN

5000 REM SHOOT SFX
5030 POKE 54272,200:POKE 54273,10     ← Overwrites Voice 1
5060 POKE 54276,128                   ← Turns Voice 1 off
5070 REM CONFLICT: Music will overwrite this next frame
5090 RETURN
```

**The difference:**
- **Assembly:** Interrupt runs independently, checks voice availability
- **BASIC:** Sequential POKEs, no coordination between music/effects

## Voice Conflict Patterns

**Pattern 1: Gunshot (1 voice needed)**
```
Before gunshot:
Voice 1: Melody (C note, pulse wave)
Voice 2: Harmony (E note, pulse wave)
Voice 3: Bass (G note, triangle wave)

Gunshot fires:
Voice 1: Gunshot (200 Hz, noise) ← OVERWRITES MELODY
Voice 2: Harmony (continues)
Voice 3: Bass (continues)

What you hear:
- Gunshot plays
- Melody missing (voice stolen)
- Harmony and bass continue
- 50ms later: Melody returns (gunshot cut short)
```

**Pattern 2: Explosion (3 voices needed)**
```
Before explosion:
Voice 1: Melody
Voice 2: Harmony
Voice 3: Bass

Explosion fires:
Voice 1: Low rumble (50 Hz, noise) ← OVERWRITES MELODY
Voice 2: Mid boom (100 Hz, noise) ← OVERWRITES HARMONY
Voice 3: High crack (150 Hz, noise) ← OVERWRITES BASS

What you hear:
- Explosion plays
- All music silent (all voices stolen)
- 600ms later: Music returns (audible gap and restart hiccup)
```

**Pattern 3: Rapid fire**
```
Frame 1: Gunshot 1 starts (Voice 1)
Frame 5: Gunshot 2 starts (Voice 1) ← OVERWRITES GUNSHOT 1
Frame 9: Gunshot 3 starts (Voice 1) ← OVERWRITES GUNSHOT 2

What you hear:
- Rapid "clicking" (gunshots cut short by next gunshot)
- Melody completely absent (Voice 1 constantly stolen)
- Confusing audio mess
```

## Why Assembly Games Work

**Commercial arcade games (Paradroid, Uridium, etc.):**

1. **Interrupt-driven music player:**
   - Runs at 50 Hz (every frame) via raster interrupt
   - Updates music voices that aren't busy with effects
   - Tracks which voices are available

2. **Voice allocation system:**
   - Music: "I need Voice 1 for melody"
   - Effect: "I need Voice 1 for gunshot (HIGH PRIORITY)"
   - System: "Voice 1 busy for 20 frames, skip melody"
   - Music: "Use Voice 2 for melody substitute"

3. **Frame counting:**
   - Gunshot duration: 20 frames
   - Explosion duration: 30 frames
   - System auto-releases voices after duration

4. **Priority levels:**
   - Explosion: Priority 10 (steals all voices if needed)
   - Gunshot: Priority 5 (steals one voice)
   - Music: Priority 1 (plays when voices free)

**BASIC has none of this:**
- No interrupts (all code runs sequentially)
- No voice allocation (just direct POKEs)
- No frame counting (FOR loop delays block everything)
- No priority system (last POKE wins)

## The Stuttering Effect

**Why music "hiccups" after sound effects:**

```basic
6000 REM EXPLOSION SFX
6140 POKE 54276,128:POKE 54283,128:POKE 54290,128
6150 REM ALL VOICES GATES OFF
6170 RETURN

4000 REM UPDATE MUSIC
4070 POKE 54276,33:REM GATE ON
4100 POKE 54283,33:REM GATE ON
4130 POKE 54290,33:REM GATE ON
```

**What happens:**
1. Explosion turns all three voices OFF (gate=128)
2. 600ms delay (FOR I=1 TO 30 loop)
3. Music update turns all three voices ON (gate=33)
4. **Audible click** as gates re-trigger ADSR envelopes
5. **Gap in audio** 600ms silence before restart

**The problem:** BASIC can't gradually fade explosion while fading in music. It's either explosion OR music, with abrupt switches between them. Assembly games use overlapping fades and smooth voice transitions.

## Comparing Approaches

**Music only (works fine):**
- Three voices playing continuously
- Smooth note transitions
- Professional sound quality
- **No conflicts** ✓

**Sound effects only (works fine):**
- Effects trigger on demand
- Full access to all three voices
- Rich explosion/gunshot sounds
- **No conflicts** ✓

**Music + effects in BASIC:**
- Music stutters when effects fire
- Effects cut short by music updates
- Audible gaps and hiccups
- **Completely broken** ✗

**Music + effects in assembly:**
- Interrupt-driven music player
- Voice allocation system
- Smooth mixing of music and effects
- **Professional quality** ✓

**Conclusion:** BASIC games must choose music OR effects, not both simultaneously.

## Alternative: Music XOR Effects

**What works in BASIC:**

```basic
100 REM GAME WITH AUDIO MODE SWITCHING
110 AM=1:REM AUDIO MODE (1=MUSIC, 2=EFFECTS)

1000 REM DURING GAMEPLAY
1010 IF AM=1 THEN GOSUB 4000:REM MUSIC
1020 IF AM=2 THEN GOSUB 5000:REM CHECK FOR EFFECTS

4000 REM MUSIC MODE
4010 REM PLAY BACKGROUND MUSIC
4020 REM IF ACTION HAPPENS, SWITCH TO EFFECTS MODE
4030 IF BULLET_FIRED THEN AM=2:GOSUB 9100
4040 RETURN

5000 REM EFFECTS MODE
5010 REM PLAY SOUND EFFECT
5020 REM WHEN EFFECT DONE, SWITCH BACK TO MUSIC
5030 IF EFFECT_COMPLETE THEN AM=1:GOSUB 9200
5040 RETURN

9100 REM SILENCE MUSIC
9110 POKE 54276,32:POKE 54283,32:POKE 54290,32
9120 RETURN

9200 REM RESTART MUSIC
9210 MN=0:RESTORE
9220 RETURN
```

**Result:**
- Music plays during quiet moments
- Sound effects play during action
- No simultaneous conflicts
- **Acceptable compromise** (not ideal, but functional)

- **Music uses all three** melody, harmony, bass = full chip utilisation

## From the Vault

**C64 SID Chip:**
- **MOS 6581 SID** (Sound Interface Device)
- **Three voices** only hardware resource for all audio
- **Voice registers:**
  - Voice 1: $D400-$D406 (54272-54278)
  - Voice 2: $D407-$D40D (54279-54285)
  - Voice 3: $D408-$D413 (54286-54292)

**Voice Control Register ($D404, $D40B, $D412):**
```
Bit 0: Gate (0=release, 1=attack)
Bit 1: Sync (synchronise oscillator)
Bit 2: Ring modulation
Bit 3: Test (disable oscillator)
Bits 4-7: Waveform (triangle=16, sawtooth=32, pulse=64, noise=128)
```

**Assembly Music Player Technique:**
- Raster interrupt at 50 Hz (every frame)
- Music player checks voice availability
- Effects set "voice busy" flag with duration counter
- Music skips busy voices or uses alternate voice
- Smooth coordination of music and effects

## Quick Reference

**SID Voice Registers:**
```basic
REM Voice 1 (54272-54278)
POKE 54272,FREQ AND 255     :REM Frequency low byte
POKE 54273,FREQ/256         :REM Frequency high byte
POKE 54276,WAVEFORM+GATE    :REM Control register
POKE 54277,ATTACK*16+DECAY  :REM Attack/Decay
POKE 54278,SUSTAIN*16+RELEASE :REM Sustain/Release

REM Voice 2 (54279-54285)
REM Voice 3 (54286-54292)
REM Same pattern as Voice 1
```

**Waveform Values:**
```
16:  Triangle wave (smooth, mellow)
32:  Sawtooth wave (bright, buzzy)
64:  Pulse wave (variable width, rich)
128: Noise (random, for effects)

Combine with gate:
33:  Pulse + Gate On (note playing)
32:  Pulse + Gate Off (note released)
129: Noise + Gate On (effect playing)
```

**Voice Conflict Example:**
```basic
REM Music playing on Voice 1
POKE 54272,100:POKE 54273,5   ← Melody (frequency 1380)
POKE 54276,33                 ← Pulse wave, gate on

REM Gunshot fires
POKE 54272,200:POKE 54273,10  ← Gunshot (frequency 2760)
POKE 54276,129                ← Noise, gate on

REM Conflict: Melody overwritten, will return next music update
REM Result: Melody silent during gunshot, gunshot cut short by next note
```

**Interrupt-Driven Alternative (Assembly):**
```assembly
; Music interrupt (50 Hz)
IRQ_MUSIC:
  LDA VOICE1_BUSY
  BNE SKIP_V1        ; Skip if busy with effect
  ; Update Voice 1 melody
  LDA MELODY_FREQ_LO
  STA $D400
SKIP_V1:
  ; Always update Voice 2, Voice 3
  RTI
```

**BASIC Compromise (Music XOR Effects):**
```basic
100 AM=1:REM AUDIO MODE (1=MUSIC, 2=EFFECTS)

200 IF AM=1 THEN GOSUB 4000:REM MUSIC
210 IF AM=2 THEN GOSUB 5000:REM EFFECTS

1000 REM WHEN ACTION HAPPENS
1010 AM=2:GOSUB 9100:REM SWITCH TO EFFECTS MODE

5000 REM EFFECTS
5010 REM PLAY EFFECT
5020 IF DONE THEN AM=1:GOSUB 9200:REM BACK TO MUSIC
```
