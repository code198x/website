## Lesson 28 – Sprite Collision

Back to sprites: this time, collisions. The VIC-II hardware tells you when sprites touch other sprites or the background. You’ll read the collision registers and trigger logic when hits occur.

> [📷 suggested: screenshot of two sprites colliding with a colour change]

---

### The One-Minute Tour

- `53278` (SPRITE-Sprite) reports sprite-sprite collisions (bits 0–7).  
- `53279` (SPRITE-Background) reports sprite-background collisions.  
- Reading the registers clears them—always read at the start of a frame.  
- Combine with velocity updates to respond (bounce, damage, etc.).

---

### Collision Demo

```basic
NEW
10 PRINT CHR$(147)
20 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
30 FOR I=896 TO 958:POKE I,170:NEXT I
40 POKE 2040,13:POKE 2041,14
50 POKE 53269,3           : REM enable sprite 0 and 1
60 POKE 53287,2:POKE 53288,7
70 X0=60:Y0=120:VX0=2
80 X1=220:Y1=120:VX1=-2
90 C=PEEK(53278)          : REM clear previous collisions
100 POKE 53248,X0:POKE 53249,Y0
110 POKE 53250,X1:POKE 53251,Y1
120 FOR D=1 TO 20:NEXT D
130 X0=X0+VX0:IF X0<24 OR X0>255 THEN VX0=-VX0
140 X1=X1+VX1:IF X1<24 OR X1>255 THEN VX1=-VX1
150 HIT=PEEK(53278)
160 IF HIT THEN PRINT CHR$(19);"COLLISION!":POKE 53280,2:GOTO 200
170 GOTO 100
200 END
```

Two sprites, opposite velocities. When they collide, the screen prints “COLLISION!” and turns red (`POKE 53280,2`).

---

### Experiment Section

- Use `HIT AND 1`, `HIT AND 2`, etc., to check which sprite was involved.  
- Add sound: trigger a SID routine when `HIT` fires.  
- Combine sprite background collisions (`PEEK 53279`) to detect when bullets hit walls.  
- Reset the background colour after a delay loop for a flash effect.

---

### Concept Expansion

Hardware collision detection saves you from manual overlap math. It’s instant and reliable—just remember to read the register every frame so you don’t miss hits.

---

### Game Integration

- Use collisions for damage: decrement lives when `HIT AND 1` fires (player involved).  
- Trigger sprite explosion animation (Lesson 27) upon collision.  
- Combine with room rules so some rooms have lethal collisions and others don’t.  
- For background collisions, create walls with sprite-background checks to detect when projectiles hit scenery.

---

### Quick Reference

```basic
HIT=PEEK(53278)     : REM sprite-sprite collision register
BG=PEEK(53279)      : REM sprite-background register
IF HIT AND 1 THEN   : REM sprite 0 involved
IF HIT AND 2 THEN   : REM sprite 1 involved
```
