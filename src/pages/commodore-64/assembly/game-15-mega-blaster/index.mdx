## What You'll Build

Turrican-style run'n'gun. Large scrolling world, multiple weapons, vehicle transformations, epic bosses.

This is Turrican. Massive levels that scroll in all directions. A grappling hook for traversal. Transform into a wheel to roll through tight spaces. Beam weapons that sweep arcs of destruction. Hidden secrets everywhere. Boss fights that test everything you've learned.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Multi-directional scrolling** — 8-way movement
- **Advanced weapons** — beam, spread, bounce, missiles
- **Grappling hook** — traversal mechanics
- **Vehicle transformation** — wheel mode
- **Massive bosses** — screen-filling encounters
- **Level streaming** — huge world data
- **Exploration design** — secrets and power-ups
- **Hidden collectibles** — reward thorough play
