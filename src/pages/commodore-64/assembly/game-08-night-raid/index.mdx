## What You'll Build

Scramble-style side-scroller. Fly through terrain, shoot enemies, manage fuel.

Navigate caves, blast ground targets, bomb fuel depots, fight bosses. This is scrolling combined with combat — and resource management adds tension. Your fuel runs low. Do you risk the dive for more?

## Skills You'll Master

- **Scrolling with shooting** — combined mechanics
- **Terrain collision** — cave walls and ground
- **Resource management** — fuel mechanics
- **Ground and air targets** — multiple enemy types
- **Level streaming** — data-driven worlds
- **Boss encounters** — multi-phase fights
- **Bomb mechanics** — gravity-affected projectiles

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 7: Scroll Runner** first.

## Time Investment

64 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 64-96 hours total.
