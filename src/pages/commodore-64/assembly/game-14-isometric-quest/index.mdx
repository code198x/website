## What You'll Build

Isometric adventure game. Explore a 3D-perspective world, solve puzzles, complete quests.

A completely different perspective — literally. Isometric projection transforms flat tiles into a 3D world. But with that comes new challenges: depth sorting, coordinate conversion, collision in three dimensions. Master this and you can build any isometric game.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Isometric projection** — the mathematics of 3D views
- **Coordinate conversion** — world to screen and back
- **Depth sorting** — painter's algorithm
- **Isometric collision** — detection in projected space
- **A* pathfinding** — navigation in isometric maps
- **Z-height handling** — elevated terrain
- **Large worlds** — 64+ interconnected screens
- **Adventure systems** — inventory, dialogue, quests
