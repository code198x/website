## What You'll Build

Space Invaders style fixed shooter. Enemy formation descends, player defends.

The aliens march. You shoot. The tension builds as they get closer. This timeless formula teaches formation AI, destructible shields, and the wave-based difficulty that defines arcade gaming.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Formation movement patterns** — coordinated enemy movement
- **Enemy bullet management** — AI shooting
- **Shields** — destructible character graphics
- **Wave progression** — increasing difficulty
- **High score persistence** — saving scores
- **UFO bonus enemies** — special targets
- **Difficulty scaling** — faster waves

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 5: Brick Basher** first.

## Time Investment

64 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 64-96 hours total.
