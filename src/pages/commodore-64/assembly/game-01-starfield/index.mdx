## What You'll Build

Single-screen space shooter. Player ship versus waves of enemies. Shoot, dodge, survive.

You control a ship at the bottom of the screen. Enemies descend in formations. Blast them before they reach you. Simple concept — but across 128 units, it teaches you every major feature of the Commodore 64: the VIC-II sprite system, SID sound chip, CIA input handling, raster interrupts, and sprite multiplexing.

## Why This Game?

Starfield is your first game on the C64. It starts with nothing more than a sprite on screen and builds — unit by unit — into a commercial-quality shooter with multiple enemy types, power-ups, boss fights, a full SID soundtrack, and raster effects.

By the end, you'll have a real game and a working knowledge of the 6510 instruction set, VIC-II graphics, SID audio, and the raster interrupt techniques that define C64 programming.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Hardware sprites** — enabling, positioning, collision, multiplexing
- **6510 assembly** — from LDA/STA to interrupt-driven code
- **Joystick input** — reading CIA ports
- **SID sound** — effects, music engine, three-voice compositions
- **Raster interrupts** — stable timing, colour bars, sprite multiplexing
- **Custom characters** — tile-based backgrounds and starfields
- **Sprite multiplexing** — more than 8 sprites using raster tricks
- **Game architecture** — state machines, wave systems, boss patterns

## Phases

128 units across 8 phases:

1. **Ignition** (1–16) — Complete game loop with hardware sprites
2. **The Fleet** (17–32) — Multiple enemy types and wave systems
3. **Firepower** (33–48) — Power-ups and sprite allocation
4. **Incoming** (49–64) — Enemies shoot back, raster interrupts
5. **Stardust** (65–80) — Visual polish and custom characters
6. **Waveform** (81–96) — Full SID soundtrack
7. **Command** (97–112) — Boss fights and sprite multiplexing
8. **Launch** (113–128) — Commercial release quality

## Time Investment

128 units at 60–90 minutes each. Roughly 128–192 hours total, spread across 8 phases.
