## Test Your Reflexes!

How fast are your reactions? The C64's TI variable can measure **reaction time** to 1/60th of a second precision—that's 16.67 milliseconds!

Every reaction-based game from Pong to fighter jets uses this exact pattern: show target, measure response time.

## Basic Example

Simple reaction test:

```basic
10 REM *** REACTION TEST ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 PRINT "PRESS ANY KEY WHEN * APPEARS"
40 FOR I=1 TO 60:NEXT I
50 PRINT CHR$(19);"*"
60 TI$="000000"
70 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN GOTO 70
80 RT=TI
90 PRINT "TIME:";RT*17;"MS"
100 END
```

Press any key when the star appears! TI measures jiffies—multiply by 17 to get milliseconds (1/60 × 1000 ≈ 17).

## Practical Example

Random delay before target:

```basic
10 REM *** RANDOM DELAY ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 PRINT "WAIT FOR THE TARGET..."
40 D=INT(RND(1)*180)+60
50 FOR I=1 TO D:NEXT I
60 PRINT CHR$(19);"*** HIT IT! ***"
70 TI$="000000"
80 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN GOTO 80
90 RT=TI
100 PRINT "REACTION:";RT*17;"MS"
110 END
```

Random delay (1-4 seconds) then target appears! This prevents anticipation—you must react to what you see.

## WOW Moment: 5-Round Reaction Challenge

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** REACTION CHALLENGE ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 PRINT "5 ROUNDS - PRESS SPACE WHEN TARGET APPEARS"
40 PRINT:PRINT "PRESS ANY KEY TO START"
50 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN GOTO 50
60 TO=0
70 FOR R=1 TO 5
80 PRINT CHR$(147)
90 PRINT "ROUND";R;"/5"
100 PRINT:PRINT "WAIT FOR TARGET..."
110 D=INT(RND(1)*120)+60
120 FOR I=1 TO D:NEXT I
130 PRINT CHR$(19)
140 PRINT "*** TARGET! ***"
150 TI$="000000"
160 GET K$:IF K$<>" " THEN GOTO 160
170 RT=TI
180 MS=RT*17
190 PRINT:PRINT "TIME:";MS;"MS"
200 TO=TO+MS
210 FOR I=1 TO 90:NEXT I
220 NEXT R
230 PRINT CHR$(147)
240 PRINT "RESULTS:"
250 PRINT "-------"
260 PRINT "AVERAGE:";INT(TO/5);"MS"
270 PRINT
280 IF TO/5<200 THEN PRINT "LIGHTNING FAST!"
290 IF TO/5>=200 AND TO/5<300 THEN PRINT "EXCELLENT!"
300 IF TO/5>=300 AND TO/5<400 THEN PRINT "GOOD!"
310 IF TO/5>=400 THEN PRINT "KEEP PRACTISING!"
320 END
```

![Reaction time test with 5 rounds](/images/lessons/commodore-64/basic/week-5/lesson-36/reaction.png)

Five rounds, random delays, average your reaction time! Under 200ms is lightning fast. The random delay prevents you from anticipating—pure reflex testing!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM REACTION TIMING
TI$="000000"      Reset timer
GET K$            Wait for keypress
RT=TI             Record reaction time
MS=RT*17          Convert to milliseconds

REM RANDOM DELAYS
D=RND(1)*180+60   Random 1-4 seconds
FOR I=1 TO D      Delay loop

REM AVERAGING
TO=TO+MS          Total accumulator
INT(TO/5)         Average of 5 results

REM MILLISECOND CONVERSION
1 jiffy = 1/60 second
1/60 × 1000 = 16.67ms
Use 17 for simplicity
```

## What You've Learnt

- TI measures reaction time precisely
- Multiply jiffies by 17 for milliseconds
- Random delays prevent anticipation
- Average multiple rounds for accuracy
- Reaction speed = game skill testing

Precise timing turns simple keypresses into competitive challenges!
