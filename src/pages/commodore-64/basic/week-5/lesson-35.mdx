## Lesson 35 – Smooth Scrolling

Paging showed us how to jump between slices of a level. Smooth scrolling turns those jumps into glides. By nudging the VIC-II’s fine-scroll registers we can slide an entire map a pixel at a time, then drop in fresh columns as the world moves. No more jerky jumps—just classic C64 motion straight out of a demo party.

### The One-Minute Tour

- Register `53270` (D016) controls horizontal fine scrolling in text mode.
- Scroll from 0 to 7, then shift screen memory by one column and reset.
- Feed new characters from a buffer so the motion never runs out of scenery.

---

### Example Program

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** SMOOTH SCROLL STRIP ***
20 MESSAGE$="   198X HYPERDRIVE ONLINE   "
30 SPEED=3
40 PRINT CHR$(147)
50 BASE=PEEK(53270) AND 248
60 FOR C=0 TO 39:POKE 1024+C,32:NEXT C
70 POS=1
80 CHAR=ASC(MID$(MESSAGE$,POS,1))-32
90 FOR SHIFT=0 TO 7
100 POKE 53270,BASE OR SHIFT
110 FOR W=1 TO SPEED:NEXT W
120 NEXT SHIFT
130 FOR COL=0 TO 38
140 POKE 1024+COL,PEEK(1025+COL)
150 NEXT COL
160 POKE 1024+39,CHAR
170 POS=POS+1:IF POS>LEN(MESSAGE$) THEN POS=1
180 CHAR=ASC(MID$(MESSAGE$,POS,1))-32
190 POKE 53270,BASE
200 GET K$:IF K$=" " THEN END
210 GOTO 90
```

This routine scrolls a marquee endlessly from right to left. Lines 50–60 grab the VIC-II’s current horizontal setting and clear a 40-column buffer. The loop at 90–120 smoothly increases the fine-scroll value, nudging the entire row by one pixel per iteration. Once we reach shift value 7, lines 130–160 move the screen memory left by exactly one character and drop in the next letter from `MESSAGE$`. Resetting the scroll bits (line 190) snaps us back to column alignment, ready to repeat.

> **Tip:** `PEEK(53270) AND 248` clears only the fine-scroll bits (0–2). You keep multicolour and sprite options intact while you animate.

---

### Experiment Section

- Adjust `SPEED` to change the glide rate; lower numbers move faster.
- Load a whole map column from lesson 34 instead of a single character—perfect for side-on caverns.
- Add vertical scroll by poking `53265` (D011) with values 0–7 inside the same loop.
- Insert `GET K$` checks inside the fine-scroll loop to let the player pause or accelerate the camera.

### Concept Expansion

Smooth scroll is the bridge between static maps and living worlds. Once you are comfortable feeding fresh columns into screen memory, you can scroll backgrounds, parallax layers, HUDs, or status tapes. Tie the scroll amount to the player’s velocity and the camera will finally follow the action instead of teleporting between pages.

---

### Game Integration

Imagine a runner game that keeps a buffer of upcoming tiles:

```basic
FOR R=0 TO 9
  POKE 1024+R*40+39,MAP(R,VIEWX+39)
NEXT R
```

Combine this write with the fine-scroll loop so every glide pulls in new terrain from your `DATA` map. The player feels continuous motion even though you still store the world in chunky tiles.

### Quick Reference

```basic
REM Clear fine scroll bits but keep mode flags
BASE=PEEK(53270) AND 248

REM Horizontal fine scroll (0-7)
FOR SHIFT=0 TO 7
  POKE 53270,BASE OR SHIFT
NEXT SHIFT

REM Shift screen one column left
FOR COL=0 TO 38
  POKE 1024+COL,PEEK(1025+COL)
NEXT COL
```

### What You’ve Learnt

- Fine-scroll registers let you move the entire screen a pixel at a time.
- Combining hardware scroll with memory shuffling creates seamless motion.
- Buffering new characters or tiles keeps the scroll continuous and interesting.
