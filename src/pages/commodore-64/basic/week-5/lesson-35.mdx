## Fire at Will!

Moving sprites and dodging enemies is brilliant—but what about **fighting back**? Fire bullets upwards, hit targets, and watch them disappear!

Every space shooter from Space Invaders to Galaga uses this exact pattern: fire projectile, check collision, destroy target.

## Basic Example

Simple bullet moving upwards:

```basic
10 REM *** BULLET UP ***
20 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
30 POKE 2040,13:POKE 53269,1
40 POKE 53287,1
50 X=160:Y=200
60 Y=Y-5
70 POKE 53248,X:POKE 53249,Y
80 IF Y>50 THEN GOTO 60
90 PRINT "MISSED!"
100 END
```

Press RUN and watch the bullet fly upwards! Simple vertical movement.

## Practical Example

Fire bullet with spacebar:

```basic
10 REM *** FIRE BULLET ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
40 POKE 2040,13:POKE 53269,1
50 POKE 53287,1
60 BX=160:BY=200:F=0
70 GET K$
80 IF K$=" " AND F=0 THEN F=1:BY=200
90 IF F=1 THEN BY=BY-5
100 IF BY<50 THEN F=0:BY=200
110 POKE 53248,BX:POKE 53249,BY
120 GOTO 70
```

Press SPACE to fire! F tracks if bullet is active (0=ready, 1=flying).

## WOW Moment: Target Shooting

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** TARGET SHOOTER ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 PRINT "PRESS SPACE TO FIRE"
40 REM SETUP SPRITES
50 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
60 POKE 2040,13:POKE 2041,13
70 POKE 53269,3:POKE 53287,1:POKE 53288,2
80 REM PLAYER & BULLET
90 PX=160:BY=200:F=0
100 REM TARGETS
110 FOR I=0 TO 4
120 POKE 1100+I*5,81
130 POKE 55396+I*5,2
140 NEXT I
150 SC=0:TA=5
160 GET K$
170 IF K$="A" THEN PX=PX-3
180 IF K$="D" THEN PX=PX+3
190 IF K$=" " AND F=0 THEN F=1:BX=PX:BY=200
200 REM MOVE BULLET
210 IF F=1 THEN BY=BY-5
220 IF BY<50 THEN F=0
230 REM CHECK HITS
240 IF F=1 THEN P=1024+INT(BY/8)*40+INT(BX/8)
250 IF F=1 AND PEEK(P)=81 THEN POKE P,32:SC=SC+10:F=0:TA=TA-1
260 REM DISPLAY
270 POKE 53248,PX:POKE 53249,200
280 IF F=1 THEN POKE 53250,BX:POKE 53251,BY
290 PRINT CHR$(19)
300 PRINT "SCORE:";SC;" TARGETS:";TA
310 IF TA=0 THEN PRINT "ALL DESTROYED!":END
320 GOTO 160
```

![Space shooter with targets](/images/lessons/commodore-64/basic/week-5/lesson-35/shooter.gif)

Move with A/D, fire with SPACE, and watch targets vanish! Destroy all 5 targets to win. This is pure arcade shooting action!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM BULLET MECHANICS
F=0              Bullet ready
F=1              Bullet flying
IF F=0 THEN...   Only fire when ready

REM FIRING PATTERN
IF K$=" " AND F=0 THEN F=1:BY=200
IF F=1 THEN BY=BY-5     Move bullet up
IF BY<50 THEN F=0       Reset at top

REM HIT DETECTION
P=1024+INT(BY/8)*40+INT(BX/8)  Bullet position
IF PEEK(P)=81 THEN...          Hit target (81=dot)
POKE P,32                      Remove target

REM TWO SPRITES
POKE 53269,3     Enable sprites 0 & 1
Sprite 0 = player (53248/53249)
Sprite 1 = bullet (53250/53251)
```

## What You've Learnt

- Fire projectiles with spacebar
- Track bullet state (ready/flying)
- Detect bullet-target collisions
- Remove hit targets from screen
- Use two sprites (player + bullet)

Shooting mechanics bring action games to life!
