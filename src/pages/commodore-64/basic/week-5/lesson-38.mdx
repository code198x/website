## Jump Like Manic Miner!

Platformer games need **gravity**—sprites fall down unless they're standing on something. Add a jump, and you've got Manic Miner, Jet Set Willy, or any classic platform game!

Every platformer from Donkey Kong to Mario uses this exact pattern: gravity pulls down, jump pushes up, land on platforms.

## Basic Example

Simple gravity—sprite falls:

```basic
10 REM *** GRAVITY FALL ***
20 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
30 POKE 2040,13:POKE 53269,1
40 POKE 53287,7
50 X=160:Y=50
60 VY=0
70 VY=VY+1
80 Y=Y+VY
90 IF Y>200 THEN Y=200:VY=0
100 POKE 53248,X:POKE 53249,Y
110 FOR D=1 TO 5:NEXT D
120 GOTO 70
```

Gravity constantly pulls down! VY increases every frame (acceleration). When Y hits the ground (200), stop falling.

## Practical Example

Add jump—press space to go up:

```basic
10 REM *** GRAVITY + JUMP ***
20 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
30 POKE 2040,13:POKE 53269,1
40 POKE 53287,14
50 X=160:Y=200
60 VY=0:ON=1
70 GET K$
80 IF K$=" " AND ON=1 THEN VY=-8:ON=0
90 VY=VY+1
100 Y=Y+VY
110 IF Y>200 THEN Y=200:VY=0:ON=1
120 POKE 53248,X:POKE 53249,Y
130 FOR D=1 TO 3:NEXT D
140 GOTO 70
```

Press space to jump! VY=-8 creates upward velocity. Gravity (+1 every frame) pulls it back down. ON flag prevents mid-air jumping.

## WOW Moment: Platform Jumper

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** PLATFORM JUMPER ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 PRINT "SPACE=JUMP  A/D=MOVE"
40 FOR I=0 TO 23:POKE 1024+I*40+5,160:NEXT I
50 FOR I=0 TO 23:POKE 1024+I*40+34,160:NEXT I
60 FOR I=10 TO 15:POKE 1024+15*40+I,160:NEXT I
70 FOR I=25 TO 30:POKE 1024+15*40+I,160:NEXT I
80 FOR I=5 TO 39:POKE 1024+23*40+I,160:NEXT I
90 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
100 POKE 2040,13:POKE 53287,3
110 POKE 53269,1
120 X=100:Y=170
130 VY=0:ON=1
140 GET K$
150 IF K$=" " AND ON=1 THEN VY=-9:ON=0
160 IF K$="A" THEN X=X-2
170 IF K$="D" THEN X=X+2
180 VY=VY+1
190 IF VY>5 THEN VY=5
200 Y=Y+VY
210 REM CHECK PLATFORMS
220 PX=INT(X/8):PY=INT(Y/8)
230 C=PEEK(1024+PY*40+PX)
240 IF C=160 AND VY>0 THEN Y=PY*8:VY=0:ON=1
250 REM FALL OFF BOTTOM
260 IF Y>229 THEN PRINT CHR$(147);"FELL!":GOTO 280
270 IF X<48 OR X>272 THEN X=100:Y=170:VY=0
280 POKE 53248,X:POKE 53249,Y
290 FOR D=1 TO 3:NEXT D
300 GOTO 140
```

Jump between platforms! Gravity constantly pulls down. Space creates upward velocity that decreases each frame (arc). PEEK detects platform characters (160). Fall off the bottom and you restart!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM GRAVITY PHYSICS
VY=0                Starting velocity
VY=VY+1             Gravity acceleration
Y=Y+VY              Apply velocity
IF VY>5 THEN VY=5   Terminal velocity

REM JUMP MECHANICS
VY=-8               Jump velocity (negative = up)
ON=1                On ground flag
IF ON=1 THEN...     Can only jump on ground

REM PLATFORM DETECTION
PX=INT(X/8)         Convert to character X
PY=INT(Y/8)         Convert to character Y
C=PEEK(1024+PY*40+PX)  Read screen character
IF C=160 THEN...    Platform detected

REM LANDING
IF C=160 AND VY>0   Platform + falling down
Y=PY*8              Snap to platform top
VY=0:ON=1           Stop falling, enable jump
```

## What You've Learnt

- VY creates gravity acceleration
- Negative velocity = jump upward
- Gravity pulls velocity back down
- PEEK detects platform characters
- ON flag prevents mid-air jumping

Gravity + jump = platformer gameplay!
