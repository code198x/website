## Face the Ultimate Challenge!

Boss fights defined memorable moments—**R-Type**'s screen-filling monsters, **Salamander**'s multi-stage battles, **Uridium**'s armoured destroyers. A boss with health, patterns, and phases creates the ultimate test!

You'll create a boss enemy with health bar, multiple attack patterns, and victory condition. This is the climactic battle that made C64 games legendary!

## Basic Example

Simple health tracking:

```basic
10 REM *** BASIC BOSS ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 BH=10:X=15
40 PRINT CHR$(19)
50 PRINT "BOSS HEALTH:";BH
60 PRINT
70 PRINT "PLAYER:";X
80 PRINT
90 PRINT "PRESS SPACE TO ATTACK"
100 GET K$:IF K$<>" " THEN GOTO 40
110 BH=BH-1
120 PRINT "HIT! BOSS HEALTH:";BH
130 IF BH>0 THEN FOR I=1 TO 500:NEXT I:GOTO 40
140 PRINT "BOSS DEFEATED!"
```

The boss has 10 health points! Each spacebar press reduces BH by 1. When BH=0, victory! But there's no visual boss, no attack pattern, no challenge!

## Practical Example

Boss with movement pattern:

```basic
10 REM *** MOVING BOSS ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 BH=15:BX=20:PX=10:DX=1
40 PRINT CHR$(19)
50 PRINT "BOSS HP:";BH;"  YOUR POS:";PX
60 PRINT
70 FOR I=0 TO 30
80 IF I=BX THEN PRINT "B";
90 IF I=PX THEN PRINT "P";
100 IF I<>BX AND I<>PX THEN PRINT ".";
110 NEXT I
120 PRINT
130 PRINT "SPACE=SHOOT A/D=MOVE"
140 REM *** BOSS MOVES ***
150 BX=BX+DX
160 IF BX>28 OR BX<5 THEN DX=-DX
170 REM *** INPUT ***
180 GET K$
190 IF K$="D" THEN PX=PX+1
200 IF K$="A" THEN PX=PX-1
210 IF K$=" " AND PX=BX THEN BH=BH-1:PRINT "HIT!"
220 IF BH>0 THEN GOTO 40
230 PRINT CHR$(147)
240 PRINT "BOSS DEFEATED!"
```

Now the boss moves back and forth! You must position yourself (PX) where the boss (BX) will be and press space. The boss reverses direction at edges (DX=-DX)!

## WOW Moment: Boss with Health Bar and Patterns

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** BOSS BATTLE ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 POKE 53280,2:POKE 53281,8
40 REM *** INIT ***
50 BH=20:BX=15:PX=10
60 DX=1:AT=0:PH=1
70 REM *** MAIN LOOP ***
80 PRINT CHR$(19)
90 REM *** HEALTH BAR ***
100 PRINT "BOSS: ";
110 FOR I=1 TO BH
120 PRINT CHR$(18);" ";CHR$(146);
130 NEXT I
140 PRINT
150 PRINT "PHASE:";PH;" PATTERN:";
160 IF PH=1 THEN PRINT "SLOW"
170 IF PH=2 THEN PRINT "FAST"
180 IF PH=3 THEN PRINT "ERRATIC"
190 PRINT
200 REM *** ARENA ***
210 FOR I=0 TO 30
220 IF I=BX THEN PRINT CHR$(18);"B";CHR$(146);
230 IF I=PX THEN PRINT "P";
240 IF I<>BX AND I<>PX THEN PRINT ".";
250 NEXT I
260 PRINT
270 REM *** ATTACK INDICATOR ***
280 AT=AT+1
290 IF AT>50 THEN AT=0:PRINT "*** BOSS ATTACKS! ***":FOR J=1 TO 500:NEXT J
300 PRINT
310 PRINT "SPACE=SHOOT A/D=MOVE"
320 REM *** PHASE LOGIC ***
330 IF BH<15 AND PH=1 THEN PH=2:DX=2:POKE 53280,10
340 IF BH<8 AND PH=2 THEN PH=3:POKE 53280,7
350 REM *** BOSS MOVEMENT ***
360 IF PH=1 THEN BX=BX+DX
370 IF PH=2 THEN BX=BX+DX
380 IF PH=3 THEN BX=BX+(INT(RND(1)*3)-1)*2
390 IF BX>28 OR BX<5 THEN DX=-DX
400 REM *** INPUT ***
410 GET K$
420 IF K$="D" AND PX<30 THEN PX=PX+1
430 IF K$="A" AND PX>0 THEN PX=PX-1
440 IF K$=" " AND ABS(PX-BX)<3 THEN BH=BH-1:PRINT "HIT!":POKE 53280,1:FOR J=1 TO 100:NEXT J:POKE 53280,2
450 IF BH>0 THEN GOTO 80
460 REM *** VICTORY ***
470 PRINT CHR$(147)
480 POKE 53280,5:POKE 53281,13
490 PRINT
500 PRINT "    *** BOSS DEFEATED! ***"
510 PRINT
520 PRINT "      YOU ARE VICTORIOUS!"
```

![Boss fight with phases and health bar](/images/lessons/commodore-64/basic/week-7/lesson-54/boss.gif)

A complete boss battle system with THREE phases:

- **PHASE 1 (HP 20-15)**: Slow, predictable movement (DX=1)
- **PHASE 2 (HP 14-8)**: Fast movement (DX=2), border turns green
- **PHASE 3 (HP 7-0)**: Erratic random movement, border turns yellow

The health bar shows remaining HP with reverse video blocks. The boss attacks every 50 frames (AT counter). Hit detection requires being within 3 spaces (ABS(PX-BX)&lt;3) and pressing space!

Each phase changes behaviour and visual appearance. Phase transitions happen automatically when health thresholds are reached. The flash on hit (border briefly white) gives immediate feedback!

Victory screen appears when boss health reaches 0, with triumphant colours and message!

Try adding boss attacks that reduce player health, more movement patterns, or multiple bosses in sequence!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM BOSS STRUCTURE
BH=20                       Boss health
BX=15                       Boss X position
PH=1                        Current phase

REM HEALTH BAR
FOR I=1 TO BH
PRINT CHR$(18);" ";CHR$(146)    Filled block
NEXT I

REM PHASE TRANSITIONS
IF BH<15 AND PH=1 THEN PH=2    Threshold check
Change speed/pattern/colour

REM ATTACK PATTERNS
PH=1: BX=BX+1               Slow steady
PH=2: BX=BX+2               Fast steady
PH=3: BX=BX+RND*3-1         Erratic random
PH=4: Teleport to random    Warp pattern

REM HIT DETECTION
IF ABS(PX-BX)<3 THEN...     Within range
BH=BH-1                     Reduce health
Flash/sound feedback

REM VICTORY CONDITION
IF BH<=0 THEN...            Boss defeated
Show victory screen
Award points/unlock next

REM BOSS BEST PRACTICES
- Visual health bar (player sees progress)
- Multiple phases (keeps fight interesting)
- Pattern changes (requires adaptation)
- Attack warnings (fairness)
- Victory fanfare (celebration)
```

## What You've Learnt

- Boss enemies have multiple health points
- Health bars show damage progress visually
- Phases change behaviour at thresholds
- Attack patterns require player adaptation
- Hit detection with range tolerance
- Victory conditions end the battle

Every epic C64 game featured boss battles—now you can create them!
