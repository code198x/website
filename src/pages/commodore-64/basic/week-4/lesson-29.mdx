## Surprise Every Time!

Predictable games get boring fast—**randomness** keeps players guessing! RND generates random numbers, perfect for spawning items in unpredictable locations.

Every game from Space Invaders to Boulder Dash uses randomness to create variety and replay value.

## Basic Example

Random position:

```basic
10 REM *** RANDOM DOT ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 X=INT(RND(1)*40)
40 Y=INT(RND(1)*25)
50 POKE 1024+Y*40+X,81
60 POKE 55296+Y*40+X,7
70 FOR I=1 TO 500:NEXT I
80 GOTO 30
```

Dots appear everywhere! RND(1) gives 0 to 0.999..., INT truncates to whole number, multiply for range.

## Practical Example

Random spawn with limits:

```basic
10 REM *** RANDOM SPAWN ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 FOR I=1 TO 10
40 X=INT(RND(1)*30)+5
50 Y=INT(RND(1)*20)+3
60 POKE 1024+Y*40+X,81
70 POKE 55296+Y*40+X,INT(RND(1)*16)
80 NEXT I
90 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN GOTO 90
100 GOTO 20
```

10 dots with random colors in safe zone! Press any key to respawn.

## WOW Moment: Collect Random Dots

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** RANDOM COLLECTOR ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 PRINT "SCORE:000"
40 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
50 POKE 2040,13:POKE 53269,1:POKE 53287,14
60 X=160:Y=120:SC=0
70 REM SPAWN 5 RANDOM DOTS
80 FOR I=0 TO 4
90 DX=INT(RND(1)*35)+3
100 DY=INT(RND(1)*20)+4
110 POKE 1024+DY*40+DX,81
120 POKE 55296+DY*40+DX,7
130 NEXT I
140 GET K$
150 IF K$="W" THEN Y=Y-3
160 IF K$="S" THEN Y=Y+3
170 IF K$="A" THEN X=X-3
180 IF K$="D" THEN X=X+3
190 POKE 53248,X:POKE 53249,Y
200 REM CHECK COLLECT
210 P=1024+INT(Y/8)*40+INT(X/8)
220 IF PEEK(P)=81 THEN POKE P,32:SC=SC+10
230 REM UPDATE SCORE
240 POKE 1030,SC/100+48
250 POKE 1031,(SC/10) AND 15+48
260 POKE 1032,SC AND 15+48
270 REM RESPAWN IF ALL GONE
280 IF SC=50 THEN SC=0:GOTO 70
290 GOTO 140
```

Every time you collect all 5 dots, they respawn in completely different positions! No two games are the same. This is the secret to infinite replay value!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM RANDOM BASICS
RND(1)              Random 0 to 0.999...
INT(RND(1)*N)       Random 0 to N-1
INT(RND(1)*N)+M     Random M to M+N-1

REM COMMON RANGES
INT(RND(1)*40)         0-39 (screen width)
INT(RND(1)*25)         0-24 (screen height)
INT(RND(1)*16)         0-15 (colors)
INT(RND(1)*100)+1      1-100 (percentage)

REM SAFE ZONES
+5  Skip edges
-10 Keep away from boundaries
```

## What You've Learnt

- RND(1) creates random decimals
- INT converts to whole numbers
- Multiply and add for ranges
- Random spawning = replayability
- Safe zones avoid edge problems

Randomness transforms static levels into endless variety!
