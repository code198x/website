## Lesson 45 – Power-Ups

Power-ups give players micro-goals between waves of enemies. They’re just another state machine: waiting to spawn, active on the field, empowering the player, then cooling down. Let’s wire one up that boosts movement speed for a few seconds.

> [📷 suggested: player sprite glowing after grabbing the power-up]

---

### The One-Minute Tour

- Keep separate timers for spawn delay and effect duration.
- Flip the player’s attributes (speed, colour, score multiplier) the instant the collision fires.
- Always provide feedback when the buff ends so players know they’re back to normal.

---

### Example Program

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** POWER-UP LOOP ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147);"WASD MOVE  SPACE QUIT"
30 FOR I=832 TO 895:POKE I,0:NEXT I
40 POKE 2040,13:POKE 2041,16
50 POKE 53269,3:POKE 53287,12:POKE 53288,7
60 PX=160:PY=200:SPD=3
70 PUX=120:PUY=120:PU_STATE=0:SPAWN=180:BUFF=0
80 R=RND(-TI)
90 GET K$:IF K$=" " THEN END
100 IF K$="A" THEN PX=PX-SPD
110 IF K$="D" THEN PX=PX+SPD
120 IF K$="W" THEN PY=PY-SPD
130 IF K$="S" THEN PY=PY+SPD
140 IF PX<24 THEN PX=24
150 IF PX>320 THEN PX=320
160 IF PY<80 THEN PY=80
170 IF PY>233 THEN PY=233
180 POKE 53248,PX:POKE 53249,PY
190 IF PU_STATE=0 THEN GOSUB 400
200 IF PU_STATE=1 THEN GOSUB 430
210 IF PU_STATE=2 THEN GOSUB 520
220 COL=PEEK(53279)
230 IF PU_STATE=1 AND (COL AND 1) AND (COL AND 2) THEN GOSUB 560
240 PRINT CHR$(19);"BUFF:";:IF PU_STATE=2 THEN PRINT "SPEED"; ELSE PRINT "--   ";
250 PRINT TAB(18);"SPAWN:";SPAWN;"   ";
260 PRINT TAB(32);"SPEED:";SPD;"   "
270 FOR D=1 TO 3:NEXT D
280 GOTO 90
400 REM *** COUNTDOWN TO SPAWN ***
410 SPAWN=SPAWN-1:IF SPAWN<=0 THEN GOSUB 440
420 RETURN
430 REM *** DRAW POWER-UP ***
440 POKE 53250,PUX:POKE 53251,PUY
450 RETURN
460 REM *** CREATE POWER-UP ***
470 PUX=40+INT(RND(1)*240)
480 PUY=70+INT(RND(1)*120)
490 POKE 53288,10:PU_STATE=1:SPAWN=0
500 RETURN
520 REM *** BUFF ACTIVE ***
530 BUFF=BUFF-1
540 IF BUFF<=0 THEN GOSUB 580
550 RETURN
560 REM *** APPLY BUFF ***
570 PU_STATE=2:BUFF=180:SPAWN=240:SPD=6
580 POKE 53288,14:POKE 53251,0
590 RETURN
580 REM *** CLEAR BUFF ***
590 PU_STATE=0:SPD=3:POKE 53288,7
600 RETURN
```

Sample output (buff active):

```
BUFF:SPEED   SPAWN:240           SPEED:6
```

While the power-up waits to spawn (`PU_STATE=0`), the countdown (lines 400–420) ticks down. When it hits zero, the spawn routine (460–500) positions the sprite on screen and switches to the active state. Once you collide with it, the apply routine (560–590) boosts your speed, hides the sprite, and starts the buff timer. When `BUFF` reaches zero, the clear routine (580–600) restores normal speed and sets a new spawn delay.

> **Tip:** Keep buff timers short so players feel a burst of power, not a permanent upgrade. That contrast is what makes power-ups exciting.

### Experiment Section

- Add a second buff that doubles score gains—toggle between them with a random pick at spawn.
- Make the power-up blink for the last 30 frames of its lifetime so players know it’s about to expire.
- Let the player cancel the buff early with a keypress, re-arming the spawn timer immediately.
- Drop the spawn delay as levels progress to create frantic bonus windows.

### Concept Expansion

Power-ups are the friendly side of state machines. Treat them just like enemies: give them states, timers, collision responses, and decay rules. Add sounds, palette changes, or HUD icons to make them sing.

### Quick Reference

```basic
REM Spawn countdown
IF PU_STATE=0 THEN SPAWN=SPAWN-1
IF SPAWN=0 THEN GOSUB 4200

REM Buff timer
IF PU_STATE=2 THEN BUFF=BUFF-1:IF BUFF=0 THEN GOSUB 4600

REM Collision test
COL=PEEK(53279)
IF PU_STATE=1 AND (COL AND PLAYER) AND (COL AND POWER) THEN GOSUB 4400
```

Reserve lines `4200`, `4400`, and `4600` for the spawn, apply, and clear routines (label each with a `REM` comment) so the flow stays readable.

### What You’ve Learnt

- Power-ups cycle through spawn, active, effect, and cooldown states just like enemies.
- Sprite collisions make pickups trivial—no need for complex geometry.
- Buff timers need feedback both when they start and when they end.
