## Welcome to Week 6: Advanced Techniques!

Every arcade game, demo, and title screen uses **smooth scrolling text**—those credits that glide across the screen like cinema!

The C64 has hardware scroll registers that shift the entire screen pixel-by-pixel. You're not redrawing characters—you're physically sliding the display! This is the secret behind demo scene effects and professional-looking presentations.

## Basic Example

Enable horizontal scroll:

```basic
10 REM *** HARDWARE SCROLL ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 POKE 53270,PEEK(53270)OR16
40 FOR S=0 TO 7
50 POKE 53270,(PEEK(53270)AND240)OR S
60 FOR D=1 TO 50:NEXT D
70 NEXT S
80 GOTO 40
```

The screen slides left! POKE 53270 controls horizontal fine scroll (0-7 pixels). When you loop 0 to 7, the display smoothly shifts one character width to the left.

## Practical Example

Scroll a message:

```basic
10 REM *** SCROLLING MESSAGE ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 MS$="*** WELCOME TO THE C64 ***"
40 MS$=MS$+SPACE$(40)
50 POKE 53270,PEEK(53270)OR16
60 P=1
70 FOR S=0 TO 7
80 POKE 53270,(PEEK(53270)AND240)OR S
90 FOR D=1 TO 30:NEXT D
100 NEXT S
110 PRINT CHR$(19);MID$(MS$,P,40)
120 P=P+1
130 IF P>LEN(MS$) THEN P=1
140 GOTO 70
```

Your message scrolls smoothly! Every 8 pixels (one character), the text shifts and a new character appears. The hardware scroll makes it buttery smooth—no flicker, no redraw!

## WOW Moment: Cinema-Style Credits

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** CINEMA CREDITS ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 POKE 53280,0:POKE 53281,0
40 PRINT CHR$(5)
50 MS$="        *** STARRING ***  "
60 MS$=MS$+"SPRITES: THE 8 HEROES  "
70 MS$=MS$+"SOUND: SID CHIP  "
80 MS$=MS$+"GRAPHICS: VIC-II  "
90 MS$=MS$+"MUSIC: YOU!  "
100 MS$=MS$+"SPECIAL THANKS: COMMODORE 64  "
110 MS$=MS$+"*** THE END ***        "
120 MS$=MS$+SPACE$(40)
130 POKE 53270,PEEK(53270)OR16
140 P=1
150 FOR S=0 TO 7
160 POKE 53270,(PEEK(53270)AND240)OR S
170 FOR D=1 TO 25:NEXT D
180 NEXT S
190 PRINT CHR$(19);MID$(MS$,P,40)
200 P=P+1
210 IF P>LEN(MS$)-40 THEN P=1
220 GOTO 150
```

![Smooth scrolling cinema credits](/images/lessons/commodore-64/basic/week-6/lesson-41/credits.gif)

Cinema-style credits scroll smoothly across a black screen! The hardware scroll register (53270) shifts the display pixel-by-pixel, while MID$ selects which part of the message to show. When the message ends, it loops back to the start—perfect for title screens and end credits!

Try changing the delay (line 170) to speed up or slow down the scroll!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM HARDWARE SCROLL
POKE 53270,PEEK(53270)OR16  Enable scroll mode

REM FINE SCROLL (0-7)
POKE 53270,(PEEK(53270)AND240)OR S
S=0 to 7                     8 pixel positions

REM MESSAGE HANDLING
MS$=MS$+SPACE$(40)           Add trailing spaces
MID$(MS$,P,40)               Extract 40 chars
P=P+1                        Advance position

REM SCROLL LOOP
FOR S=0 TO 7                 8 scroll steps
  POKE 53270,...             Update scroll
  FOR D=1 TO 30:NEXT         Delay
NEXT S

REM SCREEN UPDATE
PRINT CHR$(19)               Home cursor
PRINT MID$(MS$,P,40)         Update text
```

## What You've Learnt

- Hardware scroll registers shift the entire display
- Fine scroll (0-7) creates smooth pixel movement
- Combine scroll with text updates for marquee effect
- Loop message position for continuous scrolling
- Adjust delay loop to control scroll speed
- Black screen (POKE 53280,0) enhances cinema effect

You've unlocked the secret of professional scrolling text—the same technique used in every C64 demo and game!
