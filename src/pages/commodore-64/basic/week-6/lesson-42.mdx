## Formation Flying!

Ever watched **Space Invaders** march in perfect rows? Or **Galaga** swoop in coordinated patterns? That's **relative positioning**—each sprite's position is calculated from a base position, so the entire formation moves as one!

You'll control all 8 sprites at once, creating formations that move, rotate, and dance together. This is the technique behind enemy squadrons, particle effects, and animated logos!

## Basic Example

Position sprites in a row:

```basic
10 REM *** SPRITE ROW ***
20 FOR I=832 TO 895:POKE I,255:NEXT I
30 POKE 53269,255
40 FOR I=0 TO 7
50 POKE 2040+I,13
60 POKE 53287+I,I+1
70 POKE 53248+I*2,100+I*30
80 POKE 53249+I*2,100
90 NEXT I
100 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN GOTO 100
```

Eight sprites in a horizontal line! Each sprite is offset by 30 pixels from the last. They're positioned relative to 100,100—change that, and the whole formation moves!

## Practical Example

Formation that moves together:

```basic
10 REM *** MOVING FORMATION ***
20 FOR I=832 TO 895:POKE I,255:NEXT I
30 POKE 53269,255
40 FOR I=0 TO 7:POKE 2040+I,13:POKE 53287+I,I+1:NEXT I
50 BX=160:BY=100:DX=2:DY=1
60 FOR I=0 TO 7
70 POKE 53248+I*2,BX+I*25
80 POKE 53249+I*2,BY
90 NEXT I
100 BX=BX+DX:BY=BY+DY
110 IF BX<50 OR BX>270 THEN DX=-DX
120 IF BY<50 OR BY>200 THEN DY=-DY
130 FOR D=1 TO 30:NEXT D
140 GOTO 60
```

The entire formation bounces around the screen! BX and BY are the base coordinates—every sprite is positioned relative to them. Change the base, and all 8 sprites move together in perfect synchronisation!

## WOW Moment: Coordinated Squadron

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** SPRITE SQUADRON ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 FOR I=832 TO 895:POKE I,255:NEXT I
40 FOR I=0 TO 7
50 POKE 2040+I,13
60 POKE 53287+I,3
70 NEXT I
80 POKE 53269,255
90 BX=160:BY=100:A=0
100 FOR I=0 TO 7
110 R=30
120 X=BX+R*COS(A+I*0.785)
130 Y=BY+R*SIN(A+I*0.785)
140 POKE 53248+I*2,X
150 POKE 53249+I*2,Y
160 NEXT I
170 A=A+0.1
180 IF A>6.28 THEN A=0
190 GET K$
200 IF K$="W" THEN BY=BY-2
210 IF K$="S" THEN BY=BY+2
220 IF K$="A" THEN BX=BX-2
230 IF K$="D" THEN BX=BX+2
240 IF K$="Q" THEN END
250 IF BX<50 THEN BX=50
260 IF BX>270 THEN BX=270
270 IF BY<50 THEN BY=50
280 IF BY>200 THEN BY=200
290 FOR D=1 TO 10:NEXT D
300 GOTO 100
```

![Eight sprites in rotating formation](/images/lessons/commodore-64/basic/week-6/lesson-42/squadron.gif)

Eight sprites rotate in a perfect circle formation! Each sprite is positioned using trigonometry (COS/SIN) relative to the centre point. Move the formation with WASD keys—all 8 sprites stay locked in position!

The angle (A) increments each frame, making the formation spin. Each sprite has its own offset angle (I*0.785 = 45 degrees apart) so they form an even circle.

Try changing R=30 to R=50 for a wider circle!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM BASE POSITION
BX=160:BY=100            Centre of formation
DX=2:DY=1                Movement direction

REM RELATIVE POSITIONING
POKE 53248+I*2,BX+offset  X relative to base
POKE 53249+I*2,BY+offset  Y relative to base

REM LINEAR FORMATION
I*25                     Horizontal spacing
I*20                     Vertical spacing

REM CIRCULAR FORMATION
X=BX+R*COS(A+I*offset)   X from centre
Y=BY+R*SIN(A+I*offset)   Y from centre
A=A+0.1                  Rotate angle
I*0.785                  45° spacing (2π/8)

REM ENABLE ALL SPRITES
POKE 53269,255           Turn on sprites 0-7

REM UPDATE ALL SPRITES
FOR I=0 TO 7
  POKE 53248+I*2,X       Position sprite I
NEXT I
```

## What You've Learnt

- Relative positioning keeps formations together
- Base coordinates control entire group
- Linear spacing creates rows and columns
- Trigonometry creates circular patterns
- Angle increments produce rotation effects
- All 8 sprites can move in perfect sync

This is how Galaga creates swooping squadrons and demos create animated logos!
