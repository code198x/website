## The SID Chip Synthesizer!

Every arcade sound effect—laser blasts, explosions, jumps—is created by controlling **oscillator waveforms** and **envelopes**. The C64's SID chip is a real synthesizer with 3 voices!

You've used POKE 54296,15 for beeps, but now you'll control **frequency, waveform, attack, decay, sustain, release** to create professional sound effects just like **Impossible Mission** and **Paradroid**!

## Basic Example

Laser blast with frequency sweep:

```basic
10 REM *** LASER SOUND ***
20 POKE 54296,15
30 POKE 54277,8:POKE 54278,240
40 POKE 54276,33
50 FOR F=200 TO 50 STEP -5
60 POKE 54272,F
70 FOR D=1 TO 10:NEXT D
80 NEXT F
90 POKE 54276,32
```

A classic laser blast! The frequency sweeps from high (200) to low (50), creating that sci-fi "pew pew" sound. POKE 54277/54278 set the envelope (attack/decay), and POKE 54276,33 turns on the sawtooth waveform (bit 5).

## Practical Example

Three sound effects:

```basic
10 REM *** SOUND EFFECTS ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 PRINT "1 = LASER"
40 PRINT "2 = EXPLOSION"
50 PRINT "3 = JUMP"
60 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN GOTO 60
70 IF K$="1" THEN GOSUB 200
80 IF K$="2" THEN GOSUB 300
90 IF K$="3" THEN GOSUB 400
100 GOTO 60
110 REM
200 REM *** LASER ***
210 POKE 54296,15
220 POKE 54277,0:POKE 54278,240
230 POKE 54276,33
240 FOR F=200 TO 50 STEP -5
250 POKE 54272,F
260 FOR D=1 TO 5:NEXT D
270 NEXT F
280 POKE 54276,32
290 RETURN
300 REM *** EXPLOSION ***
310 POKE 54296,15
320 POKE 54277,9:POKE 54278,0
330 POKE 54276,129
340 FOR I=1 TO 100
350 POKE 54272,RND(1)*255
360 FOR D=1 TO 3:NEXT D
370 NEXT I
380 POKE 54276,128
390 RETURN
400 REM *** JUMP ***
410 POKE 54296,15
420 POKE 54277,8:POKE 54278,200
430 FOR F=50 TO 150
440 POKE 54272,F
450 POKE 54276,17
460 FOR D=1 TO 5:NEXT D
470 NEXT F
480 POKE 54276,16
490 RETURN
```

Three arcade sound effects! Press 1 for laser (frequency sweep down), 2 for explosion (noise waveform with random frequencies), 3 for jump (frequency sweep up with triangle wave).

Each sound uses different waveforms and envelopes to create distinct effects!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM SID CHIP REGISTERS
POKE 54296,15            Volume (0-15)
POKE 54272,freq          Frequency low byte
POKE 54273,freq          Frequency high byte
POKE 54276,waveform      Waveform + gate bit

REM WAVEFORMS (add 1 to start)
16 = Triangle wave (smooth)
32 = Sawtooth wave (bright)
64 = Pulse wave (hollow)
128 = Noise (random)

REM ENVELOPE (ADSR)
POKE 54277,attack*16+decay
POKE 54278,sustain*16+release
Attack: 0-15 (0=fast, 15=slow)
Decay: 0-15
Sustain: 0-15 (volume level)
Release: 0-15 (fade out time)

REM TYPICAL SOUNDS
Laser: Sawtooth, freq sweep down
Explosion: Noise, random freq
Jump: Triangle, freq sweep up
Coin: Triangle, two tones
Beep: Pulse, fixed freq
```

## What You've Learnt

- SID chip has oscillators controlled by frequency registers
- Waveform bits select triangle, sawtooth, pulse, or noise
- ADSR envelope shapes attack, decay, sustain, release
- Frequency sweeps create laser and jump effects
- Noise waveform with random frequencies creates explosions
- Two-tone sequences create coin and power-up sounds

You've built a complete sound effects library using direct SID chip control—the same technique used in every C64 game!
