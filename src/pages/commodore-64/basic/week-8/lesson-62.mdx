## The Original Video Game!

Pong started it all—**Tennis**, **Squash**, **Breakout**. Two paddles, one ball, pure competition. The simplicity that launched the video game industry!

You'll create a complete 2-player Pong game with ball physics, paddle collision, scoring to 5, and win screen. This is the classic that defined gaming!

## Basic Example

Basic paddle movement:

```basic
10 REM *** BASIC PADDLES ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 P1=5:P2=5
40 PRINT CHR$(19)
50 FOR Y=1 TO 10
60 IF Y=P1 THEN PRINT "1";
70 IF Y<>P1 THEN PRINT " ";
80 NEXT Y
90 PRINT TAB(30);
100 FOR Y=1 TO 10
110 IF Y=P2 THEN PRINT "2";
120 IF Y<>P2 THEN PRINT " ";
130 NEXT Y
140 GET K$
150 IF K$="W" THEN P1=P1-1
160 IF K$="S" THEN P1=P1+1
170 IF K$="I" THEN P2=P2-1
180 IF K$="K" THEN P2=P2+1
190 GOTO 40
```

Two paddles moving up/down! Player 1 uses W/S, Player 2 uses I/K. But there's no ball, no collision, no game!

## Practical Example

Paddles with ball:

```basic
10 REM *** PADDLE + BALL ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 P1=5:P2=5:BX=15:BY=5:DX=1:DY=1
40 PRINT CHR$(19)
50 FOR Y=1 TO 10
60 IF Y=P1 THEN PRINT "1";
70 IF Y<>P1 THEN PRINT " ";
80 NEXT Y
90 PRINT TAB(BX);"O";
100 PRINT TAB(30);
110 FOR Y=1 TO 10
120 IF Y=P2 THEN PRINT "2";
130 IF Y<>P2 THEN PRINT " ";
140 NEXT Y
150 GET K$
160 IF K$="W" AND P1>1 THEN P1=P1-1
170 IF K$="S" AND P1<10 THEN P1=P1+1
180 IF K$="I" AND P2>1 THEN P2=P2-1
190 IF K$="K" AND P2<10 THEN P2=P2+1
200 REM *** BALL MOVE ***
210 BX=BX+DX:BY=BY+DY
220 IF BY<1 OR BY>10 THEN DY=-DY
230 IF BX=1 AND BY=P1 THEN DX=-DX
240 IF BX=30 AND BY=P2 THEN DX=-DX
250 IF BX<1 OR BX>30 THEN PRINT "SCORE!":END
260 FOR I=1 TO 100:NEXT I
270 GOTO 40
```

Now there's a bouncing ball! It reflects off paddles and top/bottom walls. Score when ball passes paddles!

## WOW Moment: Complete Pong Game

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** PONG ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 POKE 53280,0:POKE 53281,0
40 REM *** INIT ***
50 S1=0:S2=0:FS=5
60 REM *** ROUND START ***
70 P1=10:P2=10:BX=20:BY=10
80 DX=1:DY=1
90 IF RND(1)<0.5 THEN DX=-1
100 IF RND(1)<0.5 THEN DY=-1
110 REM *** MAIN LOOP ***
120 PRINT CHR$(19)
130 PRINT "PLAYER 1: ";S1;"    PLAYER 2: ";S2
140 PRINT "FIRST TO ";FS;" WINS!"
150 PRINT
160 REM *** DRAW COURT ***
170 FOR Y=1 TO 20
180 REM *** LEFT PADDLE ***
190 IF Y>=P1-2 AND Y<=P1+2 THEN PRINT CHR$(18);"#";CHR$(146);
200 IF Y<P1-2 OR Y>P1+2 THEN PRINT " ";
210 REM *** BALL ***
220 IF Y=BY THEN PRINT TAB(BX);"O";
230 REM *** NET ***
240 IF Y MOD 2=0 THEN PRINT TAB(20);":";
250 IF Y MOD 2<>0 THEN PRINT TAB(20);" ";
260 REM *** RIGHT PADDLE ***
270 IF Y>=P2-2 AND Y<=P2+2 THEN PRINT TAB(38);CHR$(18);"#";CHR$(146)
280 IF Y<P2-2 OR Y>P2+2 THEN PRINT TAB(38);" "
290 PRINT
300 NEXT Y
310 PRINT
320 PRINT "P1: W/S    P2: I/K"
330 REM *** INPUT ***
340 GET K$
350 IF K$="W" AND P1>3 THEN P1=P1-1
360 IF K$="S" AND P1<18 THEN P1=P1+1
370 IF K$="I" AND P2>3 THEN P2=P2-1
380 IF K$="K" AND P2<18 THEN P2=P2+1
390 REM *** BALL PHYSICS ***
400 BX=BX+DX:BY=BY+DY
410 REM *** TOP/BOTTOM BOUNCE ***
420 IF BY<1 THEN BY=1:DY=-DY:POKE 53280,1:FOR I=1 TO 50:NEXT I:POKE 53280,0
430 IF BY>20 THEN BY=20:DY=-DY:POKE 53280,1:FOR I=1 TO 50:NEXT I:POKE 53280,0
440 REM *** PADDLE COLLISION ***
450 IF BX=3 AND BY>=P1-2 AND BY<=P1+2 THEN DX=-DX:DY=DY+INT(RND(1)*3)-1:POKE 53280,5:FOR I=1 TO 50:NEXT I:POKE 53280,0
460 IF BX=37 AND BY>=P2-2 AND BY<=P2+2 THEN DX=-DX:DY=DY+INT(RND(1)*3)-1:POKE 53280,5:FOR I=1 TO 50:NEXT I:POKE 53280,0
470 REM *** SCORING ***
480 IF BX<2 THEN S2=S2+1:POKE 53280,2:FOR I=1 TO 1000:NEXT I:POKE 53280,0:GOSUB 1500
490 IF BX>38 THEN S1=S1+1:POKE 53280,2:FOR I=1 TO 1000:NEXT I:POKE 53280,0:GOSUB 1500
500 REM *** WIN CHECK ***
510 IF S1>=FS THEN GOSUB 2000
520 IF S2>=FS THEN GOSUB 3000
530 FOR I=1 TO 30:NEXT I
540 GOTO 120
550 REM
1500 REM *** SCORE DISPLAY ***
1510 PRINT CHR$(147)
1520 PRINT
1530 PRINT "  PLAYER 1: ";S1
1540 PRINT "  PLAYER 2: ";S2
1550 PRINT
1560 IF S1>S2 THEN PRINT "  PLAYER 1 LEADS!"
1570 IF S2>S1 THEN PRINT "  PLAYER 2 LEADS!"
1580 IF S1=S2 THEN PRINT "  TIED!"
1590 PRINT
1600 PRINT "  PRESS FIRE"
1610 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN GOTO 1610
1620 GOTO 70
1630 REM
2000 REM *** PLAYER 1 WINS ***
2010 PRINT CHR$(147)
2020 POKE 53280,5:POKE 53281,13
2030 PRINT
2040 PRINT "  *** PLAYER 1 WINS! ***"
2050 PRINT
2060 PRINT "  FINAL SCORE"
2070 PRINT "  P1: ";S1;" - P2: ";S2
2080 PRINT
2090 PRINT "  PRESS FIRE FOR REMATCH"
2100 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN GOTO 2100
2110 S1=0:S2=0:GOTO 70
2120 REM
3000 REM *** PLAYER 2 WINS ***
3010 PRINT CHR$(147)
3020 POKE 53280,7:POKE 53281,1
3030 PRINT
3040 PRINT "  *** PLAYER 2 WINS! ***"
3050 PRINT
3060 PRINT "  FINAL SCORE"
3070 PRINT "  P1: ";S1;" - P2: ";S2
3080 PRINT
3090 PRINT "  PRESS FIRE FOR REMATCH"
3100 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN GOTO 3100
3110 S1=0:S2=0:GOTO 70
```

![Complete 2-player Pong game](/images/lessons/commodore-64/basic/week-8/lesson-62/pong.gif)

A complete Pong game with **full physics and scoring**!

**Gameplay features:**
- **5-square paddles** (player ± 2 positions)
- **Ball physics** with top/bottom bounce
- **Paddle hit adds spin** (DY changes ±1 randomly)
- **Dotted centre net** (Y MOD 2 pattern)
- **First to 5 points wins**

**Visual feedback:**
- White flash on wall bounce
- Green flash on paddle hit
- Red flash on score
- Victory screen with colour theme

Each point scored shows intermediate screen with current scores and leader. Ball direction randomises at start (DX and DY can be positive or negative)!

Paddle collision checks if ball X matches paddle X AND ball Y is within paddle range (P±2). Spin effect adds randomness to bounces, preventing predictable rallies!

Try adding difficulty (faster ball speed), AI opponent (replace player 2), or power-up effects!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM PONG STRUCTURE
P1=10:P2=10                 Paddle Y positions
BX=20:BY=10                 Ball position
DX=1:DY=1                   Ball velocity

REM PADDLE DRAW
IF Y>=P1-2 AND Y<=P1+2      5-square paddle
PRINT "#"                   Paddle character

REM BALL PHYSICS
BX=BX+DX:BY=BY+DY          Move ball
IF BY<1 OR BY>20 THEN DY=-DY  Wall bounce
IF ball hits paddle THEN DX=-DX  Paddle bounce

REM PADDLE COLLISION
IF BX=paddle_x AND BY in range
DX=-DX                      Reverse direction
DY=DY+INT(RND(1)*3)-1      Add spin

REM SCORING
IF BX<left_edge THEN P2 scores
IF BX>right_edge THEN P1 scores
IF score>=target THEN win
```
