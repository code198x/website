## Shoot, Dodge, Survive!

Arcade action defined C64 gaming—**Uridium**'s relentless waves, **R-Type**'s power-ups and bosses, **Defender**'s endless challenge. Fast reflexes, strategic power-ups, epic boss battles!

You'll create a complete arcade game with 3 enemy types, power-ups, wave system, boss fight, and high score. This is the genre that filled arcades and C64 collections!

## Basic Example

Basic shooting:

```basic
10 REM *** BASIC SHOOTER ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 PX=10:EX=20:BX=-1
40 PRINT CHR$(19)
50 FOR I=0 TO 30
60 IF I=PX THEN PRINT "P";
70 IF I=EX THEN PRINT "E";
80 IF I=BX THEN PRINT "*";
90 IF I<>PX AND I<>EX AND I<>BX THEN PRINT ".";
100 NEXT I
110 GET K$
120 IF K$="D" THEN PX=PX+1
130 IF K$="A" THEN PX=PX-1
140 IF K$=" " AND BX<0 THEN BX=PX
150 IF BX>=0 THEN BX=BX+2
160 IF BX>30 THEN BX=-1
170 IF BX=EX THEN PRINT "HIT!":END
180 GOTO 40
```

Basic shooting! Press space to fire bullet (*), hit enemy (E) to win. But there's no scoring, no lives, no waves!

## Practical Example

Multiple enemies and lives:

```basic
10 REM *** MULTI-ENEMY ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 PX=5:SC=0:LI=3
40 DIM EX(3):DIM EY(3)
50 FOR I=1 TO 3:EX(I)=10+I*5:EY(I)=1:NEXT I
60 BX=-1:BY=-1
70 PRINT CHR$(19)
80 PRINT "SCORE:";SC;" LIVES:";LI
90 PRINT
100 FOR I=1 TO 3
110 IF EY(I)>0 THEN PRINT TAB(EX(I));"E"
120 NEXT I
130 IF BY>0 THEN PRINT TAB(BX);"*"
140 PRINT TAB(PX);"P"
150 GET K$
160 IF K$="D" THEN PX=PX+1
170 IF K$="A" THEN PX=PX-1
180 IF K$=" " AND BY<0 THEN BX=PX:BY=5
190 IF BY>0 THEN BY=BY-1
200 FOR I=1 TO 3
210 IF BX=EX(I) AND BY=EY(I) THEN EY(I)=0:SC=SC+10:BY=-1
220 NEXT I
230 GOTO 70
```

Multiple enemies in arrays! Shoot to score points, but we still need waves, power-ups, and a boss!

## WOW Moment: Complete Arcade Shooter

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** ARCADE BLASTER ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 POKE 53280,0:POKE 53281,0
40 REM *** INIT ***
50 PX=15:SC=0:LI=3:WV=1:PW=0:PT=0
60 DIM EX(5):DIM EY(5):DIM ET(5)
70 REM *** WAVE START ***
80 FOR I=1 TO 5
90 EX(I)=5+I*5:EY(I)=2
100 IF WV=1 THEN ET(I)=1
110 IF WV=2 THEN ET(I)=1+INT(RND(1)*2)
120 IF WV>=3 THEN ET(I)=1+INT(RND(1)*3)
130 NEXT I
140 BX=-1:BY=-1:PU=0:BO=0
150 IF WV=4 THEN BO=1:BH=10:BX=15:BY=2
160 REM *** MAIN LOOP ***
170 PRINT CHR$(19)
180 PRINT "SCORE:";SC;" LIVES:";LI;" WAVE:";WV
190 IF PW>0 THEN PRINT CHR$(18);" POWER ";CHR$(146);:PT=PT-1:IF PT=0 THEN PW=0
200 PRINT
210 REM *** BOSS ***
220 IF BO=1 THEN PRINT TAB(BX);CHR$(18);"BOSS";CHR$(146);" HP:";BH:GOTO 280
230 REM *** ENEMIES ***
240 FOR I=1 TO 5
250 IF EY(I)>0 THEN PRINT TAB(EX(I));
260 IF ET(I)=1 THEN PRINT "E";
270 IF ET(I)=2 THEN PRINT CHR$(18);"F";CHR$(146);
280 IF ET(I)=3 THEN PRINT "Z";
290 NEXT I
300 REM *** POWER-UP ***
310 IF PU>0 THEN PRINT TAB(15);"P"
320 REM *** BULLET ***
330 IF BY>0 THEN PRINT TAB(BX);"*"
340 REM *** PLAYER ***
350 PRINT TAB(PX);CHR$(18);"A";CHR$(146)
360 REM *** INPUT ***
370 GET K$
380 IF K$="D" AND PX<28 THEN PX=PX+1
390 IF K$="A" AND PX>2 THEN PX=PX-1
400 IF K$=" " AND BY<0 THEN BX=PX:BY=18
410 REM *** BULLET MOVE ***
420 IF BY>0 THEN BY=BY-1
430 IF BY=0 THEN BY=-1
440 REM *** BOSS LOGIC ***
450 IF BO=1 THEN GOSUB 2000:GOTO 550
460 REM *** ENEMY MOVE ***
470 FOR I=1 TO 5
480 IF EY(I)>0 THEN EY(I)=EY(I)+1
490 IF ET(I)=2 THEN EX(I)=EX(I)+INT(RND(1)*3)-1
500 IF ET(I)=3 AND RND(1)<0.3 THEN EX(I)=EX(I)+INT(RND(1)*5)-2
510 IF EX(I)<2 THEN EX(I)=2
520 IF EX(I)>28 THEN EX(I)=28
530 IF EY(I)>19 THEN LI=LI-1:EY(I)=0:IF LI=0 THEN GOTO 3000
540 NEXT I
550 REM *** COLLISION ***
560 FOR I=1 TO 5
570 IF BX=EX(I) AND BY=EY(I) AND EY(I)>0 THEN EY(I)=0:SC=SC+ET(I)*10:BY=-1
580 NEXT I
590 REM *** POWER-UP ***
600 IF PU=0 AND RND(1)<0.02 THEN PU=15
610 IF PU>0 AND PX=15 AND 18=19 THEN PW=1:PT=100:PU=0
620 REM *** WAVE COMPLETE ***
630 W=0:FOR I=1 TO 5:IF EY(I)>0 THEN W=1
640 NEXT I
650 IF W=0 AND BO=0 THEN WV=WV+1:FOR I=1 TO 1000:NEXT I:GOTO 80
660 FOR I=1 TO 50:NEXT I
670 GOTO 170
680 REM
2000 REM *** BOSS BEHAVIOUR ***
2010 BX=BX+INT(RND(1)*5)-2
2020 IF BX<5 THEN BX=5
2030 IF BX>25 THEN BX=25
2040 IF BX=PX AND BY=2 THEN BH=BH-1:BY=-1:IF BH=0 THEN GOSUB 4000
2050 RETURN
2060 REM
3000 REM *** GAME OVER ***
3010 PRINT CHR$(147)
3020 POKE 53280,2:POKE 53281,8
3030 PRINT:PRINT
3040 PRINT "    GAME OVER"
3050 PRINT:PRINT "  FINAL SCORE:";SC
3060 FOR I=1 TO 3000:NEXT I
3070 END
3080 REM
4000 REM *** BOSS DEFEATED ***
4010 PRINT CHR$(147)
4020 POKE 53280,5:POKE 53281,13
4030 PRINT:PRINT
4040 PRINT "  BOSS DEFEATED!"
4050 PRINT:PRINT "  FINAL SCORE:";SC+500
4060 PRINT:PRINT "  YOU WIN!"
4070 FOR I=1 TO 3000:NEXT I
4080 END
```

![Complete arcade shooter with waves and boss](/images/lessons/commodore-64/basic/week-8/lesson-59/arcade.gif)

A complete arcade shooter! **THREE enemy types:**

- **E (Basic)**: Moves down steadily, 10 points
- **F (Fast)**: Moves down + sideways, 20 points (reverse video)
- **Z (Zigzag)**: Erratic movement, 30 points

**FOUR waves:**
- Wave 1: All basic enemies
- Wave 2: Mix of basic and fast
- Wave 3: All three types
- Wave 4: BOSS FIGHT with 10 HP!

Power-up (P) appears randomly—collect it for temporary boost! Boss moves horizontally, requires 10 hits to defeat. Enemies that reach bottom cost 1 life!

Complete all waves and defeat the boss to win! Try adding more enemy types, different boss patterns, or weapon upgrades!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM ARCADE STRUCTURE
DIM EX(5):DIM EY(5):DIM ET(5)  Arrays
WV=1                            Wave number
BO=0/1                          Boss active

REM ENEMY TYPES
ET(I)=1  Basic (steady)
ET(I)=2  Fast (erratic)
ET(I)=3  Zigzag (random)

REM WAVE SYSTEM
Check all EY(I)=0 (dead)
WV=WV+1                         Next wave
Respawn with new patterns

REM BOSS
BO=1:BH=10                      Activate, 10 HP
Move horizontally
BH=BH-1 on hit
IF BH=0 THEN win

REM POWER-UP
IF RND(1)<0.02 THEN spawn
Collect for temporary boost
```
