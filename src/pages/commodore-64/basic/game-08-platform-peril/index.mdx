## What You'll Build

Single-screen platformer pushing BASIC to its limits. Demonstrates why assembly is needed for serious games.

Jump between platforms. Avoid enemies. Collect items. This is BASIC at its most ambitious — and its most constrained. You'll implement gravity, collision, and action while feeling the language strain. The perfect preparation for 6510 assembly.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Gravity simulation** — falling physics in BASIC
- **Platform collision** — landing detection
- **Jump mechanics** — arcing motion
- **Enemy movement** — patrol patterns
- **Collectibles** — item gathering
- **Performance bottlenecks** — speed limitations
- **BASIC limitations exposed** — understanding constraints
- **Transition preparation** — ready for assembly

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 7: Memory Match** first.

## Time Investment

64 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 64-96 hours total.
