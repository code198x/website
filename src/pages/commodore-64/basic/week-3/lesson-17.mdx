## Lesson 17 – The Art of Movement

Week 3 opens by turning all that map logic into fluid motion. You already know how to POKE characters; now you’ll glide multi-tile shapes, keep colours in sync, and prepare for sprite-like movement.

> [📷 suggested: screenshot of a 2×2 block sliding smoothly across the screen]

---

### The One-Minute Tour

- Compute screen addresses with `SCREEN + ROW*40 + COL`; colour lives at `COLOR + ROW*40 + COL`.  
- Multi-tile objects need erase → update → draw order.  
- Velocity variables (`VX`, `VY`) separate direction from position.  
- Delay loops (or timers later) make motion readable.

---

### Warm-Up: Sliding a Single Character

```basic
NEW
10 PRINT CHR$(147)
20 SCREEN=1024
30 COLOR=55296
40 X=5:Y=12:CHAR=64:COL=7
50 FOR STEP=0 TO 30
60 LOC=SCREEN+Y*40+X
70 POKE LOC,CHAR:POKE COLOR+Y*40+X,COL
80 FOR D=1 TO 80:NEXT D
90 POKE LOC,32
100 X=X+1
110 NEXT STEP
```

- **Line 70** paints an `@` (`64`) in yellow (`7`).  
- **Line 90** erases before moving.  
- Increase/decrease `X` to slide left/right; swap the loop to animate vertically.

---

### Out-of-Bounds? Clamp It

```basic
120 IF X>38 THEN X=38
130 IF X<0 THEN X=0
```

Insert those before line 70 to keep the character on-screen. You’ve used this guard pattern in earlier lessons; animation makes it essential.

### Multi-Tile Block (2×2)

```basic
NEW
10 PRINT CHR$(147)
20 SCREEN=1024:COLOR=55296
30 X=8:Y=8:COL=2
40 FOR STEP=1 TO 25
50 GOSUB 2000        : REM draw block
60 FOR D=1 TO 120:NEXT D
70 GOSUB 2100        : REM erase block
80 X=X+1:Y=Y+1
90 NEXT STEP
100 END

2000 REM --- DRAW BLOCK ---
2010 FOR DY=0 TO 1
2020 FOR DX=0 TO 1
2030 LOC=SCREEN+(Y+DY)*40+(X+DX)
2040 POKE LOC,162
2050 POKE COLOR+(Y+DY)*40+(X+DX),COL
2060 NEXT DX
2070 NEXT DY
2080 RETURN

2100 REM --- ERASE BLOCK ---
2110 FOR DY=0 TO 1
2120 FOR DX=0 TO 1
2130 LOC=SCREEN+(Y+DY)*40+(X+DX)
2140 POKE LOC,32
2150 NEXT DX
2160 NEXT DY
2170 RETURN
```

Keep the draw and erase loops symmetrical so you wipe the exact pixels you painted.

### Velocity Variables

```basic
NEW
10 PRINT CHR$(147)
20 SCREEN=1024:COLOR=55296
30 X=10:Y=10:VX=1:VY=0
40 FOR STEP=1 TO 60
50 LOC=SCREEN+Y*40+X
60 POKE LOC,90:POKE COLOR+Y*40+X,1
70 FOR D=1 TO 40:NEXT D
80 POKE LOC,32
90 X=X+VX:Y=Y+VY
100 IF X<1 OR X>38 THEN VX=-VX
110 IF Y<1 OR Y>23 THEN VY=-VY
120 NEXT STEP
```

Bounce effect! Tweak `VX`/`VY` to change direction or speed. Later, enemies and projectiles will use the same pattern.

---

### Experiment Section

- Combine velocity with the 2×2 block—change both `X` and `Y` to create diagonal motion.  
- Tile-ready: replace `162` with `MAP` values to move a player made of multiple character cells.  
- Trails: forget to erase and witness after-images—useful for debugging.  
- Colour shifts: cycle `COL` inside the loop to animate glowing objects.  
- Timer-based speed: tie the delay loop to `TI` instead of fixed counts for consistent motion across PAL/NTSC.

---

### Concept Expansion

These techniques are the stepping stone to sprites: once you’re comfortable updating multiple characters per frame, migrating to hardware sprites (or blitting in assembly) is a natural progression.

### Game Integration

- Player characters composed of 2×2 blocks for chunkier art.  
- Moving obstacles that track the player by tweaking `VX/VY`.  
- Camera scroll prototypes: update an offset instead of the object to learn parallax fundamentals.  
- Blend with collision checks from Lesson 13 before moving to avoid clipping.
