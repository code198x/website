## The SID Chip: Your Sound Synthesizer!

The C64 has a **SID** chip (Sound Interface Device)—a real synthesizer built in! It has 3 voices, each with volume, frequency, and waveform control.

Making sound is simple: POKE values to SID registers. Let's start with ONE note!

## SID Registers for Voice 1

```
54272  Frequency (low byte)
54273  Frequency (high byte)
54276  Waveform (17=triangle wave on)
54296  Volume (0-15)
```

## Basic Example

Play a single note:

```basic
10 REM *** ONE NOTE ***
20 POKE 54296,15:REM VOLUME MAX
30 POKE 54276,17:REM WAVEFORM ON
40 POKE 54272,50:REM FREQUENCY LOW
50 POKE 54273,25:REM FREQUENCY HIGH
```

A steady tone! To stop: `POKE 54276,16` (waveform off).

## Practical Example

Play three notes in sequence:

```basic
10 REM *** C-E-G CHORD ***
20 POKE 54296,15:POKE 54276,17
30 POKE 54272,195:POKE 54273,16:REM C
40 FOR I=1 TO 500:NEXT I
50 POKE 54272,230:POKE 54273,20:REM E
60 FOR I=1 TO 500:NEXT I
70 POKE 54272,241:POKE 54273,24:REM G
80 FOR I=1 TO 500:NEXT I
90 POKE 54276,16:REM OFF
```

Three notes, one after another!

## WOW Moment: Keyboard Piano

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** PIANO KEYS ***
20 POKE 54296,15:REM MAX VOLUME
30 PRINT CHR$(147);"PRESS A-G FOR NOTES"
40 GET K$:IF K$="" THEN 40
50 IF K$="A" THEN F=16*256+195
60 IF K$="B" THEN F=18*256+209
70 IF K$="C" THEN F=20*256+230
80 IF K$="D" THEN F=22*256+96
90 IF K$="E" THEN F=24*256+241
100 IF K$="F" THEN F=27*256+141
110 IF K$="G" THEN F=30*256+578
120 IF F=0 THEN 40
130 POKE 54273,F/256:REM HIGH BYTE
140 POKE 54272,F AND 255:REM LOW BYTE
150 POKE 54276,17:REM WAVEFORM ON
160 FOR I=1 TO 100:NEXT I
170 POKE 54276,16:REM OFF
180 F=0:GOTO 40
```

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM SID CHIP (VOICE 1)
54272    Frequency low byte
54273    Frequency high byte
54276    Waveform (17=on, 16=off)
54296    Volume (0-15)

REM FREQUENCY FORMULA
Combined = (High * 256) + Low
High byte = Freq / 256
Low byte = Freq AND 255

REM WAVEFORM VALUES
17  Triangle wave (smooth)
33  Sawtooth wave (buzzy)
65  Pulse wave (hollow)
129 Noise (random)
```

## What You've Learnt

- SID chip makes sound via registers
- Frequency = pitch (higher = higher note)
- Waveform = tone color
- Volume 0-15 controls loudness
- Keyboard input can trigger notes

The SID chip is powerful—this is just the start!
