## Sprites Come Alive!

A stationary sprite is nice, but sprites are meant to **move**. Change the X/Y registers in a loop and the sprite glides smoothly across the screen!

The secret: POKE new values to 53248 (X) and 53249 (Y) repeatedly.

## Basic Example

Move a sprite horizontally:

```basic
10 REM *** MOVING SPRITE ***
20 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
30 POKE 2040,13:POKE 53269,1
40 X=50
50 POKE 53248,X:POKE 53249,120
60 X=X+2:IF X>250 THEN X=50
70 GOTO 50
```

The sprite slides across the screen and wraps!

**Note:** X stops at 250 because POKE only accepts values 0-255. The C64 screen is actually 320 pixels wide—later you'll learn about register 53264 which extends sprite X positions beyond 255!

## Practical Example

Move sprite up and down:

```basic
10 REM *** VERTICAL MOTION ***
20 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
30 POKE 2040,13:POKE 53269,1
40 Y=50:DY=2
50 POKE 53248,160:POKE 53249,Y
60 Y=Y+DY
70 IF Y>200 OR Y<50 THEN DY=-DY
80 GOTO 50
```

## WOW Moment: Circular Motion

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** CIRCLE SPRITE ***
20 FOR I=832 TO 894:POKE I,255:NEXT I
30 POKE 2040,13:POKE 53287,7
40 POKE 53269,1
50 REM CENTER OF CIRCLE
60 CX=180:CY=130:R=50
70 A=0
80 REM CALCULATE POSITION
90 X=CX+R*COS(A)
100 Y=CY+R*SIN(A)
110 POKE 53248,X
120 POKE 53249,Y
130 A=A+0.1
140 IF A>6.28 THEN A=0
150 GOTO 80
```

<video src="/images/lessons/commodore-64/basic/week-3/lesson-21/circular-movement.mp4" controls loop autoplay muted></video>

Perfect circle! SIN and COS create smooth circular paths. Change R for different circle sizes!

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM MOTION BASICS
53248    Sprite 0 X position (0-255)
53249    Sprite 0 Y position (0-255)
X=X+DX   Move horizontally
Y=Y+DY   Move vertically

REM X POSITION LIMITS
POKE only accepts 0-255
Screen is 320 pixels wide
53264 extends X beyond 255 (later lesson!)

REM CIRCULAR MOTION
X = CenterX + Radius * COS(Angle)
Y = CenterY + Radius * SIN(Angle)
Angle from 0 to 6.28 (2π radians)

REM SPEED CONTROL
Small step = slow motion
Large step = fast motion
```

## What You've Learnt

- Change X/Y in a loop for motion
- SIN/COS create circular paths
- Angle 0-6.28 for full circle
- Sprite moves smoothly anywhere
- Position updates = animation

Sprites + math = smooth graphics magic!
