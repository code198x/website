## From Notes to Music!

Single notes are great, but **music** needs sequences! Store notes in DATA statements, READ them in a loop, and play them with delays. That's music sequencing!

Same SID registers, different approach: loop + data = melody.

## Basic Example

Play three notes as a simple tune:

```basic
10 REM *** SIMPLE TUNE ***
20 POKE 54296,15:REM VOLUME
25 POKE 54277,0:POKE 54278,240:REM ADSR
30 FOR I=1 TO 3
40 READ F
50 POKE 54273,F/256
60 POKE 54272,F AND 255
70 POKE 54276,17:REM ON
80 FOR D=1 TO 300:NEXT D
90 POKE 54276,16:REM OFF
100 NEXT I
110 DATA 5011,5626,6321:REM C,D,E
```

## Practical Example

Play a familiar tune (first 4 notes of "Twinkle"):

```basic
10 REM *** TWINKLE MELODY ***
20 POKE 54296,15
25 POKE 54277,0:POKE 54278,240
30 FOR I=1 TO 4
40 READ F,L
50 POKE 54273,F/256
60 POKE 54272,F AND 255
70 POKE 54276,17
80 FOR D=1 TO L:NEXT D
90 POKE 54276,16
100 FOR D=1 TO 50:NEXT D
110 NEXT I
120 REM C,C,G,G
130 DATA 5011,400,5011,400
140 DATA 7505,400,7505,400
```

## WOW Moment: Complete Melody

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** SIMPLE MELODY ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147);"PLAYING..."
30 POKE 54296,15:REM MAX VOLUME
35 POKE 54277,0:POKE 54278,240:REM ADSR
40 FOR I=1 TO 8
50 READ F,L
60 IF F=0 THEN 120:REM END
70 POKE 54273,F/256:REM HIGH
80 POKE 54272,F AND 255:REM LOW
90 POKE 54276,17:REM WAVEFORM ON
100 FOR D=1 TO L:NEXT D:REM DURATION
110 POKE 54276,16:REM OFF
120 FOR D=1 TO 30:NEXT D:REM GAP
130 NEXT I
140 PRINT "DONE!"
150 REM MELODY: C-E-G-E-C-G-E-C
160 DATA 5011,300,6321,300,7505,300
170 DATA 6321,300,5011,400,7505,300
180 DATA 6321,300,5011,500,0,0
```

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM MUSIC SEQUENCING
1. Store notes in DATA
2. READ in loop
3. POKE frequency
4. Delay for duration
5. Turn off, repeat

REM DATA FORMAT
DATA frequency,duration
frequency = (high*256)+low
duration = loop count

REM COMMON FREQUENCIES
C  = 5011
D  = 5626
E  = 6321
F  = 6692
G  = 7505
A  = 8421
B  = 9445
C' = 10025 (high C)
```

## What You've Learnt

- DATA stores note sequences
- READ + loop plays melodies
- Duration = FOR loop delay
- Gaps between notes = silence
- Music is just timed frequencies

You're now a C64 composer!
