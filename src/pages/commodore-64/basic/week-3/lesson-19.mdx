## Characters Aren't Just Letters!

The C64 has 256 characters, not just A-Z. There are boxes, lines, corners, and blocks—perfect for drawing! This is **PETSCII graphics**: using characters as building blocks to create pictures.

Hold SHIFT+C= to see them all. Characters 160-191 are especially useful—they're blocks and lines!

## Useful Graphics Characters

```
Code  Character
160   Solid block (full)
162   Top-left corner
163   Horizontal line
164   Bottom-left corner
165   Vertical line
166   Top-right corner
167   Bottom-right corner
32    Space (empty)
```

## Basic Example

Draw a simple box:

```basic
10 REM *** DRAW BOX ***
20 P=1024+(10*40)+15
30 POKE P,162:REM TOP-LEFT
40 FOR I=1 TO 5:POKE P+I,163:NEXT
50 POKE P+6,166:REM TOP-RIGHT
60 FOR I=40 TO 200 STEP 40
70 POKE P+I,165:POKE P+I+6,165
80 NEXT
90 POKE P+240,164:REM BOTTOM-LEFT
100 FOR I=1 TO 5:POKE P+240+I,163:NEXT
110 POKE P+246,167:REM BOTTOM-RIGHT
```

A box made from line characters!

## Practical Example

Draw a simple house:

```basic
10 REM *** HOUSE ***
20 P=1024+(8*40)+17
30 REM ROOF
40 POKE P,47:REM /
50 POKE P+1,92:REM \
60 REM WALLS
70 POKE P+40,160:POKE P+41,160
80 POKE P+80,160:POKE P+81,160
90 REM DOOR
100 POKE P+81,166
```

Characters become pixels!

## WOW Moment: Draw a Smiley Face

```basic
NEW
10 REM *** SMILEY FACE ***
20 PRINT CHR$(147)
30 P=1024+(10*40)+17:REM CENTER
40 REM EYES
50 POKE P-80+2,81:REM LEFT EYE
60 POKE P-80+6,81:REM RIGHT EYE
70 REM SMILE - USE 163 FOR LINE
80 FOR I=1 TO 6
90 POKE P+80+I,163
100 NEXT I
110 REM ENDS OF SMILE
120 POKE P+40+1,165:REM LEFT
130 POKE P+40+6,165:REM RIGHT
140 REM COLOR IT
150 FOR I=0 TO 7
160 POKE 55296+(10*40)+17-80+I*40,7
170 NEXT I
```

## Quick Reference

```basic
REM GRAPHICS CHARACTERS
160   Full block █
162-167  Box corners/lines
32    Space (eraser)
81    Letter Q (circle)
163   Horizontal line ─
165   Vertical line │

REM DRAWING PATTERN
P = 1024 + (ROW * 40) + COLUMN
POKE P, character_code
POKE 55296 + same_offset, color
```

## What You've Learnt

- 256 characters available, not just letters
- Graphics characters (160-191) perfect for drawing
- SHIFT+C= shows all characters
- Characters can be "pixels" for pictures
- Combine screen + color memory for art

PETSCII graphics = text mode artistry!
