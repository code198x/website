## What You'll Build

Full vertical scrolling shooter. 1942-style planes, formations, weapons, bosses. 7+ stages.

The sky scrolls. Enemy formations dive at you. Bullets fill the screen. Power-ups drift by. This is the definitive Spectrum shooter — and it introduces 128K support with AY chip sound.

- **Optimised vertical scrolling** — smooth performance
- **Formation patterns** — coordinated enemies
- **Bullet hell** — dense projectile patterns
- **Weapon variety** — multiple shot types
- **Multi-phase bosses** — epic encounters
- **Score chaining** — bonus mechanics
- **Multiple ships** — player choice
- **128K detection** — hardware checking
- **AY-3-8912 sound** — 128K audio chip
- **Bank switching** — extended memory
- **Two-player simultaneous** — cooperative play
