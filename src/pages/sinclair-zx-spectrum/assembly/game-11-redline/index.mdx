## What You'll Build

Full vertical scrolling racer. Championship mode, multiple tracks, opponent AI, weather effects.

Feel the speed as the track scrolls beneath you. Weave through traffic. Race through city streets, country roads, night courses. Compete in championships. Deal with rain that changes your handling. This is a complete racing experience.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Vertical scrolling** — smooth track movement
- **Speed variation** — acceleration, braking
- **Road rendering** — curves and bends
- **Opponent AI** — overtaking behaviour
- **Collision at speed** — crash handling
- **Track variety** — multiple environments
- **Championship mode** — season progression
- **Weather effects** — rain affecting handling
- **Tape loading** — standard loader, TAP files
- **Pit stops** — damage and repair
