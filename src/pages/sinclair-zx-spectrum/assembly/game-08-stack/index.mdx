## What You'll Build

Falling blocks puzzle. Tetris-style piece rotation, stacking, and line clearing.

Pieces fall. You rotate them. Stack them to form complete lines. Lines clear, you score. The speed increases. How long can you survive? The rotation logic and line detection algorithms are classics of game programming.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Piece rotation** — 4 orientations per piece
- **Falling timers** — gravity mechanics
- **Grid collision** — fit detection
- **Line detection** — complete row finding
- **Line clearing** — row removal with collapse
- **Next piece preview** — look-ahead display
- **Speed progression** — increasing difficulty
- **Wall kicks** — rotation near edges

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 7: Slither** first.

64 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 64-96 hours total.
