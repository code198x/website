## What You'll Build

Single-screen platformer in the Manic Miner tradition. Platforms, hazards, collectibles.

Navigate caverns filled with danger. Jump between platforms. Avoid enemies. Collect the keys. Watch your air supply. This is the template that defined Spectrum platformers — Manic Miner, Jet Set Willy, and countless others.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Gravity and falling** — realistic physics
- **Jump physics** — velocity, arc, landing
- **Platform collision** — landing detection
- **Player animation** — walk cycle, jump frames
- **Hazards** — crumbling floors, patrolling enemies
- **Air supply** — time pressure mechanic
- **Multiple levels** — level progression

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 2: Shatter** first.
