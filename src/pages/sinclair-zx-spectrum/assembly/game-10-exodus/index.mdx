## What You'll Build

Scrolling platformer. Cavern's mechanics in a larger, scrolling world.

The single screen is gone. Now the world scrolls as you run, jump, and explore. This combines everything you learned in Cavern with the scrolling from Blitz — the foundation for epic platformers.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Horizontal scrolling platformer** — combined mechanics
- **Camera following** — tracking the player
- **Large level data** — extended world storage
- **Scrolling collision** — detection in moving view
- **Enemies in scrolling world** — relative positioning
- **Checkpoints** — progress saving
- **Scroll optimisation** — smooth performance
- **Level streaming** — loading as you go

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 9: Blitz** first.

64 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 64-96 hours total.
