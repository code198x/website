## What You'll Build

Snake game. Eat food, grow longer, don't hit yourself.

Guide your snake. Eat the food. Grow longer. Don't collide with your own tail. Simple rules, but the implementation teaches efficient data structures for managing a growing, moving entity.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Grid-based movement** — discrete positions
- **Growing entities** — linked list concepts
- **Self-collision** — body detection
- **Food spawning** — random placement
- **Length difficulty** — harder as you grow
- **Game loop optimisation** — tight performance
- **Obstacle mazes** — static walls
- **Speed progression** — faster over time

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 6: Chomp** first.

## Time Investment
