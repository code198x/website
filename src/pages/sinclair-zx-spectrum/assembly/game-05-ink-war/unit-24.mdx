## Timer Design

| Setting | Frames | Seconds |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Fast | 250 | 5 |
| Normal | 500 | 10 |
| Slow | 750 | 15 |
| Off | 0 | ∞ |

When timer expires, make a random valid move automatically.

## Timer Display

Show remaining time prominently:

```asm
            ; Calculate seconds from frames
            ld      a, (move_timer)
            ; Divide by 50 for seconds
            ; Display at fixed position
```
