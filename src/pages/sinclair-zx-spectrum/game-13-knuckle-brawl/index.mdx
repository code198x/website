## What You'll Build

Side-scrolling beat 'em up. Walk right, punch enemies, fight bosses. Streets of Rage/Final Fight territory.

Walk into the screen. Enemies approach from all angles. Punch, kick, throw them into each other. Pick up weapons. Fight end-of-level bosses. This teaches depth sorting — the Y-axis layering that creates the illusion of 3D.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Depth sorting** — Y-axis layering
- **Melee combat** — punches, kicks, throws
- **Combo systems** — chained attacks
- **Multiple enemies** — crowd management
- **Health and damage** — combat systems
- **Pseudo-3D movement** — walking "into" screen
- **Stage progression** — advancing through levels
- **Multiple characters** — player selection
- **Weapon pickups** — temporary power-ups
- **Boss encounters** — unique enemies
