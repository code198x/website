Over the next eight units you'll build Hot and Cold — a treasure hunt where the border colour tells you how close you are. But first, you need to put things in specific places on screen.

## The Screen Grid

The Spectrum's screen is a grid of 24 rows and 32 columns. Row 0 is the top, row 21 is the bottom of the safe area (rows 22-23 are reserved for system messages). Column 0 is the left edge, column 31 is the right.

`PRINT AT row, col` places text at that exact position.

Each `PRINT AT` jumps to a specific position before printing. The text doesn't flow from the previous line — it goes exactly where you tell it.

## A Title Screen

With `PRINT AT`, you can lay out a proper title screen.

![Hot and Cold title screen](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-04-hot-and-cold/print-at.png)

The title sits at the top centre, instructions in the middle, and "Press any key" near the bottom. Without `PRINT AT`, everything would stack from the top down. Now you control the layout.

## How PRINT AT Works

`PRINT AT row, col; "text"` does two things:

1. Moves the print position to the given row and column
2. Prints the text starting from that position

Note the semicolon after the column number — it separates the position from the text. The row comes first, then the column.

## Coordinates to Remember

| Position | Row | Column |
|----------|-----|--------|
| Top-left | 0 | 0 |
| Top-right | 0 | 31 |
| Centre | 10 | 15 |
| Bottom-left | 21 | 0 |
| Bottom-right | 21 | 31 |

Row 21 is the last safe row. Printing on rows 22-23 works but can interfere with the Spectrum's own messages.

## Try This

**Centre a word.** To centre text on a row, calculate the column: `(32 - length) / 2`. For "HELLO" (5 letters), that's column 13. Try `PRINT AT 10, 13; "HELLO"`.

**Draw a border.** Use `PRINT AT` in a loop to place dashes across the top row and bottom row, creating a frame around the screen.

## What You've Learnt

- **PRINT AT row, col** — places text at a specific screen position
- **The grid** — 24 rows (0-23) × 32 columns (0-31), with rows 0-21 as the safe area
- **Row first, column second** — `PRINT AT 10, 15` means row 10, column 15
