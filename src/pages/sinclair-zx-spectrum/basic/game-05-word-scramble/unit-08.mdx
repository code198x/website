## What Changed

**Title screen (lines 20-140).** Blue background with white text explains the rules. `INKEY$` waits for a key press before starting. The colours switch back to black-on-white for gameplay.

**Ten rounds with mixed difficulty (lines 190, 800-810).** The DATA list starts with short 3-letter words (CAT, SUN, RED) and progresses to longer words (LIGHT, DREAM, CASTLE). More letters means more possible arrangements — harder to unscramble.

**Round counter and score (lines 290-300).** `PRINT AT 0, 0` places the round number at the top-left. `PRINT AT 0, 22` places the score at the top-right. The player always knows their progress.

![Gameplay with centred word and score display](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-05-word-scramble/gameplay.png)

**Centred scrambled word (line 350).** `PRINT AT 8, (32 - LEN s$) / 2; s$` calculates the starting column to centre the word. A 3-letter word starts at column 14, a 6-letter word at column 13.

**BRIGHT display (lines 340-360).** The scrambled word prints in blue ink with BRIGHT on, making it stand out against the white background.

**Separate correct/wrong paths (lines 400-460).** Wrong answers jump past the score increment to line 490. Correct answers fall through to `LET n = n + 1`. This fixes the scoring so only correct guesses count.

**Score ratings (lines 580-610).** Four tiers of feedback:

| Score | Message |
|-------|---------|
| 10 | "Perfect! Incredible!" |
| 7-9 | "Well done!" |
| 4-6 | "Not bad, keep trying!" |
| 0-3 | "Keep practising your words!" |

![Game over screen with score rating](/images/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/game-05-word-scramble/game-over.png)

**Clean exit (lines 670-700).** Restores default colours and clears the screen, leaving the Spectrum in a tidy state.

## The Complete Structure

1. **Title screen** — blue background, instructions, wait for key
2. **Round loop** (×10) — read word, scramble, display, input, check
3. **Correct path** — green text, two-note jingle, increment score
4. **Wrong path** — red text, low buzz, skip score increment
5. **Game over** — blue background, final score, rating message

## Try This

**Add a replay option.** After the game-over screen, ask "Play again? (Y/N)". If yes, use `RESTORE` to reset the DATA pointer back to the first word and `GO TO 150` to restart.

**Expand the word list.** Add 10 more words on a new DATA line (line 820) and change line 190 to `FOR i = 1 TO 20`. The difficulty curve gets steeper with longer words later.

**Case-insensitive matching.** The game currently requires exact uppercase input. Research how `CODE` works to convert lowercase to uppercase for comparison.

## What You've Learnt

- **Title screens** — `BORDER`, `PAPER`, `INK`, and `CLS` create a branded look
- **Centring text** — `(32 - LEN s$) / 2` calculates the column
- **Branching paths** — `GO TO` sends correct and wrong answers down different routes
- **Score ratings** — chained `IF` statements for tiered feedback
- **Program structure** — title, game loop, results, cleanup
