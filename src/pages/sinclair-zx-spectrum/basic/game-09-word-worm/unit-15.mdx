## How It Works

**Line 10** sets the visual tone: `BORDER 0: PAPER 0: INK 7: CLS`. Black background, white text by default.

**Line 90** ties border colour to level: `BORDER v`. Level 1 = dark blue, level 2 = red, level 3 = magenta. Each level has its own visual identity.

**Line 580** varies the completion sound by word length:

```
580 BEEP 0.05, l(n) * 3 + 5
```

Longer words produce higher notes. A 3-letter word plays at pitch 14, a 6-letter word at pitch 23. The player *hears* the difference between easy and hard words.

**Line 582** plays a three-note fanfare for streak bonuses:

```
BEEP 0.05, 20: BEEP 0.05, 24: BEEP 0.1, 28
```

**Lines 730** plays a descending two-note phrase for misses:

```
730 BEEP 0.15, -5: BEEP 0.15, -10
```

## Sound Design

| Event | Sound | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Complete | Short high note | Quick, positive |
| Streak | Three ascending notes | Celebratory |
| Miss | Two descending notes | Warning |
| Game over | Three descending notes | Finality |

The sounds are short — never more than 0.5 seconds total. Long sounds would block the game loop.

## Try This

**Level fanfare.** When the level changes, play a short ascending scale before the game continues.

**Volume through duration.** Make the miss sound longer at higher levels — the stakes are higher: `BEEP 0.1 + v * 0.05, -5`.

## What You've Learnt

- **BORDER as level indicator** — colour changes with progression
- **Pitch from data** — word length drives the completion sound
- **Sound brevity** — keep BEEPs short to avoid blocking
- **Polish pattern** — black background, coloured elements, sound feedback
