## How It Works

**Line 80** picks a random word: `INT(RND*10)+1` gives a number from 1 to 10.

**Line 90** extracts the word without padding: `w$(n)(1 TO l(n))`.

**Lines 120-160** are the animation loop:

1. Print the word at the current row
2. `PAUSE 15` — wait about 0.3 seconds
3. Print spaces over it to erase
4. Increment the row
5. If not at the bottom, repeat

**Line 160** checks whether the word has reached row 20. If so, print "Word missed!" and stop.

## Erase and Redraw

Animation on the Spectrum is always erase-then-draw. Print spaces over the old position to clear it, then print the word at the new position. If you forget to erase, the screen fills with copies of the word.

```
120 PRINT AT r, 10; a$
130 PAUSE 15
140 PRINT AT r, 10; "     "
150 LET r = r + 1
```

The spaces on line 140 must be at least as long as the longest word. Five spaces covers all 3-letter words with room to spare.

## Try This

**Speed.** Change `PAUSE 15` to `PAUSE 5` (faster) or `PAUSE 30` (slower). The word should feel like it's gently drifting, not racing.

**Position.** Change the column from 10 to `INT(RND*20)+1`. Each word appears at a random horizontal position.

## What You've Learnt

- **Random selection** — picking a word from the array
- **Falling animation** — erase, move down, redraw
- **PAUSE for timing** — controlling animation speed
- **Boundary check** — stopping at the bottom of the screen
