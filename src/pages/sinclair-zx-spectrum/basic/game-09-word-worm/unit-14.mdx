Completing words is good. Completing five *in a row* without a miss is great. A streak bonus rewards consistent play and adds a layer of tension — one mistake resets the counter.

## How It Works

**Line 80** initialises the streak counter: `LET j = 0`.

**Line 570** increments it on every completion: `LET j = j + 1`.

**Line 582** triggers the bonus:

```
582 IF j >= 5 THEN LET e = e + 5: PRINT AT 10, 9; INK 3; BRIGHT 1; "STREAK! +5": BEEP 0.1, 20: BEEP 0.1, 24: PAUSE 15: PRINT AT 10, 9; "          ": LET j = 0
```

Five in a row: add 5 bonus points, flash "STREAK! +5" in bright green, play two ascending tones, pause, erase the message, reset the streak.

**Line 720** resets the streak on a miss: `LET j = 0`. One miss and the streak is gone.

## The Risk-Reward Loop

The streak creates tension:

```
Complete word → j = j + 1 → Will I reach 5?
                              ↓
Miss word → j = 0 → Start over
```

At streak 4, the player knows the bonus is one word away. They play more carefully — or rush and make a mistake. This emotional arc comes from a single counter variable.

## HUD Update

The HUD now shows the streak: `"Str:"; j`. The player can see how close they are to the bonus.

## Try This

**Bigger streaks.** Instead of resetting to 0 after the bonus, let the streak keep growing. Award a bonus every 5 words: `IF j / 5 = INT(j / 5) AND j > 0`.

**Streak multiplier.** Instead of a flat +5, multiply the bonus by the streak level: `LET e = e + j`. Longer streaks become much more valuable.

## What You've Learnt

- **Streak tracking** — counter increments on success, resets on failure
- **Bonus trigger** — action at a threshold
- **Visual feedback** — flash message for special events
- **Risk-reward** — the streak creates tension and rewards consistency
