## What You'll Build

Single-screen platformer pushing Sinclair BASIC to its limits. The perfect preparation for Z80 assembly.

Jump between platforms. Avoid enemies. Collect items. Attribute clash wars with your UDG sprites. Speed struggles against gameplay demands. This is Sinclair BASIC at its most ambitious — demonstrating exactly why assembly language exists.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Gravity simulation** — falling physics
- **Platform collision** — landing detection
- **Jump mechanics** — arcing motion
- **UDG-based player sprite** — custom character
- **Enemy movement** — patrol patterns
- **Collectibles** — item gathering
- **Attribute clash problems** — colour limitations
- **BASIC limitations exposed** — ready for assembly

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 7: Pattern Match** first.

## Time Investment

64 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 64-96 hours total.
