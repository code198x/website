With twenty-five puzzles across three bands, the player needs a way to choose which puzzle to play. A puzzle select screen shows all available puzzles, their star ratings, and which ones have been completed. The player picks a puzzle from the menu rather than playing them in strict sequence.

## How It Works

**Lines 800-830** draw the select screen. CLS clears the display, then PRINT AT lays out a grid of puzzle numbers. Each puzzle is shown with its number and star rating. Completed puzzles show their stars in yellow. Uncompleted puzzles show dashes.

**Lines 840-870** group puzzles by band. Band 1 puzzles appear on the first rows, Band 2 on the middle rows, Band 3 on the bottom rows. Each band has a label in its line colour — white, cyan or magenta.

**Lines 880-920** handle navigation. The player moves a highlight cursor with Q/A/O/P and presses SPACE to select a puzzle. The highlight is drawn using INVERSE 1 to show which puzzle is currently selected.

**Lines 930-940** load the selected puzzle. RESTORE jumps to the correct DATA line, and the game starts as normal. After the puzzle is completed (or the player presses a back key), the program returns to the select screen with updated stars.

## Locking and Unlocking

All Band 1 puzzles are available from the start. Band 2 puzzles unlock after completing at least 3 Band 1 puzzles. Band 3 puzzles unlock after completing at least 5 Band 2 puzzles. Locked puzzles are shown in a dim colour and cannot be selected.

This progression keeps new players from jumping straight to the hardest puzzles while giving experienced players quick access to the challenges they want.

## Return to Menu

After completing a puzzle, the player sees their star rating and then returns to the select screen. The select screen redraws with the new rating visible. This loop — select, play, rate, return — is the core game flow going forward.
