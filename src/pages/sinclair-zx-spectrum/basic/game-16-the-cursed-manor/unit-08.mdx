The ground and upper floors have fourteen rooms between them. Six more complete the manor: a cellar beneath the kitchen, a garden and courtyard outside, a tower base, a chapel, and a sealed chamber that will become important when the puzzles arrive.

## The Complete Geography

Twenty rooms, connected by compass directions and stairs. The cellar lies below the kitchen (GO DOWN). The garden lies south of the entrance hall (GO SOUTH exits the building). The courtyard, tower, and chapel spread around the grounds. The sealed chamber has no exits initially — it will be revealed by solving a puzzle later.

## How It Works

**Lines 9140-9190** define the final six rooms. The cellar is dark and damp. The garden is overgrown but peaceful — a contrast to the oppressive interior. The courtyard is stone-flagged with a dry fountain. The tower base has a spiral staircase going up (but the tower room itself is locked). The chapel is cold with broken stained glass. The sealed chamber has exits set to 0 — the player cannot reach it yet.

**The total room count** is now 20. `LET nr = 20` at the start of the program tells the loading loop how many DATA lines to read.

**The sealed chamber** (room 20) exists in the data but has no connections. No exit from any other room leads to it. It is invisible to the player until a puzzle unlocks it in Phase 3. This is a common adventure game technique — rooms exist in the data before they are accessible.

**The room numbering** follows a logical pattern: 1-8 ground floor, 9-14 upper floor, 15-20 cellar and grounds. This grouping makes the DATA section easy to navigate when you need to edit room descriptions later.

## Try This

**Walk the entire map.** Visit all 19 reachable rooms. Confirm that every connection works in both directions. Note any rooms that feel isolated or hard to reach — the map should flow naturally.

**Count the dead ends.** A dead end is a room with only one exit. Too many dead ends make navigation tedious. Aim for most rooms to have at least two exits.

**Write a map legend.** At the top of your paper map, list all 20 rooms by number and name. You will refer to this constantly as you add items and NPCs.
