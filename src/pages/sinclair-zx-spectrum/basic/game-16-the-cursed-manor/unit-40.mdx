The grimoire requires "the name of the caster, spoken aloud." Lady Ashworth's frozen lips form the word "Elara." The portrait in the drawing room shows a stern-faced woman with the name ELARA engraved on the frame. Two clues point to the same name — the player must connect them and speak it during the ritual.

## The SAY Command

A new command: SAY [word]. During the ritual, SAY ELARA triggers the curse-breaking sequence. Outside the ritual, SAY produces atmospheric responses — the word echoes in empty rooms, the frozen guests do not respond, the cat ignores you.

## How It Works

**Lines 1700-1730** handle the SAY verb. The noun is whatever word the player speaks. Outside the ritual context, the game prints "Your voice echoes through the empty room." or "The word hangs in the air, unanswered." These responses make SAY feel like a real action even before it matters.

**The ritual check** (implemented fully in unit 46) will test whether the player says "ELARA" in the drawing room while holding the lit lantern and the amulet. For now, SAY just acknowledges the input.

**Lines 1710-1720** add a special response for SAY ELARA in the drawing room: "The word resonates strangely. The air seems to shiver." This hints that the name has power here, without completing the ritual (the lantern and amulet must also be present).

**The portrait** (item 6, in the drawing room) confirms the name. Its examine text reads: "A portrait of a woman in dark clothing. Her expression is severe, almost angry. A brass plate on the frame reads: ELARA BLACKWOOD, 1782-1843." The surname connects her to Colonel Blackwood, adding story depth.

**Two confirmation sources** — Lady Ashworth's lips and the portrait — prevent the player from getting stuck if they miss one clue. Redundant clues for critical puzzle elements are good design.

## Try This

**Say the name.** Type SAY ELARA in different rooms. Note how the response changes in the drawing room versus elsewhere.

**Examine the portrait.** Read the full name — Elara Blackwood. The family connection to the Colonel adds narrative depth. Who was she? Why did she cast the curse? The game does not need to answer fully, but the question enriches the experience.

**Try other names.** Type SAY BLACKWOOD or SAY HARTLEY. The game should give a generic echo response. Only ELARA has power.
