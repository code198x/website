The grimoire is not just an item — it is a book that can be read. A new command, READ GRIMOIRE, displays a page of text that explains the ritual. This is the player's roadmap: what items they need, where to perform the ritual, and what words to speak.

## The READ Command

READ works like EXAMINE but displays different text. The player must be carrying the grimoire or standing in the same room. The grimoire is the only readable item, but building READ as a general command means you could add more readable objects later.

## How It Works

**Lines 1400-1440** handle the READ verb. If the noun matches "GRIMOIRE" or "BOOK", and the player has it (carried or in the room), the grimoire text displays. Otherwise: "There is nothing to read here."

**Lines 1410-1435** display the grimoire page. CLS clears the screen, then the text prints in a distinctive style — magenta ink on black, suggesting arcane writing. The text reads:

"To break a binding of stone, three things must align: the name of the caster, spoken aloud in the chamber of binding; a flame that burns without heat, kindled by mortal hand; and the focus — an amulet of cold light, placed at the centre."

**The text is deliberately incomplete.** It tells the player what they need (a name, a flame, an amulet, a location) but not where to find them or how to obtain them. The guest clues fill in those gaps. The grimoire is the "what"; the guests are the "where" and "how".

**After displaying the text**, the game pauses for a keypress and returns to the current room display. Like HELP, READ is an information screen that does not change game state.

## Try This

**Read the grimoire.** Take it from the tower, then type READ GRIMOIRE. The ritual requirements should be clear: a name, a flame, an amulet, and a specific location.

**Cross-reference with clues.** Compare the grimoire text to the guest clues from unit 26. The Colonel says "climb" (tower — done). Lady Ashworth gives the name "Elara". The Maid points to the cellar (tinderbox for flame). Mrs Pemberton has the combination (for the chamber). Dr Hartley confirms "name and flame". The pieces should fit.

**Try READ on other items.** Type READ KEY or READ LANTERN. The game should say "There is nothing written on that" or similar. Only the grimoire is readable.
