Frozen guests are scenery until the player examines them. EXAMINE COLONEL reveals the Colonel's clue — perhaps a note clutched in his stone hand, or words frozen on his lips. Each guest holds one piece of the puzzle. The player must examine all five to gather enough information to break the curse.

## Extending EXAMINE

The EXAMINE command already works for items. Now it needs to check guests too. When the player types EXAMINE followed by a name, the code searches items first, then guests. If a guest is found in the current room, their clue prints.

## How It Works

**Lines 700-720** extend the EXAMINE handler. After checking items, the code loops through the guest array. It matches the noun against guest names — "EXAMINE COLONEL" matches "Colonel Blackwood" because the code checks whether the noun appears within the guest name.

**Lines 97-101** set the clues for each guest:
- **Colonel Blackwood**: A note in his breast pocket reads "The tower holds the answer."
- **Lady Ashworth**: Her frozen lips are shaped around the word "chamber."
- **Dr Hartley**: The book he was reading is open to a page about binding rituals.
- **Mrs Pemberton**: She clutches a recipe card with strange symbols on the back.
- **The Maid**: She points toward the cellar stairs.

**The clue text** prints in cyan ink, visually distinct from normal descriptions. This signals to the player that the information is important — it is not just atmosphere, it is a puzzle piece.

**Frozen vs free** — when a guest is frozen (`gs(i) = 0`), the examine text describes their stone form and reveals the clue. When free (`gs(i) = 1`), the examine text changes to describe a living person. The clue is only available while they are frozen — once freed, they speak instead of holding clues.

## Try This

**Examine every guest.** Visit all five guests and examine each one. Write down the clues. Together, they should point toward: the tower, a hidden chamber, a ritual, symbols/combination, and the cellar.

**Try examining a guest in the wrong room.** Type EXAMINE COLONEL in the library. The game should say "You do not see that here." Guests, like items, can only be examined when present.

**Consider clue order.** The clues should work regardless of discovery order. A player who finds the Maid first and the Colonel last should still be able to piece together the puzzle. Review the clues with this in mind.
