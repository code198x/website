Everything leads to this moment. The player stands in the drawing room, carrying the amulet and the lit lantern with its cold flame. They type SAY ELARA. The curse breaks. The guests begin to wake. This is the climax of the game — the payoff for every puzzle solved and every clue gathered.

## The Ritual Conditions

Four things must be true simultaneously:
1. The player is in the drawing room (room 7)
2. The player carries the amulet (`ic(4) = 1`)
3. The lantern is lit with cold fire (`lt = 2`)
4. The player says "ELARA" (`v$ = "SAY"` and `noun$ = "ELARA"`)

If all four are met, the ritual triggers. If any is missing, the SAY command produces its normal atmospheric response — or a near-miss hint.

## How It Works

**Lines 1700-1750** expand the SAY handler. When the noun is "ELARA" and the player is in the drawing room, the code checks the remaining conditions:
- No amulet: "The word echoes strangely, but nothing happens. You feel you are missing something."
- No cold fire: "The word resonates. The amulet pulses — but the darkness absorbs the sound. You need light — but not ordinary light."
- All conditions met: the ritual triggers.

**Lines 1760-1820** are the ritual sequence. CLS clears the screen. A dramatic text sequence plays out:

"You speak the name: ELARA."
"The amulet blazes with white light."
"The cold flame leaps from the lantern and wraps around the amulet."
"A sound builds — not heard, but felt — a deep vibration in the stone."
"The light expands, filling the room, pouring through the doorways..."
"And then silence."

**Each line pauses** with a short PAUSE and a BEEP. The sequence takes about 15 seconds — long enough to feel significant, short enough to maintain tension.

**Lines 1830-1840** set the game state. A ritual flag `rf = 1` marks the ritual as complete. All five guests transition to waking state (`gs(i) = 1`) with staggered timers. The game continues — the player must now escape the manor before it is done.

**After the ritual**, the drawing room description changes: "The room looks different now. The dust is gone. The portraits seem warmer. Light filters through the windows where before there was only darkness."

## Try This

**Perform the ritual.** Gather all requirements and trigger it. Watch the sequence. Does it feel climactic? The pacing of the text and sound should create a peak moment.

**Try partial conditions.** Stand in the drawing room with the amulet but without the lantern. The hint should guide you. Try with the lantern but without the amulet. Each near-miss should push the player toward the solution.

**Try SAY ELARA elsewhere.** The atmospheric response should be different from the near-miss hints. The drawing room is special — the game should subtly acknowledge that.
