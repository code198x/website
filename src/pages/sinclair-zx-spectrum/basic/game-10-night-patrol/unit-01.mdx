Night Patrol is a stealth game. You sneak through a building, avoiding guards who patrol the corridors. The title screen sets the tone: black screen, white text, and a low-pitched beep that sounds like something unlocking in the dark.

## How It Works

**Line 10** sets everything to black. `BORDER 0`, `PAPER 0`, and `INK 7` create a dark screen with white text. This is the visual identity of the game — you're operating in darkness.

**Lines 30-60** display the title and instructions. `BRIGHT 1` makes "NIGHT PATROL" stand out. The instructions tell the player what to do: sneak past guards, collect the objective, reach the exit.

**Lines 70-80** play two low beeps. Short, quiet, atmospheric — not a jingle, just a sound that says "something is happening in the dark."

**Line 90** waits for a keypress with `PAUSE 0`. The 0 means "wait forever" — the program stops until the player presses any key.

## Setting the Mood

Games don't start with the game. They start with a feeling. The black screen, the brief sound, the simple instructions — this is the player's first impression. It tells them this isn't a loud arcade game. It's tense. It's quiet. Something is watching.

## Try This

**Different sounds.** Change the BEEP pitches. Higher values (like 20) sound like an alarm. Lower values (like -10) sound ominous. Which fits a stealth game better?

**Colour choice.** Try `INK 6` (yellow) for the title instead of white. Or `INK 4` (green) for a night-vision feel.
