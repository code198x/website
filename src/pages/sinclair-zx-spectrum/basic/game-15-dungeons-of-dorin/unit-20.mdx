Weapons let the player deal more damage. Armour lets them take less. The system is identical in structure: a bonus variable, floor-based scaling, automatic upgrade on pickup, and integration with the combat formula. Where weapons modify the attack roll, armour modifies the defence subtraction.

## How It Works

**Line 26** initialises the armour bonus `r` to 0. The player starts unarmoured.

**Line 760** in the chest subroutine handles armour drops. The formula `INT(e / 4) + 1` produces armour values that scale slightly slower than weapons — armour +2 does not appear until floor 4, and +3 not until floor 8. The upgrade check `IF t > r` works identically to weapons: only better armour is equipped.

**Line 506** in the status panel shows total defence as `f + r`. The player sees their combined defensive power at a glance.

**Lines 846-848** in the monster attack formula use `f + r` to reduce incoming damage. Higher armour means every monster hit deals less. Combined with the minimum damage rule (always at least 1), armour reduces average damage taken but never eliminates it entirely.

## Parallel Equipment Systems

Weapons and armour are mirror systems. Both use the same patterns:

| Aspect | Weapon | Armour |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Bonus variable | `w` | `r` |
| Stat modified | Attack | Defence |
| Floor scaling | `INT(e/3)+1` | `INT(e/4)+1` |
| Combat integration | `a + w` in player attack | `f + r` in monster attack |
| Display | AT line, green | DF line, cyan |

The slight difference in scaling means weapons improve faster than armour. This is deliberate — attack upgrades feel more impactful (bigger damage numbers) while defence upgrades are more subtle (slightly less damage taken). Both matter, but the player notices weapons more.

## Defence and Survivability

Defence operates differently from HP. Potions restore lost HP after damage. Armour prevents damage from happening. Over many fights, armour saves more total HP than potions can restore, because it applies to every hit in every combat. A +3 armour bonus reduces each incoming hit by 3 — across dozens of fights, that saves far more than 10 HP per potion.

This makes armour the most valuable long-term pickup, even though it feels less dramatic than a weapon upgrade or a healing potion. Understanding this is part of the game strategy.
