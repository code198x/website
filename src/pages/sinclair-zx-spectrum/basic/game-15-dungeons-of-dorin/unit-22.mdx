Chests are not the only source of loot. Defeated monsters can drop rewards — gold from every kill, and occasionally better equipment from tougher creatures. This gives the player a reason to fight rather than flee: combat is costly in HP but potentially profitable in gear.

## How It Works

**Lines 894-900** already award gold from defeated monsters — the monster DATA includes a gold reward column. Line 896 adds the gold to the player total and increments the kill counter.

This unit extends the victory handler to occasionally drop equipment. After the gold reward, a random roll determines whether additional loot appears. Tougher monsters have better drop chances. Rats drop nothing extra. Goblins might drop a small amount of bonus gold. Skeletons and above can drop potions or equipment upgrades.

The drop logic follows the same patterns as chest loot: floor-based weapon and armour scaling, potion carry limit checks, and automatic upgrade comparison. The only difference is the probability — monster drops are rarer than chest finds, making them bonus rewards rather than reliable sources of gear.

## Risk and Reward

Monster drops complete the risk-reward loop. Combat costs HP (risk). Avoiding combat preserves HP but yields no rewards. Fighting and winning gives gold plus a chance at equipment (reward). This creates a meaningful choice on every floor: fight every monster for maximum loot, or sneak past them to preserve health for the deeper floors.

The balance depends on the monster. Fighting a rat costs little HP and gives 2 gold. Fighting a troll costs significant HP and gives 12 gold plus a drop chance. The player must assess whether the potential reward justifies the HP expenditure — the same calculation that drives every dungeon crawl.

## Probability by Monster Type

Weaker monsters have lower drop rates because their DATA gold values are already their primary reward. Stronger monsters have higher drop rates because the player invested more HP to defeat them and deserves more in return. The dragon on floor 10 is the most rewarding fight in the game — if the player can survive it.
