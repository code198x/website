Potions heal 10 HP. Fountains heal everything. A healing fountain restores the player to full HP — but each one works only once, and they appear rarely. Not every floor has one. Finding a fountain on a deep floor can save a run. Walking past one when already healthy wastes a precious resource.

## How It Works

Healing fountains use cell value 4 in the dungeon array. During generation, a fountain is placed on roughly half the floors — a random check determines whether the current floor gets one. The placement subroutine finds an empty floor cell and sets it to 4.

In the viewport renderer, fountains display as a blue symbol. In the main loop, walking onto a fountain cell triggers healing: HP is set to maximum, a message confirms the restoration, and the cell value changes to 0 (floor) so the fountain cannot be used again.

The single-use design is critical. If fountains recharged, the player could camp on one and heal indefinitely between fights, removing all danger. One use per fountain means the player must choose when to use it — now, when lightly wounded, or later, when critically hurt.

## Rarity and Placement

Not every floor has a fountain. The random check means roughly half the floors between 2 and 8 will have one. Floor 1 is gentle enough without free healing. Floors 9 and 10 are meant to be dangerous — a fountain there would undercut the tension of the final approach to the Heart of Dorin.

Rarity makes fountains memorable. Finding one is an event. The player must decide immediately whether to use it or remember its location and come back later. Since the dungeon map shows explored cells, the player can navigate back to a fountain after a tough fight — if they remember where it was.

## World State Flags

The fountain introduces a cell that changes state. Before use, it is value 4. After use, it becomes value 0. This is the simplest form of world state — a cell that remembers whether it has been interacted with. The same pattern could apply to locked doors, broken walls, or any single-use feature.

## Try This

**Guarantee a fountain on floors 5 and 8.** These are transition floors where monster difficulty jumps. A reliable healing point makes the difficulty curve smoother.

**Add a visual change.** After a fountain is used, display it as a different character — a dried-up basin instead of a flowing source. This gives the player visual feedback that the fountain is spent.
