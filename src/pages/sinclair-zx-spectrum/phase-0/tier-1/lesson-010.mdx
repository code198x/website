One-bit audio—the Spectrum's beeper plays single tones. But with clever pitch and duration control, create recognizable game sounds: lasers sweep frequencies, explosions use random noise, jumps arc upward.

## Key Concepts

- BEEP duration,pitch creates sound
- Duration in seconds (0.01 = very short)
- Pitch in semitones (0=middle C, +12=octave up, -12=octave down)
- Single channel (one sound at a time)

## Sound Effects

```basic
10 REM Laser
20 FOR N=20 TO -20 STEP -2
30 BEEP 0.01,N
40 NEXT N
```

Sweeping pitch downward creates laser effect. Loop through pitches rapidly for characteristic descending tone.
