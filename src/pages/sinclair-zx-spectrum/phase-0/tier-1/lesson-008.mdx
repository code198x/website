PLOT draws pixels, but drawing complex shapes pixel-by-pixel is tedious. DRAW creates lines with one command. CIRCLE draws perfect circles instantly. Add INK, PAPER, and BRIGHT for colour control. Professional graphics in seconds.

## Key Concepts

- **DRAW x,y** - Relative line from last position
- **CIRCLE x,y,radius** - Draw circle centered at (x,y)
- **INK n** - Foreground colour (0-7)
- **PAPER n** - Background colour (0-7)
- **BORDER n** - Border colour (0-7)
- **BRIGHT 1** - Brighter colours

## Simple Box with DRAW

```basic
10 REM DRAW Demo
20 PAPER 0: INK 2: BORDER 0: CLS
30 PLOT 128,88
40 DRAW 50,0
50 DRAW 0,50
60 DRAW -50,0
70 DRAW 0,-50
80 PAUSE 0
```

PLOT sets starting position, then DRAW creates relative lines. Positive X moves right, negative moves left. Positive Y moves up, negative moves down.

## Circle Art

```basic
10 REM Circle Art
20 PAPER 0: INK 7: BORDER 1: CLS
30 FOR R=10 TO 80 STEP 10
40 CIRCLE 128,88,R
50 NEXT R
60 BRIGHT 1
70 FOR A=0 TO 350 STEP 30
80 LET X=128+60*COS(A)
90 LET Y=88+60*SIN(A)
100 CIRCLE X,Y,15
110 NEXT A
120 PAUSE 0
```

Concentric circles (varying radius) plus orbital pattern using trigonometry (COS/SIN).
