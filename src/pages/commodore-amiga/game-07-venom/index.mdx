## What You'll Build

Snake game. Eat food, grow longer, avoid yourself. Classic gameplay with ring buffer implementation.

Guide a growing serpent across the playfield. Each morsel consumed extends your body. The challenge: avoid colliding with your own ever-lengthening tail. This deceptively simple game teaches efficient data structures — specifically the ring buffer that makes snake growth manageable.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Ring buffer implementation** — efficient snake storage
- **Grid-based movement** — discrete motion
- **Self-collision detection** — body segment checks
- **Blitter segment rendering** — snake body drawing
- **Growth mechanics** — tail extension
- **Speed scaling** — increasing difficulty
- **Multiple game modes** — variants and options
