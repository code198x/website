## What You'll Build

Falling blocks puzzle. Tetris-style rotation and line clearing.

Blocks descend. Rotate and position them. Complete lines to clear and score. Speed increases. Competition heats up in two-player mode. This game teaches rotation mathematics, grid collision, and the satisfaction of a perfectly cleared line.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Piece rotation logic** — transformation maths
- **Grid-based collision** — can piece fit?
- **Line detection** — complete row finding
- **Line clearing** — row removal animation
- **Next piece preview** — upcoming piece display
- **Score multipliers** — combo rewards
- **Ghost piece preview** — landing position
- **Two-player mode** — competitive puzzle

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 6: Dungeon Doors** first.
