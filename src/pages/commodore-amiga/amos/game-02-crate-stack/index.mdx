## What You'll Build

Single-screen platformer. Jump between platforms, collect items, reach the exit. Introduction to physics in AMOS.

A warehouse of stacked crates. Jump from platform to platform. Collect items along the way. Reach the exit before time runs out. This game introduces gravity, velocity, and jump mechanics — the foundations of platform gaming.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Gravity and velocity** — physics variables
- **Jump mechanics** — arc and landing
- **Platform collision** — sprite-based detection
- **Bob basics** — Blitter Objects
- **Animation sequences** — sprite frames
- **Level data** — DATA statements
- **Multiple levels** — progression
- **Lives and scoring** — game systems

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 1: Star Catcher** first.

64 units at 60-90 minutes each. Roughly 64-96 hours total.
