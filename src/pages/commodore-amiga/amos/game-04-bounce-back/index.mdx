## What You'll Build

Breakout clone. Paddle, ball, and bricks with AMOS's smooth movement and sampled sound.

A wall of bricks awaits destruction. Your paddle bounces the ball upward. Break bricks to score. Catch power-ups for advantages. Double-buffered display with SCREEN SWAP ensures smooth animation.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Ball physics** — angles and reflection
- **Paddle control** — player movement
- **Brick destruction** — block removal
- **Power-up bricks** — special effects
- **Bob drawing** — brick graphics
- **SCREEN SWAP** — double buffering
- **Level editor concepts** — custom levels
- **Music during gameplay** — tracker tunes

## Prerequisites

Complete **Game 3: Space Swarm** first.
