## What You'll Build

Isometric action-adventure. 3D projection creates depth, block puzzles challenge your mind, exploration rewards the curious. Cadaver-style exploration.

Navigate a fortress rendered in isometric perspective. Depth sorting ensures objects draw in correct order. Push blocks to solve puzzles. Collect equipment to overcome obstacles. Multiple zones offer variety. Boss encounters test your combat skills.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Isometric projection maths** — 3D to 2D conversion
- **3D coordinate systems** — world representation
- **Depth sorting** — painter's algorithm
- **Isometric collision** — 3D space detection
- **Block puzzle mechanics** — push/pull logic
- **Equipment and inventory** — item management
- **Multiple zones** — world design
- **Boss encounters** — zone guardians
