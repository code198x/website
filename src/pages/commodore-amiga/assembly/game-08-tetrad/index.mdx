## What You'll Build

Falling blocks puzzle. Tetris-style rotation, stacking, and line clearing. The Blitter handles the heavy lifting.

Pieces descend. You rotate and position them. Complete lines vanish with Blitter-powered scrolling. The playfield compresses. Speed increases. This timeless puzzle teaches rotation mathematics, grid collision, and efficient Blitter usage for smooth gameplay.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Piece rotation maths** — four orientations
- **Grid collision detection** — can piece fit?
- **Line clearing** — Blitter scroll for removal
- **Next piece preview** — upcoming piece display
- **Ghost piece** — landing position indicator
- **Score multipliers** — combo rewards
- **Two-player mode** — competitive puzzle action
