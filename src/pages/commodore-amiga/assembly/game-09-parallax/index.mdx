## What You'll Build

Horizontal parallax shooter. Multiple scroll layers at different speeds — the effect that defined Amiga gaming. This is Shadow of the Beast territory.

Distant mountains drift slowly. Mid-ground terrain scrolls faster. Foreground rushes past. Your ship navigates through layered visuals while battling waves of enemies. This game teaches dual playfield mode, the Copper's role in parallax coordination, and MOD soundtrack integration.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Dual playfield mode** — layered backgrounds
- **Multiple scroll speeds** — parallax depth
- **Copper palette cycling** — gradient effects
- **Hardware scrolling** — Blitter updates
- **Enemy patterns** — scrolling world encounters
- **Terrain collision** — landscape hazards
- **Power-up system** — weapon upgrades
- **Boss encounters** — large enemies
- **Paula MOD replay** — tracker soundtrack
