## What You'll Build

Scrolling platformer. Large world exploration with camera that follows the player. Turrican-style adventure.

Explore expansive levels that scroll smoothly as you move. Dead zones keep the player centred without jerky camera motion. Checkpoints mark progress. Moving platforms challenge timing. Environmental hazards punish carelessness. This is platforming at Amiga scale.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Horizontal scrolling platformer** — smooth camera
- **Camera dead zones** — comfortable following
- **Large level data** — compression techniques
- **Checkpoints** — progress persistence
- **Moving platforms** — dynamic collision
- **Environmental hazards** — level-integrated threats
- **Parallax backgrounds** — depth layers
- **Extended enemy AI** — varied behaviours
