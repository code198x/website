## What You'll Build

Pac-Man style maze game. Collect pellets, avoid ghosts, power pills turn the tables. Ghost AI with distinct personalities.

Navigate corridors eating pellets. Four ghosts hunt you, each with unique behaviour — one chases directly, another ambushes, another patrols, another acts erratically. Grab power pills to reverse the hunt. This game teaches tile-based movement constraints and behaviour-driven AI.

## Skills You'll Master

- **Tile-based maze** — Blitter-rendered corridors
- **Constrained corridor movement** — grid-aligned motion
- **Ghost AI personalities** — scatter, chase, frightened modes
- **Power-up state machines** — mode transitions
- **Screen wrapping** — Copper-assisted edge handling
- **Speed progression** — increasing difficulty
- **Audio cues** — gameplay feedback sounds
