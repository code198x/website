## What You'll Build

Vertical scrolling shooter. Enemy formations descend, bullet patterns fill the screen, bosses require strategy. SWIV-style intensity.

The world scrolls downward as waves of enemies attack from above. Ground installations and airborne threats require different tactics. Bullet patterns create deadly mazes to navigate. Weapon upgrades grow your firepower. A dynamic rank system adjusts difficulty to your skill.

- **Ground and air targets** — mixed threats
- **Multi-phase bosses** — complex encounters
- **Weapon upgrade trees** — progression paths
- **Rank system** — dynamic difficulty
