## What You'll Build

Metroidvania. Connected world, ability-gating, backtracking, upgrades. The Amiga's answer to Super Metroid.

A vast world awaits exploration. Paths branch and reconnect. New abilities unlock previously inaccessible areas. Backtracking reveals secrets. A save system preserves your progress across sessions. Multiple endings reward thorough exploration. This expert-level game demands mastery of all previous techniques.

- **Ability-gating design** — powers unlock areas
- **Backtracking and shortcuts** — rewarding exploration

- **Save system** — battery backup
