## Core Problems Identified

### **Current Content Issues**
1. **Academic Approach**: Starts with "Meet the 6502 Processor" instead of exciting game creation
2. **Delayed Gratification**: Technical concepts taught before students see why they matter
3. **Dry Content**: Assembly mechanics in isolation rather than game development context
4. **Missing Hook**: No immediate visual/interactive results to engage students
5. **Wrong Order**: SID sound programming delayed until Lesson 21, should be Lesson 3

### **User Requirements**
- ✅ **Game-first approach**: Transform dry assembly instruction into exciting game development
- ✅ **Original games only**: No clones, derivatives, or copyrighted material
- ✅ **System-specific games**: Each platform gets unique games leveraging its strengths
- ✅ **Visual documentation**: Screenshots/videos showing progress at every lesson
- ✅ **Games not tools**: Every project must be an actual game with gameplay, challenge, fun
- ✅ **Incremental learning**: Layer assembly concepts through game development needs

## Game Development Transformation Strategy

### **New Learning Philosophy**
- **Assembly through gameplay**: Every technical concept learned in service of improving student's game
- **Immediate satisfaction**: Visible, playable results from lesson 1
- **Just-in-time teaching**: Introduce assembly concepts exactly when needed for next game feature
- **Incremental complexity**: Each lesson adds meaningful capability to their game
